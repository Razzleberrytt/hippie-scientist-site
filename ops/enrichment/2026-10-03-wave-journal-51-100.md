## Correction semantics

Ordinary enrichment is append-only. A same-entity/same-source refresh can replace an older evidence row only when it carries `corrects_evidence_key`, `expected_prior_evidence`, and `correction_reason`. The parser validates the expected prior identity and fails closed on mismatch.

## Batch rule

No trial exposure becomes a consumer dose recommendation. Null outcomes, interaction risks, formulation/species/strain identity, combination-product confounding, endpoint specificity, citation mismatches, and research gaps remain first-class structured evidence. `evidence_grade` and `safety_note` are retained through public claim/profile export.
