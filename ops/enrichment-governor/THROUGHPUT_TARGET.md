# Enrichment throughput target

The parallel A–H research system and hourly Evidence worker target approximately **5 useful source-grounded findings per hour**.

This is an execution target, not a scientific threshold and not a license to reduce quality.

## What counts

A counted finding is one distinct evidence proposition tied to an exact source identity and a scientifically material context such as formulation/species, population, comparator, exposure, duration, or outcome.

Terminal completion is preferred. A finding is terminal only after governed promotion or an explicit governed terminal disposition such as rejected, deprecated, inactive, not promoted, or quarantined.

If terminal completion is blocked by an external or governance dependency, newly validated/staged source-grounded findings may be reported separately as pipeline throughput, but staging must never be reported as terminal completion.

## What does not count

- paraphrases or near-duplicates
- generic mechanism prose
- unsourced claims
- artificial splitting of one proposition into trivial variants
- duplicate studies already represented by the same proposition
- work that bypasses source, scientific, safety, governor, or CI gates

## Operating rule

Prioritize closure backlog before lower-ROI new research. Re-rank after each completed finding/workpack cluster and continue until the run is exhausted, the approximate five-finding target is reached, or a genuine blocker remains.

A missed target with a precise blocker is preferable to lowering evidence quality or inflating the count.
