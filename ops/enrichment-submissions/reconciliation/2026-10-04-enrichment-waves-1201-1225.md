# Research enrichment Waves 1201–1225 — 2026-10-04

Ninth pass of the Waves 1001–1500 program, emphasizing nutrient status, cardiometabolic and renal evidence, liver-metabolic context, pregnancy/child-development boundaries, and safety-oriented null findings.

## Waves 1201–1225
1. **epa-dha-cardiovascular-af — PMID 42144851** — Meta-analysis of 25 RCTs/25,578 patients with established cardiovascular disease found combined EPA+DHA did not significantly reduce major adverse cardiovascular events or atrial fibrillation/postoperative atrial fibrillation; formulation, dose and background therapy may explain heterogeneity, but pooled clinical benefit was neutral.
2. **omega-3-metabolic-syndrome-psychosis — PMID 42190369** — Systematic review/meta-analysis of placebo-controlled trials in psychotic disorders found omega-3 supplementation did not consistently improve BMI, waist circumference, glucose, lipids or blood pressure; isolated subgroup signals did not survive pooled analysis.
3. **probiotic-plant-sterol-celiac-lipids — PMID 42197276** — Nine-week randomized double-blind placebo-controlled pilot in adults with coeliac disease and hypercholesterolemia found a combined Lactiplantibacillus-plus-plant-sterol/stanol formulation improved selected lipid and microbiome measures; small pilot design and combination exposure prevent ingredient-specific attribution.
4. **vitamin-d-ckd-anemia — PMID 42252089** — Meta-analysis of 12 older RCTs/779 patients with CKD-associated anemia reported higher hemoglobin and lower erythropoietin requirements with vitamin D supplementation, but the evidence corpus ended in 2018 and authors called for larger modern multicenter trials.
5. **nutraceuticals-endothelial-function — PMID 42277453** — Network meta-analysis comparing nutraceutical interventions for flow-mediated dilation in cardiovascular disease/hypertension ranked magnesium and vitamin D3 highly, with signals for flaxseed, barberry, folic acid and omega-3; indirect network rankings and variable certainty are not head-to-head proof.
6. **probiotic-triglycerides — PMID 42280356** — Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled 12-week trial in adults with mild hypertriglyceridemia found a two-strain probiotic combination reduced triglycerides and LDL from baseline and altered selected glucose/microbiome measures; per-protocol analysis and formulation specificity limit generalization.
7. **omega-3-hemodialysis — PMID 42321661** — Meta-analysis of randomized trials in hemodialysis patients found omega-3 supplementation did not reduce all-cause mortality but was associated with lower cardiovascular mortality, myocardial infarction and vascular-access events; much of the event signal was concentrated in one large trial.
8. **omega-3-oral-nutrition-heart-failure — PMID 42451160** — Open-label randomized 24-week trial in 38 heart-failure patients compared Mediterranean diet alone with diet plus omega-3-enriched oral nutritional supplements and found complex lipidomic changes that varied by sarcopenia status; combination exposure and small sample preclude omega-3-only attribution.
9. **omega-3-diabetic-kidney-disease — PMID 42517346** — Multimodal study combining Mendelian randomization, proteomic mediation and meta-analysis of 12 RCTs linked higher omega-3 status with lower DKD risk and reported selected supplementation benefits, but causal-genetic, mechanistic and trial evidence should remain analytically distinct.
10. **vitamin-d-cardiac-remodeling-ckd — PMID 42572961** — Systematic review/meta-analysis in CKD found higher observational vitamin-D status correlated with lower left-ventricular mass, but supplementation did not significantly improve LV mass index or ejection fraction; a possible septal-thickness signal was based on limited inconsistent evidence.
11. **magnesium-cardiometabolic — PMID 42588058** — Dose-response meta-analysis of 78 RCTs found small average improvements in several cardiometabolic markers with magnesium, including blood pressure, glucose, HbA1c, HOMA-IR, LDL, triglycerides and IL-6, while many anthropometric, liver, kidney and inflammatory outcomes were null.
12. **probiotics-diabetic-nephropathy — PMID 42824281** — Meta-analysis and trial-sequential analysis of nine RCTs/644 participants with diabetic nephropathy found probiotics improved several creatinine, metabolic and inflammatory outcomes, but did not significantly improve eGFR or 24-hour proteinuria; renal-function claims should preserve these mixed endpoints.
13. **probiotics-synbiotics-masld — PMID 42712386** — Meta-analysis of 30 RCT publications/1,516 patients with MASLD found adjunctive probiotics/synbiotics improved ALT, AST, TNF-alpha and selected glucose/lipid markers, while triglycerides and overall HOMA-IR were null; subgroup effects varied by age, region, duration and diabetes status.
14. **iron-folic-acid-adolescent-girls — PMID 42745534** — Cluster-randomized school trial in 306 adolescent girls in Ethiopia found weekly iron-folic acid supplementation reduced reported morbidity symptoms at later follow-up points, while growth-related effects were not broadly established; population and deficiency context remain essential.
15. **iron-pregnancy-route — PMID 42776532** — Updated systematic review/meta-analysis of randomized trials compared intravenous with oral iron for pregnancy iron-deficiency anemia across maternal hematologic, transfusion, reaction and neonatal outcomes; evidence concerns treatment route in diagnosed anemia, not routine supplementation of iron-replete pregnancies.
16. **iron-deficiency-neurophysiology — PMID 42796938** — Systematic mechanistic review across the lifespan found iron deficiency commonly associated with altered EEG/ERP patterns and delayed neural processing, with treatment-responsiveness evidence heterogeneous; electrophysiologic associations do not by themselves establish cognitive benefit from supplementation in iron-replete people.
17. **vitamin-d-thyroid-association — PMID 42826330** — Meta-analysis of 38 observational studies found lower serum vitamin D levels in thyroid disease, particularly autoimmune thyroid disorders, but the evidence is associative and cannot establish that vitamin D supplementation prevents or treats thyroid disease.
18. **vitamin-d-preeclampsia-prevention — PMID 42827236** — Network meta-analysis of eight randomized trials/4,078 high-risk pregnancies found some higher-dose vitamin-D regimens associated with lower estimated preeclampsia risk, but dose/regimen networks were sparse and indirect; no consumer dosing inference is authorized.
19. **prenatal-vitamin-d-child-cognition — PMID 42149595** — Post-hoc secondary analysis of a randomized pregnancy vitamin-D trial assessed offspring neuropsychological performance at age 10; because cognition was a later secondary endpoint and participants were unblinded years earlier, findings require cautious interpretation rather than broad prenatal-cognition claims.
20. **folic-acid-unmetabolized — PMID 42739064** — Systematic review found replacing folic acid with 5-MTHF reduced circulating unmetabolized folic acid in a small trial set, while erythrocyte-folate results were discordant and observational UMFA-outcome associations were inconsistent and confounded; clinical harm from UMFA remains unresolved.
21. **probiotic-zinc-vitamin-b-food-allergy — PMID 42760882** — Randomized trial in 796 children with food allergy found a combined compound-probiotic, zinc and vitamin-B product plus hydrolyzed formula improved several allergy and immune markers versus formula alone; multi-component design prevents attribution to probiotics, zinc or B vitamins individually.
22. **folinic-acid-fraa-pregnancy — PMID 42796952** — Preschool follow-up of a very small prenatal pilot RCT in folate-receptor-alpha-autoantibody-positive pregnancies reported fewer ASD diagnoses after folinic acid versus folic acid, but only 16 families were reassessed and confidence intervals were extremely wide; provocative pilot evidence only.
23. **choline-fasd-biomarkers — PMID 42797005** — Pilot biomarker analysis from a randomized choline trial in 24 children with fetal alcohol spectrum disorder found no robust treatment-specific biomarker pattern after multiple-testing correction; brain-derived extracellular-vesicle changes were largely time effects rather than differential choline effects.
24. **vitamin-d-elderly-mortality — PMID 42823826** — Reanalysis-style review of low-risk double-blind RCTs in adults aged 75+ found a small mortality signal favoring vitamin D, but no individual trial was confirmatory and the authors characterized the result as an evidence signal requiring confirmation.
25. **selenium-cardiovascular-status — PMID 42741176** — Systematic/bibliometric review found biologically coherent links between selenium status and cardiovascular pathways, but supplementation trials were generally neutral or inconclusive and the translational evidence remained fragmented; status associations must not be converted into routine supplementation claims.
