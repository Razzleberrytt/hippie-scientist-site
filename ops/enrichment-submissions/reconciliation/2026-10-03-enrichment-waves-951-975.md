# Research enrichment Waves 951–975 — 2026-10-03

Nineteenth pass of the Waves 501–1000 enrichment program.

## Operating rule
Every PMID was screened against Waves 1–950. Completed human studies and systematic reviews are separated from product-, combination-, category-, biomarker-, and correction-only evidence. Null findings remain first-class. No runtime/public promotion occurs before final Wave-1000 consolidation.

## Waves 951–975
1. **hmb — PMID 42441818** — Randomized triple-blind HMB free-acid trial in trained/untrained men with high-intensity functional exercise; aerobic-capacity support was more apparent than strength, with training-status and regimen boundaries.
2. **l-citrulline — PMID 42280350** — Open-label randomized post-COVID trial in 43 adults; ICAM-1 decreased and 6-minute walk improved, while body composition did not differ between groups; disease-specific and small-study evidence.
3. **beetroot-nitrate — PMID 42445795** — 33-study exercise meta-analysis; modest HI sprint, mean-power and VO2max signals with potential athlete/sport/timing modifiers; performance context only.
4. **5-htp — PMID 42160304** — Acute randomized trial in adults with high/low ADHD traits; 5-HTP produced no significant positive effect on distractibility measures, providing a useful null counterweight.
5. **citrulline-malate — PMID 42162613** — Pilot 6-week RCT in 20 physically active older women; sit-to-stand improved, while several between-group performance, biomarker and QoL outcomes were nonsignificant; preliminary evidence.
6. **citrulline-malate — PMID 42043075** — Very small randomized crossover in 11 trained male sprinters; acute 8-g CM modestly improved repeated 100-m performance and reduced pre-second-sprint RPE; acute athletic context only.
7. **fiber-blend — PMID 42782339** — Three-week proprietary fiber-blend RCT in 26 IBS patients following low-FODMAP diets; microbiota and GI-specific anxiety changed while gut symptoms, sleep and measured biomarkers remained stable/null.
8. **black-tea-inulin-dextrin — PMID 39616149** — Acute crossover T2D study; black tea with inulin+dextrin reduced postprandial glucose fluctuation/AUC and insulin response versus placebo; combination-only evidence.
9. **dietary-interventions-gut-microbiota — PMID 41501909** — Systematic review of 80 controlled diet trials; taxonomy and biomarker changes occurred across diets but alpha/beta diversity changes were not consistent; broad dietary category evidence only.
10. **gut-microbiome-cancer-interventions — PMID 41475679** — Cancer-treatment SR/MA: gut-microbiome interventions reduced GI adverse events overall with high heterogeneity, while objective cancer-treatment response did not clearly improve.
11. **dietary-fiber — PMID 40647069** — Seven-RCT/187-participant elderly-constipation meta-analysis; stool frequency was not significantly improved, while laxative/enema use fell and bifidobacteria increased.
12. **multiple-micronutrients-development — PMID 41470911** — Long-term randomized-evidence review of maternal/early-childhood micronutrient supplementation; most cognitive outcomes were null with a few modest domain-specific developmental signals.
13. **macro-micronutrients-orthopedic — PMID 42111841** — Broad orthopedic nutrition meta-analysis; BMD/biochemical/inflammation/recovery signals contrasted with null fracture-healing and functional/muscle outcomes; high heterogeneity limits attribution.
14. **berry-supplements — PMID 42196965** — Nine-RCT/547-participant hypertension/elevated-BP meta-analysis; resting SBP/DBP and cardiometabolic markers were null, with only a small ambulatory DBP signal and low/very-low certainty.
15. **polyphenols-resistance-training — PMID 42729406** — Eleven-RCT/360-participant review; chronic polyphenols did not establish reliable added strength, lean-mass or muscle-morphology adaptation beyond resistance training.
16. **probiotics — PMID 42694318** — Twenty-RCT mental-health systematic review; some psychological/sleep/microbiome signals but cortisol/stress findings inconsistent and strain/population/outcome heterogeneity substantial.
17. **polyphenols-exercise-recovery — PMID 42761264** — Twenty-four-RCT three-level meta-analysis; small soreness benefit, but muscle-damage biomarkers and performance effects remained uncertain with low/very-low certainty.
18. **broad-spectrum-micronutrients — PMID 41643810** — Eight-week RCT in 132 adolescents with irritability; some clinician/parent outcomes improved while one primary irritability measure was null; subgroup effects and pediatric formulation context retained.
19. **herbal-hemorrhoid-cream — PMID 42812757** — Triple-blind trial of a four-herb topical cream versus conventional cream; both improved symptoms, but no significant between-group effect or interaction was shown; combination-only and high-dropout context.
20. **strawberry-leaf-extract — PMID 42508219** — Older-adult trial of strawberry leaf extract for self-reported memory complaints; leaf material must remain distinct from strawberry fruit and sponsor/product provenance retained.
21. **mentha-amaranthus-combination — PMID 41519619** — Mentha spicata + Amaranthus seed flour + flavonoids/B-vitamins trial; highest-dose lipid signals belong only to the multi-ingredient combination.
22. **ulva-lactuca — PMID 42514313** — Small 28-day Ulva lactuca trial in healthy adults; biomarker-only outcomes, 18 completers and industry provenance require cautious research-only handling.
23. **ecklonia-chrysanthemum-combination — PMID 41523268** — Twelve-week Ecklonia cava + Chrysanthemum indicum RCT; respiratory signals with some QoL nulls, attributable only to the combination.
24. **naturopathy-review — PMID 42403781** — Broad naturopathy review retained for research mapping only; exact intervention-level extraction is required before any canonical claim.
25. **probiotic-bone-health-correction — PMID 42313181** — Published correction associated with probiotic bone-health evidence; correction provenance must travel with any downstream use of the corrected source.
