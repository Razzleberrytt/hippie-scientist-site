# Research enrichment Waves 976–1000 — 2026-10-04

Twentieth and final pass of the Waves 501–1000 enrichment program.

## Operating rule
Every PMID was screened against prior enrichment waves and the active 501–975 branch. Human randomized evidence and systematic reviews are separated from formulation-, combination-, route-, category-, identity-, and correction-only evidence. Null findings remain first-class. No publication, recommendation, dosing, or governance promotion occurs from research receipts alone.

## Waves 976–1000
1. **magnesium-l-threonate — PMID 41601871** — Six-week randomized double-blind trial in 100 adults with dissatisfied sleep; magnesium L-threonate improved the NIH total-cognition composite and sleep-related impairment, while objective wearable sleep outcomes and several subjective sleep measures were null; proprietary-formulation and short-duration boundaries retained.
2. **magnesium-l-threonate-correction — PMID 40567408** — Published corrigendum/erratum to the 2024 magnesium L-threonate sleep trial (PMID 39252819); correction provenance must remain attached to any downstream use of the corrected study.
3. **curcumin — PMID 42534858** — Three-RCT meta-analysis found no statistically significant homocysteine-lowering effect, with very low certainty, extreme heterogeneity, and one physiologically implausible source dataset; strong null/evidence-quality counterweight.
4. **probiotics-synbiotics-berberine — PMID 42213651** — Systematic review/meta-analysis pooled probiotics, synbiotics, and berberine in type 2 diabetes; modest glycemic signals with substantial intervention heterogeneity mean effects cannot be attributed interchangeably across the three intervention classes.
5. **beetroot-nitrate — PMID 41996285** — Twelve-study/357-participant COPD meta-analysis found a small exercise-capacity signal and lower blood pressure, but parallel and crossover trial estimates diverged materially; disease-specific methodological boundary retained.
6. **beetroot-nitrate — PMID 42588136** — Twenty-three randomized sprint studies (401 participants) found benefits for time-to-peak-power and handgrip but no clear effect on short sprint times, mean/peak power, RPE, heart rate, or lactate; certainty was low to very low.
7. **probiotics-synbiotics-sleep — PMID 42184272** — Six RCTs in exercised populations suggested subjective sleep-quality/latency signals, but strain/product heterogeneity, small total sample, and athlete-specific context prevent a generic probiotic sleep claim.
8. **vitamin-d — PMID 41923913** — Fifteen-trial meta-analysis in diagnosed depression reported lower depressive symptom scores with vitamin D but high heterogeneity; dose-response findings are retained as study-level evidence only and not converted into consumer dosing guidance.
9. **vitamin-d-containing-supplements — PMID 42594843** — Bayesian review found a favorable but heterogeneous depressive-symptom signal for vitamin-D-containing interventions; fragmented treatment networks and co-nutrient variation made treatment ranking and component-level synergy inference indefensible.
10. **melatonin — PMID 42708337** — Cancer-population sleep meta-analysis found no significant overall pooled benefit with very high heterogeneity; selected subgroup/qualitative signals did not overturn the overall uncertainty.
11. **nutraceuticals-depression — PMID 40314175** — Large network meta-analysis of 192 trials/17,437 participants compared 44 nutraceutical strategies for depressive disorders; useful comparative map but compound, adjunctive-treatment, bias, and network-inference boundaries require category-level handling.
12. **plant-foods-exercise-recovery — PMID 42797032** — Thirty-six human intervention studies of plant-derived foods/supplements for post-exercise recovery showed nonuniform soreness/recovery signals with major product, timing, dose, exercise-protocol, and outcome heterogeneity; category-level only.
13. **aromatherapy-cancer — PMID 39037416** — Twenty-six-study cancer aromatherapy meta-analysis reported sleep, fatigue, anxiety, depression, and vomiting signals, but mixed oils, delivery routes, massage co-intervention, and cancer-context boundaries prevent ingredient-level attribution.
14. **aromatherapy-menopause — PMID 42709539** — Seventeen-study menopause aromatherapy synthesis reported improvements across several symptom domains, but certainty was low/very low and intervention heterogeneity was substantial; complementary-therapy category evidence only.
15. **alpha-lipoic-acid — PMID 41077538** — Sixty-three-RCT dose-response meta-analysis found small changes across several glycemic, anthropometric, and lipid risk factors, with null findings for LDL-C, blood pressure, and HDL-C; surrogate-outcome boundary retained.
16. **alpha-lipoic-acid — PMID 42535603** — Eight-study/165-participant schizophrenia review found no significant placebo-controlled improvement in PANSS, BMI, adverse events, or discontinuation and no consistent metabolic benefit; small-study null evidence retained.
17. **inositol — PMID 41757236** — Umbrella review of 13 PCOS meta-analyses found signals across hormonal, insulin-resistance, and reproductive outcomes but no high-quality GRADE evidence and important subtype/comparator differences; formulation boundaries retained.
18. **taurine — PMID 41275513** — Thirty-four-RCT meta-analysis reported pooled improvements across several cardiometabolic biomarkers; findings remain surrogate-endpoint evidence and dose/duration subgroup patterns are not converted into optimization guidance.
19. **coenzyme-q10 — PMID 41630501** — Thirty-nine-RCT meta-analysis found small glycemic-marker changes with CoQ10, especially in diabetes populations, but overall certainty was rated very low; effect size and certainty boundaries retained.
20. **ashwagandha — PMID 42198398** — Systematic review of 23 trials/2,317 healthy adults found standardized root-only extracts generally well tolerated over studied durations, while explicitly limiting inference beyond 180 days and to nonstandardized, leaf, or multi-ingredient products.
21. **carnosine — PMID 42123986** — Thirteen-study review of carnosine/anserine-containing interventions found mostly null cognitive and mood outcomes with selective memory signals; combination products, single-team pooled effects, and low strength of evidence prevent a broad carnosine cognition claim.
22. **nad-precursors — PMID 41655607** — PRISMA review of 33 human and 80 rodent intervention studies found NR/NMN reliably changed NAD-related biomarkers but human functional, metabolic, vascular, and healthspan outcomes were heterogeneous/often null; no eligible IV/IM NAD outcome trials supported wellness claims.
23. **coenzyme-q10 — PMID 41657017** — Fourteen-RCT/433-participant exercise meta-analysis found lower malondialdehyde and creatine kinase with CoQ10 but no clear effect on lactate or maximal oxygen uptake; biomarker and performance endpoints kept separate.
24. **coenzyme-q10 — PMID 41859772** — Umbrella review of eight meta-analyses found glycemic conclusions sensitive to analytic scale: fasting glucose improved most consistently while HbA1c/HOMA-IR/insulin conclusions differed by pooling approach; inconsistency retained.
25. **coenzyme-q10 — PMID 42642774** — Twenty-RCT GRADE-assessed prediabetes/T2D meta-analysis reported improvements in triglycerides, fasting glucose, HbA1c, HOMA-IR, CRP, and HDL but null total cholesterol, LDL, blood pressure, and malondialdehyde; disease-specific surrogate evidence.
