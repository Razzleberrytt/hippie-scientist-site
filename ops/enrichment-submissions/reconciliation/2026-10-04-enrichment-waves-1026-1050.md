# Research enrichment Waves 1026–1050 — 2026-10-04

Second pass of the Waves 1001–1500 deep-enrichment program.

## Operating rule
Every PMID was screened against prior enrichment waves and the active 1001–1025 branch. Direct entity evidence is separated from category, formulation, retraction, mixed-study-design, and evidence-quality receipts. Null and contradictory findings remain first-class.

## Waves 1026–1050
1. **ginkgo-egb-761 — PMID 41908799** — Pooled meta-analysis of four randomized placebo-controlled dementia trials (488 patients) with prior cerebral infarction found statistically significant benefits for cognition, activities of daily living and global clinical impression with proprietary EGb 761; subgroup/proprietary-extract boundaries remain explicit.
2. **pomegranate — PMID 41265522** — Systematic review of seven rheumatic-disease studies found promising symptom/inflammatory signals in rheumatoid and osteoarthritis populations, but overall certainty was low to very low because of small samples, heterogeneous preparations and short follow-up.
3. **ginger — PMID 41195902** — Forty-one-RCT systematic review found pooled improvements in several glycemic, lipid, blood-pressure and inflammatory biomarkers, while multiple other outcomes were null; surrogate-endpoint and baseline-risk modifiers remain explicit.
4. **ginger — PMID 42337792** — Thirteen-trial type-2-diabetes meta-analysis found statistically significant fasting-glucose, HbA1c, systolic-BP, triglyceride and HDL changes, but HOMA-IR, diastolic BP, total cholesterol, LDL and BMI were null and effect magnitudes were often of limited clinical importance.
5. **ginger — PMID 42566667** — GRADE-based 12-RCT blood-pressure meta-analysis found modest SBP/DBP reductions with ginger but rated certainty low because of inconsistency, short duration and limited robustness; dose-response subgroup findings are study evidence rather than dosing guidance.
6. **nutritional-supplements-cognition — PMID 41764841** — Network meta-analysis of randomized trials in healthy aging/MCI found domain-specific signals for several nutrient combinations and single nutrients, but rankings were sensitive to sample size/duration and the heterogeneous interventions prevent ingredient-level equivalence claims.
7. **vitamin-b12 — PMID 42227307** — Cochrane review of 16 randomized trials (4,083 children) evaluated oral vitamin B12 for growth, development and cognition; outcomes varied by endpoint and trial context, requiring age, baseline-status, co-nutrient and duration boundaries rather than a universal pediatric cognition claim.
8. **melatonin — PMID 42070664** — Thirty-one-RCT glycemic meta-analysis found no significant fasting-glucose reduction with melatonin, while HbA1c, fasting-insulin and HOMA-IR signals improved; fasting-glucose null and metabolic-population boundaries remain first-class.
9. **melatonin — PMID 42192324** — Twelve-trial/1,977-participant cancer-fatigue meta-analysis found a pooled fatigue reduction but very low GRADE certainty and substantial heterogeneity; apparent exposure/duration patterns are not converted into consumer dosing guidance.
10. **melatonin — PMID 42230276** — Five randomized trials (323 hemodialysis patients) found improved PSQI and selected sleep domains with melatonin; the disease-specific evidence remains limited in certainty and should not be generalized to primary insomnia.
11. **melatonin — PMID 42726650** — Five RCTs/561 breast-cancer radiotherapy patients found less fatigue and improvements in cognitive/global-health measures, with several emotional, social, physical, gastrointestinal, pain and sleep domains null; small samples and heterogeneous regimens limit inference.
12. **melatonin — PMID 42770627** — Seven randomized IBS trials (391 adults) found improvements in overall symptom severity, pain frequency and defecation frequency, while quality of life and global sleep quality were not significantly improved; short-term IBS-specific evidence only.
13. **ashwagandha — PMID 40887707** — Systematic/ethnobotanical review found historical abortifacient claims vulnerable to citation distortion and available animal/human data did not establish significant teratogenic or abortifacient activity; absence of robust pregnancy-specific controlled evidence keeps reproductive-safety conclusions incomplete.
14. **saffron — PMID 41693488** — GRADE-assessed meta-analysis of 34 RCTs/1,769 adults found improvements on self-reported BDI/BAI scores but no significant HDRS, HARS or POMS effects; high heterogeneity and discordance between self- and clinician-rated outcomes constrain broad antidepressant/anxiolytic claims.
15. **polyphenols-soccer-recovery — PMID 42197097** — Eight RCTs in adult male soccer players found inconsistent biomarker/functional recovery effects, with more favorable soreness/well-being trends in selected tart-cherry, beetroot and curcumin studies; certainty was low or very low and remains category-level.
16. **black-cohosh — PMID 41401209** — Systematic review of 14 black-cohosh clinical-trial reports found wide variation in CONSORT herbal-intervention and harms-reporting quality; the review chiefly informs evidence quality and adverse-event reporting rather than establishing efficacy.
17. **menopause-complementary-therapies — PMID 41498229** — International Menopause Society evidence update reviewed 158 studies and found most complementary-therapy evidence low/very-low certainty, with moderate-certainty support for selected black-cohosh/CHM/acupuncture combinations and vitamin-D outcomes; broad category evidence only.
18. **non-soy-menopause-herbs — PMID 42451042** — Thirty-RCT review of 19 non-soy herbal interventions found substantial heterogeneity; most studies reported some symptom improvement but black cohosh and dong quai trials included null findings, preventing a class-wide efficacy conclusion.
19. **garlic — PMID 40580481** — Updated meta-analysis of 108 RCTs/7,137 adults found modest pooled improvements across several lipid, glycemic, blood-pressure, inflammatory and oxidative biomarkers, while ApoA1/ApoB, ALT, IL-6, BMI, weight and adiponectin were null; surrogate-outcome boundaries retained.
20. **aged-garlic-extract-retraction — PMID 42527229** — 2026 retraction notice for a prior aged-garlic-extract blood-pressure meta-analysis; downstream evidence systems must quarantine the retracted synthesis and preserve retraction provenance rather than continuing to cite its pooled estimate.
21. **vitamins-endometriosis — PMID 41683896** — Seven-RCT review of vitamin D and vitamins C/E in endometriosis reported selected pain/oxidative-stress signals but no significant pregnancy-rate improvement; multiple vitamins and heterogeneous regimens make this category-level adjunctive evidence.
22. **vitamin-e — PMID 42092748** — Twelve-RCT MASLD meta-analysis found a triglyceride reduction with vitamin E but no significant overall total-cholesterol, LDL, HDL or BMI change; disease-specific lipid evidence is mixed and largely surrogate.
23. **l-carnitine — PMID 40864195** — Systematic review of nine pediatric dialysis studies (194 patients) found limited, outcome-specific signals for hemoglobin/ESA use and cardiac function, with several lipid/inflammatory/QoL outcomes null; small heterogeneous evidence base limits pediatric inference.
24. **l-carnitine — PMID 41709059** — Systematic review across mechanistic and clinical CAD evidence reported possible inflammatory/oxidative and selected clinical signals, but heart-failure and reinfarction results were inconsistent; mixed study designs prevent a high-certainty treatment claim.
25. **l-carnitine-acetyl-l-carnitine-toxicity — PMID 41692009** — Systematic review of clinical and experimental poisoning evidence found toxin-specific, mostly adjunctive signals for L-carnitine/acetyl-L-carnitine, with no basis to replace established antidotes/supportive care; heterogeneous toxins and mixed human/preclinical evidence require category-level handling.
