8. **Echinacea — PMID 28406931** — common-cold synthesis with mixed prevention/treatment effects across preparations, outcomes and timing.
9. **Saw palmetto — PMID 29127377** — little/no meaningful urinary-symptom improvement versus placebo in typical preparations; important BPH counterweight.
10. **Pygeum — PMID 11349412** — historical BPH review; possible modest urinary benefit, small trials and limited long-term evidence.
11. **Pumpkin seed preparations — PMID 35206390** — LUTS/BPH evidence is promising but heterogeneous; seed oil/extract/products cannot be flattened.
12. **Nettle root — PMID 16118856** — BPH evidence includes combinations and older trials; isolated-nettle efficacy uncertain.
13. **Maca — PMID 19586488** — sexual-function/libido systematic review; limited evidence, few trials, methodological weaknesses.
14. **Maca — PMID 38151493** — menopausal-symptom evidence small/heterogeneous; symptom outcomes remain distinct from hormones.
15. **Tribulus terrestris — PMID 36398399** — insufficient/inconsistent erectile-function evidence; counterweight to testosterone-style claims.
16. **Fenugreek — PMID 30965583** — standardized-extract sexual-function evidence; proprietary formulation and population boundaries.
17. **Ashwagandha — PMID 33331579** — male-fertility evidence in stressed/infertile men; semen/hormonal measures are surrogate and population-specific.
18. **Valerian + hops — PMID 23843413** — combination-only sleep evidence; cannot be inherited by valerian alone.
19. **Bacopa — PMID 19336217** — selected memory outcomes after chronic use; not evidence of immediate cognitive enhancement.
20. **Ginkgo biloba — PMID 19807940** — healthy-adult randomized evidence does not establish broad cognitive enhancement.
21. **Ginkgo biloba — PMID 19160171** — dementia cognition/function evidence mixed and standardized-extract/disease-population specific.
22. **Panax ginseng — PMID 22179287** — possible acute domain-specific cognitive signals in controlled trials; limited evidence.
23. **Panax ginseng — PMID 23403007** — chronic-fatigue review; limited/inconsistent evidence with diagnosis/context and preparation boundaries.
24. **American ginseng — PMID 19043942** — acute cognitive-performance RCT evidence; Panax quinquefolius remains distinct from Asian ginseng.
25. **L-theanine — PMID 24791768** — selected stress/attention effects in controlled human evidence; isolated theanine, tea and combination exposures stay distinct.
