# Research enrichment Waves 1426–1450 — 2026-10-04

Eighteenth pass of the Waves 1001–1500 program, emphasizing sweeteners, fermented foods, sleep, gut health, postbiotics, dairy, immune markers, and functional-food evidence while preserving null findings and formulation boundaries.

## Waves 1426–1450
1. **sweetened-cola-hormonal-response — PMID 41016269** — Randomized crossover trial in 20 healthy adults found sucrose produced the largest acute glucose/insulin response, while aspartame/acesulfame-K resembled carbonated water and sucrose/stevia produced distinct pancreatic-polypeptide and later glucose responses; small acute physiology study only.
2. **allulose-tagatose-glycemic-meta — PMID 41985675** — Systematic review/meta-analysis of controlled human trials found allulose and tagatose lowered postprandial glucose and insulin, while longer-term cardiometabolic and body-composition effects were limited or uncertain; heterogeneity was substantial for some outcomes.
3. **erythritol-sucrose-sucralose-reward — PMID 42184911** — Randomized double-blind crossover study found erythritol, sucrose and sucralose produced similar learned liking/wanting responses despite different metabolic hormone effects; reward findings were small-sample and mechanistic.
4. **sweet-protein-glycemia — PMID 42208445** — Randomized crossover trial in 19 healthy adults found the sweet protein sweelin did not increase glucose, insulin or GLP-1 relative to stevia and produced far lower responses than dextrose; this is acute formulation-specific metabolic evidence.
5. **allulose-tolerance-safety — PMID 42375569** — Thirty-day randomized double-blind study in 50 healthy adults found D-allulose produced frequent but generally mild transient GI symptoms and no clinically significant laboratory toxicity; short duration and limited population constrain a general long-term safety conclusion.
6. **sweeteners-short-term-metabolic — PMID 42514406** — Fourteen-day randomized crossover trial found sucrose, saccharin and steviol-glycoside beverages produced broadly similar glucose-regulation outcomes in healthy adults, with some within-condition changes and higher triglycerides after sucrose; no consistent superiority of noncaloric sweeteners was shown.
7. **stevia-acute-endocrine — PMID 42680068** — Randomized crossover study across normal-weight, overweight and type-2-diabetes cohorts found acute stevia did not materially alter glycemia, gut hormones, appetite or subsequent energy intake versus water, nor modify glucose responses when co-ingested with glucose.
8. **erythritol-plasma-weightloss-observational — PMID 42759771** — Secondary observational analysis within a randomized weight-loss trial found decreases in endogenous plasma erythritol tracked with improved estimated cardiovascular risk and atherogenic lipids; association does not establish effects of consuming erythritol.
9. **blueberry-leaf-tea-sleep — PMID 41683277** — Two-week randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in adults with poor sleep found fermented blueberry-leaf tea improved sleep efficiency and wake-after-sleep-onset but not sleep latency, total sleep time or subjective sleep measures; small short-duration functional-food evidence.
10. **yogurt-oats-gut-microbiome — PMID 41687784** — Randomized crossover study in 119 healthy adults found yogurt transiently increased yogurt-associated microbes, while adding oats produced little broad effect on SCFAs or host health markers; the healthy microbiome was largely resilient and individual responses varied.
11. **fermented-whey-frailty — PMID 41805014** — Ten-week randomized blinded trial in 45 older adults found fermented whey protein improved selected physical-performance and skeletal-muscle-mass outcomes versus dextrin placebo, but the formulation also materially increased vitamin/mineral intake, limiting protein-fermentation attribution.
12. **dairy-cognition-older-adults — PMID 41822303** — Systematic review/meta-analysis found randomized dairy interventions improved selected global cognition, memory and processing-speed outcomes in older adults, while observational studies were neutral; product heterogeneity and fermented/fortified subgroups complicate category-wide claims.
13. **fermented-milk-protein-children-performance — PMID 41929774** — Pilot randomized double-blind trial in prepubertal boys found fermented and non-fermented milk protein improved short-sprint performance versus placebo, with no consistent fermented-protein superiority and no clear lean-mass advantage; exploratory microbiome findings were noncausal.
14. **brewers-spent-grain-metabolic-gut — PMID 41940993** — Small randomized trial in adults with metabolic impairment found brewer's-spent-grain biscuits increased fiber intake and shifted selected gut taxa/metabolites, while fermented BSG increased HDL; most other metabolic outcomes were unchanged.
15. **kefir-gut-microbiota-review — PMID 42036973** — Systematic review of eight human intervention studies found kefir produced modest, heterogeneous microbiome changes, with some increases in Bifidobacterium and transient kefir-associated taxa; direct functional or clinical evidence remained limited.
16. **red-ginseng-complex-hangover — PMID 42112120** — Randomized double-blind crossover trial found a multi-ingredient Korean red-ginseng complex accelerated early ethanol/acetaldehyde clearance and improved hangover symptom scores; proprietary combination prevents attribution to ginseng alone and does not make alcohol use safe.
17. **lactiplantibacillus-k014-safety — PMID 42124006** — Six-month randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in healthy adults found Lactiplantibacillus plantarum K014 was well tolerated with no probiotic-related adverse events; GI/common-cold signals were exploratory efficacy observations.
18. **fermented-noni-immune-markers — PMID 42280334** — Randomized double-blind trial in adults with recurrent URTIs found fermented noni polysaccharides did not significantly improve the primary NK-cell-activity endpoints in modified intention-to-treat analysis or reduce URTI incidence; per-protocol immune-marker signals were secondary.
19. **postbiotic-fermented-milk-constipation — PMID 42451118** — Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in 100 adults with functional constipation found a heat-inactivated fermented-milk postbiotic did not improve bowel-movement frequency, transit time, symptoms or quality of life versus placebo despite exploratory neurotransmitter-biomarker changes.
20. **postbiotic-oat-microbiome — PMID 42486470** — Four-week randomized double-blind trial of a fermented pasteurized oat postbiotic found selective species-level microbiome changes without alpha/beta-diversity shifts; cortisol, IL-8 and well-being signals were exploratory and no clinical efficacy was established.
21. **lactococcus-cremoris-sleep — PMID 42593206** — Eight-week randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in 105 adults dissatisfied with sleep found Lactococcus cremoris YRC3780 improved Athens Insomnia Scale scores and selected sleep-onset/daytime-sleepiness items; strain-specific replication remains important.
22. **fermented-dairy-cardiometabolic — PMID 42733680** — Twelve-week randomized trial in adults with overweight/obesity and elevated triglycerides found full-fat fermented dairy modestly reduced waist circumference versus nondairy control, while insulin sensitivity and most other cardiometabolic markers were unchanged.
23. **edible-birds-nest-metabolomics — PMID 42409548** — Twelve-week randomized double-blind trial in healthy women found edible bird's nest produced dose- and time-dependent metabolomic shifts in amino-acid and lipid pathways; clinical significance and health outcomes were not established.
24. **heat-treated-lp-lm1004-immune — PMID 42487409** — Randomized clinical trial found heat-treated Lactiplantibacillus plantarum LM1004 increased NK-cell activity and altered microbiome-related endpoints, while mechanistic ecosystem data came from simulation; immune-marker improvement does not establish reduced infection risk.
25. **lacticaseibacillus-hp7-helicobacter — PMID 42671007** — Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in adults with H. pylori infection found high-dose Lacticaseibacillus paracasei HP7 reduced urea-breath-test values and modestly improved selected gastric markers/symptoms, but complete eradication was uncommon and it is not a replacement for established therapy.
