## Waves 726–750
1. **ashwagandha — PMID 34559859** — Five-RCT/400-participant sleep meta-analysis; small pooled sleep benefit with stronger subgroup signals in insomnia/longer exposure, but extract/duration heterogeneity and no generic dose recommendation.
2. **lavender — PMID 31743795** — 22-RCT aromatherapy meta-analysis; anxiety and selected physiologic stress signals, with route-specific evidence and substantial contextual heterogeneity.
3. **bacopa — PMID 24252493** — Nine-trial cognition meta-analysis; attention-processing-speed signals with standardized-extract and chronic-use boundaries, not proof of broad nootropic enhancement.
4. **lavender — PMID 40600743** — 2025/2026 review of 11 RCTs/628 adults; lavender essential oil improved pooled sleep-quality scores, but route, blinding and subjective-outcome limits remain.
5. **soy-isoflavones — PMID 19653225** — Systematic review of randomized trials for menopausal vasomotor symptoms; inconsistent results and study-quality variation prevent a uniform hot-flash claim.
6. **black-cohosh — PMID 37192826** — Updated 22-study/2,310-participant meta-analysis; overall menopausal/hot-flash signals with anxiety/depression nulls and combination-regimen heterogeneity.
7. **soy-isoflavones — PMID 35651836** — 11-RCT menopause meta-analysis; estradiol/Kupperman signals with hot-flash/FSH/LH nulls and extreme heterogeneity.
8. **black-cohosh — PMID 20085176** — Older placebo-controlled meta-analysis suggesting vasomotor improvement but with significant heterogeneity; retained as historical positive evidence.
9. **evening-primrose-oil — PMID 41883983** — 2025 six-RCT menopause systematic review/meta-analysis; menopausal-symptom evidence remains limited and indication/formulation specific.
10. **red-clover — PMID 42269521** — GRADE-assessed hot-flash systematic review/meta-analysis; red-clover isoflavone efficacy remains formulation/population specific.
11. **black-cohosh — PMID 22972105** — Cochrane review of 16 RCTs/2,027 women; no significant difference versus placebo for hot-flush frequency or menopausal symptom scores, a major counterweight to positive syntheses.
12. **black-cohosh — PMID 18585461** — Older systematic review of six double-blind RCTs; efficacy inconsistent and uncertain, supporting explicit historical disagreement.
13. **red-clover — PMID 33920485** — Eight-trial meta-analysis; modest reduction in daily hot-flash frequency with substantial heterogeneity and formulation/dose dependence.
14. **black-cohosh-icr — PMID 33021111** — Review/meta-analysis of isopropanolic Cimicifuga racemosa extract; product-specific menopausal evidence with manufacturer-affiliation provenance retained.
15. **ginger — PMID 31519467** — 2019 CINV systematic review/meta-analysis; selected acute-vomiting/fatigue signals but many nausea/delayed outcomes null, adjunctive oncology context only.
16. **ginger — PMID 36501010** — 2022 review of 23 CINV RCTs; overall acute/delayed nausea/vomiting largely null, with a limited low-dose/longer-duration acute-vomiting subgroup signal.
17. **ginger — PMID 30299420** — Ten-RCT CINV meta-analysis; historical pooled antiemetic evidence retained alongside newer conflicting syntheses.
18. **ginger — PMID 34312974** — 14-study postoperative-nausea/vomiting meta-analysis; selected nausea/rescue-antiemetic outcomes improved while vomiting comparisons were mixed.
19. **soy-isoflavones — PMID 20199985** — RCT meta-analysis in menopausal women; spine BMD signal with intervention-duration, geography, dose and preparation heterogeneity.
20. **maca — PMID 21840656** — Four-RCT menopause systematic review; limited favorable symptom evidence with small samples, methodological limitations and insufficient safety evidence.
21. **soy-isoflavones — PMID 36012916** — 18-RCT meta-analysis; modest BMD preservation at lumbar spine/femoral neck/hip with preparation, duration and population modifiers.
22. **soy-isoflavones — PMID 34067865** — Bone-turnover-marker meta-analysis in postmenopausal women; osteocalcin/BAP/PYD/DPD pooled changes were not statistically significant.
23. **isoflavones — PMID 39214463** — 2024 umbrella review of ten RCT meta-analyses; BMD signals across several bone sites, but inherits heterogeneity and quality limits of underlying reviews.
24. **soy-isoflavones — PMID 20673147** — Meta-analysis in Western peri/postmenopausal women; isoflavone mixtures did not significantly preserve lumbar-spine BMD, useful counterweight to broader positive syntheses.
25. **isoflavones — PMID 37875614** — 63-RCT postmenopausal BMD meta-analysis; pooled bone-density signals with major intervention, duration and population heterogeneity.
