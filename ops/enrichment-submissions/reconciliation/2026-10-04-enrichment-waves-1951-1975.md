Nineteenth pass of the Waves 1501–2000 deep-enrichment program.

## Operating rule
Every PMID was screened against the cumulative branch index and source-verified. Null findings, combination products, formulation-specific effects, disease-specific cohorts, small pilots and evidence-quality limitations remain explicit; no publication, recommendation, dosing, or governance promotion occurs during authoring.

## Waves 1951–1975
1. **vitamin-d-cirrhosis-glycemic-rct — PMID 39882304** — Double-blind RCT in 60 patients with cirrhosis found weekly vitamin D increased 25(OH)D and modestly improved fasting glucose and HOMA-IR over 12 weeks; cirrhosis-specific metabolic evidence does not generalize to healthy adults.
2. **vitamin-d-knee-osteoarthritis-overview — PMID 39931432** — Overview of systematic reviews/meta-analyses on vitamin D for knee osteoarthritis found most reviews were low or very low quality with high risk of bias and low-certainty evidence, underscoring major uncertainty despite frequent clinical use.
3. **zinc-vitamin-e-cabg-inflammatory-markers — PMID 40071246** — Secondary analysis of a randomized CABG trial found zinc plus vitamin E did not significantly change testosterone but reduced early IL-6 and later TLR-4 expression; combination therapy and perioperative context prevent nutrient-specific inference.
4. **alternate-day-oral-iron-ida-rct — PMID 40224710** — Randomized trial in women with iron-deficiency anemia found alternate-day oral iron produced a larger short-term hemoglobin rise and fewer adverse effects than daily dosing, with no clear compliance difference; regimen-specific treatment evidence.
5. **vitamin-c-plus-iron-ida-meta — PMID 40765927** — Systematic review/meta-analysis found adding vitamin C to oral iron produced only a small, likely clinically insignificant hemoglobin/ferritin advantage; authors concluded routine adjunctive vitamin C is not supported.
6. **cistanche-ginkgo-cognitive-rct — PMID 38972848** — Ninety-day double-blind RCT in 100 middle-aged/older adults found a combined Cistanche tubulosa plus Ginkgo biloba product improved several cognitive and memory measures versus placebo; combination exposure prevents ingredient-specific attribution.
7. **saffron-type1diabetes-combination-rct — PMID 38999837** — Six-month double-blind trial in type 1 diabetes tested a product containing saffron plus probiotics, prebiotics and magnesium; between-group metabolic effects were limited, with triglycerides differing while most glycemic endpoints did not.
8. **ashwagandha-anxiety-insomnia-meta — PMID 39083548** — Meta-analysis of five ashwagandha RCTs found improvements in anxiety and several sleep outcomes, but heterogeneity was extreme for anxiety and the evidence base remained small; extract/formulation differences matter.
9. **ashwagandha-tryptophan-enriched-milk-sleep — PMID 39189195** — Ninety-day double-blind trial found ashwagandha-enriched milk, with or without tryptophan, improved subjective sleep more than control milk; adding tryptophan did not clearly outperform ashwagandha alone.
10. **saffron-repeated-sprint-null — PMID 39227898** — Double-blind crossover trial in 22 active young men found acute saffron did not improve repeated-sprint performance, lactate, heart rate, perceived exertion or affect versus placebo; useful direct null evidence.
11. **shoden-ashwagandha-high-stress-rct — PMID 39286132** — Sixty-day double-blind RCT in 60 high-stress participants found low-dose standardized ashwagandha extract reduced anxiety, perceived stress and cortisol versus placebo; product-standardization and small sample remain important.
12. **phosphatidylserine-mci-multiingredient-rct — PMID 39317299** — Twelve-month double-blind RCT in 190 adults with mild cognitive impairment found a multi-ingredient supplement containing phosphatidylserine, ALA, Ginkgo flavonoids and B vitamins improved selected cognitive tests and biomarkers; no single ingredient can be isolated.
13. **adhd-dietary-supplements-systematic-review — PMID 39429382** — Systematic review of 14 ADHD RCTs found mixed signals across vitamin D, magnesium, saffron, marine oils, probiotics and dietary strategies; intervention diversity and short follow-up preclude broad supplement recommendations.
14. **citrulline-vs-citrulline-malate-null-rct — PMID 39662304** — Double-blind crossover trial in 43 trained adults found neither L-citrulline nor citrulline malate improved maximal strength, ballistic performance, strength endurance, exertion or affect versus placebo; malate showed no additive benefit.
15. **weizmannia-coagulans-bc99-protein-strength — PMID 39683384** — Twelve-week double-blind trial in 72 male physical-education students found protein plus W. coagulans BC99 improved strength, muscle mass and digestion-related biomarkers versus protein alone; strain-plus-protein context limits probiotic class inference.
16. **creatine-acute-stroke-rct-null — PMID 39683542** — Double-blind RCT in 30 older adults hospitalized for acute stroke found seven days of creatine did not improve function, strength, muscle mass or 90-day outcomes versus placebo, though it altered progranulin; direct clinical null.
17. **l-carnitine-hemodialysis-lipids-meta-null — PMID 39687901** — Meta-analysis of 28 RCTs in hemodialysis patients found L-carnitine did not significantly improve triglycerides, cholesterol fractions, BMI or blood pressure, regardless of oral versus IV route; strong category-level null evidence.
18. **probiotics-pediatric-diarrhea-meta — PMID 39703988** — WHO-commissioned systematic review/meta-analysis of 98 studies found probiotics slightly increased clinical cure and shortened acute pediatric diarrhea by about half a day, but heterogeneity was very high and certainty low.
19. **pro-pre-synbiotics-anemia-meta — PMID 39716076** — Systematic review/meta-analysis of randomized trials evaluated probiotics, prebiotics and synbiotics for anemia and iron-related outcomes; small heterogeneous trials support only cautious adjunctive interpretation rather than replacement of standard iron therapy.
20. **synbiotic-vs-probiotic-type2diabetes-rct — PMID 39719724** — Double-blind RCT in 120 adults with type 2 diabetes found a B. animalis plus GOS synbiotic modestly lowered fasting glucose more than placebo and probiotic alone, while most other glycemic endpoints did not differ between groups.
21. **l-paracasei-peritoneal-dialysis-microbiome — PMID 39732948** — Double-blind trial in peritoneal-dialysis patients found L. paracasei N1115 plus FOS altered gut composition and improved selected GI symptoms but did not significantly increase SCFAs versus placebo; probiotic-plus-prebiotic formulation evidence.
22. **probiotics-diabetes-esrd-hemodialysis-rct — PMID 39735058** — Double-blind placebo-controlled trial in diabetic hemodialysis patients found 12-week probiotics improved HOMA-IR and several oxidative/glycemic markers with minor GI adverse events; small disease-specific RCT requires replication.
23. **kombucha-human-microbiome-controlled-study — PMID 39738315** — Small controlled clinical study in healthy adults found four weeks of kombucha caused only modest microbiome shifts and no clear improvement in inflammatory biomarkers; fasting insulin/HOMA-IR increased within the kombucha group.
24. **pediococcus-acidilactici-depression-rct — PMID 39741947** — Four-week randomized placebo-controlled trial in 39 adults with depressive disorder found P. acidilactici CCFM6432 improved depression, anxiety, sleep and GI symptom scores with microbiome/metabolite changes; tiny strain-specific trial.
25. **prebiotic-fiber-ptsd-pilot-rct — PMID 39840022** — Pilot double-blind trial in 70 veterans tested prebiotic fiber alongside CBT for PTSD and found microbiome associations plus possible benefit in a subset; proof-of-concept design does not establish broad PTSD efficacy.
