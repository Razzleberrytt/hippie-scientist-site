# Research enrichment Waves 2026–2050 — deep pass

## Waves
- **2026 · magnesium-sulfate-craniotomy-meta · PMID 42355804** — Meta-analysis of 9 RCTs in adults undergoing supratentorial craniotomy found perioperative magnesium sulfate reduced mean arterial pressure, fentanyl use, and S100B, while blood loss was not significantly changed; evidence is perioperative IV use, not oral supplementation. _State:_ `research_only_route_firewall`
- **2027 · saffron-diabetes-metabolic-safety-meta · PMID 39818282** — Meta-analysis of 13 RCTs in diabetes/prediabetes found saffron/crocin reduced systolic blood pressure, fasting glucose, and AST, but not ALT, renal markers, or diastolic pressure; authors judged observed AST, SBP, and FBG effect sizes not clinically important. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2028 · choline-human-milk-meta · PMID 40913782** — Systematic review/meta-analysis of 17 studies found human-milk total choline rises from colostrum into transitional milk and then stabilizes in mature milk, with substantial variation by assay method, geography, and country income; this is compositional physiology rather than supplementation efficacy. _State:_ `research_only_observational`
- **2029 · caffeine-swimming-performance-meta · PMID 42323844** — Meta-analysis of 13 randomized crossover studies found acute caffeine improved swimming performance by about 1.7% on average and increased post-exercise lactate; heterogeneity was substantial and apparent dose-response subgroup findings were exploratory rather than dosing guidance. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2030 · prenatal-balanced-energy-protein-meta · PMID 41701774** — Individual-participant-data meta-analysis of 8 RCTs found prenatal balanced energy-protein supplementation reduced several small-vulnerable-newborn phenotypes, including preterm-SGA and term-SGA categories; control regimens and supplement composition varied across trials. _State:_ `research_only_category_firewall`
- **2031 · omega3-adhd-biomarker-meta · PMID 42433395** — Biomarker-stratified meta-analysis of 7 pediatric ADHD RCTs found a small overall omega-3 effect, with larger estimated benefit in low-baseline omega-3 subgroups and no significant effect in normal/high or unstratified groups; evidence is preliminary and biomarker thresholds varied. _State:_ `research_only_population_firewall`
- **2032 · high-protein-critical-illness-umbrella · PMID 42253728** — Systematic review/meta-analysis plus umbrella review found high-protein supplementation did not improve mortality or other major clinical outcomes versus conventional protein in critically ill patients; interventions lasting more than 14 days showed a slight mortality increase signal. _State:_ `research_only_population_firewall`
- **2033 · probiotics-synbiotics-bone-umbrella · PMID 42369220** — Umbrella review found modest pooled improvements in bone mineral density, muscle mass, and strength with probiotics/synbiotics, but heterogeneity was substantial, muscle findings were unstable in sensitivity analyses, and the authors concluded evidence does not support clinical recommendations. _State:_ `research_only_category_firewall`
- **2034 · capsaicin-orofacial-pain-meta · PMID 42653103** — Systematic review/meta-analysis found placebo-controlled capsaicin comparisons for orofacial pain did not reach statistical significance overall, while uncontrolled pre-post studies showed large pain reductions; adverse effects were more frequent but generally mild and local. _State:_ `research_only_route_firewall`
- **2035 · zinc-l-carnosine-oral-mucosa-meta · PMID 42505716** — Systematic review/meta-analysis of zinc L-carnosine for oral mucosal injury found a severe-mucositis reduction under a common-effect model that was not significant under random effects; evidence certainty was low to very low across heterogeneous local formulations. _State:_ `research_only_formulation_firewall`
- **2036 · folic-acid-uptake-parity-meta · PMID 42082233** — Meta-analysis of 81 observational studies found multiparous women were less likely than primiparous women to use folic acid before and around conception and early pregnancy, with high heterogeneity; this informs uptake behavior rather than folic-acid efficacy. _State:_ `research_only_observational`
- **2037 · vitamin-d-drug-resistant-epilepsy-meta · PMID 42226400** — Systematic review/meta-analysis found vitamin D supplementation was associated with fewer seizures and higher vitamin D levels in drug-resistant epilepsy, but only four small studies contributed to the supplementation meta-analysis and larger trials are needed. _State:_ `research_only_population_firewall`
- **2038 · chinese-herbal-pediatric-oncology-meta · PMID 41447839** — Meta-analysis of 49 RCTs reported higher remission measures, improved immune/hematologic markers, and fewer treatment-related toxicities when Chinese herbal medicine was added to conventional pediatric oncology care; 29 prescriptions and 136 substances make ingredient-level attribution impossible. _State:_ `research_only_category_firewall`
- **2039 · chinese-botanical-migraine-network-meta · PMID 42147339** — Bayesian network meta-analysis of 57 RCTs ranked several Chinese botanical decoctions favorably for migraine pain, frequency, or duration, but study-quality variation and indirect network comparisons limit confidence and prevent generalization to individual botanicals. _State:_ `research_only_formulation_firewall`
- **2040 · melatonin-musculoskeletal-pain-meta · PMID 42378431** — Systematic review/meta-analysis of 23 trials found melatonin was not clearly superior to placebo for chronic musculoskeletal pain in the main analysis, though some sensitivity and postoperative analyses favored melatonin; most effect estimates did not reach clinically important thresholds and certainty was low to moderate. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2041 · saffron-metabolic-syndrome-meta · PMID 39931766** — Meta-analysis of 25 RCTs found saffron modestly reduced fasting glucose, HbA1c, total cholesterol, and blood pressure in metabolic syndrome-related conditions, with no significant changes in insulin resistance, triglycerides, LDL-C, HDL-C, BMI, or waist circumference. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2042 · choline-heart-failure-observational-meta · PMID 41202941** — Meta-analysis of 9 prospective cohorts linked higher circulating choline/phosphatidylcholine with greater heart-failure incidence and higher betaine with adverse outcomes in established heart failure; these are observational biomarker associations, not evidence that choline supplementation causes harm. _State:_ `research_only_observational`
- **2043 · caffeine-breast-cancer-cohort-meta · PMID 42327457** — Meta-analysis of 31 cohort studies found caffeine intake itself was not significantly associated with breast-cancer risk, while regular coffee showed a modest inverse association; the coffee signal should not be attributed to caffeine. _State:_ `research_only_observational`
- **2044 · leucine-rich-protein-sarcopenia-rct · PMID 41483327** — Twelve-week double-blind RCT in older adults with or at risk of sarcopenia found leucine-rich high-protein supplementation did not significantly improve body composition or physical performance, despite changes in selected gene-expression markers. _State:_ `research_only_combination_firewall`
- **2045 · omega3-periodontal-adjunct-meta · PMID 42458370** — Systematic review/meta-analysis found omega-3 added to scaling and root planing produced small improvements in periodontal attachment and pocket depth, especially over longer follow-up, but heterogeneity was very high and evidence certainty was moderate to low. _State:_ `research_only_combination_firewall`
- **2046 · alpha-lipoic-acid-nafld-systematic-review · PMID 42368730** — Systematic review of 27 NAFLD studies found strong preclinical mechanistic signals but only 6 human studies; human findings suggested at most modest improvements in liver and metabolic markers, with insufficient evidence to establish alpha-lipoic acid as disease-modifying therapy. _State:_ `research_only_evidence_quality`
- **2047 · probiotics-schizophrenia-negative-symptoms-meta · PMID 42392560** — Meta-analysis of adjunctive probiotic RCTs in schizophrenia found no significant benefit for negative symptoms, although inflammatory markers such as hs-CRP decreased; probiotics were generally well tolerated but symptom evidence remains very low certainty. _State:_ `research_only_category_firewall`
- **2048 · capsaicin-analogue-athletic-performance-meta · PMID 42816054** — Three-level meta-analysis of 21 randomized crossover trials found acute capsaicin-analogue intake produced small improvements in overall athletic performance, particularly power and muscular endurance, but heterogeneity, publication bias, and very low certainty limit confidence. _State:_ `research_only_formulation_firewall`
- **2049 · vitamin-c-urinary-oxalate-meta · PMID 42535313** — Systematic review/meta-analysis found vitamin C supplementation significantly increased 24-hour urinary oxalate in both stone formers and non-stone formers, with no significant effect on urinary citrate or calcium; this supports a clinically relevant kidney-stone safety caution. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2050 · iron-folate-pregnancy-education-meta · PMID 39545365** — Meta-analysis found pregnancy nutrition education increased iron-folic-acid supplement adherence, raised hemoglobin, and reduced anemia risk; because the intervention was education rather than supplement efficacy itself, the finding belongs behind an intervention-context firewall. _State:_ `research_only_category_firewall`
