# Research enrichment Waves 2001–2025 — deep pass

First abstract-verified pass of the Waves 2001–2500 program. Every retained record was manually calibrated for result direction, population/formulation limits, and whether it can support supplement-facing runtime evidence.

## Key quality decisions
- Rejected PMID 41628578 because CT contrast-media iodine is an identity false positive, not supplement/nutrient evidence.
- Preserved negative and clinically-small findings rather than filtering for positive results.
- Kept observational biomarker evidence, pediatric/clinical-population evidence, and non-oral routes behind explicit firewalls.
- No dosing recommendation was inferred from meta-regression or subgroup findings.

## Waves
- **2001 · melatonin · PMID 40588412** — Melatonin improved subjective sleep quality, daytime sleepiness, sleep-onset latency, and total sleep time, but subjective improvements did not reach the minimal clinically important difference and objective sleep outcomes were not consistently improved. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2002 · magnesium · PMID 41978212** — Magnesium levels were lower in people with diabetic retinopathy than diabetic controls without retinopathy, and lower still in proliferative versus non-proliferative disease; certainty was rated low and heterogeneity was very high. _State:_ `research_only_observational`
- **2003 · magnesium · PMID 42098865** — Perioperative magnesium modestly reduced 24-hour pain versus placebo, but the effect did not reach a minimal clinically important difference and did not improve opioid use, rescue analgesia timing, or length of stay. _State:_ `research_only_route_firewall`
- **2004 · milk-protein · PMID 40471664** — Milk-protein supplementation produced small reductions in total cholesterol, systolic blood pressure, and triglycerides, with no significant pooled effects on LDL-C, HDL-C, diastolic pressure, several oxidative-stress markers, or liver enzymes. _State:_ `research_only_category_firewall`
- **2005 · protein-supplementation · PMID 40876553** — Protein/amino-acid supplementation did not clearly improve health-related quality of life; pooled analyses showed small increases in serum albumin and BMI. _State:_ `research_only_category_firewall`
- **2006 · omega-3 · PMID 40940198** — EPA or EPA+DHA supplementation did not improve pain-free walking distance, maximal walking distance, ankle-brachial index, flow-mediated dilation, inflammatory markers, cholesterol, or blood pressure versus placebo. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2007 · caffeine · PMID 41320553** — Overall caffeine intake was not significantly associated with IBD risk, while subgroup signals varied substantially by region, age, caffeine source, smoking, and education. _State:_ `research_only_observational`
- **2008 · alpha-lipoic-acid · PMID 41619689** — Alpha-lipoic acid improved multiple neuropathic symptom outcomes, including paresthesia, numbness, and pain measures, while HbA1c and selected nerve-conduction outcomes were not significantly improved. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2009 · probiotics · PMID 40836501** — Probiotic interventions reduced several gastrointestinal chemotherapy complications in pooled analyses, including nausea, diarrhea, bloating, vomiting, and indigestion; constipation reporting was less convincing despite the authors' overall positive interpretation. _State:_ `research_only_category_firewall`
- **2010 · probiotics-synbiotics · PMID 41102471** — Probiotic/synbiotic supplementation modestly improved BMI-z and inflammatory markers CRP and TNF-alpha; benefits varied by age, duration, complications, and intervention type and disappeared in the NAFLD subgroup. _State:_ `research_only_category_firewall`
- **2011 · probiotics · PMID 41259365** — The review found no consistent evidence that probiotics improve depression or schizophrenia overall; heterogeneous subgroup signals were insufficient to establish reliable benefit. _State:_ `research_only_category_firewall`
- **2012 · dietary-nitrate · PMID 42130877** — Dietary nitrate did not significantly reduce body weight or BMI, but pooled analyses suggested modest reductions in systolic blood pressure, total cholesterol, and triglycerides. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2013 · chlorella-vulgaris · PMID 41652544** — Chlorella supplementation was associated with improvements in weight, fasting glucose, hs-CRP, LDL-C, total cholesterol, ALP, and AST. _State:_ `research_only_low_certainty`
- **2014 · vitamin-d · PMID 41670949** — Vitamin D supplementation was associated with improved dominant-follicle count, ovulation rate, and pregnancy rate, while AMH was not significantly changed. _State:_ `research_only_evidence_quality`
- **2015 · zinc · PMID 41677652** — Parkinson disease was associated with altered copper, ceruloplasmin, zinc, and manganese concentrations across different tissues and fluids, with direction varying by specimen type. _State:_ `research_only_observational`
- **2016 · vitamin-d · PMID 41701309** — Low-dose and high-dose vitamin D regimens produced no statistically or clinically meaningful differences in 25(OH)D, radiologic improvement, PTH, or alkaline phosphatase at 12 weeks. _State:_ `research_only_population_firewall`
- **2017 · folic-acid · PMID 41705996** — Folic acid given with antifolate antimalarials reduced parasite clearance and increased treatment failure at several time points, especially in trials using doses above 1 mg/day. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2018 · dietary-supplements · PMID 41871665** — Pooled dietary supplements modestly improved irritability and overall emotional symptoms but not anxiety; vitamin subgroups showed some irritability benefit while other supplement classes were nonsignificant. _State:_ `research_only_category_firewall`
- **2019 · ginseng · PMID 40923100** — Ginseng improved several inflammatory and oxidative-stress biomarkers but did not significantly improve anthropometric measures, blood pressure, glycemic profile, lipid profile, adipokines, or heart rate overall. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2020 · lutein · PMID 42247243** — Lutein-fortified formulas consistently increased circulating lutein versus unfortified formulas, but bioavailability remained lower than breast milk and evidence that fortification reproduces breast-milk-associated developmental effects was lacking. _State:_ `research_only_formulation_firewall`
- **2021 · saw-palmetto · PMID 41652806** — A proprietary saw-palmetto fatty-acid extract increased terminal hair count, vellus hair count, and hair density versus placebo at 180 days. _State:_ `research_only_formulation_firewall`
- **2022 · melatonin · PMID 41604838** — Salivary melatonin was substantially lower in periodontal disease, but heterogeneity was extreme and publication-bias signals were present. _State:_ `research_only_observational`
- **2023 · alpha-lipoic-acid · PMID 41917882** — Alpha-lipoic acid did not significantly improve liver enzymes, lipid profile, glycemic indices, adipocytokines, or obesity measures versus placebo, including across subgroup and sensitivity analyses. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2024 · hyaluronic-acid-chondroitin · PMID 41139434** — Intravesical hyaluronic acid with or without chondroitin improved pain and urinary symptoms and reduced recurrent-UTI risk; randomized-trial subsets favored combined HA/ChS versus placebo or standard care. _State:_ `research_only_route_firewall`
- **2025 · beetroot-juice · PMID 41642915** — Beetroot juice reduced pooled oxidative-damage biomarkers and increased total antioxidant capacity, with moderate-to-high heterogeneity across outcomes. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
