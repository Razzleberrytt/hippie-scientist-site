## Waves 826–850
1. **ashwagandha — PMID 42738987** — Four-RCT resistance-training meta-analysis; bench/lower-body strength and chest-circumference signals, body-fat null, low/very-low GRADE certainty, and HKSJ sensitivity limiting robustness.
2. **epicor-postbiotic — PMID 42625679** — Single-dose crossover RCT in 30 healthy adults; rapid cytokine and immune-cell biomarker changes only, with no established clinical immune benefit.
3. **shatavari — PMID 42571384** — Proprietary SheVari4 8-week RCT in 60 peri/post-menopausal women; MENQOL and hormone changes reported, with product-specific and short-duration boundaries.
4. **shatavari — PMID 38725785** — Multicenter placebo-controlled Shatavari root-extract trial in 70 menopausal women; symptom/QoL improvements with no significant adverse events, but small sample and short follow-up.
5. **saffron — PMID 42389708** — Affron RCT in 86 women aged 50–70 with low mood and poor sleep; depression/self-esteem/sleep-impairment signals, while sleep-disturbance and appearance outcomes were null.
6. **ashwagandha — PMID 42780946** — Sustained-release AshwaSR 90-day RCT in recreationally active adults; strength/VO2max/QoL improved, but between-group testosterone change was not significant and formulation is proprietary.
7. **lemon-balm — PMID 41782777** — Zensera acute RCT in 106 moderately stressed young adults; primary calmness outcome null, with selected executive-function and post-demand calmness-recovery signals.
8. **cistanche-ginkgo-combination — PMID 41847135** — 30-day RCT in 117 healthy adults; Cistanche+Ginkgo improved multiple memory scores with MRI correlates; combination-only and short-term evidence.
9. **boswellia-terminalia-combination — PMID 41438191** — 120-day proof-of-concept RCT in 100 adults with subjective memory complaints; cognition/sleep/BDNF signals belong to LN19184 blend only.
10. **plant-active-cognition-network — PMID 41640686** — 25-study NMA in 1,861 healthy older adults comparing 10 plant-active interventions; rankings are indirect and intervention-specific, not head-to-head proof.
11. **botanical-mci-network — PMID 41333026** — 19-trial NMA/4,956 participants in MCI; botanical rankings are indirect and heterogeneous, and safety reporting was inconsistent.
12. **milk-thistle — PMID 39097726** — 24-week silymarin RCT in 83 MASLD patients; liver-stiffness/GGT/ApoB signals with steatosis, most biochemical, anthropometric and fibrosis-index outcomes null.
13. **camu-camu — PMID 39168095** — Randomized crossover trial in 30 overweight hypertriglyceridemic adults; camu-camu reduced liver fat and AST/ALT versus placebo, with small-sample exploratory microbiome context.
14. **ginger — PMID 35031435** — 10-trial T2D meta-analysis; FBS/HbA1c and SBP/DBP reductions with very high heterogeneity, while lipid outcomes were null.
15. **beetroot-nitrate — PMID 42815860** — Older-adult beetroot review; nitrate biomarkers and some functional measures improved, while BP findings were heterogeneous and evidence remained preliminary.
16. **live-microbial-aging — PMID 42819170** — 24-trial review/1,809 older adults; preliminary cognition/handgrip signals were highly heterogeneous and sensitive to individual studies.
17. **restless-legs-supplements — PMID 39064758** — Ten-RCT RLS supplement review/482 participants; magnesium/B6 and other signals coexist with vitamin-D and valerian null/uncertain findings and high risk of bias.
18. **probiotics — PMID 42032711** — 41-RCT cortisol meta-analysis; modest cortisol reduction with I² 96.9% and very-low GRADE certainty, so no firm stress-treatment inference.
19. **creatine — PMID 42712402** — Eleven-RCT older-adult meta-analysis: creatine plus resistance training modestly improved strength, while added muscle-mass and physical-function effects were not significant.
20. **rhodiola — PMID 42738995** — Four-week DanceSport RCT; caffeine-containing arms improved Jive outcomes, Rhodiola alone did not, and no caffeine×Rhodiola synergy was established.
21. **green-tea-kombucha — PMID 42799570** — 12-week runner RCT; primary antioxidant and acute immune/oxidative outcomes were null, with nominal chronic biomarker changes becoming exploratory after multiplicity adjustment.
22. **nad-precursors — PMID 42774067** — 27-study NMA/1,599 participants; no significant perceived-fatigue or sleep benefit, with domain-specific endurance/cognition signals varying by precursor.
23. **beetroot-nitrate — PMID 42642885** — Pilot RCT in 22 older adults with metabolic syndrome; nitrate-rich beetroot juice increased nitrate but had no significant primary cerebral-autoregulation or cognition effect.
24. **probiotics — PMID 42514426** — Nine-RCT pediatric ADHD meta-analysis/482 participants; no clear benefit for core ADHD measures, with small exploratory parent-reported behavioral signals and substantial heterogeneity.
25. **performance-supplement-network — PMID 42821324** — 119-study Bayesian NMA in physically active people/athletes; selected HMB, beta-alanine, creatine, vitamin-D, caffeine and betaine signals, but most pairwise comparisons were low/very-low certainty and rankings were indirect.
