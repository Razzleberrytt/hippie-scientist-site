# Research enrichment Waves 1826–1850 — 2026-10-04

Fourteenth pass of the Waves 1501–2000 program, emphasizing vitamin E and N-acetylcysteine while separating deficiency replacement from general supplementation and preserving direct nulls, observational associations, local formulations and preclinical boundaries.

## Waves 1826–1850
1. **vitamin-e-ms-observational — PMID 42060044** — Case-control/cross-sectional analysis found lower serum vitamin E in MS and associations with cognition/disability, but authors explicitly call for longitudinal studies and RCTs before supplementation benefit can be inferred.
2. **vitamin-e-centennial-review — PMID 42096852** — Centennial review confirms vitamin E essentiality in deficiency while highlighting persistent uncertainty around broader disease-prevention/therapeutic effects of tocopherols and metabolites.
3. **vitamin-e-vancomycin-emr — PMID 42168788** — Large Japanese electronic-record analysis evaluated vitamin E exposure in relation to vancomycin-associated nephrotoxicity; retrospective pharmacoepidemiology should remain association evidence rather than a prophylaxis recommendation.
4. **vitamin-e-depression-mr — PMID 42213642** — Two-sample Mendelian-randomization study examined genetically proxied vitamin-E status and depression; genetic causal-inference evidence remains distinct from clinical supplementation trials.
5. **aved-chinese-pedigrees — PMID 42259017** — Genetic series identified TTPA-related ataxia with profound vitamin-E deficiency; three of four patients improved after high-dose replacement, supporting disease-specific deficiency treatment rather than general neuroenhancement.
6. **selenium-vitamin-e-esrd-uncontrolled — PMID 42334563** — Uncontrolled 60-day selenium-plus-vitamin-E study in ESRD found improved oxidative/inflammatory markers but nonsignificant erythrocyte outcomes; combination and absence of control prevent causal attribution.
7. **vitamin-e-homocysteine-crosssectional — PMID 42418008** — Large Chinese cross-sectional study found sex-specific nonlinear associations between plasma vitamin E and homocysteine; U-shaped patterns in men argue against simplistic more-is-better interpretation.
8. **vitamin-e-dental-pellicle-review — PMID 42242612** — Narrative review of four experimental studies suggests topical vitamin-E-containing formulations may modify acquired enamel pellicle and reduce erosion; evidence is local/topical and largely experimental.
9. **vitamin-e-topical-nanodelivery-review — PMID 42105366** — Review describes liposomal/nanoemulsion approaches to improve topical vitamin-E stability and skin penetration; formulation technology evidence does not establish clinical superiority.
10. **vitamin-e-oxygen-nanomaterial — PMID 41962577** — Cell-model nanomaterial study combining oxygen delivery, citric acid and vitamin E showed divergent redox effects in healthy versus cancer cells; entirely preclinical and formulation-specific.
11. **nac-contrast-nephropathy-meta-null — PMID 42015439** — Meta-analysis of 49 studies found neither oral nor IV N-acetylcysteine reduced contrast-associated AKI, dialysis or mortality; strong direct null against routine prophylactic benefit.
12. **nac-mci-cardiac-rehab-rct — PMID 42045982** — Randomized trial evaluated NAC for cognitive symptoms during exercise-based cardiac rehabilitation in patients with mild cognitive impairment; co-rehabilitation context requires endpoint-specific interpretation rather than NAC-only generalization.
13. **nac-spata22-poi-preclinical — PMID 42098781** — Genetic/animal/cell study identified SPATA22-related ovarian dysfunction and found NAC partially rescued mouse/cell phenotypes; translational rescue is preclinical, not human fertility efficacy.
14. **nac-nf1-youth-rct-null — PMID 42173046** — Double-blind pilot RCT in youth with NF1 found NAC was generally tolerated but did not improve primary motor, secondary cognitive/behavioral or exploratory biomarker outcomes; two participants stopped for worsening behavior.
15. **nac-dermatology-review — PMID 42183686** — Dermatology review describes plausible and early clinical uses of NAC but notes most studies remain small and methodologically limited; condition-specific evidence should not be flattened into broad skin-benefit claims.
16. **nac-cannabis-craving-secondary — PMID 42184587** — Secondary analysis of a 302-person cannabis-use-disorder NAC trial identified heterogeneous craving trajectories associated with psychiatric symptoms and outcomes; craving-class analysis does not demonstrate NAC efficacy.
17. **nac-dysmenorrhea-rct — PMID 42289121** — Double-blind RCT in 50 adolescents found cyclical oral NAC reduced menstrual pain, rescue ibuprofen use and urinary oxidative-stress marker versus placebo; small short study needs independent replication.
18. **nac-preeclampsia-protocol — PMID 42308185** — Protocol describes a planned double-blind trial of oral NAC for early-onset preeclampsia progression; no efficacy data are available yet.
19. **nac-ovarian-xenograft-null — PMID 42309178** — Human ovarian-tissue xenograft study found oral NAC in mice produced little to no reduction in oxidative stress or follicle-survival improvement; preclinical direct null.
20. **nac-dendrimer-drug-delivery — PMID 42101951** — NAC-functionalized dendrimer study improved experimental drug-carrier characteristics and glioma-cell uptake; nanocarrier engineering evidence is unrelated to oral NAC clinical effects.
21. **nac-mrna-lnp-inhaled-preclinical — PMID 42124519** — NAC-enabled inhaled mRNA lipid nanoparticles improved delivery and antitumor immunity in mouse models through surface remodeling/mucolysis; this is platform-preclinical evidence, not systemic NAC anticancer efficacy.
22. **nac-urea-cycle-leukocyte-invitro — PMID 42171752** — Human-leukocyte in-vitro study found NAC reduced metabolite/ammonia-induced DNA damage in most conditions; cellular protection does not establish clinical benefit in urea-cycle disorders.
23. **nac-hcc-preclinical — PMID 42177959** — Rat/cell hepatocellular-carcinoma study found NAC altered Nrf2/redox and transcriptomic pathways and reduced experimental tumor progression; entirely preclinical.
24. **nac-fucoidan-nanoparticle-liver — PMID 42229330** — Targeted fucoidan/NAC nanoparticles improved delayed acetaminophen liver injury in mice by combining antioxidant delivery with neutrophil-recruitment blockade; nanoformulation animal evidence only.
25. **nac-dental-sealer-invitro — PMID 42298535** — In-vitro endodontic study found NAC medicament altered sealer penetration relative to calcium hydroxide; local dental material interaction is not evidence for systemic NAC effects.
