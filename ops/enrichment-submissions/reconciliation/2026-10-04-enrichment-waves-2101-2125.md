## Waves 2101–2125
1. **n-acetylcysteine — PMID 42340796** — NAC has broad antioxidant and anti-inflammatory plausibility and condition-specific clinical signals, but trial results across organ systems are mixed and inconsistent. Boundary: Narrative synthesis and variable doses, formulations, and populations do not support a single broad therapeutic claim; gastrointestinal adverse effects were the most commonly noted. State: `research_only_evidence_quality`.
2. **liposomal-iron — PMID 42183880** — Liposomal iron was generally non-inferior to conventional oral iron, showed a larger hemoglobin increase at six months, and caused fewer adverse events and less constipation. Boundary: Evidence remains preliminary with substantial heterogeneity for some endpoints and only four trials; formulation-specific benefit should not be generalized to all iron products. State: `research_only_formulation_firewall`.
3. **n-acetylcysteine — PMID 41663453** — NAC increased serum antioxidant markers and shifted granulosa-cell apoptosis-related gene expression toward reduced apoptosis versus placebo. Boundary: This tiny mechanistic study did not establish fertility, pregnancy, or live-birth benefit; gene-expression changes should not be promoted as clinical efficacy. State: `research_only_evidence_quality`.
4. **vitamin-d — PMID 41774579** — CRS patients had lower vitamin-D levels than controls; supplementation improved SNOT-22 symptoms before three months but the benefit was not maintained at longer follow-up. Boundary: Low vitamin-D status association and short-term treatment signal should be kept distinct; durable efficacy remains unproven. State: `research_only_evidence_quality`.
5. **iodine-status — PMID 42124075** — Suboptimal maternal iodine status was associated with modestly poorer child neurodevelopment, particularly cognition; exploratory fitted curves were most favorable in a mid-range of iodine intake. Boundary: This is observational exposure evidence, not proof that supplementation improves neurodevelopment; nonlinearity was not statistically robust after cohort consolidation. State: `research_only_observational`.
6. **nutraceuticals — PMID 41637226** — Multiple nutrients and nutraceuticals have plausible retinal-neuroprotection or pressure-related mechanisms, but the strength and clinical maturity of evidence varies greatly by compound. Boundary: This review is useful for evidence mapping, not as proof that nutraceuticals prevent or treat glaucoma. State: `research_only_category_firewall`.
7. **vitamin-c — PMID 42602406** — Microneedling with vitamin C and with tranexamic acid produced comparable short-term pigment outcomes; the pooled difference was not statistically significant. Boundary: Procedural transdermal vitamin-C evidence is not evidence for oral supplementation; follow-up was short and equivalence was not formally established. State: `research_only_route_firewall`.
8. **nigella-sativa-oil — PMID 41880716** — Topical Nigella sativa oil and sesame oil reduced incidence and severity of infusion-related phlebitis versus no topical intervention, with sesame showing the lowest rate. Boundary: This is topical catheter-site care, not evidence for oral black-seed supplementation or systemic cardiovascular benefit. State: `research_only_route_firewall`.
9. **polyphenols-nutraceuticals — PMID 42280426** — Polyphenols and selected nutraceuticals affect multiple carcinogenesis pathways in preclinical systems, but clinical translation is constrained by poor bioavailability, formulation variability, and lack of high-quality trials. Boundary: Mechanistic anticancer plausibility must not be presented as clinical treatment efficacy or as a substitute for oncology therapy. State: `research_only_evidence_quality`.
10. **melatonin — PMID 39428939** — Melatonin supplementation reduced fasting glucose, insulin, HbA1c, and HOMA-IR in pooled analyses. Boundary: Small evidence base and exploratory duration/dose subgroup results do not establish an optimal regimen; larger trials are needed. State: `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`.
11. **probiotics — PMID 42688031** — Probiotic supplementation was associated with less neonatal jaundice, lower peak bilirubin, less phototherapy, lower respiratory-distress incidence, and shorter respiratory support. Boundary: Moderate heterogeneity, strain/dose variability, and limited long-term safety data preclude standardized routine-use recommendations. State: `research_only_population_category_firewall`.
12. **l-carnitine-n-acetylcysteine — PMID 40350672** — L-carnitine and NAC monotherapy improved several semen parameters in pooled analyses; L-carnitine also increased testosterone, while other hormones were unchanged. Boundary: Semen surrogate improvements do not establish pregnancy or live-birth benefit, and the two antioxidants should not be treated as interchangeable. State: `research_only_surrogate_outcome`.
13. **omega-3-epa-dha — PMID 41568426** — EPA:DHA ratio influenced blood fatty-acid composition and pooled inflammatory markers, with different ratios favoring different biochemical outcomes. Boundary: Ratio/dose subgroup findings are formulation-level meta-analytic associations, not validated personalized dosing rules. State: `research_only_formulation_firewall`.
14. **ginkgo-diterpene-lactone-meglumine — PMID 39880062** — Ginkgo diterpene lactone meglumine added to conventional treatment improved 90-day functional-outcome measures in pooled trials. Boundary: This is an injectable Chinese patent medicine/formulation used adjunctively in acute stroke, not evidence for oral generic Ginkgo biloba supplements. State: `research_only_formulation_route_firewall`.
15. **caffeine — PMID 40317363** — Caffeine intake was not significantly associated with early or late AMD development; limited vote-counting suggested a possible progression benefit, largely from tea consumption. Boundary: Observational beverage evidence is sparse and confounded; tea-associated signals should not be attributed to caffeine itself. State: `research_only_observational`.
16. **probiotics — PMID 42050902** — Probiotics reduced HbA1c and insulin requirements in pooled analyses, while fasting glucose reduction was not significant; formulation subgrouping explained much between-study variance. Boundary: Small pediatric evidence base and strong formulation dependence require cautious adjunctive interpretation. State: `research_only_population_strain_firewall`.
17. **multistrain-probiotics — PMID 41433106** — Multistrain probiotics reduced total IBS symptom-severity scores and several subscores, but did not consistently improve abdominal pain or IBS-specific quality of life. Boundary: Heterogeneity was extreme for most outcomes, so pooled averages should not be generalized across multistrain products. State: `research_only_formulation_firewall`.
18. **probiotics — PMID 40895298** — Probiotic supplementation was associated with lower gestational weight gain and postpartum weight retention and modest improvements in several metabolic markers. Boundary: Substantial strain, dose, and duration heterogeneity means the pooled effect cannot define a standardized prenatal probiotic regimen. State: `research_only_population_category_firewall`.
19. **probiotics — PMID 41453849** — Most trials reported modest adjunctive symptom benefit, most consistently in younger outpatients with mild-to-moderate MDD and multi-strain Lactobacillus/Bifidobacterium formulations. Boundary: Context, geography, strain composition, and trial design strongly affected findings; evidence supports at most individualized adjunctive use, not class-wide antidepressant efficacy. State: `research_only_category_firewall`.
20. **turmeric-nanocurcumin — PMID 41176175** — Turmeric interventions, commonly nano-curcumin adjuncts, were associated with lower mortality, less mechanical ventilation, less deterioration, and greater symptom resolution in pooled trials. Boundary: Formulation-specific adjunctive evidence with multiple high-risk-of-bias trials should not be generalized to ordinary turmeric supplements or substitute for antiviral/medical care. State: `research_only_formulation_population_firewall`.
21. **iron — PMID 42271116** — Ferrous-sulfate supplementation significantly increased hemoglobin and ferritin in active females. Boundary: Findings apply to iron-status interventions in a population at elevated deficiency risk and do not support indiscriminate iron use in iron-replete athletes. State: `research_only_population_firewall`.
22. **iron — PMID 41736325** — Two months of iron therapy reduced fatigue and increased ferritin and hemoglobin relative to baseline/placebo comparisons reported by the trial. Boundary: Benefit is specific to documented iron deficiency despite absence of anemia; iron treatment should not be generalized to fatigue without deficiency. State: `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`.
23. **calcium-citrate-calcium-carbonate — PMID 41806228** — Calcium citrate produced better single-dose calcium exposure and PTH suppression than calcium carbonate, but chronic interventions showed no significant PTH difference between formulations. Boundary: Acute pharmacokinetic superiority should not be presented as proven long-term clinical superiority. State: `research_only_formulation_firewall`.
24. **maternal-vitamin-d-status — PMID 42234108** — Higher maternal vitamin-D status was associated with modestly lower ASD and ADHD risk/symptom estimates in observational analyses. Boundary: Maternal vitamin-D status is an exposure biomarker; association does not establish that supplementation prevents ASD or ADHD. State: `research_only_observational`.
25. **nutraceuticals — PMID 41653484** — The review surveys nutraceutical use for symptom and quality-of-life management around breast-cancer treatment, reflecting widespread use but heterogeneous clinical evidence. Boundary: Cancer-treatment interactions and variable evidence quality require compound-specific clinical review; a broad nutraceutical category cannot be assumed safe or effective. State: `research_only_category_firewall`.
