# Research enrichment Waves 2101–2125 — deep pass

Fifth deep pass of the Waves 2001–2500 program.

## Quality notes
- Two title-level false positives were rejected: injectable facial collagen biostimulators and starch-aerogel nutraceutical delivery engineering.
- Replacements are a human synbiotic hyperoxaluria RCT and a probiotic oral-health network meta-analysis.
- Saffron/diabetes effects are retained with the authors' explicit note that several statistically significant changes were not clinically important.
- EGb 761 is kept formulation-specific rather than generalized to all Ginkgo.
- Quercetin ovarian evidence remains predominantly mechanistic/preclinical.
- Collagen-ORC diabetic-foot evidence is topical wound care, not oral collagen supplementation.

## Waves
- **2101 · melatonin · PMID 39621359** — Oral melatonin and midazolam did not differ significantly for sedation or perioperative anxiety outcomes in pooled comparisons. _State:_ `research_only_population_firewall`
- **2102 · saffron-crocin · PMID 39818282** — Saffron/crocin modestly reduced systolic blood pressure, fasting glucose, and AST, but did not significantly improve diastolic pressure, ALT, or kidney-function markers; the authors judged observed effects clinically unimportant. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`

- **2105 · ginkgo-egb761 · PMID 39895346** — The standardized EGb 761 extract improved cognition, global assessment, activities of daily living, and quality of life versus placebo, with similar adverse-event frequency. _State:_ `research_only_formulation_firewall`
