## Waves 1976–2000
1. **beetroot-female-endurance-athletes-rct — PMID 39796497** — Small randomized trial in 18 female endurance athletes found acute beetroot juice improved selected ventilatory variables and increased VO2max modestly versus placebo; sample size was very small and results are specific to trained women.
2. **protein-basketball-congested-schedule-rct — PMID 39796561** — Double-blind crossover trial in 18 male basketball players found milk-protein supplementation increased erythrocyte glutathione but did not accelerate recovery of muscle function across a congested three-game schedule; playing time was a stronger recovery determinant.
3. **acute-supplements-young-athletes-review — PMID 39770925** — Systematic review of 16 studies found limited, mixed evidence for acute carbohydrate, beetroot and sodium-citrate use in young athletes, highlighting the thin evidence base and need for age-specific guidance rather than extrapolation from adults.
4. **prebiotics-iron-fortification-liver-renal-rct — PMID 39707781** — Double-blind randomized fortification trial in iron-deficient women found some prebiotic-plus-iron flour combinations increased liver-function and renal markers, suggesting that fortification safety outcomes can vary by compound and matrix.
5. **oral-probiotic-pregnancy-vulvovaginal-infection-null — PMID 39771026** — Multicenter double-blind RCT found a defined oral multi-strain probiotic did not reduce vulvovaginal infections during pregnancy and did not colonize the vagina with the administered strains; direct product-specific null evidence.
6. **cocoa-flavanol-gut-microbiota-null — PMID 39776521** — Randomized double-blind crossover study in eight healthy adults found short-term high-dose cocoa flavanols did not clearly increase Bifidobacterium or Lactobacillus and were not consistent with a prebiotic effect; tiny mechanistic null.
7. **probiotic-zinc-hepatic-encephalopathy-rct — PMID 39803228** — Open-label randomized trial in cirrhosis found probiotic with or without zinc did not reduce hepatic-encephalopathy recurrence, ammonia or MELD score, though quality-of-life and selected sleep outcomes improved; recurrence null is clinically important.
8. **synbiotic-periodontal-t2d-rct — PMID 39811858** — Double-blind RCT in type 2 diabetes with periodontal disease tested synbiotic supplementation alongside nonsurgical periodontal therapy; combination with standard periodontal treatment limits attribution of metabolic and inflammatory changes to the synbiotic alone.
9. **resistant-starch-prebiotic-metformin-t2d-rct — PMID 39835082** — Double-blind RCT in 70 adults with type 2 diabetes on metformin found a branded resistant-starch prebiotic improved fasting/postprandial glucose and GLP-1 but not HbA1c, insulin or HOMA-IR over 12 weeks; product-specific adjunctive evidence.
10. **anti-inflammatory-diets-supplements-schizophrenia-review — PMID 39839138** — Systematic review found mixed evidence for anti-inflammatory diets, probiotics, fish oil and vitamin D in schizophrenia-related metabolic and symptom outcomes; heterogeneity and adherence barriers preclude broad recommendations.
11. **probiotic-caloric-restriction-obesity-rct — PMID 39842252** — Double-blind RCT in men with obesity evaluated probiotic supplementation during 30% caloric restriction; any body-composition or psychobiological effects must be interpreted as adjunctive to a strong diet intervention.
12. **probiotics-mci-alzheimers-meta-null — PMID 40034358** — Systematic review/meta-analysis of 10 RCTs found probiotics did not significantly improve MMSE, MoCA, memory, attention, language or visuospatial outcomes in MCI/Alzheimer's disease; domain effects were heterogeneous and nonsignificant.
13. **rhodiola-polysaccharides-preclinical-review — PMID 38960222** — Review of Rhodiola polysaccharides focuses on extraction, structure and predominantly preclinical bioactivities; useful chemistry/mechanistic context but not evidence for human efficacy.
14. **ginkgo-donepezil-vascular-dementia-meta — PMID 39021830** — Meta-analysis of 15 RCTs reported better cognitive and daily-function outcomes with Ginkgo extract plus donepezil versus donepezil alone in vascular dementia, but combination therapy and source-trial quality constrain inference about Ginkgo alone.
15. **ginkgo-ischemic-cerebrovascular-rct — PMID 39340861** — Randomized trial in 120 older patients with ischemic cerebrovascular disease reported better neurologic, inflammatory and hemorheology outcomes with Ginkgo extract added to conventional care; adjunctive design limits monotherapy inference.
16. **egb761-alzheimers-narrative-review — PMID 39422946** — Narrative review emphasizes that most positive Ginkgo dementia evidence applies to the standardized EGb 761 extract rather than generic Ginkgo products; clinical heterogeneity and incomplete mechanism remain key boundaries.
17. **bacopa-schizophrenia-systematic-review — PMID 39498770** — Systematic review found only nine eligible Bacopa-related schizophrenia studies and a mixture of mechanistic/preclinical and limited clinical evidence; claims of symptom benefit require much stronger human replication.
18. **vitamin-d-addiction-scoping-review — PMID 40083958** — Scoping review found inverse associations between vitamin D status and several addictive/impulse-control conditions but substantial methodological limitations and sparse intervention evidence; deficiency association should not be treated as treatment efficacy.
19. **vitamin-d-pregnancy-depression-cohort-null — PMID 40226727** — Prospective pregnancy cohort found serum vitamin D levels were not significantly associated with depressive symptoms across pregnancy after adjustment; a change-score signal attenuated with covariate control.
20. **calcium-oxalate-stone-prevention-review — PMID 40458577** — Systematic review identified several non-guideline therapies with urinary-risk or stone-formation signals, including cranberry juice, magnesium compounds, EPA, probiotics and herbs, but intervention diversity and limited replication prevent simple recommendations.
21. **bariatric-surgery-medication-supplement-pk-meta — PMID 38710656** — Systematic review/meta-analysis found bariatric surgery altered pharmacokinetics for some drugs and supplements but not consistently for calcium, ferritin or zinc; heterogeneity and missing data made most supplement-PK conclusions uncertain.
22. **iron-pregnancy-uspstf-evidence-review — PMID 39163033** — USPSTF evidence review found routine iron supplementation reduced maternal iron deficiency and iron-deficiency anemia at term but did not clearly improve major maternal or infant clinical outcomes; screening evidence remained absent.
23. **vitamin-d-prediabetes-prevention-meta — PMID 39421597** — Meta-analysis of 11 RCTs in prediabetes found vitamin D modestly reduced progression to type 2 diabetes and increased regression to normoglycemia, though the effect size was small and clinical applicability depends on population and regimen.
24. **iron-absorption-infants-systematic-review — PMID 39599621** — Systematic review of isotopically measured iron absorption in infants and young children supports current supplementation guidance and identifies ascorbic acid as an absorption enhancer, while lactoferrin/prebiotic strategies remain promising but insufficiently established.
25. **inverse-publication-bias-safety-meta-epidemiology — PMID 39456055** — Empirical analysis of 277 adverse-event meta-analyses found inverse publication bias in roughly 14-16% and showed poor-to-moderate agreement of visual assessments, reinforcing that safety evidence synthesis requires direction-aware bias checks and qualitative review.
