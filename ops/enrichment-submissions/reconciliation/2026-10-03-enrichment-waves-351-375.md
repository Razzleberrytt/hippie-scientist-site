Third 25-finding pass of the Waves 301–500 enrichment program.

## Operating rule
This pass deliberately includes evidence-quality, safety, category-level and combination-level reviews. These are preserved as research receipts and cannot silently attach to narrower entities. Full runtime admission remains deferred until Wave 500.

## Pass 3 — Waves 351–375
1. **plant-bioactive-polysaccharides — PMID 41707853** — 2026 umbrella review of 33 SR/MAs across 111 metabolic indicators; category-level evidence only, with no inheritance to an individual polysaccharide.
2. **herbal-erectile-dysfunction — PMID 41696741** — 14 RCTs/1,227 men across mixed herbal products; product-specific signals coexist with insufficient evidence for some botanicals, so no category-wide efficacy claim.
3. **herbal-safety-t2d — PMID 41539457** — Cross-sectional safety/interaction map across 19 countries and 49 herbs in T2D; contextual safety evidence, not efficacy and not uniform risk across herbs.
4. **herbal-pPI-adjuncts — PMID 42280436** — 18 studies/1,984 participants of herbal/natural products with PPI therapy for H. pylori; combination-context evidence with nonstandard outcomes and exploratory microbiome findings.
5. **mdd-nutraceutical-network — PMID 42680855** — 163 studies/15,757 participants; network meta-analysis with high heterogeneity and author-flagged implausible effects; sensitivity/disagreement must stay visible.
6. **east-asian-herbal-schizophrenia — PMID 42426034** — 270 studies/26,053 participants; adjunct-to-antipsychotic evidence only and low certainty, not monotherapy efficacy.
7. **herbal-pregnancy-exposure — PMID 41519415** — 111-study observational synthesis; no clear overall major adverse-outcome signal after adjustment but herb-specific signals, confounding and evidence quality require separation.
8. **chinese-herbal-dosage-forms — PMID 41618670** — 82 meta-analyses/1,263 RCTs; no universal decoction-versus-capsule/granule effect-size rule; formulation claims require intervention-specific evidence.
9. **mci-ad-supplement-network — PMID 42494057** — 29 trials/2,000 participants across supplements in MCI/AD; multi-node network evidence must remain intervention- and endpoint-specific.
10. **polyphenols — PMID 42280405** — 50 RCTs/2,042 participants; microbiome and short-chain-fatty-acid findings are associative/mechanistic and not proof of clinical prebiotic benefit.
11. **endometriosis-supplements — PMID 42319865** — Dietary-supplement evidence in endometriosis is intervention- and outcome-specific; no category-level therapeutic claim.
12. **rhodiola — PMID 41080184** — Endurance RCT meta-analysis; athletic context and biomarker-versus-performance endpoints remain separate.
13. **lemon-balm — PMID 34449930** — Randomized-trial meta-analysis reported anxiety/depression score improvements without serious adverse effects; acute-setting emphasis and heterogeneity retained.
14. **valerian — PMID 17145239** — 16 randomized placebo-controlled studies/1,093 participants; sleep-quality benefit appeared in a subset but methodological issues, preparation variability and publication bias limit certainty.
15. **psyllium — PMID 18985059** — 21 controlled studies/1,717 participants; total/LDL cholesterol reductions with dose/time relationships; historical limitations and dietary-adjunct context retained.
16. **pelargonium-sidoides — PMID 19435703** — Cochrane acute-respiratory evidence suggested possible symptom benefit in acute bronchitis; product identity and study quality remain explicit.
17. **boswellia-serrata — PMID 25705405** — Osteoarthritis clinical evidence suggests possible pain/function benefit but trials are small and extract/duration specific.
18. **devils-claw — PMID 17348898** — Systematic review of osteoarthritis/low-back pain; possible pain reduction with preparation and trial-quality limitations.
19. **ayurvedic-cognitive-botanicals — PMID 42359794** — 2026 node-resolved review of 49 controlled reports across 20 Ayurvedic dravya nodes; botanical node, plant material and identity certainty must remain separate.
20. **chinese-herbal-insomnia — PMID 40826721** — Network meta-analysis of 186 RCTs/13 interventions; heterogeneous formulations and network rankings cannot be treated as head-to-head proof.
21. **melatonin — PMID 41240058** — Meta-analysis in older adults with cognitive impairment evaluating sleep/cognitive outcomes; age and cognitive-status population boundary retained.
22. **herbal-dementia-interventions — PMID 42100836** — 2026 review of human herbal trials in Alzheimer/dementia; herb-specific efficacy and safety must not be collapsed into a single herbal-medicine claim.
23. **natural-products-ad-mci — PMID 41226670** — 31 clinical-trial articles/3,582 participants across natural compounds in AD/MCI; effects varied by compound, endpoint and duration.
24. **herbal-dementia-evidence-quality — PMID 36728740** — Umbrella review found most underlying herbal dementia reviews critically low or weak quality; evidence-quality counterweight to positive signals.
25. **herbal-toxicity-mechanisms — PMID 41265502** — Systematic toxicology review of toxic phytoconstituents/mechanisms; safety-layer evidence only, not efficacy.
