# Research enrichment Waves 776–800 — 2026-10-03

Twelfth pass of the Waves 501–1000 program.

## Waves 776–800
1. **phytosterols — PMID 40672367** — 2025 14-RCT/1,088-participant hyperlipidemia meta-analysis; TC/LDL-C reductions and modest HDL-C increase, CRP null and triglyceride result borderline/heterogeneous.
2. **l-citrulline — PMID 41786156** — 2026 nine-RCT meta-analysis of long-term L-citrulline plus exercise; small body-fat/android-fat signals while body weight, BMI, gynoid fat and total lean mass were null.
3. **l-citrulline — PMID 41097202** — 2025 21-RCT dose-response meta-analysis; no substantial overall effect on BMI, weight, fat mass, waist, body-fat percentage or fat-free mass, with exploratory subgroup signals only.
4. **phytosterols — PMID 39432657** — 2024 28-RCT/1,777-participant meta-analysis; TC, LDL-C and apoB-related improvements with dose-response patterns, but lipid-surrogate evidence does not establish event reduction.
5. **fiber — PMID 35816465** — 16-RCT/1,251-participant chronic-constipation meta-analysis; overall response improved, with psyllium and pectin among fiber types showing significant effects and heterogeneity retained.
6. **vitamin-b12 — PMID 39655146** — Nine-RCT systematic review/meta-analysis; vitamin B12 supplementation showed no significant pooled benefit for cognitive memory or depressive symptoms.
7. **phytosterols — PMID 39476284** — 2024 inflammatory-marker meta-analysis; CRP overall null while hs-CRP decreased modestly, requiring biomarker-specific interpretation.
8. **citrulline-malate — PMID 42356270** — 2026 three-level exercise-performance meta-analysis; small performance signal must remain distinct from perceived-exertion outcomes and acute/chronic protocol differences.
9. **vitamin-k — PMID 31076817** — 36-RCT bone-health meta-analysis; selected fracture/BMD signals in postmenopausal/osteoporotic populations with questioned historical evidence quality explicitly retained.
10. **b-vitamins — PMID 40966571** — 2025 17-RCT/5,275-participant cognition meta-analysis in adults >=60; apparent larger pooled benefit collapsed to a very small high-certainty effect after removing outliers/single-blind studies.
11. **folic-acid — PMID 38465839** — 2024 22-trial/3,604-participant cognitive-impairment meta-analysis; cognition and homocysteine findings remain population- and endpoint-specific.
12. **folic-acid — PMID 36518821** — Nine-RCT/641-participant memory meta-analysis; overall memory effect was null despite subgroup/dose-response signals, providing a direct counterweight to broad memory claims.
13. **b-vitamin-complex — PMID 31527485** — Systematic review/meta-analysis of multi-B-vitamin supplementation for mood in healthy and at-risk adults; mood/stress signals were inconsistent across populations and outcomes.
14. **multivitamin-mineral — PMID 41308839** — 2026 rapid review of 19 meta-analyses spanning >5.5 million participants; heterogeneous cognitive, psychological, BP and other outcomes must remain outcome- and population-specific.
15. **multivitamin-mineral — PMID 38244989** — COSMOS clinic trial plus meta-analysis of three cognitive substudies; modest global-cognition and episodic-memory signals with no clear executive/attention benefit.
16. **vitamin-e — PMID 33057114** — 33-RCT/2,102-participant inflammatory-biomarker meta-analysis; CRP decreased, IL-6 overall null and TNF-alpha effects depended on high-dose/formulation subgroups.
17. **selenium — PMID 40898469** — 2025 Hashimoto meta-analysis of 21 studies/1,610 participants; TPOAb and selected TSH/TgAb reductions are antibody/biochemical outcomes, not proof of improved long-term thyroid clinical outcomes.
18. **calcium — PMID 36164828** — 43-study/7,382-participant meta-analysis in people <=35; selected femoral-neck/total-body BMD/BMC gains with site-specific nulls and no direct fracture-prevention evidence.
19. **vitamin-c — PMID 33932734** — RCT meta-analysis of vitamin C and adult mood; overall mood effect was not broadly established, with signals concentrated in selected subgroups.
20. **phytosterols — PMID 36891733** — 2023 hypercholesterolemia RCT meta-analysis; TC/LDL-C reductions with HDL-C and triglyceride nulls, and effects modified by food matrix/dose/esterification/duration.
21. **folate — PMID 15260915** — Older controlled-trial review of folate in depressive disorders; limited adjunctive evidence from a small trial base retained as historical context.
22. **potassium — PMID 32500831** — Dose-response RCT synthesis of potassium intake and blood pressure; nonlinear/baseline-dependent effects require caution and do not support a universal optimal supplemental dose.
23. **chromium — PMID 33197598** — Randomized-trial meta-analysis of chromium and lipid profile; endpoint-specific lipid changes with substantial formulation/population heterogeneity.
24. **chromium — PMID 17519436** — Historical systematic review of chromium supplementation for glucose/lipid outcomes; inconsistent trial quality and formulation/dose differences retained as counterweight.
25. **selenium — PMID 24847462** — Cochrane-era Hashimoto evidence review found the small heterogeneous trial base insufficient for reliable clinical conclusions; retained as historical evidence-quality counterweight.
