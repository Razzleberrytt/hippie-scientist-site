# Research enrichment Waves 1301–1325 — 2026-10-04

Thirteenth pass of the Waves 1001–1500 program, broadening sleep, gut health, exercise recovery, women’s health, dietary fibers, botanical extracts, and high-value null findings while preserving formulation and surrogate-outcome boundaries.

## Waves 1301–1325
1. **cricket-protein-concurrent-training — PMID 42813244** — Randomized trial in 44 older adults found concurrent training plus cricket protein hydrolysate improved selected blood-pressure-related measures versus placebo, but showed no clear added benefit over training or protein alone and did not uniquely improve arterial stiffness or most HRV outcomes.
2. **black-cumin-sleep-circadian — PMID 42819242** — Small randomized double-blind crossover trial in 24 adults with non-restorative sleep found a black-cumin-oil extract produced a distinct nocturnal melatonin profile and reduced daytime sleepiness/cortisol, while synthetic melatonin improved sleep latency and total sleep time more; active-comparator design and proprietary formulation limit generalization.
3. **bcaa-postoperative-muscle — PMID 42819727** — Single-blind randomized trial in 50 gastrointestinal-cancer surgery patients found BCAA-enriched supplementation after exercise did not significantly improve thigh muscle area, strength or six-minute-walk distance compared with amino-acid-free supplementation.
4. **caffeine-postexercise-recovery — PMID 42823978** — Systematic review and three-level meta-analysis found caffeine had no clear effect on perceptual or physiological recovery, a small possible benefit for functional performance, and worse sleep recovery; prediction intervals crossed the null and certainty varied by domain.
5. **postbiotic-overweight-gut-health — PMID 42824179** — Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in adults with excess weight and GI discomfort found a heat-killed Lactiplantibacillus plantarum postbiotic improved GSRS symptoms, perceived stress and digestion-related quality of life with microbiome shifts, while TNF-alpha, lipids and body composition were unchanged.
6. **igy-helicobacter-pylori — PMID 42824818** — Randomized double-blind adjunctive trial in 60 H. pylori patients found IgY-enriched egg-yolk powder numerically improved eradication and symptom recovery, but the eradication difference was not statistically significant; promising adjunct signal without confirmatory efficacy.
7. **pediococcus-functional-constipation — PMID 42824822** — Randomized double-blind trial in 87 adults with functional constipation found Pediococcus acidilactici PA53 improved stool form and altered gut microbiota, while between-group bowel-movement-frequency and biomarker differences were not significant.
8. **chickpea-protein-exercise-recovery — PMID 42828502** — Randomized double-blind trial in 24 trained male cyclists found 14 days of chickpea protein hydrolysate improved selected LDH and CRP responses after training, while body composition and direct recovery/performance outcomes were not improved or not measured.
9. **corn-fiber-cardiometabolic — PMID 42829185** — Meta-analysis of randomized trials found corn-derived dietary fibers lowered total and LDL cholesterol modestly, while glycemic control, other lipids, anthropometrics, blood pressure and CRP were unchanged.
10. **beetroot-resistance-training-older-adults — PMID 42829363** — Twelve-week randomized trial in middle-aged and older adults found resistance training improved mitochondrial and strength measures, but nitrate-rich beetroot juice provided no additional benefit on molecular or functional outcomes.
11. **green-coffee-magnesium-t2d — PMID 42531580** — Randomized double-blind pilot in women with type 2 diabetes found a green-coffee-extract plus magnesium-dimalate capsule regimen was feasible, but glycemic and cardiovascular biomarker effects were exploratory and inconclusive.
12. **fenugreek-postpartum-sleep — PMID 42547874** — Triple-blind randomized trial in 60 primiparous postpartum women found fenugreek extract improved later PSQI sleep scores after baseline adjustment, but baseline sleep differed between groups and the study was small.
13. **asparagus-extract-sleep — PMID 42571331** — Randomized double-blind crossover trial in 38 adults dissatisfied with sleep found standardized Asparagus officinalis extract did not improve the primary N3-sleep endpoint but reduced wake after sleep onset and selected insomnia-symptom scores; subgroup findings were post hoc.
14. **propolis-bioavailability — PMID 42598803** — Small randomized double-blind crossover pharmacokinetic study in healthy adults characterized rapid absorption and urinary elimination of phenolics from a standardized propolis extract; evidence informs bioavailability rather than clinical efficacy.
15. **egcg-obesity-iron-inflammation — PMID 42613905** — Pilot randomized double-blind trial in 17 women with obesity found no between-group metabolic, inflammatory or iron-status differences surviving multiple-comparison correction after EGCG; metabolomic findings were nominal and exploratory.
16. **moringa-obesity-metabolomics — PMID 42666703** — Small randomized double-blind trial in adults with obesity found Moringa oleifera leaf extract reduced BMI and improved selected HbA1c, HDL, microbiome and metabolomic measures while preserving lean-mass-related measures; modest sample and multi-omic multiplicity require replication.
17. **shatavari-low-libido — PMID 42751626** — Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in 70 women with low libido found standardized Shatavari root extract improved FSFI and several fatigue, sleep, anxiety and psychosocial scores over eight weeks; single-center proprietary-extract evidence requires independent replication.
18. **andrographolide-urti — PMID 42760788** — Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in 56 adults with uncomplicated URTI found a standardized andrographolide formulation improved symptom scores and mucus weight faster than placebo, while CRP and IL-8 changes were not clearly significant.
19. **ginger-primary-dysmenorrhea — PMID 42765050** — Multicenter randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial found standardized ginger extract reduced menstrual pain, dysmenorrhea severity, symptom burden and menstrual flow over three cycles, with favorable tolerability; formulation-specific replication remains important.
20. **multivitamin-cocoa-neurodegeneration-biomarkers — PMID 42775737** — COSMOS-clinic ancillary analysis found neither a multivitamin-multimineral nor cocoa flavanol extract significantly changed two-year blood neurodegeneration biomarkers NfL, NT1 tau fragment or oligomeric amyloid-beta versus placebo.
21. **walnut-mood-young-adults — PMID 42808314** — Open-label randomized crossover trial in 31 mostly female young adults found eight weeks of walnut consumption modestly reduced total mood disturbance and increased urinary 5-HIAA and urolithins; small sample and open-label food intervention limit causal certainty.
22. **shatavari-postmenopause-discomfort — PMID 42769795** — Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in 214 postmenopausal women found a standardized Asparagus racemosus extract improved several sexual-function and menopausal-symptom measures and oxidative-stress markers; proprietary formulation and broad endpoint set warrant independent replication.
23. **dietary-nitrate-postmenopause-reactivity — PMID 42782334** — Randomized crossover trial in 14 healthy normotensive postmenopausal women found short-term nitrate-rich beetroot juice did not alter blood-pressure or heart-rate reactivity to handgrip, post-exercise ischemia or cold pressor stress.
24. **gochujang-menopause — PMID 42787012** — Eight-week randomized double-blind three-arm trial in 59 postmenopausal women found all gochujang formulations improved symptoms within groups, but there were no between-formulation differences and no placebo arm, so efficacy cannot be established.
25. **prenatal-vitamin-d-offspring-bone — PMID 42829785** — Thirteen-year follow-up of a randomized prenatal vitamin D trial found no lasting benefit of higher-dose gestational vitamin D on offspring bone mineral content, bone density or fracture risk versus standard-dose supplementation.
