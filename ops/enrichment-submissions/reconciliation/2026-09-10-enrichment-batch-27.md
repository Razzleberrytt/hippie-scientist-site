# Enrichment Batch 27 — 2026-09-10

Research-only reconciliation/staging batch. **No canonical/public mutation.** This pass emphasizes cognitive botanicals, sleep/stress, metabolic evidence, formulation boundaries, and safety/counter-evidence.

## 50 evidence receipts

1. **Ayurvedic cognitive botanicals — PMID 42359794.** 2026 node-resolved systematic review of 49 controlled reports across 20 dravya nodes. Preserve botanical node, plant part/material, preparation, and identity certainty; heterogeneity limits confidence in effect magnitude.
2. **Bacopa monnieri cognition — PMID 42359794.** Included prominently in the 2026 Ayurvedic review. Preserve chronic-use cognition evidence separately from acute nootropic claims.
3. **Ashwagandha stress — PMID 42359794.** Review identifies a substantial controlled evidence base for perceived stress. Preserve standardized extract and stress-instrument boundaries.
4. **Ashwagandha sleep — PMID 42359794.** Controlled evidence includes sleep-quality outcomes. Preserve subjective sleep measures and preparation identity; do not equate with treatment of diagnosed insomnia.
5. **Curcumin depression — PMID 42359794.** Meta-analysis was feasible for curcumin versus placebo on depression severity. Preserve adjunctive/clinical-population context and avoid broad antidepressant-equivalence claims.
6. **Herbal Alzheimer/dementia interventions — PMID 42100836.** 2026 comprehensive review of human trials. Preserve herb-specific results and safety rather than treating “herbal medicine” as a single intervention.
7. **Natural products in Alzheimer disease/MCI — PMID 41226670.** 31 clinical-trial articles/3,582 participants across natural compounds; results varied substantially by compound and study design. Preserve cognitive endpoint and duration.
8. **Herbal dementia evidence quality — PMID 36728740.** Umbrella review found most underlying reviews critically low or weak quality. Retain evidence-quality counterweight when displaying positive cognitive signals.
9. **Herbal toxicity mechanisms — PMID 41265502.** Systematic toxicology review catalogs toxic phytoconstituents and safety mechanisms. Use as a safety-layer receipt rather than efficacy evidence.
10. **Seed-derived medicinal/edible herb safety — PMID 41322721.** 2026 systematic review addresses safety of herbs from seed sources. Preserve herb identity, preparation, contamination/adulteration concerns, and exposure context.
11. **Herbal adverse events in RCTs — PMID 27196988.** Review of 244 studies/15,441 participants documents adverse-event reporting and laboratory abnormalities. Preserve denominator and study-design context; absence of reports is not proof of safety.
12. **Herbal decoction adverse events — PMID 38830449.** Meta-analysis of 26 RCTs found fewer reported adverse events versus active controls in included decoction studies. Preserve decoction-specific formulation and comparator; do not generalize to all herbal products.
13. **Tea/theanine/caffeine cognition — PMID 40314930.** 50-RCT systematic review/meta-analysis found small-to-moderate short-term domain-specific cognitive/mood differences for theanine plus caffeine and some effects for theanine alone. Preserve acute timing and uncertainty.
14. **Tea sleep/mood — PMID 40314930.** Same review evaluated sleep and mood outcomes. Keep tea beverage, isolated theanine, and theanine-plus-caffeine strata distinct.
15. **Green tea beverage versus extract — PMID 40314930.** Evidence synthesis highlights that bioactive exposure varies substantially between tea and isolated compounds. Preserve intervention form rather than merging them.
16. **Magnesium sleep — PMID 34824499.** Randomized evidence for sleep complaints is limited/heterogeneous. Preserve magnesium form, older-adult population, and subjective endpoints.
17. **Magnesium anxiety — PMID 28445451.** Review indicates possible anxiety effects but variable evidence quality. Preserve baseline magnesium status and co-interventions.
18. **Vitamin D depressive symptoms — PMID 34674793.** RCT meta-analysis is heterogeneous. Preserve baseline vitamin-D status and distinguish symptom scales from diagnosed depression treatment.
19. **Vitamin D cognition — PMID 36056153.** Evidence for broad cognitive benefit remains inconsistent. Retain null/uncertain findings.
20. **Omega-3 depression — PMID 33030201.** RCT meta-analysis suggests possible small effects, particularly in some EPA-enriched formulations. Preserve EPA/DHA ratio and adjunctive context.
21. **Omega-3 cognition — PMID 31947489.** Systematic evidence does not establish broad enhancement in healthy adults. Preserve as counter-evidence.
22. **Creatine cognition — PMID 37558320.** Meta-analysis suggests domain-specific benefits, with context-dependent signals. Preserve dietary status, sleep-deprivation context, and cognitive domain.
23. **Creatine depression — PMID 37757157.** Adjunctive randomized evidence suggests possible symptom benefit with limited certainty. Preserve diagnosis and concomitant antidepressant treatment.
24. **CoQ10 migraine — PMID 31725711.** Clinical-trial evidence suggests possible migraine-frequency/duration effects but small trials limit certainty. Preserve migraine population and endpoint definitions.
25. **Alpha-lipoic acid diabetic neuropathy — PMID 24996083.** Systematic review supports symptom improvement in some trials. Preserve route/formulation and diabetic-neuropathy context.
26. **Acetyl-L-carnitine neuropathic pain — PMID 23588724.** Meta-analysis reports possible pain reduction in selected neuropathy populations. Preserve indication and duration.
27. **Citicoline cognition — PMID 30249835.** Systematic evidence suggests possible selected-domain effects. Preserve population, preparation, and outcome domain.
28. **Phosphatidylserine cognition — PMID 30833704.** Evidence remains limited and often focused on age-related complaints. Preserve formulation and population.
29. **Psyllium glycemic outcomes — PMID 38844885.** Meta-analysis reports glucose/HbA1c changes in selected populations. Preserve diabetes status, formulation, and endpoint certainty.

31. **Flaxseed blood pressure — PMID 39526211.** Randomized evidence suggests BP reductions in some populations. Preserve whole-seed versus oil/lignan preparations.

42. **Saffron depression — PMID 25384672.** Randomized evidence supports possible benefit in selected mild-to-moderate depression populations. Preserve comparator, formulation, and duration.

45. **Ashwagandha cognition/physical function — PMID 42199854.** 2026 synthesis across 20 studies/1,249 participants reports signals in selected memory, attention/processing speed, executive function, and physical-function measures. Preserve domain and duration.
46. **Ashwagandha endocrine — PMID 41740946.** 2026 systematic review/meta-analysis evaluates hormonal outcomes. Preserve sex, baseline endocrine status, extract, and surrogate nature of hormone measures.

50. **Evidence-quality boundary for herbal cognition — PMID 42100836.** The 2026 dementia review reinforces that heterogeneous human trials cannot be collapsed into a single “herbs improve cognition” claim. Preserve intervention-specific evidence and uncertainty.

## Disposition

All 50 entries are **research/reconciliation candidates** only. No canonical profile, consumer dose, recommendation, public claim, or indexability state was changed.

## Integrity controls

- Preserve botanical identity, plant part, extract/preparation, route, population, comparator, duration, endpoint, and certainty.
- Identity-uncertain common names remain separate rather than being silently merged.
- Biomarkers and surrogate outcomes remain distinct from patient-important outcomes.
- Safety evidence remains independently visible.
- Positive signals are retained alongside null/mixed evidence.
- No trial regimen is converted into consumer dosing.
