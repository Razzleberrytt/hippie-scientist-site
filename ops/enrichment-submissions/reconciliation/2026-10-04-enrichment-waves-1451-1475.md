# Research enrichment Waves 1451–1475 — 2026-10-04

Nineteenth pass of the Waves 1001–1500 program, emphasizing botanicals and nitrate-rich beetroot while preserving formulation boundaries, null findings, surrogate limitations, and safety signals.

## Waves 1451–1475
1. **curcumin-hashimoto-antiinflammatory-diet — PMID 41329567** — Twelve-week randomized double-blind trial in Hashimoto thyroiditis found curcumin added to an anti-inflammatory diet reduced anti-TPO more than diet plus placebo, while adjusted thyroid-hormone and lipid differences were not significant; combined-diet context limits curcumin-only inference.
2. **curcumin-turmeric-blood-pressure-diabetes — PMID 41388744** — Meta-analysis of 15 RCTs in prediabetes/type 2 diabetes found a modest systolic-blood-pressure reduction with curcumin/turmeric but no significant overall diastolic effect; formulation and dose subgroup signals require caution.
3. **turmeric-formulation-safety-tolerance — PMID 41459382** — Five-week randomized double-blind placebo-controlled safety trial in healthy adults found a highly bioavailable turmeric formulation was well tolerated without clinically significant GI, laboratory, vital-sign or adverse-event differences versus placebo.
4. **curcumin-egcg-mood-bdnf — PMID 41830024** — Eight-week randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial found combined curcumin plus EGCG did not improve mood disturbance, sleep or serum BDNF beyond placebo despite within-group improvements in both arms.
5. **curcuminoids-menopause-vasomotor — PMID 41877056** — Pilot randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in 40 postmenopausal women found curcuminoids reduced weekly hot-flash frequency, while global menopause quality-of-life measures were less consistently improved; proof-of-concept signal needs larger replication.
6. **meriva-gastric-chemoprevention — PMID 42077070** — Phase-IIa randomized double-blind trial in gastric premalignant conditions found a bioavailable curcumin formulation reduced gastric-body IL-1β but did not improve histology, DNA-damage or most other inflammatory endpoints; biomarker signal does not establish cancer prevention.
7. **curcumin-obese-t2d-inflammation — PMID 42123439** — Twelve-month randomized placebo-controlled trial in adults with obesity and type 2 diabetes reported improvements in inflammatory, oxidative-stress and glycemic markers with curcumin; single-trial biomarker-heavy evidence needs independent replication and clinical-outcome confirmation.
8. **curcumin-piperine-prediabetes — PMID 42153019** — Triple-blind randomized trial in prediabetes found curcumin plus piperine did not significantly improve fasting glucose, HbA1c or anthropometrics; apparent CRP and insulin/HOMA differences disappeared after adjustment.
9. **nanoliposomal-curcumin-bioavailability — PMID 42225688** — Small randomized double-blind pharmacokinetic trial found a double-layered nano-liposomal curcumin formulation produced far higher dose-normalized plasma and urinary exposure than conventional curcumin; PK superiority does not establish superior clinical efficacy.
10. **curcumin-high-altitude-body-composition — PMID 42231385** — Randomized trial during high-altitude acclimatization found curcumin modestly reduced body-fat percentage and altered gut-microbiome composition, while lean-mass changes were not clearly different between groups; microbiome associations were noncausal.
11. **korean-red-ginseng-diabetic-retinopathy — PMID 42395017** — Small randomized double-blind trial in diabetic retinopathy found Korean red ginseng changed selected deep-capillary OCTA perfusion metrics without improving visual acuity, macular thickness, retinopathy stage or edema incidence; surrogate microvascular evidence only.
12. **ashwagandha-liver-injury-rucam — PMID 42529606** — Case report with updated RUCAM assessment documented probable cholestatic herb-induced liver injury after ashwagandha exposure; useful safety signal but not an incidence estimate or proof of typical risk.
13. **beetroot-nitrate-dose-response-cardiovascular — PMID 41704173** — Randomized double-blind crossover trial found acute nitrate-rich beetroot improved arterial stiffness across tested doses, while endothelial function and aortic systolic pressure showed dose-specific effects; brachial pressure and cGMP were unchanged.
14. **beetroot-postmenopause-muscle-quality — PMID 41830030** — Twelve-week randomized double-blind trial in 20 postmenopausal women found nitrate-rich beetroot extract improved morphological muscle quality and rate-of-force development, while maximal strength and functional muscle-quality effects were less clear; very small sample.
15. **saffron-osteoarthritis — PMID 40965059** — Randomized double-blind osteoarthritis trial found saffron reduced IL-1β and NSAID use while both groups improved in pain and physical activity; small sample, differential dropout and self-reported adherence limit confidence in analgesic efficacy.
16. **saffron-pms-dysmenorrhea-meta — PMID 41151539** — Systematic review/meta-analysis of randomized trials found saffron improved pooled PMS and dysmenorrhea symptom scores, but the evidence base was small and likely sensitive to trial quality and formulation differences.
17. **saffron-parkinsons — PMID 41439298** — Triple-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial in Parkinson disease reported improvements in CRP, mood, sleep, fatigue, cognition and quality-of-life domains with saffron; broad multi-endpoint findings warrant replication before strong clinical claims.
18. **berberine-sex-specific-lipids — PMID 42470424** — Analysis based on two placebo-controlled berberine trials found reductions in apoB, LDL, triglycerides and lipoprotein(a), with larger lipid responses in women and opposite testosterone-direction signals by sex; cross-trial sex comparisons remain exploratory.
19. **cinnamon-exercise-overweight-women — PMID 42683508** — Small randomized trial found exercise improved anthropometric and reproductive-hormone measures in overweight/obese women, while adding cinnamon produced no clear additional benefit except chest circumference; no convincing hormone-specific cinnamon effect.
20. **htd1801-berberine-udca-t2d-masld — PMID 42249406** — Phase-Ib randomized trial found a berberine-ursodeoxycholic-acid compound was generally tolerated and produced dose-related glucose, lipid and liver-enzyme changes over 28 days; combination drug exposure prevents attribution to berberine alone and clinical durability is unknown.
21. **berberine-schizophrenia-metabolic-syndrome — PMID 42433212** — Meta-analysis of three RCTs found adjunctive berberine improved weight, BMI, waist circumference, triglycerides, total cholesterol and fasting glucose in schizophrenia with metabolic syndrome, while several other metabolic outcomes were null; evidence base is small.
22. **berberine-mulberry-chromium-obesity — PMID 42738972** — Twelve-week randomized double-blind trial found a fixed combination of berberine, white-mulberry extract and chromium improved BIA-derived body composition and OGTT insulin sensitivity versus placebo; no single-agent arms permit component attribution.
23. **beetroot-resistance-training-mobility — PMID 41408034** — Pilot randomized double-blind trial found nitrate-rich beetroot plus resistance training improved gait speed, stride length and timed-up-and-go more than training plus nitrate-depleted placebo; sample was small and other training outcomes were similar.
24. **beetroot-resistance-training-muscle-vascular — PMID 41525148** — Randomized 12-week resistance-training trial found nitrate-rich beetroot did not add significant benefits for lean mass, muscle cross-sectional area, strength, flow-mediated dilation, angiogenesis or muscle molecular markers versus nitrate-depleted placebo.
25. **beetroot-balanced-placebo-cycling — PMID 41886769** — Balanced-placebo randomized study in trained cyclists found neither actual beetroot juice nor the expectation of receiving beetroot improved 20-minute virtual cycling performance, heart rate, cadence or related outcomes.
