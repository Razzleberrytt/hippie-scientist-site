# Research enrichment Waves 1251–1275 — 2026-10-04

Eleventh pass of the Waves 1001–1500 program, emphasizing vitamin K/K2, HMB, fiber/prebiotics, PCOS/inositol, metabolic outcomes, and direct null-vs-meta-analysis reconciliation.

## Waves 1251–1275
1. **menaquinone7-glycemic-lipids — PMID 40054729** — Meta-analysis of six randomized trials found MK-7 improved selected glycemic measures, but heterogeneity was very high and most other anthropometric/lipid outcomes were null; the small evidence base does not support broad metabolic efficacy claims.
2. **menaquinone7-vascular-stiffness-menopause — PMID 40077685** — Post-hoc analysis of a one-year randomized trial in women with low vitamin-K status found MK-7 lowered dp-ucMGP and attenuated vascular-stiffness progression in postmenopausal subgroups, especially those with higher baseline stiffness; subgroup/post-hoc design limits confirmatory inference.
3. **vitamin-k2-d3-cardiovascular-inflammation — PMID 41100911** — AVADEC substudy in 388 older men found two years of vitamin K2 plus D3 markedly improved vitamin-K status but did not significantly change epicardial/pericoronary adipose inflammation or systemic inflammatory biomarkers; useful surrogate-target null evidence.
4. **vitamin-k2-bone-turnover — PMID 41268154** — Meta-analysis of nine studies/2,570 postmenopausal osteoporosis patients found vitamin K2 improved several bone-turnover biomarkers, including osteocalcin carboxylation, while NTX was unchanged and clinical benefits such as fracture or BMD improvement remain unconfirmed.
5. **vitamin-k-glycemic-control — PMID 41599883** — Meta-analysis of eight RCTs in prediabetes/type-2 diabetes found modest reductions in fasting glucose, HbA1c and HOMA-IR with vitamin K, while fasting insulin and HOMA-beta were unchanged; isoform and duration heterogeneity limit treatment claims.
6. **vitamin-k2-exercise-recovery — PMID 41843412** — Twelve-week randomized double-blind trial in 71 young and older adults found vitamin K2 did not improve muscle strength, physical function, soreness or overall inflammatory recovery after muscle-damaging exercise; age-specific electrophysiologic and CK/IL-6 signals were exploratory.
7. **menaquinone-producing-probiotics-vitamin-k — PMID 42000184** — Very small double-blind RCT in 20 older adults at cardiovascular risk found menaquinone-producing probiotics improved average dp-ucMGP and serum MK-7 over 12 weeks, but group differences diminished by week 12 and no clinical outcomes were tested.
8. **vitamin-k2-overweight-cardiometabolic — PMID 42193338** — Twelve-week randomized double-blind trial in 46 young adults with overweight/obesity found MK-4 lowered total and LDL cholesterol, while body composition, glucose metabolism and vitamin-K-dependent proteins were unchanged; small sample and multiple endpoints require replication.
9. **menaquinone7-coronary-calcification — PMID 42268593** — Two-year randomized placebo-controlled trial in symptomatic coronary disease found MK-7 slowed progression of coronary calcium scores relative to placebo while strongly raising circulating MK-7; imaging progression is a surrogate and does not establish reduction in cardiovascular events.
10. **creatine-hmb-older-adults — PMID 41073834** — Randomized double-blind crossover trial in 30 physically active older adults found creatine plus HMB during structured training improved several functional-strength/endurance measures, with little clear muscle-mass gain; combination exposure prevents attribution to either ingredient alone.
11. **hmb-protein-turnover-older-adults — PMID 42124050** — Double-blind crossover tracer study in 24 older adults found HMB added to a 40-g whey bolus further increased muscle-protein synthesis in older women and suppressed later protein breakdown; acute mechanistic responses do not establish long-term functional benefit.
12. **hmb-critical-illness-inflammation — PMID 42202485** — Secondary post-hoc analysis of a critical-care HMB RCT found complex amino-acid and cytokine changes, including several higher pro-inflammatory cytokines in the HMB group; the findings challenge a simple anti-inflammatory narrative and remain exploratory.
13. **prebiotic-akkermansia-gut-health — PMID 42158814** — Eight-week randomized trial of a prebiotic blend designed to promote Akkermansia increased its relative abundance and shifted selected taxa, with only a trend toward lower GI discomfort; microbiome changes were clearer than clinical effects.
14. **inulin-type1-diabetes-children — PMID 42165942** — Double-blind pilot RCT in 68 children with type-1 diabetes found oligofructose-enriched inulin enriched SCFA-producing taxa but did not significantly improve HbA1c over 12 weeks; microbiome modulation did not translate into short-term glycemic benefit.
15. **oatmeal-resistant-starch-uric-acid — PMID 42171138** — Eight-week three-arm RCT in 99 young adults found both oatmeal and resistant-starch fiber diets lowered serum uric acid versus control, with a larger reduction in the oatmeal group; multi-omics mediation findings remain mechanistic and population-specific.
16. **synbiotic-pediatric-masld — PMID 42185448** — Eight-week double-blind placebo-controlled pediatric MASLD trial found a nine-strain synbiotic improved liver enzymes, lipids, BMI and ultrasound steatosis grade versus prebiotic-only placebo; short duration, combined formulation and surrogate liver measures require cautious interpretation.
17. **inulin-fructans-psoriasis-metabolism — PMID 42356231** — Randomized double-blind psoriasis trial found chicory inulin-type fructans did not significantly improve body composition and largely stabilized metabolic variables while placebo worsened on some measures; authors cautioned that placebo composition may explain apparent differences.
18. **prebiotics-celiac-disease — PMID 42365652** — Systematic review of 12 celiac-disease studies found prebiotic foods/supplements appeared generally safe and influenced SCFAs, microbiota and selected nutrient markers without compromising mucosal healing; intervention heterogeneity and mixed study designs prevent a class-wide efficacy claim.
19. **probiotic-synbiotic-pcos — PMID 42420973** — Meta-analysis of 11 RCTs/780 women with PCOS found probiotics/synbiotics improved selected insulin, testosterone, lipid, BMI and antioxidant outcomes, while fasting glucose, HOMA-IR, several hormones and waist measures were null; effects were outcome-specific rather than global.
20. **inulin-functional-food-obesity — PMID 42485520** — Small randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in 50 women with obesity found an inulin-enriched tapioca product reduced body weight, body fat and selected lipid measures over 12 weeks; proprietary functional-food format and limited sample require replication.
21. **resistant-starch-polydextrose-bile-acids — PMID 42489745** — Randomized controlled factorial trial in healthy adults found resistant starch and polydextrose produced distinct plasma/fecal bile-acid and microbiome associations; mechanistic bile-acid shifts should not be interpreted as proven clinical benefit.
22. **resistant-starch-blood-pressure-autonomic — PMID 42754828** — Eight-week double-blind pilot in 30 women at metabolic-syndrome risk found resistant starch lowered systolic pressure within the intervention group, but the adjusted between-group difference was not significant and heart-rate-variability effects were exploratory.
23. **garlic-pcos-metabolic-syndrome — PMID 40770379** — Eight-week randomized placebo-controlled trial in 97 women with PCOS and metabolic syndrome found garlic improved several glucose, lipid, blood-pressure, inflammatory and quality-of-life measures; short follow-up and broad multiple-endpoint testing warrant replication.
24. **myo-inositol-pregnancy-pcos — PMID 40920401** — Large double-blind placebo-controlled multicenter trial in 464 pregnant people with PCOS found myo-inositol did not reduce the composite risk of gestational diabetes, preeclampsia or preterm birth; strong direct null evidence against routine benefit in this population.
25. **inositol-gestational-diabetes-umbrella — PMID 41792927** — Umbrella meta-analysis reported lower gestational-diabetes and selected maternal/neonatal risks with inositol across prior meta-analyses, but the positive pooled signal should be reconciled with newer large direct RCT null findings such as PMID 40920401.
