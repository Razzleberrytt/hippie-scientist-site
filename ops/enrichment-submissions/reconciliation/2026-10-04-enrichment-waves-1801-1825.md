## Waves 1801–1825
1. **pomegranate-igf1-telomere-older-adults — PMID 41010500** — Twelve-week double-blind RCT in 72 adults aged 55-70 found pomegranate extract increased serum IGF-1 but did not significantly change telomere length; biomarker change should not be reframed as demonstrated anti-aging benefit.
2. **cocoa-extract-tmao-cosmos-pilot — PMID 41127993** — Pilot analysis of 37 COSMOS participants found cocoa-extract assignment produced only nonsignificant trends toward lower TMAO/choline over one year; tiny biomarker substudy was designed for feasibility/sample-size estimation rather than clinical efficacy.
3. **cocoa-flavanols-diastolic-function — PMID 41143317** — Double-blind randomized substudy in 63 healthy older adults found 30 days of cocoa flavanols reduced left-atrial volume and systolic blood pressure with selected ventricular-volume changes but no clear strain/systolic-function effect; imaging surrogate evidence.
4. **pomegranate-mouthrinse-gingivitis — PMID 41185719** — Randomized double-blind trial in 60 adults found 30% pomegranate-peel mouthrinse reduced plaque/gingival indices and bacterial counts over 21 days, with some outcomes outperforming chlorhexidine; topical oral formulation evidence does not support systemic pomegranate claims.
5. **garlic-cholesterol-mechanisms-review — PMID 41273868** — Systematic mechanistic review maps garlic effects on cholesterol synthesis, absorption, LDL uptake, HDL biology and microbiota, while emphasizing that clinical effects depend on sustained use, preparation and bioactive composition; useful evidence map rather than a single pooled efficacy estimate.
6. **garlic-ms-fatigue-rct — PMID 41341173** — Randomized single-blind placebo-controlled trial in adults with multiple sclerosis found four weeks of garlic extract improved fatigue and selected quality-of-life domains versus placebo; short duration and formulation-specific extract warrant replication.
7. **pomegranate-tea-covid-rct-null — PMID 41473214** — Double-blind randomized trial in hospitalized COVID-19 patients found pomegranate tea did not significantly improve hospitalization duration, time to improvement, mortality, respiratory-support duration or symptom severity versus placebo.
8. **garlic-mouthwash-cariogenic-meta — PMID 41485319** — Systematic review/meta-analysis of nine RCTs found garlic-based mouthrinses had antimicrobial activity against cariogenic organisms, but effects versus chlorhexidine varied by follow-up and heterogeneity was high; certainty was rated low.
9. **pomegranate-extract-gut-microbiota-shime — PMID 41427997** — Controlled SHIME gut-model study found a commercial pomegranate extract altered selected microbial taxa and lactate/metabolite patterns in a dose-dependent manner; ex-vivo fermentation evidence should not be presented as a human clinical outcome.
10. **celery-cardiometabolic-meta — PMID 40765738** — Meta-analysis of 10 randomized trials found celery preparations reduced blood pressure, fasting glucose and triglycerides while total cholesterol, LDL and HDL were not consistently improved; heterogeneous preparations and subgroup dose signals require caution.
11. **creatine-kidney-function-meta — PMID 41199218** — Systematic review/meta-analysis found creatine caused a small rise in serum creatinine but no significant deterioration in GFR, supporting the distinction between creatinine turnover and renal impairment; study durations/populations still limit very-long-term certainty.
12. **pediatric-nutraceutical-adverse-event-reporting — PMID 40652350** — Systematic review of 98 pediatric neuropsychiatric nutraceutical/phytoceutical trials found adverse-event monitoring and attribution were inconsistently specified, with GI symptoms and headache most commonly reported; safety-reporting quality itself is a major evidence limitation.
13. **artri-king-undeclared-drugs-safety — PMID 40663123** — Systematic review/case series found serious adverse outcomes among users of Artri King, including Cushing syndrome, adrenal insufficiency and worsened hyperglycemia, consistent with FDA warnings about undeclared diclofenac/dexamethasone; product-safety signal only.
14. **nutraceuticals-rheumatoid-arthritis-review — PMID 41026372** — Systematic review summarizes probiotics, omega-3s, CoQ10, vitamin D and polyphenols in rheumatoid arthritis, but heterogeneous interventions, microbiome endpoints and clinical outcomes prevent class-wide treatment conclusions.
15. **sports-supplements-volleyball-network-meta — PMID 41373993** — Network meta-analysis of 35 RCTs in 838 volleyball athletes ranked β-alanine, creatine and caffeine for selected explosive-performance outcomes, but evidence certainty was low-to-moderate and network rankings should not be treated as universal superiority.
16. **multiple-micronutrients-anemia-meta — PMID 41451061** — Meta-analysis of 12 studies found some intermittent/daily multiple-micronutrient regimens reduced anemia or iron-deficiency prevalence but did not consistently increase hemoglobin; certainty ranged from very low to moderate and formulations varied.
17. **curcuma-metabolic-syndrome-meta — PMID 41517125** — Large systematic review/meta-analysis of 104 RCTs found standardized turmeric/curcumin supplements improved several glycemic, lipid and inflammatory markers across metabolic conditions, but formulations were highly heterogeneous and often bioavailability-enhanced rather than culinary turmeric.
18. **dietary-supplements-androgenetic-alopecia-network-meta — PMID 41561175** — Network meta-analysis of 19 RCTs compared diverse dietary supplements for androgenetic alopecia; small studies, heterogeneous products and limited direct comparisons constrain rankings and safety/efficacy certainty.
19. **vitamin-d-diabetes-dose-response-meta — PMID 41280391** — Systematic review/meta-analysis of seven studies found higher vitamin-D regimens raised serum vitamin D and modestly improved HbA1c versus lower doses, while total cholesterol was not significantly different; mixed designs and dose-comparison framing do not justify consumer dosing guidance.
20. **curcumin-elderly-cardiometabolic-review — PMID 40516827** — Systematic review of seven RCTs in older adults found curcumin improved selected inflammatory, glycemic, blood-pressure and lipid markers, while other cardiovascular outcomes were inconsistent; formulation/protocol heterogeneity limits broad recommendations.
21. **medicine-food-bioactives-parkinsons-review — PMID 41356826** — Review of medicine-food homology bioactives in Parkinson's disease found evidence predominantly preclinical with sparse disease-specific randomized trials and pharmacokinetic limitations for compounds such as curcumin and EGCG; useful translational boundary evidence.
22. **nutrition-inflammatory-arthritis-systematic-review — PMID 41030267** — Systematic review of 49 RCTs across rheumatoid, axial spondyloarthritis and psoriatic arthritis found selected diet/supplement signals but substantial heterogeneity prevented meta-analysis; disease-, nutrient- and intervention-specific conclusions must remain separate.
23. **antiatherosclerotic-supplements-bleeding-interactions — PMID 41155474** — Safety-focused review highlights potential bleeding and drug-interaction concerns with omega-3s, garlic, nattokinase, ginkgo, ginger, ginseng, curcumin and other nutraceuticals, especially with antithrombotic therapy; interaction context should accompany efficacy claims.
24. **bpc157-sports-medicine-systematic-review — PMID 40756949** — Systematic review found 35 of 36 BPC-157 studies were preclinical and only one clinical study was identified; despite growing use, human efficacy/safety remain poorly characterized and professional-sport use is prohibited.
25. **functional-foods-cholesterol-review — PMID 40871675** — Review summarizes plant sterols, viscous fibers, soy protein, red-yeast rice, berberine, garlic and other cholesterol-lowering food/nutraceutical approaches, but its proposed composite index is conceptual and should not be treated as a validated clinical ranking.
