# Research enrichment Waves 2126–2150 — deep pass

Sixth deep pass of the Waves 2001–2500 program.

## Quality notes
- Rejected radioactive iodine fertility literature as a nutrient-identity false positive.
- Replaced a questionnaire-methodology review with direct clinical melatonin/sepsis evidence.
- Added a 114,592-participant omega-3 randomized-trial meta-analysis identifying an atrial-fibrillation safety signal concentrated in high-dose treatment among high-CV-risk patients.

## Waves
- **2126 · bipolar-cognition-nutraceuticals · PMID 41843701** — Among nutraceutical-relevant agents, ashwagandha showed cognitive benefit in a single study while DHA showed no significant effect; the wider review found inconsistent cognitive outcomes across agents and study quality ranged from good to poor. _State:_ `research_only_evidence_quality`
- **2127 · curcumin · PMID 41327719** — Curcumin significantly reduced DAS-28, rheumatoid factor, ESR, and CRP in pooled analyses, but heterogeneity was substantial and longer exposure-verified trials are needed. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2128 · iron · PMID 42240175** — Oral iron probably reduced low-hemoglobin donation deferral and may improve hemoglobin and ferritin; gastrointestinal adverse events were more frequent. IV iron increased hemoglobin and ferritin but evidence for several outcomes remained uncertain. _State:_ `research_only_population_firewall`
- **2129 · selenium · PMID 41666659** — The review describes selenium-dependent antioxidant mechanisms and limited human signals for sperm motility/fertility, while emphasizing a U-shaped exposure relationship in which excessive intake can impair sperm motility. _State:_ `research_only_evidence_quality`
- **2130 · vitamin-d · PMID 41983136** — MS was associated with lower vitamin-D status, but overall supplementation did not significantly reduce annualized relapse rate or improve disability; high-dose subgroup signals suggested lower relapse risk without clear disability benefit. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2131 · iron-mhealth-adherence · PMID 41955565** — Nine of 11 trials reported improved hemoglobin with mHealth-supported nutrition/supplement interventions, with some clinically large changes, but delivery modes and intervention components were too heterogeneous for meta-analysis. _State:_ `research_only_implementation`
- **2132 · xanthohumol · PMID 41683342** — Xanthohumol modulates multiple inflammatory, redox, metabolic, and cell-signaling pathways; human studies mainly support tolerability and biomarker effects rather than established disease efficacy. _State:_ `research_only_preclinical`
- **2133 · vitamin-c · PMID 41692856** — High-dose IV then oral vitamin C corrected plasma vitamin-C levels, while trends toward less graft-versus-host disease and oral mucositis were not statistically significant. _State:_ `research_only_route_firewall`
- **2134 · korean-red-ginseng · PMID 38576235** — Korean Red Ginseng did not significantly outperform placebo for fatigue improvement at 12 weeks; both groups improved. _State:_ `research_only_formulation_firewall`
- **2135 · melatonin · PMID 39786701** — Melatonin improved drug-induced AKI markers in animal models but did not significantly reduce creatinine, BUN, or AKI incidence in clinical studies; clinical certainty was very low. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2136 · ashwagandha · PMID 40746175** — Ashwagandha significantly reduced cortisol but did not significantly improve perceived-stress scores in pooled analysis. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2137 · early-life-protein · PMID 41338694** — Higher-protein interventions did not substantially change weight-for-length, fat-free mass, or fat mass in pooled analyses. _State:_ `research_only_population_firewall`
- **2138 · omega-3 · PMID 42433395** — Omega-3 produced a small overall symptom benefit; estimated effects were larger in children with low baseline omega-3 biomarkers and absent in normal/high or unstratified groups. _State:_ `research_only_population_firewall`
- **2139 · omega-3 · PMID 40836005** — Omega-3 supplementation was associated with modest improvements across several cognitive domains, with low-to-moderate certainty and nonlinear dose-response patterns for some outcomes. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2140 · protein-exercise · PMID 40805978** — Exercise plus nutritional supplementation ranked best for several strength/function outcomes, while nutritional supplementation alone did not significantly improve any assessed outcome. _State:_ `research_only_combination_firewall`
- **2141 · vitamin-d · PMID 42015226** — Women with GDM had lower vitamin-D status, and supplementation reliably raised 25(OH)D; evidence for downstream metabolic benefit was less clearly established than correction of deficiency. _State:_ `research_only_population_firewall`
- **2142 · probiotic-prebiotic-synbiotic · PMID 41511913** — Microbiome-targeting supplements improved constipation-related outcomes; motor-symptom improvement was uncertain and evidence certainty was very low. _State:_ `research_only_category_firewall`
- **2143 · steamed-ginger-extract · PMID 41599979** — A proprietary GD-standardized steamed ginger extract reduced body-fat percentage/mass, weight, BMI, waist and hip circumference, triglycerides, and total cholesterol versus placebo. _State:_ `research_only_formulation_firewall`
- **2144 · hypericum-perforatum · PMID 41253417** — Both intravaginal Hypericum gel and clotrimazole improved symptoms; Hypericum was not superior overall, with symptom-domain differences between groups. _State:_ `research_only_route_firewall`

- **2147 · omega-3 · PMID 42517224** — High-dose EPA/DHA above 1,500 mg/day increased new-onset atrial-fibrillation risk in people at high cardiovascular risk (OR 1.43; absolute risk difference about 0.8%); lower-dose regimens did not show a significant AF increase. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2148 · calcium · PMID 42141946** — Higher total calcium intake was associated with modestly lower breast-cancer risk, but supplemental calcium alone was not significantly associated with lower risk. _State:_ `research_only_observational`
- **2149 · astaxanthin · PMID 41673418** — Astaxanthin increased oocyte maturity and favorably shifted inflammatory RAGE-NFκB-related biomarkers, but the pilot was not powered to confirm the primary OHSS endpoint. _State:_ `research_only_population_firewall`
- **2150 · melatonin · PMID 39988019** — Adjunctive melatonin was associated with lower mortality in pooled trials (OR 0.42), alongside anti-inflammatory signals. _State:_ `research_only_population_firewall`
