# Research enrichment Waves 2126–2150 — deep pass

Sixth deep pass of the Waves 2001–2500 program, reconciled for cumulative PMID uniqueness.

## Quality notes
- One duplicate omega-3/ADHD PMID already present at Wave 2031 was removed.
- Replacement Wave 2138 is an abstract-reviewed vitamin-C/hemodialysis systematic review.
- Null, safety, route, formulation, population, observational, and evidence-quality boundaries remain explicit.

## Waves
- **2126 · bipolar-cognition-nutraceuticals · PMID 41843701** — Among nutraceutical-relevant agents, ashwagandha showed cognitive benefit in a single study while DHA showed no significant effect; the wider review found inconsistent cognitive outcomes across agents and study quality ranged from good to poor. Boundary: Single-study nutraceutical signals are insufficient for an ingredient-level cognitive recommendation in bipolar disorder. State: `research_only_evidence_quality`.
- **2127 · curcumin · PMID 41327719** — Curcumin significantly reduced DAS-28, rheumatoid factor, ESR, and CRP in pooled analyses, but heterogeneity was substantial and longer exposure-verified trials are needed. Boundary: Adjunctive RA evidence is condition-specific and does not establish long-term disease modification or a universal formulation/dose. State: `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`.
- **2128 · iron · PMID 42240175** — Oral iron probably reduced low-hemoglobin donation deferral and may improve hemoglobin and ferritin; gastrointestinal adverse events were more frequent. IV iron increased hemoglobin and ferritin but evidence for several outcomes remained uncertain. Boundary: Benefit and gastrointestinal harm should be preserved together; donor-specific supplementation should be targeted by iron status rather than generalized to healthy non-donors. State: `research_only_population_firewall`.
- **2129 · selenium · PMID 41666659** — The review describes selenium-dependent antioxidant mechanisms and limited human signals for sperm motility/fertility, while emphasizing a U-shaped exposure relationship in which excessive intake can impair sperm motility. Boundary: Mixed animal/human evidence and selenium's narrow safety margin prevent dose inference; toxicity risk is central to interpretation. State: `research_only_evidence_quality`.
- **2130 · vitamin-d · PMID 41983136** — MS was associated with lower vitamin-D status, but overall supplementation did not significantly reduce annualized relapse rate or improve disability; high-dose subgroup signals suggested lower relapse risk without clear disability benefit. Boundary: Status association, overall randomized effect, and high-dose subgroup findings must remain separate; subgroup signals do not justify unsupervised high-dose dosing. State: `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`.
- **2131 · iron-mhealth-adherence · PMID 41955565** — Nine of 11 trials reported improved hemoglobin with mHealth-supported nutrition/supplement interventions, with some clinically large changes, but delivery modes and intervention components were too heterogeneous for meta-analysis. Boundary: This evaluates implementation/adherence strategies rather than isolating iron-supplement efficacy. State: `research_only_implementation`.
- **2132 · xanthohumol · PMID 41683342** — Xanthohumol modulates multiple inflammatory, redox, metabolic, and cell-signaling pathways; human studies mainly support tolerability and biomarker effects rather than established disease efficacy. Boundary: Clinical relevance remains unproven for most disease claims; bioavailability/formulation is a major translational limitation. State: `research_only_preclinical`.
- **2133 · vitamin-c · PMID 41692856** — High-dose IV then oral vitamin C corrected plasma vitamin-C levels, while trends toward less graft-versus-host disease and oral mucositis were not statistically significant. Boundary: Very small pilot, mixed IV/oral route, high-risk transplant population, and non-significant clinical outcomes preclude efficacy promotion. State: `research_only_route_firewall`.
- **2134 · korean-red-ginseng · PMID 38576235** — Korean Red Ginseng did not significantly outperform placebo for fatigue improvement at 12 weeks; both groups improved. Boundary: Pruritus and urticaria were among the most frequent adverse events; results are specific to Korean Red Ginseng and rheumatic-disease fatigue. State: `research_only_formulation_firewall`.
- **2135 · melatonin · PMID 39786701** — Melatonin improved drug-induced AKI markers in animal models but did not significantly reduce creatinine, BUN, or AKI incidence in clinical studies; clinical certainty was very low. Boundary: Human null evidence must take precedence over positive animal signals when discussing clinical efficacy. State: `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`.
- **2136 · ashwagandha · PMID 40746175** — Ashwagandha significantly reduced cortisol but did not significantly improve perceived-stress scores in pooled analysis. Boundary: Biomarker improvement and subjective clinical benefit diverged; cortisol reduction should not be presented as proof of stress relief. State: `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`.
- **2137 · early-life-protein · PMID 41338694** — Higher-protein interventions did not substantially change weight-for-length, fat-free mass, or fat mass in pooled analyses. Boundary: This is early-life feeding evidence, not evidence for protein powders or adult supplementation. State: `research_only_population_firewall`.
- **2138 · vitamin-c · PMID 41829943** — Hemodialysis patients commonly had low vitamin-C intake and blood levels; supplementation showed the clearest benefit for functional iron deficiency and restless-legs symptoms, while effects on inflammation and other comorbidities were inconsistent. Boundary: Dialysis-specific deficiency and losses make this a special population; current evidence does not justify routine vitamin C for all hemodialysis comorbidities. State: `research_only_population_firewall`.
- **2139 · omega-3 · PMID 40836005** — Omega-3 supplementation was associated with modest improvements across several cognitive domains, with low-to-moderate certainty and nonlinear dose-response patterns for some outcomes. Boundary: Nonlinear meta-regression cannot define an optimal dose; long-term confirmatory trials are needed. State: `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`.
- **2140 · protein-exercise · PMID 40805978** — Exercise plus nutritional supplementation ranked best for several strength/function outcomes, while nutritional supplementation alone did not significantly improve any assessed outcome. Boundary: Combined-intervention benefit cannot be assigned to protein supplementation alone. State: `research_only_combination_firewall`.
- **2141 · vitamin-d · PMID 42015226** — Women with GDM had lower vitamin-D status, and supplementation reliably raised 25(OH)D; evidence for downstream metabolic benefit was less clearly established than correction of deficiency. Boundary: Pregnancy and diabetes are special clinical contexts, and dose/timing regimens varied widely. State: `research_only_population_firewall`.
- **2142 · probiotic-prebiotic-synbiotic · PMID 41511913** — Microbiome-targeting supplements improved constipation-related outcomes; motor-symptom improvement was uncertain and evidence certainty was very low. Boundary: Interventions pooled different probiotic, prebiotic, and synbiotic products, so category-wide neurologic efficacy is unsupported. State: `research_only_category_firewall`.
- **2143 · steamed-ginger-extract · PMID 41599979** — A proprietary GD-standardized steamed ginger extract reduced body-fat percentage/mass, weight, BMI, waist and hip circumference, triglycerides, and total cholesterol versus placebo. Boundary: No product-related adverse events were reported, but findings are specific to one standardized proprietary extract and a small trial. State: `research_only_formulation_firewall`.
- **2144 · hypericum-perforatum · PMID 41253417** — Both intravaginal Hypericum gel and clotrimazole improved symptoms; Hypericum was not superior overall, with symptom-domain differences between groups. Boundary: This is intravaginal topical therapy and cannot support oral St John's wort efficacy; no adverse events were reported. State: `research_only_route_firewall`.
- **2145 · curcumin-curcuma · PMID 41372521** — Curcumin/Curcuma did not significantly improve DAS-28, ESR, or CRP, with very high heterogeneity across all outcomes. Boundary: Useful direct null/mixed evidence; inflammatory-marker claims should not be inferred from smaller individual trials when pooled evidence is null. State: `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`.
- **2146 · iodine · PMID 42720098** — Iodine supplementation slightly improved cognition, likely reduced hypothyroidism, and reduced infant mortality in one study; growth changed little and evidence for goiter was very uncertain. Boundary: Adverse events were not reported; applicability depends on iodine-deficiency risk and several studies used large single doses rather than routine daily supplementation. State: `research_only_population_firewall`.
- **2147 · omega-3 · PMID 42517224** — High-dose EPA/DHA above 1,500 mg/day increased new-onset atrial-fibrillation risk in people at high cardiovascular risk (OR 1.43; absolute risk difference about 0.8%); lower-dose regimens did not show a significant AF increase. Boundary: Important dose-and-risk-context safety signal; benefit-risk assessment should preserve both cardiovascular indication and arrhythmia risk rather than imply a universal cutoff. State: `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`.
- **2148 · calcium · PMID 42141946** — Higher total calcium intake was associated with modestly lower breast-cancer risk, but supplemental calcium alone was not significantly associated with lower risk. Boundary: Observational dietary associations do not establish calcium-supplement cancer prevention. State: `research_only_observational`.
- **2149 · astaxanthin · PMID 41673418** — Astaxanthin increased oocyte maturity and favorably shifted inflammatory RAGE-NFκB-related biomarkers, but the pilot was not powered to confirm the primary OHSS endpoint. Boundary: Exploratory assisted-reproduction population; surrogate/mechanistic changes do not establish prevention of OHSS. State: `research_only_population_firewall`.
- **2150 · melatonin · PMID 39988019** — Adjunctive melatonin was associated with lower mortality in pooled trials (OR 0.42), alongside anti-inflammatory signals. Boundary: Small total sample in a high-acuity clinical setting; this is not general supplement-use evidence and requires larger high-quality trials before practice-level inference. State: `research_only_population_firewall`.
