## Waves 1726–1750
1. **e-coli-nissle-recurrent-uti — PMID 42803671** — Pilot multicenter double-blind RCT in 50 women with recurrent uncomplicated UTI found fewer microbiological recurrences with E. coli Nissle 1917 numerically, but the primary comparison did not reach statistical significance; strain-specific recurrence-free trends remain exploratory.
2. **probiotics-neonatal-jaundice-meta — PMID 42808098** — Systematic review/meta-analysis of 19 RCTs found probiotics may reduce phototherapy use/duration and bilirubin in neonatal physiological jaundice, with strain-specific differences across C. butyricum, L. reuteri and S. boulardii; category pooling limits broad claims.
3. **l-reuteri-pediatric-functional-gi-meta — PMID 42811720** — Meta-analysis of five RCTs each in pediatric functional abdominal pain and constipation found L. reuteri DSM 17938 did not significantly improve responder rates, pain frequency/intensity or stool frequency versus placebo; a useful direct null for this strain.
4. **probiotics-child-oral-health-systematic-review — PMID 42819081** — Systematic review of pediatric probiotic interventions found many trials reported lower caries incidence/progression or salivary S. mutans, but strain, design and outcome heterogeneity prevented a single class-wide efficacy conclusion.
5. **vitamin-c-nac-lugol-topical-rct — PMID 42715145** — Large multicenter double-blind RCT found topical vitamin C solution and topical NAC both reduced short-term discomfort after Lugol chromoendoscopy versus saline and accelerated decolorization; route-specific mucosal evidence cannot be generalized to oral supplementation.
6. **nac-tuberculosis-adjunct-rct — PMID 42718567** — Open-label randomized single-center trial in 90 newly diagnosed pulmonary-TB patients found adjunctive oral or nebulized NAC improved several treatment-response, oxidative-stress and inflammatory measures versus standard therapy alone; open-label design and concomitant HRZE therapy require cautious attribution.
7. **nac-multiple-sclerosis-pet-mri — PMID 42732209** — Small randomized study in 36 patients with mild-to-moderate MS found combined IV/oral NAC altered resting functional connectivity and cerebral glucose metabolism, with correlations to self-reported cognition/attention; neuroimaging and symptom associations remain exploratory.
8. **nac-endometriosis-icsi-rct — PMID 42781143** — RCT in 60 women with endometriosis undergoing ICSI found six weeks of NAC reduced serum lead/nickel and several lipid measures but also reduced HDL, with no LDL change; fertility-treatment context and biomarker endpoints limit clinical inference.
9. **beetroot-hypoxia-exercise — PMID 42738952** — Randomized crossover study in 12 healthy men found acute nitrate-rich beetroot attenuated hypoxia-associated skeletal-muscle deoxygenation during submaximal exercise, while broader performance implications were not established.
10. **l-arginine-cognitive-impairment-pilot — PMID 42621366** — Small double-blind randomized pilot in older adults with cognitive impairment found more participants receiving oral L-arginine normalized ACE-III scores after 30 days, but only 35 completed and domain gains were partly within-group; confirmation is needed.
11. **choline-apoe4-prevention-pilot — PMID 42746101** — Six-month open-label phase-1 study in 13 asymptomatic APOE ε4 carriers found oral choline increased plasma/CSF choline and betaine with exploratory lipidomic and AD-biomarker shifts; study was not powered for clinical efficacy and lacked placebo control.
12. **esports-supplements-mental-fatigue — PMID 42715390** — Randomized crossover study in 20 high-level FPS esports athletes compared caffeine, nitrate, Ginkgo extract, catechins and placebo after induced mental fatigue; several supplements improved shooting accuracy, but no clear winner emerged and some abilities improved similarly with natural recovery.
13. **vitamin-c-dermatology-systematic-review — PMID 42761976** — Systematic review of 44 dermatology studies found vitamin C effectiveness depended strongly on derivative chemistry, vehicle, pH, stabilization and delivery route; heterogeneity and sparse standardized data make formulation-specific interpretation essential.
14. **postbiotic-b-breve-b3-sarcopenia — PMID 42797058** — 24-week randomized double-blind pilot in sarcopenia found heat-killed B. breve B-3 did not improve the primary knee-extension-strength outcome; exploratory BIA and biomarker signals were not corroborated by DXA or functional outcomes.
15. **microbiota-directed-acute-pancreatitis-meta — PMID 42798434** — Systematic review/meta-analysis found prebiotic/probiotic/synbiotic adjuncts were associated with shorter hospital stay and fewer infections in acute pancreatitis, while mortality lost significance in sensitivity analysis and organ-failure outcomes were not consistently improved.
16. **omega3-obesity-inflammation-ffar4 — PMID 41373925** — Eight-week double-blind RCT in 55 adults with obesity found marine EPA+DHA increased PBMC FFAR4 activation and reduced several inflammatory markers during calorie restriction; mechanistic and biomarker outcomes do not establish clinical-event benefit.
17. **omega3-cardiometabolic-markers-meta — PMID 41494879** — Meta-analysis of 24 randomized studies found omega-3 supplementation reduced triglycerides and modestly improved several metabolic markers, with subgroup-specific LDL/total-cholesterol effects; diverse populations and endpoints require outcome-specific interpretation.
18. **omega3-lppla2-meta — PMID 41545244** — Meta-analysis of 16 RCTs found omega-3 supplementation reduced circulating Lp-PLA2 mass but not Lp-PLA2 enzymatic activity; biomarker changes should not be converted into cardiovascular event-reduction claims.
19. **vitamin-d-omega3-anemia-vital — PMID 42630947** — Randomized VITAL ancillary study in 839 nonanemic adults found neither vitamin D nor omega-3 supplementation reduced two-year anemia incidence or meaningfully changed hemoglobin overall; subgroup interactions were exploratory.
20. **probiotics-ventilator-pneumonia-umbrella — PMID 41834846** — Umbrella review of 24 meta-analyses reported lower ventilator-associated pneumonia and nosocomial infection with probiotics, but methodological quality ranged from critically low to high and the pooled literature spans diverse formulations and ICU contexts.
21. **b-adolescentis-lactose-intolerance — PMID 41909904** — Small randomized placebo-controlled trial in 21 lactose maldigesters found B. adolescentis iVS-1 reduced overall daily symptoms and selected urgency/diarrhea outcomes during two weeks, with detectable strain persistence after treatment; replication is needed.
22. **selenium-yeast-diabetic-neuropathy — PMID 41504164** — Triple-blind RCT in 50 adults with diabetic peripheral neuropathy found selenium-enriched yeast did not significantly improve between-group neuropathy symptom scores or oxidative balance, though some within-group severity and quality-of-life measures improved; primary neuropathy benefit remains uncertain.
23. **beetroot-bfr-strength-fatigue — PMID 41048224** — Four-week randomized study in 21 male college athletes found blood-flow-restriction training improved strength/fatigue measures and adding beetroot juice produced larger late-set fatigue-resistance effects; combination design prevents beetroot-only attribution.
24. **beetroot-judo-performance — PMID 41112748** — Randomized double-blind crossover trial in 35 adolescent recreational judokas found acute nitrate-rich beetroot improved jump height, strength and judo-specific performance metrics versus placebo; adolescent recreational-athlete context limits generalization.
25. **carnitine-glycemic-overweight-women-meta — PMID 41177307** — Meta-analysis of eight studies/nine RCT arms found carnitine modestly reduced fasting glucose, insulin and HOMA-IR in women with overweight/obesity, while QUICKI was unchanged; subgroup dose/duration signals remain exploratory and should not drive dosing guidance.
