- **2080 · whey-weight-loss-fat-free-mass-review · PMID 41754212** — Systematic review of 14 RCTs found whey protein may help preserve fat-free mass during weight loss in adults with obesity, especially alongside resistance exercise or anabolic-enriched formulations, but several trials were neutral and overall certainty was limited by heterogeneity and small samples. _State:_ `research_only_combination_firewall`

- **2082 · creatine-kidney-health-meta · PMID 42507286** — Meta-analysis of 26 studies found creatine increased serum creatinine and lowered creatinine-based estimated GFR, but did not reduce Cr-EDTA-measured GFR or worsen urea, albuminuria, or proteinuria; changes likely reflect creatinine metabolism rather than kidney injury. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`

- **2084 · green-tea-postmenopausal-hormones-meta · PMID 42770106** — Meta-analysis of 8 RCTs in postmenopausal women found green tea reduced ghrelin but not overall leptin, adiponectin, or insulin; subgroup signals were highly heterogeneous with some publication-bias concerns and should not be converted into dose prescriptions. _State:_ `research_only_population_firewall`
- **2085 · perioperative-vitamin-c-pain-meta · PMID 42705006** — Meta-analysis of 27 RCTs found perioperative vitamin C reduced acute postoperative pain, opioid consumption, rescue analgesia, and early nausea/vomiting, but heterogeneity and publication bias limit certainty and evidence was insufficient for routine clinical recommendation. _State:_ `research_only_population_firewall`

- **2093 · caffeine-women-exercise-meta · PMID 42500255** — Meta-analysis found a small average ergogenic effect of acute caffeine in women, but no clear moderation by menstrual-cycle phase or hormonal-contraceptive status; certainty was low to very low and evidence does not support rigid phase-specific caffeine prescriptions. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
