## Waves
- **2076 · n-acetylcysteine · PMID 41663453** — NAC increased serum antioxidant markers and shifted granulosa-cell apoptosis-related gene expression toward lower pro-apoptotic and higher anti-apoptotic signaling. _State:_ `research_only_biomarker`
- **2077 · vitamin-d · PMID 41774579** — People with chronic rhinosinusitis had lower vitamin-D levels than controls, and supplementation modestly improved SNOT-22 symptoms before three months, but the benefit was not maintained at three months or longer. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2078 · iodine-status · PMID 42124075** — Suboptimal maternal iodine status was associated with modestly poorer child neurodevelopment, especially cognition, with exploratory curves suggesting best outcomes around mid-range iodine exposure. _State:_ `research_only_observational`
- **2079 · glaucoma-nutraceuticals · PMID 41637226** — The review identifies plausible retinal-ganglion-cell and metabolic targets for several nutraceuticals, but evidence strength varies widely and no unified supplement regimen is established. _State:_ `research_only_evidence_quality`
- **2080 · vitamin-c · PMID 42602406** — Microneedling with vitamin C produced short-term melasma outcomes statistically similar to microneedling with tranexamic acid in this limited evidence base. _State:_ `research_only_route_firewall`
- **2081 · nigella-sativa-oil · PMID 41880716** — Topical sesame oil and Nigella sativa oil reduced incidence and severity of infusion-related phlebitis versus no intervention, with sesame oil showing the lowest observed rate. _State:_ `research_only_route_firewall`
- **2082 · kava · PMID 38829029** — A heavy kava user developed hyperactive delirium and autonomic agitation after stopping and improved with phenobarbital-based treatment; prior controlled low-dose cessation studies generally reported no withdrawal, while severe heavy-use evidence was sparse. _State:_ `research_only_rare_safety_signal`

- **2088 · ginkgo-diterpene-lactone-meglumine · PMID 39880062** — Ginkgo diterpene lactone meglumine added to conventional treatment improved 90-day functional outcomes with moderate-certainty evidence. _State:_ `research_only_formulation_firewall`
