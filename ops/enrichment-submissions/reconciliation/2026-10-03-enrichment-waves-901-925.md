## Waves 901–925
1. **zinc — PMID 41617950** — 41-RCT/44-effect-size synthesis; most body-composition/adipokine endpoints were null with only small weight/waist changes.
2. **zinc — PMID 42594341** — Glycemic systematic review/meta-analysis; metabolic surrogate evidence remains population- and baseline-status specific.
3. **selenium — PMID 42355878** — Five-RCT/303-participant Graves orbitopathy synthesis; clinical-activity score improved while QoL/visual-function outcomes were null.
4. **citicoline-phosphatidylserine — PMID 42538853** — Review of 11 controlled studies/10 RCTs; pediatric ADHD and adult populations plus citicoline/phosphatidylserine identities must remain separated.
5. **coenzyme-q10 — PMID 41294251** — Five-RCT/474-participant mood synthesis; depressive-symptom signal with fatigue null and small heterogeneous clinical populations.
6. **coenzyme-q10 — PMID 40495903** — 45-RCT blood-pressure synthesis; modest SBP reduction with DBP and heart-rate nulls.
7. **vitamin-d — PMID 42521227** — 45-RCT cardiometabolic synthesis; modest LDL/SBP/glucose/HbA1c shifts with uncertain clinical importance and no event-level inference.
8. **vitamin-d — PMID 42100929** — Cognition systematic review/meta-analysis; preserve cognitive domain, baseline vitamin-D status, age and population modifiers.
9. **beetroot-nitrate — PMID 40679494** — 13-RCT endothelial-function synthesis; FMD improved by about 1.48 percentage points with I² 98.2%, making heterogeneity central.
10. **beetroot-nitrate — PMID 41619653** — Blood-pressure synthesis of nitrate/beetroot interventions; dietary nitrate, beetroot juice and isolated nitrate remain distinct exposures.
11. **astaxanthin — PMID 41710469** — Seven-RCT/188-participant exercise/oxidative-stress synthesis; AOPP signal while most oxidative/injury biomarkers were null.
12. **spirulina — PMID 41873104** — Eight-RCT inflammation synthesis; small CRP signal with TNF-alpha and IL-6 nulls.
13. **curcumin — PMID 41652864** — Seven trials/571 critically ill participants; ALT/bilirubin/SOFA/ICU-stay signals apply only to critical illness and cannot transfer to healthy users.
14. **olive-leaf-extract — PMID 42303499** — 30-RCT/1,726 synthesis; olive-leaf cardiometabolic/inflammatory signals with glycemic nulls, while olive pomace showed no consistent cardiometabolic benefit and an IL-8 signal.
15. **garlic — PMID 40580481** — 108-RCT/7,137-participant synthesis; multiple lipid/glycemic/BP/inflammatory marker signals with several BMI/weight/adiponectin nulls.
16. **aged-garlic-extract — PMID 40628369** — Nineteen-trial aged-garlic synthesis; modest SBP and LDL signals with preparation-specificity.
17. **aged-garlic-retraction — PMID 42527229** — Retraction notice governing earlier PMID 39437887; preserve as quarantine metadata so the retracted synthesis cannot re-enter aggregation.
18. **ashwagandha — PMID 41644067** — Twenty-two-RCT mental-health synthesis; stress/anxiety/depression findings remain heterogeneous across products, routes, doses and populations.
19. **ashwagandha — PMID 42641800** — Eleven-trial/667-participant glucose synthesis; fasting-glucose signal but HbA1c estimate crossed zero, preserving mixed metabolic evidence.
20. **ginger — PMID 42566667** — Twelve-RCT BP synthesis; SBP/DBP reductions reported with low certainty and substantial heterogeneity.
21. **garlic — PMID 42398591** — 27-RCT/1,619-participant glycemic synthesis; fasting-glucose/HbA1c signals with insulin/HOMA-IR nulls.
22. **curcumin — PMID 42254374** — Osteoarthritis umbrella review; review-level consistency/heterogeneity evidence cannot replace formulation-specific RCT interpretation.
23. **resveratrol — PMID 42471257** — Seven-RCT apolipoprotein synthesis; ApoB and ApoA-I outcomes were null, directly constraining broad lipid claims.
24. **nmn — PMID 41901064** — Ten-RCT/349-participant elevated-BP synthesis; NMN-specific blood-pressure evidence must stay distinct from NR, NAD+, niacin and other precursors.
25. **nmn — PMID 42514320** — Fifteen-trial safety review; short-term 250–2000 mg/day studies found no significant AE/SAE/withdrawal/ALT/AST signals, which does not establish long-term safety.
