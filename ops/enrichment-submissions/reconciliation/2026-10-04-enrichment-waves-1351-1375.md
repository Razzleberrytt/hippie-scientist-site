# Research enrichment Waves 1351–1375 — 2026-10-04

Fifteenth pass of the Waves 1001–1500 program, emphasizing vitamin D, omega-3, protein-source evidence, cognition, cardiometabolic endpoints, and biomarker-versus-clinical-outcome reconciliation.

## Waves 1351–1375
1. **vitamin-d-shift-workers-adiposity — PMID 42659412** — Four-month randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in 198 male shift workers found vitamin D raised serum 25(OH)D but did not reduce central adiposity; biomarker correction did not translate into an obesity benefit.
2. **vitamin-k2-d3-severe-coronary-calcification — PMID 42661461** — DANCODE multicenter double-blind randomized trial found vitamin K2 plus D3 slowed severe coronary-calcification progression over 24 months, but the imaging effect does not establish fewer cardiovascular events.
3. **targeted-vitamin-d-post-mi — PMID 42702500** — TARGET-D randomized trial found titrated vitamin D management after myocardial infarction did not significantly reduce major adverse cardiovascular events; one recurrent-MI secondary signal does not overturn the primary null.
4. **vitamin-d-biofortified-eggs-chicken — PMID 42716471** — Randomized winter feeding trial found 25(OH)D3-biofortified chicken and eggs prevented seasonal vitamin-D decline, but standard vitamin D3 supplementation increased serum 25(OH)D more.
5. **vitamin-d-prediabetes-dose-network — PMID 42721218** — Network meta-analysis of 21 prediabetes RCTs produced different dose rankings across glycemic outcomes; indirect comparisons and sparse low-dose evidence make apparent optimal-dose signals exploratory.
6. **vitamin-d3-dha-mci — PMID 42738950** — Twelve-month randomized four-arm trial in older adults with MCI found vitamin D3, DHA and their combination improved selected cognitive and pyroptosis measures, with the combination appearing stronger; combination superiority needs independent replication.
7. **vitamin-d3-omega3-adrd-biomarkers — PMID 42746141** — VITAL secondary analysis found neither vitamin D3 nor marine omega-3 significantly reduced selected Alzheimer-related plasma biomarkers over four years; subgroup signals were uncorrected and hypothesis-generating.
8. **vitamin-d-athletic-performance-inflammation — PMID 42760983** — Short randomized double-blind trial found vitamin D changed selected inflammatory responses but did not improve repeated Wingate anaerobic performance.
9. **vitamin-d-melanoma — PMID 42770385** — Meta-analysis of five randomized trials found vitamin D supplementation did not reduce melanoma incidence or recurrence; randomized evidence is currently neutral.
10. **vitamin-d-probiotic-cardiometabolic — PMID 42778435** — Meta-analysis of vitamin D plus probiotic/synbiotic co-supplementation found a modest fasting-insulin signal while HOMA-IR, glucose, lipids and anthropometrics were null overall; combination evidence remains limited.
11. **vitamin-d-benign-breast-disease-metabolic — PMID 42785554** — Randomized placebo-controlled trial in premenopausal women with benign breast disease found cholecalciferol improved selected insulin-sensitivity indices but not fasting glucose, lipids or anthropometrics between groups.
12. **omega3-omega9-pediatric-insulin-resistance — PMID 42654225** — Triple-blind pediatric trial found within-group metabolic improvements during omega-3/omega-9 interventions, but intervention assignment did not independently predict response and between-group differences were absent.
13. **curcumin-omega3-lung-chemoprevention — PMID 42649879** — Tiny randomized phase-II pilot found curcumin plus omega-3 was well tolerated but did not significantly change primary pulmonary-nodule imaging endpoints; no chemopreventive efficacy was established.
14. **omega3-omega6-autism-cytokines — PMID 42730828** — Double-blind randomized trial in young children with autism found omega-3/omega-6 supplementation had no overall effect on cytokines or autism-related behaviors; sex-specific findings were exploratory.
15. **omega3-muscle-mass-strength — PMID 42735611** — Systematic review and meta-analysis found no clear benefit of isolated omega-3 supplementation on fat-free mass or muscle strength in healthy adults, with very-low-certainty evidence.
16. **omega3-autism-meta-analysis — PMID 42738993** — Updated randomized-trial meta-analysis found small statistical improvements in irritability and communication but no significant effects across several other autism behavioral domains; clinical relevance remains uncertain.
17. **omega3-schizophrenia-adjunct — PMID 42793353** — Systematic review/meta-analysis found no significant pooled benefit of adjunctive omega-3 on PANSS total, clinical response or discontinuation outcomes in schizophrenia.
18. **omega3-youth-migraine — PMID 42796966** — Quasi-randomized pilot found omega-3 changed whole-blood EPA/DHA and fatty-acid ratios but did not improve patient-reported migraine pain, disability or distress.
19. **liposomal-omega3-pharmacokinetics — PMID 42821481** — Randomized pharmacokinetic comparison of liposomal and non-liposomal omega-3 formulations informs formulation/exposure differences, not clinical superiority.
20. **collagen-post-hip-arthroplasty-bone — PMID 41905472** — Randomized double-blind trial after total hip arthroplasty found hydrolyzed collagen did not alter postoperative bone-turnover biomarkers versus placebo; one pain signal was secondary.
21. **fava-bean-protein-heat-exercise — PMID 41917722** — Small randomized crossover study found fava-bean protein altered thermoregulatory and intestinal-damage markers during exercise in heat but did not improve graded exercise time or GI symptoms.
22. **whey-resistance-training-t2d — PMID 41931150** — Secondary analysis of a triple-blind RCT found resistance training improved strength across groups while whey protein reduced HOMA-IR without significant HbA1c, fasting-glucose, lipid or hypertrophy benefits.
23. **almonds-anxiety-depression-gut-brain — PMID 42463057** — Twelve-week randomized trial found almonds did not improve the primary anxiety/depression score or microbiome/SCFA outcomes versus control, though stool hardness and some functional-impairment analyses favored almonds.
24. **plant-vs-animal-protein-long-term — PMID 41994089** — Meta-analysis of 18 RCTs found no meaningful long-term differences between plant- and animal-protein supplementation in body composition, strength, physical performance or major cardiometabolic risk factors when protein intake was adequate.
25. **presleep-whey-casein-sleep — PMID 42413910** — Very small randomized crossover trial found pre-sleep whey improved several sleep measures and next-day soreness versus isocaloric control but not consistently versus casein; the result is preliminary.
