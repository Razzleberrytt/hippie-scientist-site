## Waves 851–875
1. **chlorella-vulgaris — PMID 35331862** — Ten-RCT/539-participant lipid meta-analysis; total and LDL cholesterol decreased while triglycerides and HDL were null, with weak supplement-characterization reporting across most trials.
2. **edible-algae — PMID 40726022** — 2025 29-RCT/1,583-participant blood-pressure meta-analysis across edible algae; small pooled BP reductions with high heterogeneity and species-specific differences.
3. **cocoa-flavanols — PMID 42679502** — 2026 updated RCT meta-analysis of cocoa flavanols for blood pressure and vascular function; BP and vascular indices remain surrogate endpoints with intervention heterogeneity.
4. **chlorella — PMID 42420253** — 2026 24-study GRADE dose-response meta-analysis; multiple metabolic/anthropometric biomarker signals but most outcome certainty was very low.
5. **prebiotics — PMID 39800313** — Eleven-study/546-participant prediabetes meta-analysis; body-fat percentage decreased while fasting glucose, insulin, HbA1c, HOMA-IR and lipid outcomes were null.
6. **artichoke — PMID 41270328** — 2025 cardiometabolic synthesis; BMI/waist, BP, selected lipids and insulin/HOMA-IR improved while weight, hip circumference, HDL, fasting glucose and HbA1c were null.
7. **spirulina — PMID 40655486** — 2025 23-study/1,035-participant review in overweight/obesity; weight/lipid/DBP signals with exercise-combination effects and substantial endpoint heterogeneity.
8. **spirulina — PMID 34578932** — Five-RCT/230-participant historical BP meta-analysis; SBP/DBP reductions with small samples and subgroup dependence, useful against newer broader evidence.
9. **astaxanthin — PMID 41596351** — 2025 human systematic review of oxidative, inflammatory and metabolic outcomes; broad positive narrative signals require trial-level separation rather than a universal efficacy claim.
10. **lycopene — PMID 37496241** — GRADE-assessed RCT meta-analysis of lycopene/tomato and cardiovascular risk factors; lipid, glycemic, BP, inflammatory and anthropometric outcomes remain endpoint-specific.
11. **astaxanthin — PMID 35091276** — Twelve-RCT/380-participant meta-analysis; oxidative/inflammatory biomarker effects were modest and condition-dependent, without direct clinical-outcome evidence.
12. **olive-leaf-extract — PMID 41848522** — 2026 11-RCT meta-analysis found no clinically meaningful continuous-intake glycemic or lipid benefit and sparse low-certainty inflammatory evidence—a strong counterweight to earlier positive OLE syntheses.
13. **cocoa-flavanols — PMID 41849087** — 2026 COSMOS cardiovascular-outcome reanalysis using win-ratio methods; outcome-level evidence remains separate from short-term biomarker meta-analyses.
14. **inulin-type-fructans — PMID 34401107** — 33-RCT meta-analysis; glucose, total cholesterol and triglyceride benefits appeared mainly in diabetes, with weight and insulin null overall and high unexplained heterogeneity.
15. **lycopene — PMID 41369612** — 2026 umbrella review of tomato-derived lycopene intervention reviews; cardiovascular-risk signals inherit underlying review quality, formulation and outcome heterogeneity.
16. **astaxanthin — PMID 42100673** — 2026 nine-RCT/403-participant prediabetes/T2D meta-analysis; glycemic/lipid signals with HOMA-IR, weight and BMI nulls.
17. **astaxanthin — PMID 35631193** — Seven-study/321-participant metabolic-syndrome-risk review; marginal/selected lipid and BP signals with need for more robust evidence.
18. **inulin-type-fructans — PMID 38309832** — 55-RCT/2,518-participant cardiovascular-risk meta-analysis; small LDL, triglyceride and weight reductions with little/no effect on most other outcomes and low/very-low certainty.
19. **astaxanthin — PMID 39269488** — 2025 PCOS systematic review/single-arm meta-analysis; oxidative and reproductive outcomes remain female-PCOS specific and limited by small trial evidence.
20. **astaxanthin — PMID 38243785** — 2024 RCT meta-analysis of fatigue, motor function and cognition; domain-specific findings remain distinct rather than generalized to global energy/cognitive enhancement.
21. **cocoa — PMID 38931273** — 2024 31-RCT/1,986-participant cardiometabolic meta-analysis; TC/LDL/glucose/BP signals with weight, BMI, waist, triglyceride, HDL and HbA1c nulls.
22. **cocoa — PMID 41858752** — 2026 metabolic-syndrome/related-disease meta-analysis; limited triglyceride-focused benefit with duration/disease modifiers and low certainty for broader metabolic claims.
23. **resistant-starch — PMID 41080169** — 2025 23-RCT systematic review of adults with metabolic-syndrome-related risks; anthropometric, glycemic, lipid, inflammatory and oxidative outcomes are resistant-starch-type and delivery-context dependent.
24. **cocoa — PMID 40886195** — 2025 11-RCT/506-participant T2D lipid meta-analysis; triglycerides decreased but other lipids did not, with severe heterogeneity and low-certainty/clinically small effects.
25. **oat-beta-glucan — PMID 35631184** — 13-RCT/927-participant hypercholesterolemia meta-analysis; total/LDL cholesterol reductions with triglyceride/HDL nulls and source/dose/duration modifiers.
