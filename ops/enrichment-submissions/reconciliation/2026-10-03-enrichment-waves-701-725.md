# Research enrichment Waves 701–725 — 2026-10-03

Ninth pass of the Waves 501–1000 program.

## Waves 701–725
1. **vitamin-d — PMID 39993397** — 2025 updated acute-respiratory-infection prevention meta-analysis of 40 studies/61,589 participants; overall protection did not reach statistical significance, providing a major counterweight to earlier positive syntheses.
2. **vitamin-d — PMID 41387808** — 2025 pediatric ARI meta-analysis of 17 RCTs/18,372 participants; overall infection incidence, hospitalization, recovery and mortality were null, with subgroup/safety signals kept exploratory.
3. **vitamin-d — PMID 39143549** — 2024 43-RCT/49,320-participant ARI-prevention meta-analysis; overall pooled effect was null and subgroup dose/frequency findings must not become dosing recommendations.
4. **vitamin-d — PMID 40005471** — 2025 allergic-rhinitis meta-analysis of five RCTs; overall symptom benefit was nonsignificant with extreme heterogeneity, while selected no-corticosteroid subgroup findings remain exploratory.
5. **probiotics — PMID 42197372** — 2026 meta-analysis spanning upper-respiratory infection, allergic rhinitis and chronic rhinosinusitis; disease, strain and product identity must remain separated.
6. **probiotics — PMID 42602161** — 2026 pediatric respiratory-infection network meta-analysis of eight RCTs/1,158 participants; strain-specific signals were low-certainty and network rankings are not head-to-head proof.
7. **zinc — PMID 28515951** — Seven-trial/575-participant common-cold lozenge meta-analysis; roughly one-third shorter duration in selected high-dose lozenge trials, with formulation and dose context essential.
8. **zinc — PMID 27378206** — Individual-patient-data meta-analysis of three zinc-acetate trials/199 participants; shorter cold duration with no clear modification by allergy/status variables, but restricted to acetate lozenges.
9. **zinc — PMID 22566526** — 17-trial/2,121-participant common-cold meta-analysis; shorter duration in adults but not children, with more adverse events and substantial heterogeneity.
10. **zinc — PMID 10801968** — Older systematic review found evidence insufficient/inconclusive for zinc in the common cold; retained as historical counterweight.
11. **probiotics — PMID 33293995** — Six-RCT/1,551-adult URTI meta-analysis; low-quality evidence suggested selected incidence/duration benefits with very high heterogeneity for episode-rate outcomes.
12. **zinc — PMID 21769305** — Systematic review of zinc dose/formulation in common-cold trials; heterogeneity in salts, lozenge composition and dosing prevents a generic zinc equivalence claim.
13. **zinc — PMID 28480298** — Individual-patient-data analysis of three zinc-acetate trials found faster recovery rates; formulation-specific and small-trial evidence.
14. **zinc — PMID 31980506** — Randomized placebo-controlled zinc-acetate lozenge trial in 87 common-cold cases found no meaningful recovery benefit, providing a direct counterweight to prior positive lozenge meta-analyses.
15. **zinc — PMID 9361579** — Historical zinc-lozenge meta-analysis with null/inconclusive findings; useful for tracing evidence evolution and formulation heterogeneity.
16. **vitamin-c — PMID 38082300** — Meta-analysis of vitamin C for severe common-cold symptoms; treatment-context and symptom-severity findings remain distinct from prevention claims.
17. **vitamin-c — PMID 34967304** — Respiratory-infection meta-analysis; incidence/severity were not consistently reduced while illness duration was shorter in selected analyses.
18. **vitamin-c-antiviral — PMID 33102597** — Meta-analysis of vitamin C used with antiviral therapy for common cold; combination-treatment evidence cannot be attributed to vitamin C alone.
19. **probiotics — PMID 21901706** — Older randomized-trial review of probiotics for acute URTI prevention; incidence signals with less consistent duration evidence and substantial intervention heterogeneity.
20. **pelargonium-sidoides — PMID 18222667** — Six-RCT acute-bronchitis evidence for Pelargonium sidoides preparations; acute indication and standardized-product identity retained.
21. **vitamin-d — PMID 37606475** — Systematic review of vitamin-D supplementation for respiratory infections in children under six; findings were inconsistent and age-specific.
22. **vitamin-d — PMID 33798465** — 2021 large ARI meta-analysis; modest protective signal in some analyses with regimen/population modifiers, retained to compare with later null updates.
23. **vitamin-d — PMID 23840373** — Older respiratory-infection meta-analysis reported a protective signal but also heterogeneity/publication-bias concerns; historical context only.
24. **vitamin-d — PMID 23326099** — Early small-trial respiratory-infection meta-analysis suggested benefit; retained as historical evidence showing how conclusions changed as trial volume increased.
25. **zinc — PMID 23775705** — Older Cochrane-style common-cold synthesis; possible duration benefit alongside adverse events and major formulation/dose heterogeneity.
