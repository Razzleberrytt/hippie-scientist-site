# Research enrichment Waves 751–775 — 2026-10-03

Eleventh pass of the Waves 501–1000 program.

## Waves 751–775
1. **probiotics — PMID 41827145** — 2026 IBS umbrella review of 27 systematic reviews; modest symptom signals but predominantly low/very-low certainty, high review overlap, and generally weak methodology.
2. **probiotics — PMID 40551240** — 2025 umbrella meta-analysis across gastrointestinal disorders; diarrhea/nausea/epigastric-pain/bloating signals remain disorder-, strain-, duration-, and formulation-specific.
3. **constipation-dietary-management — PMID 40888045** — 2025 evidence review of chronic-constipation diet/supplement strategies; psyllium, specific probiotics, magnesium oxide and selected foods show differing evidence strength, not category equivalence.
4. **constipation-supplements — PMID 37243443** — Systematic review/meta-analysis of food/vitamin/mineral supplements for chronic constipation; magnesium oxide improved several outcomes while senna/kiwifruit evidence was limited by few trials.
5. **probiotics-diet-ibs — PMID 39862384** — 2025 network meta-analysis comparing probiotics, dietary management and combinations in IBS; indirect rankings and heterogeneous interventions remain research-only.
6. **herbal-functional-dyspepsia — PMID 34851546** — 49-RCT/6,987-participant meta-analysis of herbal therapies for functional dyspepsia; broad category evidence requires product-level separation.
7. **non-chinese-herbal-dyspepsia — PMID 37323050** — Cochrane review of non-Chinese herbal medicines for functional dyspepsia; individual botanicals showed mixed benefits/nulls and adverse-event differences, preventing a generic herbal claim.
8. **probiotics — PMID 38446227** — 13-RCT/930-participant ulcerative-colitis meta-analysis; active-disease remission signal did not reach significance, maintenance/response/mucosal healing were null, while relapse prevention in remission showed benefit.
9. **gerd-diet-nutraceuticals — PMID 36513474** — Systematic review/meta-analysis of dietary, nutraceutical and probiotic GERD interventions; evidence was sparse and highly heterogeneous, with only selected interventions showing signals.
10. **curcumin — PMID 39612780** — Updated placebo-controlled RCT meta-analysis of curcumin adjunctive therapy in IBD; UC response/remission signals with heterogeneity, but Crohn disease outcomes were not superior to placebo.
11. **probiotics — PMID 35745212** — Ten-RCT/757-participant IBS-C meta-analysis; stool consistency and fecal bacterial counts improved, while abdominal pain, bloating, quality of life and adverse events were null.
12. **probiotics — PMID 37541528** — Large 82-RCT/10,332-participant IBS meta-analysis; effects differed materially by strain and outcome, with certainty ranging from moderate to very low.
13. **probiotics — PMID 38479936** — 2024 20-study/3,011-participant IBS meta-analysis; global symptom and quality-of-life signals remain limited by heterogeneous probiotic formulations.
14. **pro-pre-synbiotics — PMID 37995508** — 2024 network meta-analysis of adjunctive probiotic/prebiotic/synbiotic formulations in mild-moderate UC; formulation rankings are indirect and disease-specific.
15. **probiotics — PMID 37686889** — 81-RCT/9,253-participant IBS network meta-analysis; apparent strain/mixture rankings are outcome-specific and not direct head-to-head proof.
16. **siho-sogan-san — PMID 37127143** — 12-study/867-participant functional-dyspepsia meta-analysis; traditional multi-herb formula evidence remains formula- and comparator-specific.
17. **probiotics — PMID 39106167** — 2024 IBD overview plus updated RCT meta-analysis; benefit appeared more consistent in UC/pouchitis than Crohn disease, with low overall certainty.
18. **uc-dietary-supplements-network — PMID 42100281** — 2026 24-RCT/1,491-participant UC network meta-analysis across 14 dietary supplements; efficacy rankings remain indirect, outcome-specific and limited by trial number/quality.
19. **curcumin — PMID 40196017** — 2025 13-placebo-controlled-RCT IBD meta-analysis; adjunctive curcumin improved selected UC outcomes but not Crohn clinical/endoscopic remission.
20. **probiotics — PMID 36831029** — 33-study/2,713-participant IBD systematic review; probiotic benefit was more consistent for UC than Crohn disease, with formulation-specific differences.
21. **microbiome-targeted-ibd — PMID 39482823** — 2024 network meta-analysis of prebiotics/probiotics/synbiotics/FMT as IBD adjuncts; intervention-class and disease-type distinctions remain explicit.
22. **probiotics — PMID 41029747** — 2025 umbrella meta-analysis of IBD relapse/recurrence/remission; relapse signal versus placebo but not mesalazine, and no consistent remission benefit.
23. **plant-derived-ibs — PMID 41599795** — 2026 review positioning plant-derived IBS treatments against guideline evidence; peppermint oil has the most consistent botanical support while many other extracts are mixed/null.
24. **probiotics-gi-qol — PMID 39371511** — 2024 systematic review of probiotic effects on quality of life across GI diseases; disease, strain, and QoL instrument heterogeneity prevent a general probiotic QoL claim.
25. **functional-dyspepsia-nutraceuticals — PMID 41936486** — 2026 narrative evidence synthesis of diet/nutraceutical approaches in functional dyspepsia; peppermint-caraway, ginger, STW-5, curcumin and selected probiotics remain product/phenotype specific.
