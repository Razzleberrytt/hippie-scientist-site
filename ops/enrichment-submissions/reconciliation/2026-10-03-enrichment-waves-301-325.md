# Research enrichment Waves 301–325 — 2026-10-03

First 25-finding pass of the second 200-finding continuation program (Waves 301–500).

## Operating rule

Research is accumulated in eight 25-finding passes on one branch. During authoring, use cheap duplicate/entity/source screening only. Do not run the expensive full repository suite after each pass. After Wave 500, consolidate the full 200-finding corpus, revalidate source identity, materialize only reader-ready canonical evidence, run admission/runtime/build/site-health/Atomic/CI on the exact head, and merge once.

Negative, null, subgroup-limited, formulation-specific, age-specific, diagnosis-specific, combination-only and safety-relevant findings remain first-class evidence.

No entity creation, governance override, publication/indexing promotion, recommendation promotion, monetization mutation or consumer-dose inference is authorized by this pass.

## Pass 1 — Waves 301–325

1. **saffron — PMID 15852492** — Six-week randomized placebo-controlled Crocus sativus stigma-extract trial in mild-to-moderate depression; preserve extract identity, short duration, and diagnosis boundary.
2. **passionflower — PMID 11679026** — Small double-blind GAD trial versus oxazepam; comparable symptom improvement with slower onset in the trial, but small sample and comparator design prevent broad equivalence claims.
3. **rhodiola — PMID 19016404** — Standardized SHR-5 randomized trial in stress-related fatigue; preserve extract identity and fatigue-context boundary.
4. **bacopa-monnieri — PMID 41091332** — 2025 12-week randomized trial in 101 healthy adults testing Bacumen for cognition, stress and fatigue; proprietary extract and healthy-population limits retained.
5. **panax-ginseng — PMID 32055589** — Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in 90 Korean volunteers with mild cognitive impairment; MCI-specific cognition evidence, not healthy nootropic evidence.
6. **l-theanine — PMID 31623400** — Four-week randomized crossover trial in healthy adults; selected stress-related and cognitive outcomes, with isolated-theanine and chronic-exposure boundaries.
7. **magnesium-bisglycinate — PMID 40918053** — 2025 randomized placebo-controlled trial in 155 adults with self-reported poor sleep; magnesium-bisglycinate/formulation-specific sleep evidence.
8. **melatonin — PMID 33157425** — Four-week randomized trial of fast-release melatonin in middle-aged adults with primary insomnia; formulation, age, duration and insomnia-diagnosis boundaries retained.
9. **lavender — PMID 24456909** — 10-week randomized trial in 539 adults with GAD comparing Silexan doses, placebo and paroxetine; Silexan-specific oral lavender evidence.
10. **chamomile — PMID 19593179** — Randomized placebo-controlled Matricaria recutita extract trial in mild-to-moderate GAD; preliminary diagnosis- and extract-specific evidence.
11. **curcumin — PMID 25046624** — Eight-week randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in major depressive disorder; curcumin regimen and psychiatric-population boundaries retained.
12. **omega-3 — PMID 20584525** — Large randomized trial in major depressive episodes; overall primary analysis only trended toward benefit while a prespecified non-anxious subgroup improved, so no uniform antidepressant claim.
13. **creatine — PMID 22864465** — Eight-week randomized trial of creatine augmentation of escitalopram in women with MDD; adjunctive, sex-specific psychiatric evidence.
14. **tart-cherry — PMID 20438325** — Small randomized crossover pilot in older adults with chronic insomnia; some subjective benefits but several objective sleep outcomes did not differ from placebo.
15. **cocoa-flavanols — PMID 19942640** — Randomized double-blind crossover study in healthy adults during sustained mental effort; acute mood/cognition effects with healthy-population and short-term boundaries.
16. **green-tea-l-theanine-combination — PMID 21303262** — 16-week randomized trial in mild cognitive impairment; combination-only evidence with subgroup-limited memory/attention signals and no attribution to either ingredient alone.
17. **cranberry — PMID 22777630** — 2012 randomized-trial review; lower UTI risk in women with recurrent UTIs with substantial heterogeneity; prevention not active-infection treatment.
18. **beetroot-nitrate — PMID 23596162** — 16-trial review of inorganic nitrate/beetroot juice; systolic-BP signal, diastolic null, short heterogeneous interventions and dose-response meta-regression kept together.
19. **fenugreek — PMID 24438170** — Earlier glycemic meta-analysis; glucose reductions concentrated in medium/high-dose diabetes trials with substantial heterogeneity; dose and disease status remain explicit.
20. **garlic — PMID 25557383** — 17-trial BP meta-analysis; modest SBP/DBP reductions overall but systolic benefit concentrated in hypertensive rather than normotensive participants.
21. **cranberry — PMID 28288837** — Clinical-trial meta-analysis finding reduced UTI incidence versus controls; prevention-only evidence with product heterogeneity.
22. **elderberry — PMID 30670267** — Small meta-analysis of 180 participants reporting reduced upper-respiratory symptom severity; limited evidence base and no specific influenza-treatment claim.
23. **echinacea — PMID 31126553** — 2019 review showing possible lower URI risk but no convincing shortening of illness duration; varied preparations and selective-reporting concerns retained.
24. **cinnamon — PMID 37316893** — Umbrella meta-analysis documenting conflicting glycemic conclusions across prior cinnamon syntheses; explicit counterweight to a single efficacy label.
25. **cinnamon — PMID 37818728** — 24-RCT T2D dose-response meta-analysis; fasting glucose/HbA1c signals with insulin null and substantial preparation/trial heterogeneity.
