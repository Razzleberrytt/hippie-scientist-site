# Research enrichment Waves 651–675 — 2026-10-03

Seventh pass of the Waves 501–1000 program.

## Waves 651–675
1. **chromium — PMID 39541030** — 2024 systematic review of chromium in T2D; possible glycemic/lipid signals coexist with wide formulation/dose/duration heterogeneity and uncertain long-term risk.
2. **chromium — PMID 40245649** — 2025 overweight/obesity meta-analysis; selected insulin-resistance/metabolic effects with several near-null estimates and mixed anthropometric/BP/liver findings.
3. **potassium — PMID 40612568** — 2025 dose-response meta-analysis of 10 RCTs; BP reduction was much larger in hypertensive than normotensive participants, emphasizing baseline-BP dependence.
4. **vitamin-k — PMID 38282652** — Systematic review/meta-analysis of vitamin-K supplementation and cardiovascular risk factors; biomarker/risk-factor evidence is not clinical-event prevention evidence.
5. **chromium — PMID 39671146** — Updated randomized-trial meta-analysis of inflammatory mediators; inflammatory biomarker evidence remains surrogate and population dependent.
6. **selenium — PMID 41738711** — 2026 randomized-trial meta-analysis in metabolic diseases; cardiometabolic, inflammatory and oxidative-stress findings remain phenotype- and endpoint-specific.
7. **selenium — PMID 40243093** — 2025 dose-response meta-analysis of 27 experimental human studies on blood lipids; possible nonlinear lipid effects and overexposure concerns remain first-class.
8. **zinc — PMID 37399684** — 75-study GRADE meta-analysis; several glycemic/lipid/inflammatory/oxidative markers improved while LDL, HDL, insulin, BP and liver enzymes were null.
9. **b-vitamins — PMID 41615824** — 2026 13-study/14,539-participant CHD meta-analysis of folic acid+B6+B12; homocysteine lowering and vascular outcomes remain distinct from hard-event efficacy.
10. **vitamin-b12 — PMID 37495210** — 21-RCT/1,625-participant meta-analysis; isolated B12 lowered homocysteine, a surrogate biomarker, without implying cardiovascular-event reduction.
11. **folic-acid — PMID 42231372** — 2026 36-RCT hyperhomocysteinemia meta-analysis; tHcy and modest BP/lipid surrogate changes with high heterogeneity/bias and no adjudicated hard cardiovascular endpoints.
12. **selenium — PMID 35963054** — Randomized-trial meta-analysis found little overall lipid benefit, increased SBP, and selected LDL/TC subgroup effects—important cardiovascular-safety counterweight.
13. **selenium — PMID 36432623** — Ten-RCT/526-participant cardiometabolic-disease meta-analysis; insulin/HOMA-IR/HDL signals with FPG, TC, TG, LDL and VLDL nulls and high heterogeneity.
14. **selenium — PMID 37574154** — 20-RCT GRADE dose-response review; fasting-insulin/QUICKI signals with FBS, HbA1c and HOMA-IR nulls.
15. **chromium — PMID 33783683** — Ten-RCT/509-participant T2D meta-analysis of glucose/lipid outcomes; disease-specific evidence with formulation and trial-quality limitations.
16. **chromium — PMID 32730903** — 28-study T2D meta-analysis; FPG/insulin/HbA1c/HOMA-IR pooled reductions accompanied by extreme heterogeneity for several outcomes.
17. **chromium — PMID 24635480** — Older diabetes meta-analysis including mono- and combination supplementation; glycemic/lipid signals with inadequate long-term safety evidence and combination uncertainty.
18. **folic-acid — PMID 34478339** — 22-study/41,633-participant BP meta-analysis; small SBP/DBP reductions with dose/duration/subgroup variability—large sample but surrogate outcome.
19. **potassium — PMID 28024910** — Hypertension-specific meta-analysis; BP lowering was stronger with lower baseline potassium, higher sodium and no antihypertensive therapy.
20. **potassium — PMID 28419159** — 23-RCT/1,213-participant essential-hypertension meta-analysis; modest BP reduction with moderate heterogeneity and adjunctive-context framing.
21. **t2d-nutrient-supplements — PMID 35684094** — Umbrella/hierarchical synthesis of nutrient supplements for T2D; most HbA1c evidence was low or very-low certainty and intervention-specific.
22. **selenium-status — PMID 39882034** — Observational dose-response meta-analysis found a U-shaped association between blood selenium and metabolic syndrome; association evidence must not be treated as supplementation efficacy.
23. **calcium-intake — PMID 39379073** — Observational GRADE dose-response meta-analysis linked higher dietary calcium with lower MetS odds; dietary association is not supplement-trial evidence.
24. **zinc — PMID 31494808** — Earlier cardiometabolic RCT meta-analysis; glycemic/lipid signals with BP/anthropometric nulls, useful historical comparison with newer zinc syntheses.
25. **selenium — PMID 28992632** — Older metabolic-disease RCT meta-analysis of glucose/lipid profiles; retained as historical evidence to compare with later contradictory selenium syntheses.
