# Research enrichment Waves 1776–1800 — 2026-10-04

Twelfth pass of the Waves 1501–2000 program, emphasizing fermented foods, prebiotics and fiber while preserving food-matrix, strain, combination, observational and protocol boundaries.

## Waves 1776–1800
1. **psyllium-cellulose-inulin-gas-rct — PMID 41636227** — Randomized healthy-human trial examined whether gel-forming psyllium/modified cellulose alters colonic gas production during inulin co-administration; formulation-mechanism evidence should remain separate from broad clinical fiber claims.
2. **inulin-type1-diabetes-pilot — PMID 41636295** — Pilot RCT in 49 adults with type 1 diabetes found inulin modestly reduced body weight and insulin requirement but did not improve HbA1c/CGM metrics or increase Akkermansia; small metabolic signal with clear null outcomes.
3. **kefir-adhd-rct — PMID 41286799** — Six-week double-blind RCT in children with ADHD found kefir did not improve primary ADHD symptoms or overall microbiome diversity; actigraphy showed less wake time while self-reported sleep problems worsened, yielding mixed sleep evidence.
4. **kefir-healthy-young-adults-rct — PMID 41314291** — RCT in 65 healthy young adults found kefir improved gastrointestinal symptoms and selected biochemical markers, but sleep, mental-health, immunity and inflammation changes were not significantly different; small healthy-population study.
5. **kefir-postbiotic-muscle-rct — PMID 41470885** — Double-blind RCT found a kefir-derived postbiotic preparation modestly improved dominant-hand grip strength and selected biomarkers/microbiota without improving lean mass or non-dominant grip strength; product-specific pilot evidence.
6. **kefir-probiotic-autism-null-between-group — PMID 41547752** — Double-blind RCT in 182 children with ASD found within-group adaptive/behavioral improvements with kefir-derived probiotics but no statistically significant between-group primary-outcome differences; biomarker/microbiome changes do not rescue the null comparative result.
7. **l-paragasseri-yogurt-stress-sleep — PMID 41679635** — Double-blind RCT during academic stress found yogurt containing L. paragasseri OLL2716 improved selected subjective/objective sleep measures and stress biomarkers versus placebo yogurt; strain- and food-matrix-specific evidence.
8. **bb12-yogurt-aad-null — PMID 41699149** — Randomized double-blind pediatric trial found B. animalis BB-12 yogurt did not prevent antibiotic-associated diarrhea when the prescribed antibiotics caused little microbiome disruption; context-dependent direct null.
9. **milk-kefir-exercise-recovery — PMID 41800708** — Crossover study compared milk and kefir as post-exercise recovery beverages in young female athletes; food-matrix comparative evidence should not be generalized to isolated probiotic or kefir supplements without endpoint-specific extraction.
10. **probiotic-yogurt-biomarkers-meta — PMID 41802686** — Meta-analysis found probiotic yogurt modestly increased fecal acetate and IL-10 versus conventional yogurt but did not clearly improve other SCFAs, CRP or oxidative-stress markers; certainty was very low and clinical relevance uncertain.
11. **protein-yogurt-whey-older-adults — PMID 42151371** — Randomized strength-training study found protein yogurt and whey produced comparable muscle gains but different microbiome shifts in older adults; food-matrix microbiome differences did not establish superior functional efficacy.
12. **yogurt-bb536-multimodal-aging — PMID 42377118** — Exploratory RCT tested a multimodal exercise/diet program including daily B. longum BB536 yogurt against aging-biomarker outcomes; combined intervention prevents attribution of epigenetic-aging changes to yogurt or probiotic alone.
13. **inulin-rheumatoid-arthritis-rct — PMID 40841439** — Triple-blind RCT in 60 people with rheumatoid arthritis found inulin improved CRP and several disease-activity/quality-of-life measures versus maltodextrin, while pain intensity and ESR were not significantly different; disease-specific small trial.
14. **inulin-ibs-c-rct — PMID 41034384** — Small placebo-controlled RCT in constipation-predominant IBS found inulin/oligofructose improved symptom severity and quality-of-life measures; sample size was only 34 and replication is needed.
15. **inulin-obesity-brain-metabolites — PMID 41057493** — Randomized pediatric obesity study examined inulin-related gut-microbiota metabolites linked to brain function; mechanistic metabolite shifts should not be equated with demonstrated cognitive benefit.
16. **inulin-functional-constipation-rct — PMID 41233756** — Double-blind crossover trial in 39 adults found chicory inulin improved stool frequency, abdominal symptoms and quality of life with bifidogenic/butyrate-producer shifts, but carry-over effects complicated interpretation.
17. **synbiotic-visceral-fat-rct — PMID 41512635** — Double-blind RCT in 112 adults found a multi-strain/inulin/FOS/chrysanthemum synbiotic modestly reduced visceral-fat area, especially in exploratory subgroups, without significant BMI, waist or blood-biochemistry changes; multi-component evidence.
18. **fiber-bmd-observational-meta — PMID 41739242** — Meta-analysis of observational studies associated higher dietary fiber intake with slightly higher bone mineral density; observational design cannot establish supplementation benefit or fracture prevention.
19. **fiber-endometriosis-review — PMID 41754207** — Up-to-date review examines mechanistic and clinical links between dietary fiber and endometriosis; preserve as evidence mapping rather than direct therapeutic efficacy.
20. **green-tea-inulin-visceral-obesity — PMID 41830022** — Double-blind RCT of green-tea catechin plus inulin improved insulin resistance but not visceral fat and produced exploratory microbiome signals; combination design prevents inulin-only attribution.
21. **oat-bran-post-bariatric-rct — PMID 41832846** — RCT in 63 post-bariatric patients found oat-bran fiber altered gut taxa, gut hormones and selected glycemic/lipid markers, while between-group excess-weight-loss difference was not significant; surgery-specific adjunct evidence.
22. **inulin-psoriasis-protocol — PMID 41840402** — INGUTSKIN protocol describes an eight-week double-blind placebo-controlled trial of chicory inulin-type fructans for mild psoriasis and gut-skin-axis biomarkers; no efficacy result is available yet.
23. **psyllium-schizophrenia-feasibility — PMID 41956574** — Pilot feasibility study evaluated nurse-led psyllium supplementation for metabolic health in hospitalized people with schizophrenia; feasibility/context evidence should not be treated as definitive metabolic efficacy.
24. **fiber-gestational-diabetes-rct — PMID 41982769** — Small RCT in high-risk pregnancy found soluble fiber improved glycemic excursions and short-term gestational weight gain but did not significantly reduce GDM incidence; zero preterm births versus 12% control is intriguing but requires replication.
25. **inulin-fos-anxiety-students-rct — PMID 41989194** — Randomized trial examined inulin/FOS supplementation and GAD-7 anxiety scores in college students; preserve as direct gut-brain intervention evidence with outcome-specific interpretation rather than broad anxiolytic claims.
