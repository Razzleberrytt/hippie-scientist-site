# Research enrichment Waves 1151–1175 — 2026-10-04

Seventh pass of the Waves 1001–1500 program, emphasizing sleep, stress, mood, cognition, and safety while preserving disease, formulation, combination, and evidence-quality boundaries.

## Waves 1151–1175
1. **omega-3-inositol-youth — PMID 42462928** — Open-label randomized comparison in emotionally dysregulated youth found omega-3 plus inositol and N-acetylcysteine each produced modest mania/depression improvements without a clear overall between-group winner; autism-trait status appeared to modify some NAC responses, so the combination cannot be attributed to omega-3 alone.
2. **melatonin — PMID 42533530** — Small double-blind placebo-controlled trial in 40 patients receiving adjuvant breast-cancer chemotherapy found melatonin improved selected sleep-quality measures across treatment, while anxiety/depression signals were phase-dependent and the disease-specific sample limits generalization.
3. **creatine-cognition-aging — PMID 42578920** — Twelve-week randomized creatine trial in middle-aged and older adults reported gains in lean mass, strength and selected cognitive/memory measures with and without exercise/diet intervention; cognitive and biomarker findings were explicitly exploratory and the exercise-versus-nonexercise allocation was not randomized.
4. **melatonin — PMID 42714195** — Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in 70 head-and-neck cancer patients with sleep disturbance found 14 days of oral melatonin improved PSQI sleep scores and fatigue versus placebo; evidence is short-term and disease-context specific.
5. **guanidinoacetic-acid-creatine — PMID 42730704** — Six-week exploratory trial in 58 healthy adults found guanidinoacetic acid, alone or with creatine, produced selected reaction-time and stress signals while many cognitive domains were unchanged and some sleep effects were potentially undesirable; multi-arm design prevents simple creatine attribution.
6. **matcha-green-tea-stress — PMID 42775228** — Systematic evidence map of matcha and green-tea bioactives found lower caffeine-plus-EGCG to theanine-plus-arginine preparations were more consistently associated with cortisol-related stress signals, but evidence mixed human, animal and in-vitro studies and GRADE certainty was low to very low.
7. **panax-ginseng-fatigue — PMID 41838185** — Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in 65 gastrointestinal-cancer survivors found four weeks of standardized Panax ginseng reduced cancer-related fatigue and improved several daily-function domains, with no treatment-related adverse events reported; findings remain short-term and population-specific.
8. **bacopa-monnieri — PMID 42102930** — Small exploratory MRS study in older adults assigned to Bacopa formulations or ghee reported preliminary neurometabolite and cognitive changes after three months; intervention groups were tiny and the study was not a definitive efficacy trial.
9. **silexan-lavender — PMID 42550483** — Pre-planned item-level analysis from a 498-participant randomized trial found the proprietary lavender-oil preparation Silexan improved several MADRS symptom items versus placebo with a profile broadly similar to sertraline; this is formulation-specific and secondary/exploratory evidence.
10. **saffron-low-mood — PMID 42465135** — Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in 56 adults with low mood found a standardized saffron formulation improved mood, affect, DASS-21, sleep quality and cortisol over 56 days; small sample size and proprietary formulation require cautious generalization.
11. **saffron-sleep-sarcoidosis — PMID 42483079** — Single-blind randomized trial in pulmonary sarcoidosis found 60 days of saffron plus sleep-hygiene counseling improved PSQI, daytime sleepiness, fatigue and quality-of-life measures versus counseling alone; disease context and lack of double blinding limit generalization.
12. **ashwagandha-long-term-safety — PMID 42694770** — Large multicenter randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial after COVISHIELD vaccination found 24 weeks of standardized ashwagandha had safety and tolerability comparable to placebo, while exploratory immunogenicity and breakthrough-infection outcomes showed no clear intervention benefit.
13. **probiotic-prebiotic-synbiotic-cognition — PMID 42806137** — Systematic review of 14 older-adult studies found probiotic, prebiotic and synbiotic interventions sometimes improved cognition, with a small pooled global-cognition signal in a subgroup; intervention heterogeneity and limited high-quality evidence prevent class-wide efficacy claims.
14. **multi-strain-probiotic — PMID 42811022** — Open-label 12-week pilot in 30 adults with depressive and anxiety symptoms reported large within-person improvements during multi-strain probiotic use, but the absence of a placebo or control group prevents causal attribution.
15. **theanine-gaba-magnesium-same-sleep — PMID 42742055** — Three-week randomized double-blind placebo-controlled menopause trial found a multi-ingredient supplement containing L-theanine, GABA, magnesium, SAM-e and propyl gallate improved sleep disturbance, sleep-related impairment, total sleep time and sleep efficiency; ingredient-specific attribution is not possible.
16. **chamomile-lavender-vetiver-aromatherapy — PMID 42630137** — Randomized placebo-controlled trial in intensive-care nurses found bedtime aromatherapy massage with chamomile-lavender or vetiver improved fatigue and PSQI sleep scores more than jojoba placebo over four weeks; results apply to an olfactory/topical intervention rather than oral supplementation.
17. **lavender-bergamot-herbal-sachet — PMID 42644519** — Open-label randomized hemodialysis trial found a combined lavender/bergamot essential-oil plus herbal-sachet intervention improved self-reported sleep and anxiety versus usual care over three months; combined exposure and inability to blind prevent single-ingredient attribution.
18. **nutritional-interventions-schizophrenia — PMID 42547027** — Systematic review of nine schizophrenia RCTs found selected metabolic, inflammatory and neuropsychiatric signals across probiotics, vitamin combinations, omega-3 and plant-derived bioactives, but substantial intervention heterogeneity and short follow-up preclude a unified nutritional recommendation.
19. **nutrition-neuroinflammation-ad-mci — PMID 42661181** — Systematic review of 22 clinical and preclinical studies found omega-3, ketogenic, B-vitamin, vitamin D and microbiota-targeted interventions can modulate neuroinflammatory biomarkers, but evidence strength varied widely and much of the ketogenic/mechanistic support was preclinical.
20. **coq10-taurine-stress — PMID 42514298** — Small randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in 24 under-19 soccer players found CoQ10 and taurine produced different oxidative, inflammatory and hormonal biomarker patterns during repeated match simulations; tiny groups and biomarker focus do not establish broad performance superiority.
21. **omega-3-pediatric-ist — PMID 42518088** — Randomized trial in 60 children with inappropriate sinus tachycardia found three months of omega-3 improved heart-rate and heart-rate-variability measures versus standard follow-up, while anxiety scores did not differ; findings are condition-specific and require replication.
22. **l-theanine-caffeine-adhd — PMID 42096377** — Double-blind three-way crossover trial in 21 adolescents with ADHD found an acute L-theanine-plus-caffeine combination reduced false alarms and altered P3b measures versus placebo, while hits and discrimination sensitivity were unchanged and only methylphenidate improved overall reaction time; combination attribution remains explicit.
23. **melissa-phospholipids-cortisol — PMID 42232738** — Secondary analysis in a predefined 12-person subpopulation receiving a phospholipid-based Melissa officinalis formulation found salivary cortisol fell over three weeks alongside improved sleep and mood measures; tiny uncontrolled secondary analysis is hypothesis-generating only.
24. **ashwagandha-menopause-sleep — PMID 42742071** — Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in 66 postmenopausal women found 12 weeks of ashwagandha root extract improved PSQI sleep quality, menopausal symptoms, body-fat percentage and several quality-of-life domains, while bone mineral density did not differ; single-center population limits generalization.
25. **rhodiola-football-performance — PMID 41829897** — Small randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in 24 competitive male football players reported improved intermittent performance, selected sprint and decision-making outcomes and lower post-exercise lactate after four weeks of Rhodiola rosea; numerous endpoints and tiny groups make the findings exploratory.
