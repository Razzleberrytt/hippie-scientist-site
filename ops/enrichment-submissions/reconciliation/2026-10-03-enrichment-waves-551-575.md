## Pass design

This pass deliberately pairs **20 unused historical randomized human evidence nodes** with **5 newer synthesis-level updates**. The goal is evidence trajectory: preserve what early trials showed while preventing old, small or formulation-specific results from becoming decontextualized current claims.

No entity creation, governance override, publication/indexing/recommendation promotion, monetization mutation or consumer-dose inference is authorized. Nulls, mixed results, formulation identity, population selection, baseline nutrient status, combination attribution and safety context remain first-class.
