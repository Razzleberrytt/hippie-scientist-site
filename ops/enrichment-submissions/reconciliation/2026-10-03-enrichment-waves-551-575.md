# Research enrichment Waves 551–575 — 2026-10-03

Third pass of the Waves 501–1000 program.

## Waves 551–575
1. **beta-alanine — PMID 39032921** — 2024 systematic review/meta-analysis in trained young men; maximal-intensity performance evidence is sex-, training-status-, duration-, and chronic-supplementation specific.
2. **ginger — PMID 40226035** — 2025 systematic review/meta-analysis for hyperemesis gravidarum; pregnancy-specific nausea/vomiting evidence across differing ginger forms and active comparators.
3. **cranberry — PMID 39030132** — 2024 network meta-analysis of cranberry juice/tablets and increased fluids for UTI prevention; product form and comparator identity remain explicit.
4. **cranberry — PMID 39668896** — 2024/2025 PAC-dose meta-analysis; prevention signal concentrated in products providing at least a specified proanthocyanidin threshold, reinforcing composition-specificity.
5. **inositol — PMID 38163998** — International-guideline-supporting PCOS systematic review/meta-analysis; some metabolic/reproductive signals but broad outcome uncertainty and generally mild GI tolerability versus metformin.
6. **ginger — PMID 40808693** — 2025 systematic review of ginger meta-analyses; umbrella-level synthesis across inflammation, T2D, oxidative stress and pregnancy nausea with indication-specific evidence kept separate.
7. **taurine — PMID 38755142** — 2024 25-RCT/1,024-participant metabolic-syndrome meta-analysis; BP/FBG/TG signals with HDL null and no universal dose inference.
8. **caffeine — PMID 42356375** — 2026 dose-response meta-analysis of aerobic time-trial RCTs; low and moderate acute doses improved completion time, with no qualifying high-dose evidence and no dosing recommendation.
9. **caffeine — PMID 37844569** — 2024 CYP1A2 genotype/exercise meta-analysis; genotype appeared to modify ergogenic response, with strong caution about small CC-genotype samples.
10. **caffeine — PMID 41127092** — 2025 resistance-exercise meta-analysis; acute caffeine improved mean velocity/power with subgroup differences by sex and habitual use.
11. **ganoderma-lucidum — PMID 40510787** — 2025 17-RCT/971-participant GRADE review; selected BMI/creatinine/GPx/heart-rate signals but most cardiometabolic endpoints null and overall certainty very low.
12. **hericium-erinaceus — PMID 40959699** — 2025 systematic review of lion's mane human/preclinical evidence; limited RCT cognition signals with substantial evidence-type heterogeneity.
13. **beetroot-nitrate — PMID 39203900** — 2024 cycling-sprint meta-analysis; dietary nitrate improved time-to-peak power but not mean, peak, or minimum power—performance-domain specificity preserved.
14. **tart-cherry — PMID 40964149** — 2025 systematic review of seven interventional sleep studies; sleep evidence remains small and heterogeneous across tart-cherry products/outcomes.
15. **caffeine — PMID 35894639** — Systematic review/meta-analysis of cardiopulmonary responses during maximal graded exercise; physiologic responses are exercise-test specific and distinct from performance outcomes.
16. **caffeine-placebo — PMID 39339818** — 2024 systematic review of expectancy/placebo effects in sport and exercise; useful context separating pharmacologic caffeine effects from expectancy effects.
17. **dietary-supplements-skin-photoaging — PMID 40761858** — 2025 40-RCT review of oral supplements for photoaging; collagen/flavanol/polyphenol signals coexist with null hyaluronic-acid/lycopene/carotenoid outcomes.
18. **herbal-adverse-reaction-signals — PMID 40601107** — 2025 pharmacovigilance scoping analysis of herbal adverse-reaction signals; safety surveillance evidence only, not incidence or causal-risk estimates.
19. **non-soy-herbal-menopause — PMID 42451042** — 2026 systematic review of 30 RCTs/19 oral herbal interventions; high heterogeneity, with black cohosh/dong quai trials among null results.
20. **ding-kun-dan — PMID 41143285** — 2025 21-RCT/1,981-participant menopausal-syndrome meta-analysis; multi-herb formula and adjunctive-treatment contexts remain product specific.
21. **plant-derived-supplements-menopause-lipids — PMID 40613540** — 2025 60-study/6,127-participant meta-analysis; modest lipid signals across heterogeneous plant-derived supplements, with soy/phytoestrogen subgroup concentration.
22. **herbal-masld-network — PMID 42668494** — 2026 network meta-analysis of herbal products on metabolic outcomes in MASLD; rankings remain network-level and formulation-specific.
23. **dietary-supplement-caers — PMID 42323077** — 2026 FDA CAERS disproportionality analysis; herbal/botanical and weight-loss categories carried safety signals, but spontaneous-reporting data cannot establish incidence or causality.
24. **plant-derived-menopause-symptoms — PMID 38189863** — Updated meta-analysis of plant-derived supplements for menopausal symptoms; pooled symptom improvements coexist with heterogeneous products and generally suboptimal evidence quality.
25. **microbiota-masld — PMID 38879003** — 2024 RCT meta-analysis of probiotics/prebiotics/synbiotics/FMT and related gut-microbiota interventions in MASLD; intervention classes, duration and mechanistic endpoints remain separate.
