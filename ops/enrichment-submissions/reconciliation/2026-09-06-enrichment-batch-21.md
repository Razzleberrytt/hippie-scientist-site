# Enrichment Batch 21 — 2026-09-06

## Purpose

Research-only enrichment pass continuing Batch 20. This batch is **reconciliation/staging evidence, not canonical/public mutation**. Targets are deduplicated against the 50 Batch-20 receipts and preserve exact intervention, formulation, population, endpoint, certainty, and safety boundaries.

## Operating boundaries

- Human clinical evidence remains distinct from observational, mechanistic, animal, traditional, biomarker, and microbiome evidence.
- Reviews/meta-analyses remain review-level evidence; they do not become synthetic primary trials.
- Exact intervention identity, formulation, species, route, population, indication, and endpoint remain attached.
- Null, mixed, heterogeneous, low-certainty, and safety-counterweight findings remain first-class.
- No consumer dose or recommendation is inferred from a studied regimen.
- Category-level or combination evidence does not automatically transfer to an individual ingredient.
- No source is promoted here; canonical source admission and governed implementation remain separate.

## Batch findings — 50 targets

1. **PUFA cognition — PMID 42137868.** Thirteen trials/2,451 participants; cognitive signal was highly heterogeneous (I² 96%) and attenuated after exclusion of high-risk studies. Preserve the sensitivity analysis and do not treat the pooled estimate as settled efficacy.

2. **Polyphenols / gut microbiota — PMID 42280405.** Fifty RCTs/2,042 participants; microbiome and short-chain-fatty-acid associations are mechanistic/biomarker findings, not proof of a clinical prebiotic effect.

3. **Anthocyanins — PMID 42547104.** Mixed cohort and RCT evidence; separate disease-risk associations from randomized FMD/insulin findings and preserve sponsor provenance.

4. **Anthocyanins in impaired glucose tolerance — PMID 41619973.** 2026 RCT evidence requiring exact preparation/dose and remission-definition extraction. IGT remission must not become diabetes-prevention or diabetes-treatment evidence.

5. **Resveratrol — PMID 42471257.** Seven RCTs; ApoB/ApoA-I outcomes were null. Preserve endpoint specificity and avoid importing mechanistic antioxidant claims into clinical lipid claims.

6. **NMN efficacy — PMID 41901064.** Ten RCTs/349 participants with elevated blood pressure; preserve NMN identity and do not merge NMN evidence with NR, NAD+, niacin, or generic NAD-boosting claims.

7. **NMN safety — PMID 42514320.** Fifteen trials/ten safety studies; 250–2,000 mg/day and 14 days–24 weeks, with no significant differences across several adverse-event outcomes. This supports only short-term safety context, not long-term safety.

8. **Lutein/carotenoids in AMD — PMID 42028334.** Disease-specific ophthalmic evidence; keep age-related macular degeneration outcomes separate from generic cognition/eye-health claims.

9. **Pre/pro/synbiotics for anxiety/depression — PMID 42010690.** GRADE-assessed evidence must remain strain-, condition-, and outcome-specific; do not convert category-level biotic evidence into generic mental-health efficacy.

10. **Probiotics in depression — PMID 42495752.** July-2026 systematic review/meta-analysis; preserve psychiatric population, strain/product identity, and outcome boundaries.

11. **Probiotics in multiple sclerosis — PMID 42580083.** MS-specific evidence; do not generalize inflammatory or microbiome findings to healthy users or other neurologic conditions.

12. **Biotics umbrella review — PMID 42289829.** Umbrella review of 128 systematic reviews/meta-analyses; substantial strain, population, condition, and safety variability. Pediatric sepsis safety concerns remain a distinct counterweight.

13. **Lactobacillus/Bifidobacterium and fasting glucose — PMID 41449894.** Twelve RCTs/1,113 participants; fecal microbiome endpoints must remain distinct from clinical glycemic efficacy.

14. **Biotics in athletes — PMID 41773190.** Umbrella/re-analysis centered on immune/inflammatory biomarkers; do not convert biomarker findings into performance enhancement.

15. **Longer-term supplement safety in at-risk populations — Front Nutr 2026, DOI 10.3389/fnut.2026.1895136.** Use as safety-governance context; retain population and duration boundaries and do not treat a pooled safety signal as universal safety.

16. **Early-life probiotic/synbiotic respiratory infection prevention — PMID 42451071.** Children <=24 months; strain-specific prevention evidence. Do not generalize to adult respiratory prevention.

17. **Phytosterols/stanols — PMID 42275989.** Umbrella evidence applies to phytosterol/stanol interventions as studied; do not propagate category evidence to beta-sitosterol or other individual sterols without identity matching.

18. **Green tea extract substrate utilization — PMID 42155001.** GRADE-assessed RCT meta-analysis reports enhanced fat oxidation during/after exercise with limited carbohydrate effects. This is substrate-utilization evidence, not direct fat-loss evidence.

19. **Chicory + green tea for insomnia — PMID 42160360.** Combination intervention with null insomnia evidence; do not transfer the result to green tea extract or chicory alone.

20. **Dietary supplements in endometriosis — PMID 42319865.** Disease-specific evidence should remain intervention- and outcome-specific; category-level supplement claims are not justified.

21. **DHM + vitamins C/E + choline in MASLD — PMID 41612982.** Multi-ingredient combination evidence only; do not attribute observed outcomes to DHM, vitamins, or choline individually.

22. **Proanthocyanidins / grape-seed extract — PMID 42364670.** Review found only one human RCT among 15 studies (54 athletes; OPC 210 mg/day for 9 days) with very-low certainty. Preserve the human-evidence floor and athlete-specific context.

23. **Propolis — PMID 42551575.** Eighteen RCTs/498 participants; 250–1,500 mg/day for 4–104 weeks with inflammatory/oxidative signals but heterogeneous, low-certainty evidence. Origin/phenotype differences matter.

24. **MPFF — PMID 42331095.** Five studies/411 participants, 1,000 mg/day, early chronic venous disease C0s-C1; pain/heaviness/QoL signals. Formulation-specific and sponsor/provenance context must remain attached.

25. **Syzygium cumini / Myrtaceae dysglycemia — PMID 42450449.** Thirteen trials/802 participants; FPG MD −14.40 mg/dL with very high heterogeneity (I² 98.57%). Preserve species/formulation and heterogeneity; do not generalize to all Myrtaceae.

26. **Curcumin depression — PMID 42472817.** GRADE systematic review/meta-analysis; keep depression evidence separate from piperine, nanocurcumin, anxiety, and other curcumin formulations.

27. **L-theanine cognition/affective outcomes — PMID 42410082.** Thirty-one RCTs/1,168 participants. A single 200 mg dose improved choice reaction time in the pooled analysis; acute stress effect was modest and influenced by high-risk studies; fatigue was null and anxiety findings inconsistent. Preserve acute-dose and population boundaries.

28. **L-theanine sleep outcomes — PMID 40056718.** Nineteen articles/897 participants; subjective sleep-onset latency, daytime dysfunction, and overall subjective sleep quality improved modestly. Keep subjective outcomes distinct from objective sleep measures.

29. **L-theanine dietary-supplement sleep review — PMID 41176609.** Thirteen trials/550 participants; standalone L-theanine trials used 50–900 mg/day. Preserve the review's 200–450 mg/day interpretation as a studied-evidence range, not a consumer dosing recommendation, and note author-industry provenance.

30. **Tea / L-theanine / caffeine — PMID 40314930.** Fifty RCTs in healthy participants; theanine plus caffeine produced small-to-moderate short-term cognitive/mood differences, while uncertainty remained substantial. Do not collapse tea-beverage, isolated theanine, and theanine+caffeine evidence.

31. **Berberine and gut microbiota — PMID 42356246.** Seven RCTs across T2DM, hyperlipidemia, colorectal adenoma, psychiatric disorders, and Parkinson's disease; preserve disease/population and microbiome-method boundaries and do not treat microbiome shifts as causal clinical mechanisms.

32. **Berberine obesity indices — PMID 41310257.** 2026 systematic review/meta-analysis of adult RCTs evaluating weight, BMI, waist circumference, and waist-to-hip ratio. Preserve obesity-population and endpoint-specific evidence; do not convert pooled anthropometrics into treatment claims.

33. **Berberine metabolic syndrome — PMID 40740996.** RCT meta-analysis found reductions in TG, FPG, waist circumference, LDL-C, TC, BMI, and 2-hour OGTT, while HDL-C and BP outcomes were null. Preserve heterogeneity, duration effects, and safety evidence rather than treating berberine as a replacement for standard therapy.

34. **Bacopa vs Ginkgo cognition — PMID 41678913.** Network meta-analysis of 29 RCTs/2,107 healthy adults. High-dose Bacopa ranked favorably for working/short-term memory, but direct comparisons were limited. Preserve extract identity, dose category, and indirect-comparison limitations.

35. **Ginkgo biloba cognitive impairment/dementia — PMID 41641880.** 2026 Cochrane review. Keep dementia/cognitive-impairment evidence separate from healthy nootropic claims and from older healthy-adult evidence; extract formulation and population details before promotion.

36. **Rhodiola endurance performance — PMID 41080184.** Systematic review/meta-analysis of RCTs evaluating endurance performance and physiological biomarkers. Preserve athletic context, outcome-specific effects, and biomarker-vs-performance distinctions.

37. **Alpha-lipoic acid in diabetic polyneuropathy — PMID 41619689.** Fifteen articles, twelve analyzed for outcomes; evidence suggests symptom benefit, especially around 600 mg/day, while HbA1c, nitric oxide, sural sensory potential, and peroneal motor conduction were not significantly changed. Preserve DPN population and symptom-vs-glycemic boundaries.

38. **N-acetylcysteine in COPD — PMID 42473447.** Fourteen RCTs/2,856 patients; NAC was associated with fewer acute exacerbations overall, with a signal at <=600 mg/day but not >600 mg/day. COPD-specific evidence; do not generalize to healthy respiratory prevention.

39. **N-acetylcysteine in depression — PMID 39504621.** Twelve RCTs/904 patients; SMD −0.24 for depressive symptoms with doses 1,000–3,000 mg/day over 8–24 weeks. Preserve adjunctive/psychiatric population and modest effect size; do not infer a general antidepressant indication.

40. **N-acetylcysteine for chemotherapy-induced peripheral neuropathy prevention — PMID 42055451.** 2026 systematic review/meta-analysis. Cancer-treatment context is mandatory; do not transfer results to idiopathic neuropathy or general antioxidant supplementation.

41. **Quercetin in MASLD — PMID 41404533.** Seven RCTs/540 patients; reductions in liver enzymes, direct bilirubin, CRP, total cholesterol, LDL, and triglycerides were reported, while BMI/body fat and several other markers were null. Preserve MASLD-specific population and heterogeneity.

42. **Quercetin weight loss — PMID 31114281.** Nine RCTs/525 participants; no significant effects on body weight, BMI, waist circumference, or waist-to-hip ratio. Retain as a null/counterweight source against broad weight-loss claims.

43. **Quercetin blood pressure/endothelial function — PMID 31092175.** Eight RCTs in metabolic-syndrome-related populations; SBP reduction was small while DBP and endothelial markers were not significant. Preserve population and surrogate limitations.

44. **Inositol in PCOS — PMID 41947399.** 2026 systematic review/meta-analysis evaluating biochemical response by metabolic phenotype. Preserve PCOS population, stereoisomer/formulation identity, and phenotype-response boundaries.

45. **Inositol cardiometabolic outcomes — PMID 41163174.** Eighteen RCTs/898 participants; modest anthropometric and cardiometabolic improvements were reported with high heterogeneity and low/very-low certainty for anthropometric outcomes. Preserve certainty and avoid universal weight-loss claims.

46. **Prenatal omega-3 and offspring cognition — PMID 41655662.** Systematic review/meta-analysis of prenatal omega-3 supplementation and cognitive outcomes. Keep prenatal exposure, developmental endpoint, and offspring population separate from adult cognition claims.

47. **Omega-3 cognition dose-response — PMID 40836005.** Fifty-eight RCTs; dose-response analyses reported improvements in several cognitive domains but with low-to-moderate GRADE certainty and non-linear patterns. Preserve domain-specific results and avoid a single universal cognitive effect.

48. **Milk thistle in liver disease — PMID 12427501.** Fourteen randomized placebo-controlled trials; mortality and most liver outcomes were null, with a small ALT signal that lost significance in higher-quality/longer-duration analyses. Retain as a counterweight to generic liver-detox claims.

49. **Magnesium for insomnia in older adults — PMID 33865376.** Three RCTs/151 older adults; pooled sleep-onset latency favored magnesium by about 17 minutes, but evidence was based on a small trial set. Preserve age/insomnia population and low evidence volume.

50. **Rhodiola adaptogenic/psychometric evidence — PMID 41906501.** Systematic review of RCTs covering *Rhodiola rosea* and *Withania somnifera*. Use the Rhodiola-specific findings without importing the ashwagandha arm into Rhodiola claims; preserve psychometric endpoint and mechanism interpretation boundaries.

## Disposition

Items with exact human-outcome boundaries are eligible for the existing governed review path; category-level, combination, surrogate, population-specific, formulation-sensitive, or low-certainty items retain those restrictions.
