## Waves
- **2151 · multimodal-covid-supplement-regimen · PMID 41693032** — A five-component regimen containing ivermectin, doxycycline, zinc, vitamin C, and vitamin D, with or without famotidine, was reported as generally well tolerated; the study was not designed to isolate the supplements' efficacy. _State:_ `research_only_combination_firewall`
- **2152 · ginseng-ginsenosides · PMID 41120028** — Ginsenosides show antiviral, antibacterial, immunomodulatory, and anti-inflammatory activity across experimental systems, but the evidence base is dominated by mechanistic and preclinical work. _State:_ `research_only_preclinical`
- **2153 · saffron-authenticity · PMID 40792566** — The review found substantial geographic variability in saffron adulteration and documented substitution, dilution, and use of synthetic dyes including potentially hazardous colorants. _State:_ `research_only_product_quality`
- **2154 · whey-protein · PMID 41754212** — Whey protein may help preserve fat-free mass during weight loss, especially when paired with resistance exercise or anabolic-enriched formulations, but many trials were neutral and certainty was frequently downgraded. _State:_ `research_only_combination_firewall`
- **2155 · omega-3 · PMID 40990181** — Evidence was inadequate to show meaningful improvement in peripheral-nerve impairment, neuropathy symptoms, pain, or quality of life; available adverse-event data did not show a clear harm signal but were very uncertain. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2156 · whey-protein · PMID 40871607** — Whey protein combined with exercise increased myofibrillar fractional synthetic rate and activated AKT/mTOR-related signaling after exercise. _State:_ `research_only_combination_firewall`
- **2157 · alpha-lipoic-acid · PMID 40327515** — Alpha-lipoic acid improved sperm morphology and motility measures over about three months but did not significantly improve pooled pregnancy rate, sperm concentration, live-sperm percentage, or total antioxidant capacity. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2158 · probiotics · PMID 41547378** — Probiotics added to antifungals modestly improved some short-term cure outcomes and reduced six-month recurrence in low-certainty analyses, while probiotics alone were generally less effective than antifungals for sustained cure. _State:_ `research_only_category_firewall`
- **2159 · synbiotics · PMID 41883170** — Twelve weeks of synbiotics reduced LBP, hs-CRP, IL-6, and sICAM-1 but did not significantly change creatinine, urea, MDA, or galectin-3. _State:_ `research_only_population_firewall`
- **2160 · hypericum-perforatum-gum · PMID 41510676** — Hypericum-containing gum reduced Streptococcus mutans count, plaque index, and gingival bleeding more than control gum, while between-group salivary-pH differences were not significant. _State:_ `research_only_route_firewall`
- **2161 · curcumin · PMID 41601662** — Curcumin improved pooled disease-activity, inflammatory, pain, and joint-count outcomes, but certainty was very low for most endpoints and low for rheumatoid factor. _State:_ `research_only_low_certainty`
- **2162 · magnesium-oxide · PMID 42091979** — Single 750-mg and 2,000-mg magnesium-oxide doses produced non-dose-proportional serum magnesium exposure; the higher dose did not proportionally increase Cmax or AUC. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2163 · astaxanthin · PMID 42627484** — Astaxanthin has plausible antioxidant and mitochondrial mechanisms and some studies report semen-parameter improvements, but clinical findings remain limited and inconsistent. _State:_ `research_only_evidence_quality`
- **2164 · calcium-vitamin-d · PMID 42161415** — Calcium alone, vitamin D alone, and combined supplementation produced little to no clinically meaningful reduction in fractures or falls; evidence was mostly moderate to high certainty. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2165 · vitamin-d · PMID 41802552** — Prenatal cholecalciferol did not significantly alter offspring tibial cortical/trabecular microarchitecture or volumetric bone mineral density at age 6-7. _State:_ `research_only_population_firewall`
- **2166 · cetoleic-acid · PMID 41699650** — Cetoleic and related long-chain monounsaturated fatty acids have plausible neuroprotective mechanisms, but direct evidence for cetoleic acid is sparse and largely preclinical. _State:_ `research_only_preclinical`
- **2167 · vitamin-c-hesperidin · PMID 41739218** — Vitamin-C-containing drinks reduced serum uric acid without changing urinary uric-acid excretion or clearance; hesperidin alone did not drive the effect. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2168 · panax-ginseng · PMID 41169144** — The review summarizes wide-ranging proposed cognitive, immune, hepatic, bone, and lipid effects of Panax ginseng but does not provide a focused quantitative clinical synthesis for any single indication. _State:_ `research_only_evidence_quality`
- **2169 · melatonin · PMID 40288252** — Melatonin modestly improved sleep quality/daytime sleepiness overall, with positive subgroup estimates for short-term and immediate-release regimens, while lower-dose, longer-duration, and prolonged-release subgroups were not consistently significant. _State:_ `research_only_formulation_firewall`
- **2170 · saffron · PMID 40842498** — Saffron reduced TNF-alpha but did not significantly change CRP, IL-6, IL-10, MDA, or total antioxidant capacity. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2171 · l-carnitine · PMID 41879202** — Topical and systemic L-carnitine showed signals across acne, sebum, hyperpigmentation, cellulite, and other dermatologic contexts, but interventions, routes, combinations, and evidence quality varied substantially. _State:_ `research_only_evidence_quality`
- **2172 · omega-3 · PMID 42521226** — Omega-3 supplementation showed no overall reduction in childhood asthma/wheezing; apparent benefit was confined to high-dose subgroup analyses. _State:_ `research_only_population_firewall`
- **2173 · caffeine · PMID 42151857** — Habitual moderate caffeine exposure was generally not linked to higher migraine burden, while abrupt withdrawal and acute high intake could trigger attacks in susceptible people; genetic-instrument studies suggested lower migraine risk with higher lifelong coffee/caffeine exposure. _State:_ `research_only_observational`
- **2174 · creatine · PMID 40944139** — Creatine, especially with resistance training, produced small significant gains in bench/chest press, squat, vertical jump, and Wingate peak power, while handgrip and overall leg-press effects were not significant. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2175 · turmeric-curcumin · PMID 40813857** — Turmeric/curcumin produced modest reductions in body weight and waist circumference, with some additional anthropometric improvements, but BMI and waist-to-hip ratio were not consistently improved and certainty ranged from very low to moderate. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
