# Research enrichment Waves 1276–1300 — 2026-10-04

Twelfth pass of the Waves 1001–1500 program, emphasizing taurine, carnitine, NAC, GABA, alpha-lipoic acid, and mechanistic-versus-clinical outcome boundaries.

## Waves 1276–1300
1. **taurine-liver-transplant — PMID 41605371** — Randomized double-blind trial in 169 liver-transplant recipients found 30 days of taurine improved selected graft-function markers and was associated with shorter ICU/hospital stay, ventilation duration and lower mortality; single-center peri-transplant findings need independent replication.
2. **taurine-long-covid-context — PMID 41803812** — Systematic review/meta-analysis pooled taurine trials from metabolic and inflammatory conditions plus convalescent taurine-status studies to argue biological relevance to long COVID; because the intervention trials were not direct PASC treatment trials, the evidence is indirect and should not be presented as established long-COVID efficacy.
3. **taurine-cirrhosis-fatigue — PMID 42043864** — Open-label randomized trial in 220 patients with decompensated cirrhosis found taurine did not significantly improve fatigue overall versus standard care; a large apparent benefit in the non-anemic subgroup was post hoc and hypothesis-generating.
4. **taurine-caffeine-phosphatidylserine-football — PMID 42280328** — Short randomized single-blind football trial found a taurine+caffeine+phosphatidylserine protocol improved selected physical, reaction-time and tactical outcomes versus placebo, but differing caffeine doses and multi-ingredient exposure prevent attribution to phosphatidylserine or taurine alone.
5. **coq10-l-carnitine-male-infertility — PMID 41509099** — Double-blind placebo-controlled trial in men with idiopathic oligoasthenoteratozoospermia found CoQ10, L-carnitine and their combination improved semen measures and DNA fragmentation versus baseline/placebo, with the combination appearing stronger; fertility outcomes such as live birth were not established.
6. **l-carnitine-renal-anemia — PMID 41644022** — Randomized double-blind pilot in hemodialysis patients hyporesponsive to erythropoiesis-stimulating agents found intravenous L-carnitine reduced erythropoietin resistance index versus placebo; the small pilot and specialized treatment population limit generalization.
7. **acetyl-l-carnitine-mood-disorders — PMID 42261369** — Systematic review/meta-analysis of 15 studies/809 participants reported lower depressive symptoms with acetyl-L-carnitine versus placebo and broadly comparable effects to antidepressants, but the corpus mixed RCTs, open-label and observational designs and needs larger confirmatory trials.
8. **pomegranate-carnitine-tmao — PMID 42293209** — Double-blind randomized crossover challenge study found a single pomegranate-extract dose did not reduce post-carnitine TMAO exposure overall in healthy high producers; age/sex subgroup signals were post hoc and require confirmation.
9. **l-carnitine-hemodialysis-cardiometabolic — PMID 42316068** — Meta-analysis of 16 trials/699 hemodialysis patients found no significant overall effect of L-carnitine on weight, BMI, triglycerides, LDL, total cholesterol, HDL, albumin or hemoglobin; favorable subgroup findings were exploratory and vulnerable to multiple-testing error.
10. **l-carnitine-lipids-overweight-women — PMID 42332819** — Meta-analysis of ten RCTs in women with overweight/obesity found modest reductions in triglycerides, total cholesterol and LDL with L-carnitine, while HDL was unchanged; dose/duration subgroup findings remain exploratory.
11. **alpha-lipoic-acid-diabetic-neuropathy — PMID 42643396** — Bayesian network meta-analysis of nine RCTs found route-dependent alpha-lipoic-acid effects in diabetic peripheral neuropathy, but apparent sequential-therapy superiority disappeared after excluding high-bias evidence; route rankings should not be treated as simple universal superiority.
12. **gaba-ibs-d — PMID 42197028** — Double-blind placebo-controlled crossover pilot in 20 IBS-D patients found oral GABA improved IBS symptom score and selected emotional/inflammatory measures without substantial microbiome remodeling; very small sample makes the result preliminary.
13. **nac-bipolar-alcohol-use — PMID 42236678** — Randomized double-blind crossover study in bipolar disorder plus alcohol-use disorder found one-week NAC lowered dACC glutamate/glutamine and heavy-drinking days versus placebo, while depressive symptoms were unchanged; short mechanistic trial requires larger clinical confirmation.
14. **blueberry-mild-depressive-symptoms — PMID 42628247** — Tiny three-month randomized placebo-controlled pilot in 18 older adults found blueberry powder changed selected gut-microbial enzyme functions but not species abundance, metabolites or between-group depressive symptoms; microbiome signals were preliminary.
15. **caffeine-taurine-performance — PMID 41032459** — Bayesian network meta-analysis suggested caffeine+taurine may outperform either alone for selected anaerobic and reaction-time outcomes, while endurance and physiologic responses were variable; indirect network rankings and combination exposure prevent single-ingredient claims.
16. **food-bioactives-t2d-oxidative-stress — PMID 41533751** — Systematic review/meta-analysis of 109 T2D RCTs found outcome-specific antioxidant/glycemic signals across ALA, vitamin D, curcumin, NAC, citrulline, taurine, zinc, selenium and chromium; heterogeneous category-level evidence cannot justify a universal antioxidant-supplement recommendation.
17. **food-first-anxiety-depression — PMID 42123920** — Focused evidence synthesis concluded that diet quality, omega-3s, microbiota-targeted foods and micronutrients show modest, context-dependent mood signals, with the most defensible strategy being food-first and correction of insufficiency rather than blanket supplementation.
18. **nac-masld — PMID 42587287** — Open-label randomized trial in 60 non-diabetic MASLD patients found high-dose NAC added to lifestyle intervention did not improve oxidative stress, insulin resistance, FibroScan steatosis/fibrosis or most metabolic outcomes versus lifestyle alone; strong direct null evidence.
19. **nac-postoperative-cognition — PMID 42736670** — Randomized controlled day-surgery study in older adults found perioperative oral NAC did not improve early postoperative MoCA scores or discharge readiness, though quality-of-recovery scores were higher; cognitive-protection efficacy was not demonstrated.
20. **nac-parkinsons-pilot — PMID 42776345** — One-year randomized placebo-controlled Parkinson pilot found more favorable exploratory UPDRS and dopamine-transporter imaging trajectories with NAC among completers, but only 32 completed follow-up, multiplicity was uncorrected and the study does not establish disease modification.
21. **nac-preeclampsia — PMID 42797390** — Double-blind randomized pilot in 51 women with preeclampsia found NAC did not materially alter redox/inflammatory markers or delivery mode; a gestational-age difference lost significance after covariate adjustment and remains exploratory.
22. **gaba-functional-nutrient — PMID 42514458** — Review of oral GABA as a functional nutrient found extensive mechanistic/preclinical rationale but limited human evidence and no definitive clinical efficacy; future work needs powered RCTs, dose-response and long-term safety data.
23. **caffeine-gaba-firefighter-balance — PMID 42739045** — Very small double-blind crossover trial in 14 firefighters found caffeinated chewing gum improved a functional-balance test after GABA supplementation, with small-to-moderate effects; design cannot establish a GABA benefit and performance context is highly specific.
24. **nac-icu-respiratory-disease — PMID 42780769** — Meta-analysis of five RCTs/340 ICU respiratory patients found NAC did not significantly reduce mortality, mechanical-ventilation duration or ICU stay, although hospital stay was modestly shorter; core clinical outcomes were largely neutral.
25. **acetyl-l-carnitine-peripheral-neuropathy — PMID 41824218** — Narrative review summarized mechanistic, preclinical and clinical evidence for acetyl-L-carnitine in peripheral neuropathies; useful background for neurotrophic/analgesic mechanisms but not a quantitative efficacy estimate.
