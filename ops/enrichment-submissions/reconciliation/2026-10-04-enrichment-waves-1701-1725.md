# Research enrichment Waves 1701–1725 — 2026-10-04

Ninth pass of the Waves 1501–2000 program, focused on gastrointestinal health, IBS, constipation, microbiota-targeted interventions, and strict strain/formulation specificity.

## Waves 1701–1725
1. **probiotics-ibs-updated-meta — PMID 41820241** — Updated meta-analysis of 40 IBS probiotic RCTs found improvement in symptom-severity scores but not quality of life; trial-sequential analysis showed the evidence had not yet reached required information size for firm benefit or futility.
2. **multistrain-probiotic-metformin-gi-tolerance — PMID 41852479** — Randomized controlled evidence found a multistrain probiotic reduced gastrointestinal side effects in women with elevated HOMA-IR receiving metformin; benefits are adjunctive and product-specific rather than proof of class-wide probiotic efficacy.
3. **probiotic-subclinical-gi-students — PMID 41857208** — Eight-week placebo-controlled RCT evaluated a defined probiotic intervention for subclinical gastrointestinal symptoms in students; population and formulation specificity should be preserved rather than generalized to IBS or chronic GI disease.
4. **weizmannia-coagulans-bc99-constipation — PMID 41886091** — Eight-week double-blind RCT in adults with constipation found Weizmannia coagulans BC99 improved bowel frequency, stool characteristics and transit-related measures with microbiome/SCFA changes; findings are strain-specific.
5. **multistrain-probiotic-metformin-multiomics — PMID 41985330** — Post-hoc multi-omics analysis of the randomized ProGasMet trial linked improved metformin tolerance from a multistrain probiotic to bile-acid and selected microbiome/metabolome changes, without global diversity differences; mechanistic findings remain exploratory.
6. **bifidobacterium-bifidum-g91-t2d-gi — PMID 42017290** — Open-label randomized trial found Bifidobacterium bifidum G9-1 improved gastrointestinal symptoms in type 2 diabetes; lack of blinding and strain-specific design limit broader probiotic inference.
7. **spore-forming-probiotic-functional-constipation — PMID 42030329** — Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial found a spore-forming probiotic blend improved bowel habits and physical well-being in adults with functional constipation; multistrain/blend findings cannot be transferred to other probiotics.
8. **l-plantarum-z6-infant-constipation — PMID 42068035** — Randomized placebo-controlled trial in 100 infants with functional constipation found Lactiplantibacillus plantarum Z6 improved bowel frequency, stool scores and constipation severity with microbiome changes; infant and strain specificity remain explicit.
9. **probiotics-after-bariatric-surgery — PMID 42091844** — RCT in 200 bariatric-surgery patients found six months of probiotic supplementation improved quality-of-life, bowel-habit, excess-weight-loss and fecal-calprotectin outcomes versus standard care, but products and surgery context limit generalization.
10. **l-plantarum-lp05-hpylori-adjunct — PMID 42117710** — Double-blind RCT in 72 H. pylori-positive adults found L. plantarum Lp05 did not improve eradication rate but reduced selected GI adverse symptoms and attenuated antibiotic-associated dysbiosis during quadruple therapy.
11. **gutbio-plus-chronic-constipation — PMID 42152386** — Single-center randomized placebo-controlled study found a multispecies Lactobacillus/Bifidobacterium product improved complete spontaneous bowel movements and constipation symptoms, but the open-label description and unbalanced allocation warrant caution.
12. **microbiota-modulation-chemo-gi-toxicity-meta — PMID 42185245** — Meta-analysis of 29 RCTs found probiotics and synbiotics reduced chemotherapy-associated diarrhea, while evidence for prebiotics alone was limited; category-level findings span diverse strains, cancer regimens and formulations.
13. **heyndrickxia-coagulans-tbc169-constipation — PMID 42226670** — Double-blind trial in 140 middle-aged/older adults found a Heyndrickxia coagulans TBC169 plus fiber synbiotic improved constipation and quality-of-life measures versus fiber alone; no baseline stool samples made microbiome findings exploratory.
14. **saccharomyces-boulardii-hpylori-meta — PMID 42470702** — Meta-analysis found Saccharomyces boulardii added to H. pylori eradication therapy modestly improved eradication and reduced diarrhea, nausea and epigastric pain, while several other adverse symptoms were unchanged.
15. **b-animalis-cp9-healthy-infants — PMID 42528393** — Three-month double-blind RCT in healthy infants found B. animalis subsp. lactis CP-9 increased Bifidobacterium abundance and improved stool/tolerability measures without treatment-related adverse changes; safety/benefits are strain- and age-specific.
16. **multibifidobacterium-short-term-travel — PMID 42533554** — Five-day double-blind RCT in 74 travelers found a multi-Bifidobacterium product changed selected post-travel taxa and symptom patterns while alpha/beta diversity remained stable; travel-context and blend specificity limit generalization.
17. **heyndrickxia-coagulans-idcc1201-functional-bowel — PMID 42588089** — Eight-week double-blind RCT found H. coagulans IDCC1201 improved selected bloating, post-defecation discomfort and quality-of-life measures in functional bowel disorders, with modest microbiome/metabolome changes; strain-specific evidence.
18. **prebiotic-fiber-bread-older-adults — PMID 42594505** — 12-week feasibility RCT in older adults with low fiber intake found prebiotic-fiber bread produced mostly null cognitive, mood, GI and biomarker outcomes, with a selective hostility signal and an unexpected HDL direction; useful null/mixed evidence.
19. **probiotic-consolidation-rifaximin-ibs-d — PMID 42609157** — Multicenter randomized trial in rifaximin-responsive IBS-D found four weeks of Bacillus subtilis/Enterococcus faecium consolidation reduced relapse versus no further treatment and improved stool frequency/consistency; open-label adjunctive design limits probiotic-only inference.
20. **probiotic-long-term-safety-at-risk-meta — PMID 42682452** — Meta-analysis of five studies and 920 at-risk participants found no clear increase in reported adverse events but concluded long-term probiotic safety cannot be established because adverse-event reporting was inconsistent and rare/strain-specific harms were not assessable.
21. **sourdough-versus-yeast-bread-gut — PMID 42739025** — Small double-blind crossover RCT in 20 healthy adults found sourdough bread reduced diarrhea-domain symptoms versus baker's-yeast bread and altered selected taxa, but overall IBS-like symptom improvement was borderline and exposure lasted only one week.
22. **microbiota-dependent-fiber-response — PMID 40914513** — Randomized crossover proof-of-concept study in 23 healthy adults found arabinoxylan and inulin produced microbiota-type-dependent short-chain-fatty-acid responses, supporting personalized rather than universal assumptions about fermentable-fiber effects.
23. **probiotic-pathogen-exclusion-meta — PMID 41829966** — Meta-analysis reported a pooled pathogen-exclusion signal from probiotic interventions, but only four studies contributed quantitative data and multiple genera/strains were pooled; strain-specific replication remains necessary.
24. **early-life-microbiota-neurodevelopment-meta — PMID 41972726** — Systematic review/meta-analysis of infant probiotic, prebiotic and synbiotic RCTs evaluated neurodevelopment/behavior, emphasizing that human evidence remains heterogeneous despite stronger mechanistic animal data; interventions should not be pooled into generic gut-brain efficacy.
25. **zinc-probiotic-acute-watery-diarrhea — PMID 42033762** — Prospective randomized comparative pediatric study found low-dose zinc with or without probiotics had similar overall diarrhea duration to standard-dose zinc, although early resolution differed across groups; combined-treatment design prevents probiotic-only attribution.
