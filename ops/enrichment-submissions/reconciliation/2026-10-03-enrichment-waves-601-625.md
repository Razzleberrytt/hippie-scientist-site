# Research enrichment Waves 601–625 — 2026-10-03

Fifth pass of the Waves 501–1000 program.

## Waves 601–625
1. **probiotics — PMID 40697756** — 2025 MASLD meta-analysis of 12 RCTs/657 participants; liver-enzyme and selected metabolic/inflammatory signals remain disease-, strain-, and outcome-specific.
2. **curcumin-resveratrol-silymarin — PMID 39723101** — 27-study/1,691-participant MASLD comparison of curcumin, resveratrol and silymarin; intervention-specific steatosis/transaminase/metabolic effects must not be collapsed into a generic polyphenol claim.
3. **herbal-oral-health — PMID 40077632** — 2025 systematic review of 17 human oral-health studies spanning multiple herbal extracts; adjunctive gingivitis/periodontitis evidence is product- and route-specific.
4. **curcumin — PMID 38213188** — 15-RCT/905-participant NAFLD GRADE review; ALT/AST decreased with curcumin while ALP was null and curcumin-plus-piperine aminotransferase effects were nonsignificant.
5. **curcumin — PMID 42499105** — 26-RCT/1,266-participant periodontal comparison with chlorhexidine; plaque/gingival/bleeding outcomes similar while chlorhexidine was superior for probing depth/attachment loss overall; very high heterogeneity/risk of bias retained.
6. **probiotics-synbiotics — PMID 39166132** — 21-study CKD meta-analysis; modest BUN/CRP signals with heterogeneous renal, uremic-toxin and metabolic outcomes; CKD-specific and strain/intervention-class dependent.
7. **curcumin — PMID 38232906** — 2024 NAFLD systematic review/meta-analysis of glycemic, lipid, BP, inflammatory and anthropometric outcomes; multi-endpoint evidence remains disease-specific and heterogeneous.
8. **curcumin — PMID 38965866** — 15-study/835-participant NAFLD meta-regression; AST improved while ALT was null overall, high heterogeneity persisted, and adding piperine did not improve aminotransferases—important counterweight to other syntheses.
9. **natural-products-nafld — PMID 38886838** — Network meta-analysis comparing seven natural products in NAFLD; indirect rankings and product heterogeneity prevent ingredient-wide superiority claims.
10. **probiotics — PMID 38643738** — 2024 network meta-analysis of probiotic combinations in NAFLD; strain/composition rankings are indirect and cannot be generalized across probiotics.
11. **probiotics — PMID 42245504** — 2026 11-RCT/726-participant diabetic-kidney-disease meta-analysis; creatinine/eGFR and related signals remain DKD-, strain-, and duration-specific.
12. **probiotics — PMID 38968867** — 15-RCT/899-participant NAFLD meta-analysis; ALT/HOMA-IR/insulin improved while fasting glucose, HbA1c, AST, lipids, IL-6 and TNF-alpha were null.
13. **mafld-supplements-network — PMID 41695987** — 2026 Bayesian network meta-analysis of dietary supplements and pharmacologic agents in MAFLD; ranking evidence is indirect and outcome-specific.
14. **natural-products-periodontal — PMID 41828611** — 2026 review of 90 periodontal RCTs/33 pooled trials across curcumin, resveratrol, propolis, Aloe, green-tea catechins, omega-3 and others; adjunct/route/product boundaries retained.
15. **nutritional-interventions-ibs — PMID 39110917** — Umbrella review of randomized evidence in IBS; most nutritional interventions had small low/very-low-certainty effects, with stronger evidence concentrated in probiotics and low-FODMAP diets.
16. **probiotics-exercise — PMID 39090657** — Nine-study/615-participant NAFLD meta-analysis of probiotics plus exercise versus exercise alone; selected liver/lipid/HOMA-IR signals with HDL/insulin/FBG nulls; combination-only.
17. **vitamin-d — PMID 37356648** — Systematic review of vitamin-D therapy in adults with CKD assessing mortality, cardiovascular, bone and kidney outcomes; CKD-MBD context and clinically important outcomes retained.
18. **pcos-nutraceuticals — PMID 41287200** — 2025 78-study PCOS nutraceutical review; fasting-insulin/LH/SHBG signals with inconsistent follicle-count/menstrual outcomes and substantial intervention heterogeneity.
19. **aloe-vera — PMID 39909775** — 2025 oral-mucositis meta-analysis of randomized trials in cancer therapy; Aloe evidence is cancer-treatment-context and route/formulation specific.
20. **pcos-supplements-network — PMID 40611279** — 2025 network meta-analysis of 79 RCTs/5,501 women with PCOS; supplement rankings are endpoint-specific indirect evidence, not head-to-head proof.
21. **probiotics — PMID 38551321** — 2024 five-study/286-participant psoriasis meta-analysis; PASI/DLQI signals come from a small disease-specific probiotic evidence base.
22. **pcos-phytotherapy — PMID 40663739** — 2025 systematic review of clinical phytotherapy in PCOS; herb/product-specific findings must remain separated from category-level efficacy.
23. **pcos-dietary-supplements — PMID 41235304** — 2025 umbrella meta-analysis of dietary supplements in PCOS; pathway-level metabolic/hormonal signals across inositol, probiotics, omega-3, curcumin and others remain intervention-specific.
24. **psoriasis-supplements-network — PMID 41459063** — 2025 21-RCT/1,463-participant plaque-psoriasis network meta-analysis; no supplement dominated all outcomes and certainty was low-to-moderate.
25. **l-carnitine — PMID 40436023** — 2025 systematic review/dose-response meta-analysis of fertility outcomes in PCOS; reproductive evidence remains PCOS-specific and distinct from generic carnitine metabolic claims.
