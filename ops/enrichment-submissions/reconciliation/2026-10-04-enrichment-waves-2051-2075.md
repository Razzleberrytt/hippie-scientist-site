# Research enrichment Waves 2051–2075 — deep pass

Third abstract-verified pass of the Waves 2001–2500 program.

## Waves 2051–2075
1. **melatonin — PMID 41772302** — The only human trial reported a 6-day longer diagnosis-to-delivery interval with melatonin, while proteinuria increased; most favorable blood-pressure and oxidative-stress findings came from animal studies. Boundary: Human fetal safety and fetal benefit remain weak, conflicting, and insufficient for clinical recommendation. State: `research_only_evidence_quality`.
2. **creatine — PMID 41798953** — Creatine did not significantly reduce acute or chronic CRP and did not significantly reduce chronic IL-6; isolated signals under intense endurance conditions were inconsistent. Boundary: Moderate-certainty evidence supports a null broad anti-inflammatory claim; protocols and populations varied. State: `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`.
3. **curcumin-turmeric — PMID 42260483** — Local curcumin/turmeric adjuncts produced modest periodontal-pocket-depth reductions, but prediction intervals crossed the null and heterogeneity was high. Boundary: Low-certainty local-delivery evidence does not establish dose-response, formulation superiority, safety, or replacement of conventional antimicrobials. State: `research_only_formulation_firewall`.
4. **zinc — PMID 41872524** — Zinc supplementation delayed oral-mucositis onset and reduced incidence and severe mucositis at several treatment time points, with benefits for pain and xerostomia. Boundary: Route- and dose-dependent subgroup findings are oncology-specific and should not be converted into general consumer dosing advice. State: `research_only_population_firewall`.
5. **melatonin — PMID 41925053** — Effects on mortality, neurodevelopmental disability, and MRI abnormalities remain very uncertain; the critical composite outcome was reported by only one small pilot study. Boundary: Very-low-certainty neonatal evidence is insufficient to establish benefit or safety. State: `research_only_evidence_quality`.
6. **omega-3 — PMID 41493572** — Omega-3 supplementation did not significantly improve pulse-wave velocity, but improved augmentation index and flow-mediated dilation; effects differed by fatty-acid formulation. Boundary: Certainty ranged from moderate to low; dose-response estimates should not be treated as individualized dosing guidance. State: `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`.
7. **alpha-lipoic-acid-plus-buyang-huanwu — PMID 42147097** — The herbal-decoction plus alpha-lipoic-acid combination improved response and symptom scores, while nerve-conduction results were extremely heterogeneous and HbA1c was not different. Boundary: Combination therapy prevents attribution to alpha-lipoic acid alone; adverse events did not differ, but methodological heterogeneity was substantial. State: `research_only_combination_firewall`.
8. **probiotics-synbiotics — PMID 40782191** — Probiotic/synbiotic supplementation reduced fasting glucose, fasting insulin, HbA1c, and HOMA-IR on average. Boundary: Substantial heterogeneity and strain/product diversity prevent treating this as a uniform class effect or standardized regimen. State: `research_only_category_firewall`.
9. **probiotics — PMID 40929652** — Some strains and probiotic combinations ranked better than placebo for quality of life, depression, or anxiety, but effects were strongly strain-specific and based partly on indirect network comparisons. Boundary: Network rankings should not be interpreted as proof of universal psychobiotic efficacy. State: `research_only_strain_firewall`.
10. **probiotics — PMID 41938967** — Probiotics produced small average reductions in body weight, BMI, waist circumference, and body-fat percentage, with no significant improvement in total cholesterol or triglycerides. Boundary: Effects were strain-, dose-, and context-specific; geographic concentration limits generalizability. State: `research_only_category_firewall`.
11. **probiotics — PMID 41605120** — Probiotic supplementation was associated with improved depressive symptoms, but IL-6 and TNF-alpha were not significantly changed. Boundary: Clinical relevance and mechanisms remain uncertain; strain and formulation differences limit broad probiotic claims. State: `research_only_category_firewall`.
12. **curcumin — PMID 40720673** — Many pooled outcomes reached nominal statistical significance, but none were supported by high-certainty evidence. Boundary: Methodological shortcomings across source reviews leave curcumin's therapeutic effect uncertain despite favorable signals. State: `research_only_evidence_quality`.
13. **n-acetylcysteine — PMID 41862922** — A NAC nano-spray reduced mucositis severity and improved oral-health quality-of-life scores versus conventional therapy over six weeks. Boundary: Small sample and nano-spray delivery make this formulation-specific; it does not establish efficacy of oral NAC supplements. State: `research_only_formulation_firewall`.
14. **iodine-status — PMID 41675569** — Iodine status showed a U-shaped observational association with thyroid-nodule risk, with higher odds at deficiency and signals of increased risk at excess. Boundary: Cross-sectional evidence cannot establish that iodine supplementation causes or prevents nodules; residual confounding and heterogeneity remain. State: `research_only_observational`.
15. **astaxanthin — PMID 41714661** — Astaxanthin improved progressive motility, viability, morphology, and selected kinematic parameters after thawing, but not total motility or DNA damage. Boundary: This is ex-vivo cryopreservation evidence, not evidence that oral astaxanthin improves male fertility. State: `research_only_route_firewall`.
16. **vitamin-d — PMID 41740615** — Vitamin D supplementation was associated with improved early fusion and modest functional outcomes versus placebo/no supplement, while bisphosphonates provided stronger fracture protection and similar one-year fusion. Boundary: Perioperative spine-surgery evidence is condition-specific and intertwined with active-drug comparator evidence. State: `research_only_population_firewall`.
17. **folic-acid — PMID 41825593** — Higher-dose folic acid was associated with lower miscarriage risk among women who became pregnant and a higher pregnancy rate, but the intention-to-treat miscarriage analysis was not significant. Boundary: Evidence certainty was low to moderate; dose-comparison findings are insufficient for changing standard folic-acid dosing outside clinical guidance. State: `research_only_dose_comparison`.
18. **chlorella — PMID 42356400** — Most included studies reported favorable gut-microbiota or neurobehavioral associations, but the evidence base was overwhelmingly preclinical and rarely measured gut and brain outcomes together. Boundary: Human efficacy remains unestablished; mechanistic/preclinical signals should not be promoted as clinical benefit. State: `research_only_evidence_quality`.
19. **zinc — PMID 42121064** — Lower systemic zinc was associated with VVC, but supplementation evidence was sparse: one RCT showed a nonsignificant reduction in reinfection and another reported improved pruritus with a zinc-containing adjunct. Boundary: Causality cannot be inferred from zinc-status associations, and current clinical trials are insufficient to support routine zinc therapy for VVC. State: `research_only_evidence_quality`.
20. **ginseng — PMID 42280421** — Ginseng components show microbiome, barrier, inflammatory, and neurotransmitter effects across disease models, but the review explicitly notes that most evidence is preclinical. Boundary: Mechanistic plausibility does not establish clinical efficacy; human validation is still required. State: `research_only_evidence_quality`.
21. **lutein — PMID 42449082** — A novel oil-based lutein formulation produced substantially higher Cmax and systemic exposure than a reference lutein formulation after one dose. Boundary: Both products were well tolerated, but this establishes formulation-dependent pharmacokinetics, not superior clinical efficacy. State: `research_only_formulation_firewall`.
22. **saw-palmetto-active-comparator — PMID 42280395** — Salvia miltiorrhiza extract outperformed saw palmetto on several symptom and quality-of-life endpoints; the study does not establish saw palmetto efficacy versus placebo. Boundary: Small pilot and active-comparator design make this comparative evidence rather than a direct efficacy trial for saw palmetto. State: `research_only_active_comparator`.
23. **probiotics — PMID 40815363** — Probiotics improved selected antioxidant/oxidative-stress markers, including total antioxidant capacity and malondialdehyde, while nitric oxide, 8-OHdG, IL-6, and TNF-alpha were not significantly changed. Boundary: Very high heterogeneity across several pooled markers and strain/population diversity limit class-wide claims. State: `research_only_category_firewall`.
24. **omega-3 — PMID 41506327** — Omega-3 interventions improved several oxygenation and inflammatory measures and were associated with shorter mechanical-ventilation duration and hospital stay. Boundary: Enteral and parenteral routes were pooled and the population is critically ill; findings cannot be generalized to ordinary oral omega-3 supplementation. State: `research_only_route_population_firewall`.
25. **probiotics — PMID 40983638** — Cognitive benefits varied by age and domain, with the strongest pooled signals in older adults for global cognition, processing speed, memory, and spatial ability; smaller domain-specific signals appeared in younger groups. Boundary: Age, strain, dose, and outcome heterogeneity prevent a universal probiotic-cognition claim; the review's apparent regimen optimum should not be treated as standardized dosing guidance. State: `research_only_category_firewall`.
