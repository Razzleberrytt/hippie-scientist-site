# Research enrichment Waves 2051–2075 — deep pass

Third deep pass of the Waves 2001–2500 program. The batch preserves direct nulls, observational boundaries, food-matrix distinctions, topical-route evidence, pediatric and surgical populations, and product-specific formulations.

## Quality notes
- Glycine/alcohol-use evidence is retained as a direct human null rather than discarded.
- Magnesium/depression and folic-acid uptake remain observational and cannot support treatment claims.
- Zinc mucositis efficacy is explicitly route-dependent; topical benefit does not validate oral zinc prophylaxis.
- Grass-finished lamb is treated as food-matrix omega-3 exposure, not a supplement trial.
- AREDS evidence remains tied to defined formulations and AMD disease stage.
- Alpha-synuclein nutraceutical review remains mechanistic/preclinical.

## Waves
- **2051 · magnesium · PMID 38812090** — Higher dietary magnesium intake was associated with lower depression risk, with an approximately 7% lower risk per 100 mg/day increment in pooled observational analyses. _State:_ `research_only_observational`
- **2052 · curcumin · PMID 40977174** — Curcumin improved pain more than corticosteroids at one week, but pain differences were not significant at later follow-ups and lesion outcomes were broadly comparable. _State:_ `research_only_formulation_firewall`
- **2053 · glycine · PMID 41189445** — Five days of oral glycine did not reduce alcohol self-administration, craving, or most subjective alcohol effects versus placebo. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2054 · vitamin-b12 · PMID 42144864** — Vitamin B12 concentrations tended to decline with longer metformin exposure; pooled deficiency prevalence was low in randomized trials but substantially higher and heterogeneous in observational cohorts. _State:_ `research_only_safety_or_interaction`
- **2055 · vitamin-d · PMID 42004696** — Vitamin D supplementation produced small improvements in semen volume, sperm concentration, progressive motility, and testosterone, but clinical pregnancy and live-birth outcomes were not significantly improved. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2056 · vitamin-d · PMID 41744130** — Preoperative vitamin D was associated with lower postoperative hypocalcemia and symptomatic hypocalcemia risk and a modestly shorter hospital stay. _State:_ `research_only_population_firewall`
- **2057 · folic-acid · PMID 42082233** — Multiparous women were less likely than first-time mothers to use folic acid before or around conception, with an even larger gap in some high-risk groups. _State:_ `research_only_observational`
- **2058 · omega-3-food-matrix · PMID 40690019** — Eating grass-finished lamb for four weeks increased plasma ALA, EPA, and DPA relative to concentrate-finished lamb, without clear changes in measured cardiovascular risk factors. _State:_ `research_only_food_matrix`
- **2059 · zinc · PMID 42346271** — Zinc reduced severe oral mucositis overall, but benefit was driven mainly by topical zinc mouthwash; systemic oral zinc was borderline and not reliably effective. _State:_ `research_only_route_firewall`
- **2060 · panax-ginseng · PMID 39428934** — The review summarizes ginsenoside chemistry and diverse reported pharmacologic and clinical effects but does not provide a focused quantitative efficacy synthesis for a single indication. _State:_ `research_only_evidence_quality`
- **2061 · areds-nutrients · PMID 41984164** — The review supports defined AREDS-type formulations for reducing progression from intermediate to advanced AMD, while evidence for individual nutrients such as lutein, zeaxanthin, zinc, and omega-3 varies by context. _State:_ `research_only_formulation_firewall`
- **2062 · nutraceuticals-alpha-synuclein · PMID 41683750** — Multiple nutraceutical classes may alter alpha-synuclein aggregation or related oxidative/inflammatory pathways, but clinical translation is limited by bioavailability, blood-brain-barrier exposure, and sparse human efficacy evidence. _State:_ `research_only_preclinical`
- **2063 · melatonin · PMID 42556907** — Adjunctive melatonin improved clinical attachment at several follow-ups and probing depth at earlier follow-ups, while bleeding on probing was not clearly improved; prediction intervals crossed the null for key periodontal outcomes. _State:_ `research_only_combination_firewall`
- **2064 · probiotics · PMID 41274661** — Probiotics did not significantly reduce anxiety overall; a small neurodevelopmental-disorder subgroup suggested possible benefit but was based on limited studies. _State:_ `research_only_category_firewall`
- **2065 · protein-supplementation · PMID 40312971** — Protein supplementation modestly favored body weight, muscle mass, fat-free mass, and fat mass, while BMI and some lean-mass measures were not significantly changed; certainty ranged from moderate to low. _State:_ `research_only_population_firewall`
- **2066 · omega-3 · PMID 41515289** — Omega-3-containing perioperative nutrition improved several nutritional, immune, and inflammatory markers and was associated with shorter hospitalization and fewer infectious or surgical complications, without a mortality benefit. _State:_ `research_only_population_firewall`
- **2067 · probiotics · PMID 41630163** — Probiotics modestly improved pooled global cognition and some cognitive domains and altered selected BDNF, inflammatory, and oxidative-stress markers, with substantial heterogeneity. _State:_ `research_only_category_firewall`
- **2068 · creatine · PMID 42765482** — Creatine added to resistance training improved upper- and lower-body strength and modestly increased lean mass while slightly reducing body-fat percentage; fat mass itself was not significantly reduced. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2069 · probiotics-synbiotics · PMID 41961895** — Probiotic/synbiotic interventions modestly lowered total cholesterol, LDL-C, and ALT but did not clearly improve BMI-z, waist circumference, triglycerides, HDL-C, or fasting glucose; pooled HOMA-IR unexpectedly increased. _State:_ `research_only_category_firewall`
- **2070 · probiotics · PMID 41328641** — Probiotics reduced ALT and showed a borderline AST reduction but did not clearly improve bilirubin, albumin, or GGT; subgroup results varied by region, sex distribution, and duration. _State:_ `research_only_category_firewall`
- **2071 · probiotics · PMID 40859213** — Probiotics did not significantly improve adverse pregnancy outcomes overall; a subgroup using Bifidobacterium plus Lactobacillus showed lower small-for-gestational-age risk. _State:_ `research_only_category_firewall`
- **2072 · probiotics · PMID 40669008** — Probiotics reduced pooled depressive-symptom scores, including HAM-D, but heterogeneity was high and multi-strain interventions appeared more consistently positive than single strains. _State:_ `research_only_category_firewall`
- **2073 · curcumin · PMID 41115177** — Topical curcumin delivered as mouthwash, ointment, or photodynamic adjunct reduced Candida burden and clinical signs in included trials. _State:_ `research_only_route_firewall`
- **2074 · n-acetylcysteine · PMID 42340796** — The review summarizes NAC's established mucolytic and acetaminophen-antidote uses plus broader antioxidant and anti-inflammatory research across organ systems, without providing a focused quantitative efficacy estimate for a new indication. _State:_ `research_only_evidence_quality`
- **2075 · liposomal-iron · PMID 42183880** — Liposomal iron produced broadly similar hematologic responses to conventional iron, with a later hemoglobin advantage at six months and fewer overall adverse events and constipation. _State:_ `research_only_formulation_firewall`
