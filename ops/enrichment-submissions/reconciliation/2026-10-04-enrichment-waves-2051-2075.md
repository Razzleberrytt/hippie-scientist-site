## Waves
- **2051 · nebulized-magnesium-postop-sore-throat · PMID 42410503** — Meta-analysis of 12 RCTs found preoperative nebulized magnesium sulfate substantially reduced postoperative sore throat through 24 hours without increasing hemodynamic risk, while hoarseness and cough were not significantly reduced; evidence is for a perioperative nebulized route, not oral magnesium. _State:_ `research_only_route_firewall`
- **2052 · rhodiola-injection-hfref-meta · PMID 40098619** — Meta-analysis of 25 RCTs reported improved ventricular remodeling, inflammatory markers, natriuretic peptides, and clinical effectiveness with standardized Rhodiola rosea injection in HFrEF without more adverse reactions; methodological shortcomings and the injectable standardized-drug formulation prevent generalization to oral Rhodiola supplements. _State:_ `research_only_route_firewall`
- **2053 · dietary-choline-betaine-t2d-meta · PMID 41496893** — Meta-analysis of observational studies found no statistically significant association between dietary choline or betaine intake and incident type 2 diabetes; certainty was very low and heterogeneity was substantial. _State:_ `research_only_observational`
- **2054 · caffeine-carbohydrate-hiie-meta · PMID 42356256** — Meta-analysis of 11 randomized crossover studies found caffeine plus carbohydrate improved high-intensity interval exercise performance versus placebo or carbohydrate controls, but not versus caffeine alone; publication bias was detected and overall certainty was low, suggesting caffeine may account for much of the effect. _State:_ `research_only_combination_firewall`
- **2055 · ckd-protein-cholecalciferol-bicarbonate-network-meta · PMID 42027563** — Network meta-analysis in CKD found possible intervention-specific advantages for cholecalciferol, sodium bicarbonate, and protein supplementation, but no significant differences among interventions for several muscle and metabolic outcomes; small heterogeneous studies limit comparative conclusions. _State:_ `research_only_category_firewall`
- **2056 · omega3-early-life-asthma-meta · PMID 42521226** — Meta-analysis of 12 RCTs found no overall reduction in childhood asthma or wheeze with early-life omega-3 supplementation; benefit appeared only in high-dose and high-EPA subgroups, so dose-response findings remain subgroup evidence rather than dosing guidance. _State:_ `research_only_population_firewall`
- **2057 · inositol-metformin-diet-pcos-rct · PMID 42517336** — Twelve-week RCT in 192 women with PCOS/PMOS found metformin, inositol, diet, and combined therapy all improved metabolic or endocrine outcomes, with the multimodal combination producing the largest changes; the design does not isolate the combination benefit to inositol. _State:_ `research_only_combination_firewall`
- **2058 · probiotics-immunotherapy-outcomes-meta · PMID 42434798** — Meta-analysis across microbiota-modulating exposures in cancer immunotherapy associated probiotic use with better response and survival, while antibiotics were associated with worse outcomes and FMT showed favorable response rates; heterogeneous intervention classes and likely observational evidence prevent a standalone probiotic efficacy claim. _State:_ `research_only_category_firewall`
- **2059 · capsaicin-burning-mouth-systematic-review · PMID 42499217** — Systematic review of four studies found topical or systemic capsaicin was associated with lower burning-mouth pain scores, but study risk of bias was moderate to critical and overall certainty was low or very low. _State:_ `research_only_route_firewall`
- **2060 · vitamin-c-gingival-hyperpigmentation-meta · PMID 42598683** — Systematic review/meta-analysis found vitamin C-based gingival depigmentation showed short-term improvement at one month, but no significant difference at three months and high heterogeneity; protocols and adjunctive approaches varied. _State:_ `research_only_route_firewall`
- **2061 · intermittent-daily-iron-folate-pregnancy-meta · PMID 39780191** — Meta-analysis of 22 pregnancy trials found daily iron-folate produced slightly higher hemoglobin overall, while intermittent regimens caused fewer gastrointestinal adverse effects and had better adherence; anemia incidence did not clearly differ. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2062 · vitamin-d-childhood-asthma-null-meta · PMID 42239713** — Meta-analysis of 12 RCTs found vitamin D supplementation did not improve lung function, asthma control scores, FeNO, or IgE in children with asthma; this is a direct condition-specific null finding. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2063 · chinese-herbal-icteric-hepatitis-meta · PMID 41568498** — Meta-analysis of 8 RCTs reported better liver-function and inflammatory outcomes when Chinese herbal medicine was added to Western medicine for icteric hepatitis, with no significant adverse-event difference; high risk of bias, heterogeneity, and multi-herb treatment prevent ingredient-level attribution. _State:_ `research_only_category_firewall`
- **2064 · ginkgo-terpene-antiplatelet-stroke-meta · PMID 40176902** — Meta-analysis of 27 RCTs reported better response, NIHSS, and Barthel outcomes when injectable ginkgo terpene lactone preparations were added to antiplatelet therapy for ischemic stroke, without more adverse events; combination therapy and injectable formulations prevent generalization to oral ginkgo. _State:_ `research_only_combination_firewall`
- **2065 · magnesium-infusion-neurosurgery-pain-meta · PMID 42417898** — Meta-analysis of 9 RCTs found perioperative magnesium sulfate infusion reduced postoperative pain scores and opioid consumption after spinal or neurologic surgery without major adverse events; evidence is route- and procedure-specific. _State:_ `research_only_route_firewall`
- **2066 · rhodiola-crenulata-altitude-meta · PMID 40584626** — Meta-analysis of 19 RCTs found Rhodiola crenulata extract was not clearly superior to Western medicine for oxygenation but outperformed placebo on oxygenation and clinical symptoms in acute high-altitude disease; evidence is population- and extract-specific with low-certainty concerns. _State:_ `research_only_formulation_firewall`
- **2067 · ginkgo-extract-hyperlipidemia-meta · PMID 42102021** — Meta-analysis of 10 RCTs found no broad lipid superiority for Ginkgo biloba extract versus proprietary Chinese or Western medicines; isolated HDL, age, and dose subgroup signals were heterogeneous and should not be converted into dosing guidance. _State:_ `research_only_formulation_firewall`
- **2068 · caffeine-female-volleyball-meta-null · PMID 42488219** — Systematic review/meta-analysis of five RCTs in 66 female volleyball players found no clear ergogenic benefit of acute caffeine for sport-specific, physical, or physiological performance outcomes; hand tremor, anxiety, and excessive stimulation were reported and certainty was very low. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2069 · protein-resistance-training-network-meta · PMID 42625626** — Bayesian network meta-analysis of 36 RCTs found whey ranked highest for lean-mass gains when combined with resistance training, but dose-response and age subgroup estimates were exploratory and certainty for comparisons was low to very low. _State:_ `research_only_category_firewall`

- **2071 · cinnamon-aromatherapy-cesarean-rct-null · PMID 42412022** — Randomized trial found cinnamon leaf-oil inhalation, alone or with higher oxygen concentration, did not reduce nausea or vomiting during the first six hours after cesarean section under spinal anesthesia; evidence is aromatherapy-route specific. _State:_ `research_only_route_firewall`
- **2072 · probiotics-fecal-scfa-meta-null · PMID 42437924** — Meta-analysis of randomized trials in healthy people found probiotic supplementation did not significantly change fecal butyrate, acetate, or propionate concentrations; this is a direct mechanistic null in healthy populations. _State:_ `reviewed_receipt_pending_final_admission`
- **2073 · ginger-oncology-supportive-care-review · PMID 42613614** — Systematic review of 24 cancer-supportive-care studies found heterogeneous ginger results across nausea/vomiting, xerostomia, nutrition, fatigue, and quality of life, including some studies reporting no benefit or disadvantage; formulations and endpoints varied widely. _State:_ `research_only_formulation_firewall`
- **2074 · vitamin-c-microneedling-melasma-meta · PMID 42602406** — Meta-analysis found no significant short-term efficacy difference between microneedling with vitamin C and microneedling with tranexamic acid for melasma; both appeared tolerable, but long-term safety and recurrence were not established and evidence is procedure/route-specific. _State:_ `research_only_route_firewall`
