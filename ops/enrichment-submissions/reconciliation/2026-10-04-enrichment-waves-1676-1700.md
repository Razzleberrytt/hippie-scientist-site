# Research enrichment Waves 1676–1700 — 2026-10-04

Eighth pass of the Waves 1501–2000 program, focused on bone health, osteoarthritis, sarcopenia, frailty, HMB, collagen and the distinction between surrogate markers and functional outcomes.

## Waves 1676–1700
1. **prunes-bone-health-older-men — PMID 42356242** — One-year RCT in 59 men age 55-80 found daily prunes did not improve total or lumbar-spine BMD beyond calcium/vitamin D control, although selected bone-turnover markers changed less than control.
2. **potassium-citrate-postmenopause-bone-turnover — PMID 42423994** — GRADE-assessed meta-analysis of five RCTs and 401 postmenopausal women found potassium citrate reduced urinary NTX at one month but did not consistently improve CTX or P1NP at later time points; trial-sequential evidence remained insufficient for key formation markers.
3. **greek-yogurt-postexercise-bone-markers — PMID 42561457** — Randomized crossover secondary analysis in 20 premenopausal women with obesity found Greek yogurt altered acute post-exercise bone-turnover markers versus an isoenergetic carbohydrate pudding; these short-term surrogate responses do not establish fracture or BMD benefit.
4. **synbiotic-protein-resistance-training-older-t2d — PMID 42616143** — Triple-blind RCT in older men with type 2 diabetes found resistance training improved physical outcomes across groups, with protein and synbiotic-plus-protein arms showing selected metabolic, inflammatory and strength differences; combined interventions prevent strain- or protein-only attribution.
5. **frailty-exercise-nutrition-systematic-review — PMID 42620771** — Systematic review of frail/pre-frail older adults found exercise consistently improved strength, gait and frailty status, while nutrition alone was modest/inconsistent and combined exercise-nutrition strategies generally outperformed nutrition-only approaches.
6. **low-carbohydrate-availability-bone-turnover — PMID 42670629** — Randomized crossover study in eight trained men found four days of low carbohydrate availability under energy balance increased beta-CTX and lowered IGF-1 without changing P1NP or several endocrine markers; tiny acute physiology study does not establish long-term bone harm.
7. **milk-postexercise-bone-markers — PMID 42673620** — Randomized crossover study in 20 young adults found milk and Greek-yogurt conditions altered acute post-exercise bone-status markers compared with carbohydrate or water; biomarker responses are not evidence of long-term BMD or fracture prevention.
8. **vegan-diet-older-adults-muscle — PMID 42680254** — Twelve-week RCT in 72 healthy adults age 65 and older found a self-selected vegan diet reduced protein intake and skeletal-muscle measures versus habitual omnivorous eating, while a resistance-exercise vegan arm tested whether training could mitigate losses; diet quality/protein context is critical.
9. **hmb-exercise-frailty-older-adults — PMID 42688609** — Twelve-week double-blind RCT in 136 frail older adults found calcium-HMB added to the same exercise program produced greater improvement in SPPB physical-function scores and selected secondary measures than placebo.
10. **nutrition-exercise-sarcopenia-vietnam — PMID 42797063** — Cluster RCT in 90 community-dwelling Vietnamese older adults with sarcopenia found exercise improved grip strength and gait speed versus education, while adding counseling plus a multinutrient supplement did not consistently outperform exercise alone on all outcomes.
11. **vitamin-d-omega3-exercise-bone-markers — PMID 39657964** — Three-year DO-HEALTH secondary analysis in 1,848 older adults found vitamin D and omega-3 alone did not significantly change sclerostin, P1NP or beta-CTX, while home strength exercise reduced sclerostin; combination effects were marker-specific.
12. **boswellia-knee-osteoarthritis-aflapin — PMID 39700461** — 180-day placebo-controlled RCT in 80 adults with mild-to-moderate knee osteoarthritis found proprietary standardized Boswellia extract improved pain/function scores and selected MRI/cartilage biomarkers; efficacy remains formulation-specific.
13. **multi-type-collagen-knee-osteoarthritis — PMID 39719905** — Eight-week double-blind RCT in 31 adults with grade 2-3 knee osteoarthritis found a type I/II/III collagen product improved several pain, quality-of-life and selected functional outcomes versus placebo; very small sample and proprietary blend limit certainty.
14. **hmb-older-women-muscle-quality-null — PMID 35288513** — Factorial RCT in older women with low muscle mass found HMB did not significantly add to resistance training for functional or compositional muscle quality; resistance training itself provided the clearer benefit.
15. **collagen-peptides-achilles-resistance-training — PMID 35403756** — 14-week RCT in 40 active young men found specific collagen peptides plus resistance training increased Achilles tendon cross-sectional area and muscle thickness more than training alone, but tendon stiffness and strength gains did not differ between groups.
16. **hmb-aging-clinical-umbrella-review — PMID 35818771** — Umbrella review of 15 systematic reviews found HMB evidence inconsistent for lean mass and strength and no review established a clear positive physical-function effect across aging/clinical populations.
17. **hmb-sarcopenia-rct — PMID 37248756** — Twelve-week double-blind RCT in older adults with sarcopenia found HMB plus resistance exercise improved grip strength, gait speed, chair-stand performance and muscle quality more than exercise plus placebo; small sample limits precision.
18. **collagen-peptides-active-adults-pain-function — PMID 37551682** — Longer-term double-blind RCT in active middle-aged adults found collagen peptides improved selected pain, activities-of-daily-living and mental/physical health measures in dose- and subgroup-dependent ways; effects were not uniform across all participants.
19. **collagen-whey-eccentric-recovery-null — PMID 37922892** — Three-week double-blind trial in fit men found replacing part of whey protein with collagen peptides did not improve muscle-damage markers or recovery of muscular performance versus the same total protein from whey alone.
20. **hmb-postacute-sarcopenia-rehabilitation — PMID 38171034** — Twelve-week double-blind trial in post-acute older adults with sarcopenia found calcium-HMB added to resistance rehabilitation improved selected SPPB domains and female grip strength, with some gains persisting at one year; findings are rehabilitation-context specific.
21. **bovine-collagen-typej-knee-oa — PMID 38235711** — Five-arm RCT in 100 adults with knee osteoarthritis found proprietary Type-J bovine collagen peptides improved WOMAC, quality-of-life and selected imaging/biomarker outcomes, but product-specific dose comparisons should not be generalized to all collagen.
22. **multicomponent-training-hmb-institutionalized-older-adults — PMID 38489992** — Cluster RCT in 72 institutionalized older adults found multicomponent exercise improved cognition, SPPB and muscle power, whereas HMB alone did not; adding HMB did not clearly outperform exercise for most endpoints.
23. **hmb-sarcopenia-meta — PMID 39071082** — Meta-analysis of six sarcopenia RCTs found HMB/HMB-rich supplements modestly improved grip strength but not gait speed, fat-free mass, skeletal-muscle index or several body-composition outcomes.
24. **collagen-connective-protein-synthesis-null — PMID 39086044** — One-week double-blind resistance-training study in 25 young men found collagen peptides raised collagen-derived amino acids but did not further increase muscle connective or myofibrillar protein-synthesis rates versus placebo.
25. **exercise-hmb-sarcopenia-meta — PMID 39360288** — Meta-analysis of five RCTs and 257 older adults with sarcopenia found exercise plus HMB improved gait speed versus exercise plus placebo but did not significantly improve skeletal-muscle index; benefits were outcome-specific.
