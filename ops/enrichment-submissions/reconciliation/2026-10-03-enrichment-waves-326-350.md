## Pass 2 — Waves 326–350
1. **curcumin — PMID 38857152** — Eight-RCT anxiety meta-analysis (567 participants); pooled symptom signal with very high heterogeneity, so direction and certainty remain separate.
2. **curcumin — PMID 40308636** — 2025 nine-RCT cognition meta-analysis (501 participants); global-cognition signal concentrated in longer studies and adults 60+, making duration and age explicit modifiers.
3. **nigella-sativa — PMID 40714301** — 2025 GRADE synthesis of 82 RCTs/5,026 participants; cardiometabolic biomarker signals across widely varying forms, doses and durations; surrogate-outcome boundary retained.
4. **nigella-sativa — PMID 41112345** — 2025 pediatric systematic review of 15 trials; no significant pooled side-effect increase but limited pediatric evidence and no established pediatric dose/formulation inference.
5. **ginger — PMID 41123858** — 2025 GRADE meta-analysis of 29 RCTs; CRP/TNF-alpha/IL-6 and oxidative-stress biomarker improvements, not direct proof of disease prevention or treatment.
6. **ginger — PMID 41101746** — 2025 36-RCT GRADE review; waist/body-fat and adiponectin signals while body weight/BMI were null—explicit counterweight to broad weight-loss claims.
7. **cranberry — PMID 41704730** — 2026 inflammation meta-analysis; overall CRP and IL-6 null with high heterogeneity plus subgroup increases in CRP/IL-6 in selected populations/formulations.
8. **milk-thistle — PMID 41199840** — 2025 network meta-analysis of 18 RCTs/2,177 breast-cancer patients; silymarin signal for grade >=2 radiation dermatitis but no superiority for severe grade >=3 dermatitis.
9. **cranberry — PMID 42137440** — 2026 ten-study synthesis; BMI signal with body-weight/waist nulls and overall liver-enzyme nulls, including subgroup AST increase requiring caution.
10. **fenugreek — PMID 42514462** — 2026 50-RCT systematic review; glycemic effects more consistent than body-weight/BMI effects, with substantial heterogeneity across seed powder, seed extract and leaf extract.
11. **panax-ginseng — PMID 40646515** — 2025 vascular and GRADE syntheses; FMD/NO/PWV and selected inflammatory/adiponectin signals, augmentation-index and anthropometric nulls retained.
12. **chamomile — PMID 40665590** — 2025 oral/mucosal-health systematic review; mucositis/pain/inflammation signals with antimicrobial and gingival-index nulls and limited trial counts.
13. **curcumin — PMID 40105038** — 2025 14-RCT meta-analysis in postmenopausal women; BP, antioxidant-capacity and vasomotor findings remain population-specific.
14. **curcumin — PMID 40579315** — 2025 cognition synthesis; global cognition null despite working-memory/processing-speed subgroup signals, plus higher adverse-event incidence versus controls.
15. **saffron — PMID 37484523** — Sleep systematic review of five trials/379 participants; preliminary duration/quality signals with a small evidence base.
16. **curcumin — PMID 41211694** — 2026 T2D-specific body-composition review; weight/BMI/waist evidence stays diabetes-population specific pending exact quantitative extraction.
17. **curcumin — PMID 42005325** — 2026 34-RCT dose-response meta-analysis in prediabetes/T2D; glucose/HbA1c/insulin/HOMA-IR signals with HOMA-B null and formulation/health-status modifiers.
18. **ginger — PMID 42178586** — 2026 umbrella review of 36 publications/296 trials/43,570 participants; CRP/IL-6 and oxidative-stress consistency is biomarker-level, not clinical-outcome proof.
19. **ashwagandha — PMID 42356302** — 2026 exercise meta-analysis of 13 RCTs/599 participants; objective performance evidence concentrated in young/athletic populations.
20. **magnesium — PMID 41000008** — 38-RCT BP meta-analysis/2,709 participants; modest SBP/DBP reductions with stronger hypertensive/hypomagnesemic subgroup signals, normotensive null and no clear dose-response.
21. **berberine — PMID 40740996** — Placebo-controlled metabolic-syndrome meta-analysis; TG/FPG/waist/LDL/TC signals with HDL and BP nulls, short-duration differences and no excess pooled safety signal.
22. **melatonin — PMID 41014554** — 2026 scoping review of 57 reviews/227 meta-analyses; most inactive-comparator syntheses favored melatonin but methodology quality and sleep-outcome definitions were highly variable.
23. **creatine — PMID 41558805** — 2026 psychiatric systematic review; five RCTs, all adjunctive and short term, with one strong MDD augmentation signal, other null trials, and hypomania/mania safety events in a small subset.
24. **ginkgo-biloba — PMID 41641880** — 2026 Cochrane review of 82 studies/10,613 participants; small low-certainty dementia signals, little/no effect in MCI, and overall adverse-event rates near placebo.
25. **zingiberaceae — PMID 42199754** — 2026 18-RCT cognition review spanning curcumin/turmeric/ginger; memory signal but executive/global/attention nulls and very uncertain heterogeneous evidence; category-level only.
