# Research enrichment Waves 1501–1525 — 2026-10-04

First pass of the Waves 1501–2000 deep-enrichment program, emphasizing sleep/circadian nutrition, melatonin, magnesium, microbiome formulations, and explicit null/safety boundaries.

## Operating rule
Every PMID was screened against prior enrichment waves, including the still-pending Waves 501–1000 branch. Direct entity evidence is separated from category, combination, formulation, population-specific and cross-therapy safety receipts. Null and contradictory findings remain first-class. No publication, recommendation, dosing, or governance promotion occurs from research receipts alone.

## Waves 1501–1525
1. **melatonin-cerebral-palsy-sleep — PMID 41369870** — Randomized double-blind trial in 120 children with cerebral palsy and persistent sleep problems found melatonin improved diary-derived total sleep time and sleep-onset latency over 12 weeks with few reported adverse effects; pediatric neurologic-population findings should not be generalized to healthy children or adults.
2. **melatonin-glaucoma-sleep — PMID 41386535** — Randomized double-masked crossover trial in 64 adults with advanced glaucoma found no significant melatonin-versus-placebo benefit for subjective or actigraphic sleep outcomes or vision-related quality of life despite within-time improvements in both groups; useful direct null sleep evidence in a visually impaired population.
3. **melatonin-advanced-cancer-qol — PMID 41421991** — Multicentre placebo-controlled trial in adults age 70 and older receiving systemic cancer treatment was stopped early for futility and found no significant global quality-of-life benefit from melatonin among compliant participants; supplementation appeared safe, but attrition and advanced-cancer context limit broader inference.
4. **dietary-supplements-sleep-meta — PMID 41470897** — Meta-analysis of 28 randomized trials pooled heterogeneous dietary-supplement interventions and reported modest improvements in PSQI, sleep efficiency, total sleep time, latency and WASO, but cross-ingredient category pooling prevents attributing the effect to any one nutrient or product.
5. **heat-treated-l-fermentum-ps150-sleep — PMID 41515133** — Eight-week RCT in 84 adults with poor sleep found no significant treatment-by-time effect on primary PSQI or ISI outcomes for heat-treated Limosilactobacillus fermentum PS150; exploratory biomarker and higher-insomnia-severity subgroup signals remain strain/formulation-specific and hypothesis-generating.
6. **chamomile-l-theanine-dysmenorrhea — PMID 41525193** — Small randomized double-blind study in 30 young women with primary dysmenorrhea found a chamomile-plus-L-theanine beverage improved several pain, mood and daytime-function measures versus baseline/control, but the multi-ingredient beverage and short exposure prevent attributing effects to either ingredient alone.
7. **walnut-peptide-theanine-sleep — PMID 41531275** — Translational study including an eight-week randomized human trial reported improved subjective sleep quality and sleep duration with a walnut-peptide plus theanine combination, alongside animal HPA/GABA findings; the human effect is combination-specific and cannot be assigned to theanine alone.
8. **mushroom-blend-stress-fatigue-sleep — PMID 41540766** — Twelve-week randomized placebo-controlled trial in 50 adults found a proprietary mushroom blend improved several anxiety, fatigue, depression and PSQI measures with changes in cortisol, ACTH and CRP; small sample and multicomponent proprietary formulation require a product-specific firewall.
9. **melatonin-preoperative-dental-anxiety-sleep — PMID 41578909** — Randomized double-blind trial in 48 third-molar surgery patients found a single preoperative sublingual melatonin dose did not improve perioperative anxiety, psychomotor performance, postoperative sleep or early post-traumatic-stress symptoms versus placebo; useful acute high-exposure null evidence.
10. **melatonin-hospital-delirium-restoration — PMID 41592826** — Double-blind RCT in 109 medically hospitalized adults age 65 and older was stopped early for futility and found no significant benefit from short-course melatonin for delirium incidence, sleep duration, 28-day mortality or readmission.
11. **melatonin-melatoninergic-icu-delirium — PMID 41602948** — Meta-analysis of 22 ICU randomized trials involving 3,706 patients found melatonin or melatonin-receptor-agonist strategies associated with lower delirium incidence, but no significant effect on delirium duration, ICU stay or mortality; intervention heterogeneity and mixed-agonist evidence constrain melatonin-only inference.
12. **melatonin-migraine-prophylaxis — PMID 41627537** — Nine-RCT meta-analysis involving 788 participants found melatonin improved several migraine-frequency, severity and sleep measures versus placebo, while active-comparator results favored amitriptyline on some efficacy endpoints; supports condition-specific adjunctive evidence, not general sleep or analgesic superiority.
13. **melatonin-tryptophan-5htp-neurodevelopmental-sleep — PMID 41643230** — Tiny randomized pilot in children under five with neurodevelopmental disorders comparing melatonin plus 5-HTP versus melatonin plus L-tryptophan found no significant between-group sleep differences; a within-group motor-activity signal in 5-HTP completers is hypothesis-generating amid high attrition.
14. **melatonin-pediatric-atopic-dermatitis — PMID 41647028** — Meta-analysis of three pediatric atopic-dermatitis RCTs found melatonin shortened sleep-onset latency and modestly lowered clinician-rated severity, but total sleep duration and IgE were not improved and the SCORAD change did not clearly exceed the minimal clinically important difference.
15. **magnesium-leg-cramps — PMID 41680812** — Three-arm randomized trial in adults age 50 to 85 with recurrent leg cramps found magnesium hydrochloride did not reduce cramp frequency versus placebo, while compression stockings did; direct null evidence against routine magnesium efficacy for this indication.
16. **melatonin-menopause-bone-sleep — PMID 41693954** — Systematic review and meta-analysis in menopausal women found suggestive femoral-neck bone-density benefit but no consistent improvements in sleep, menopausal symptoms, mood, sexual function, BMI or insulin; prevalent combination interventions and heterogeneity prevent isolating melatonin's independent effect.
17. **high-dose-melatonin-ocrelizumab-hepatic-safety — PMID 41706381** — Phase I/II adjunctive trial of very-high-dose melatonin with ocrelizumab in primary progressive multiple sclerosis was halted after grade 1 to 2 hypertransaminasemia in three participants; this is regimen-specific cross-therapy safety evidence, not a general low-dose melatonin risk estimate.
18. **melatonin-chronic-kidney-disease — PMID 41727206** — Meta-analysis of 10 chronic-kidney-disease RCTs found melatonin modestly increased HDL, reduced malondialdehyde and improved PSQI, while triglycerides, total and LDL cholesterol and CRP were null; very high heterogeneity for several outcomes limits certainty.
19. **melatonin-anxious-children-insomnia — PMID 41781909** — Randomized blinded three-arm study in 72 children with anxiety disorders and insomnia found both melatonin and clonidine improved sleep-habit scores versus placebo with no significant difference between active treatments; pediatric psychiatric context and short follow-up remain explicit.
20. **melatonin-adult-atopic-dermatitis — PMID 41787942** — Four-week randomized placebo-controlled trial in 80 adults with mild-to-moderate atopic dermatitis found melatonin improved SCORAD, pruritus, sleep and quality-of-life measures but not pain; short duration and disease-specific context limit broader claims.
21. **low-dose-melatonin-climacteric-shift-workers — PMID 41841489** — Small randomized trial in 46 climacteric female nurses working fixed shifts found low-dose melatonin reduced symptom scores and improved reported sleep quality on days off without changing total sleep duration or reproductive hormones; sample size and shift-specific timing matter.
22. **magnesium-fluoxetine-depression — PMID 41916937** — Six-week study in 40 adults receiving fluoxetine reported greater HDRS improvement with adjunctive magnesium than fluoxetine alone, while serum serotonin did not change; adjunctive design, small sample and allocation method prevent magnesium-monotherapy attribution.
23. **tryptophan-rich-diet-breast-cancer-sleep — PMID 42033145** — Six-week randomized dietary trial in 50 women receiving breast-cancer chemotherapy found a tryptophan-rich meal plan increased actigraphic total sleep time at selected weeks and reduced WASO at selected weeks, without deep-sleep or fatigue benefit; food-pattern intervention cannot be treated as isolated tryptophan-supplement evidence.
24. **melatonin-caffeine-athlete-performance — PMID 42124027** — Four-condition crossover trial in 14 trained men found nocturnal melatonin did not improve measured sleep, while morning caffeine drove shuttle-run performance benefits; combination biomarker changes cannot establish an independent melatonin performance effect.
25. **melatoninergic-icu-delirium-dose-response — PMID 42310686** — Updated 24-RCT dose-response meta-analysis found melatoninergic agonists reduced ICU delirium incidence, with a similar signal in the melatonin subgroup, but mortality and adverse-event rates were unchanged; pooled duration and dose modeling is research context and must not be converted into dosing guidance.
