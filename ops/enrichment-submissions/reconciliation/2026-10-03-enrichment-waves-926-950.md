# Research enrichment Waves 926–950 — 2026-10-03

Eighteenth pass of the Waves 501–1000 program.

## Waves 926–950
1. **endometriosis-supplements — PMID 40288359** — Nine-RCT/545-participant review of supplements for endometriosis-associated pain; pelvic pain, dysmenorrhea and dyspareunia were not significantly improved overall, with trial trustworthiness concerns.
2. **osteoarthritis-supplement-network — PMID 40425393** — Network meta-analysis of dietary supplements for osteoarthritis; rankings are indirect, product-specific and not evidence of category-wide superiority.
3. **antioxidant-supplements-endometriosis — PMID 38221738** — Systematic review/meta-analysis of antioxidant supplementation for dysmenorrhea/endometriosis-related pain; heterogeneous compounds and small trials require intervention-specific interpretation.
4. **endometriosis-dietary-interventions — PMID 39358652** — Systematic review of 11 RCTs/716 participants; positive dietary/supplement signals are limited by heterogeneous interventions and moderate-to-high risk of bias.
5. **vitamin-d — PMID 42443965** — 2026 NAFLD/MASLD meta-analysis; ALT and alkaline-phosphatase signals with AST/GGT nulls, preserving disease and surrogate-enzyme boundaries.
6. **migraine-supplement-network — PMID 42707444** — 2026 network meta-analysis of 14 RCTs/791 adults across vitamins, magnesium, CoQ10, probiotics and combinations; rankings remain exploratory and indirect.
7. **fatty-acid-supplements — PMID 39823374** — 2025 migraine systematic review/meta-analysis; fatty-acid composition, intervention duration and migraine outcomes must remain explicit.
8. **green-tea — PMID 33737989** — Systematic review/meta-analysis of green-tea interventions for oral/periodontal health; evidence was insufficient for first-line dental use.
9. **vitamin-c — PMID 39201285** — 2024 systematic review of vitamin C in periodontal/oral health; high heterogeneity across 16 studies/17,853 participants limits uniform clinical claims.
10. **probiotics — PMID 39407177** — 2024 network meta-analysis of probiotics adjunctive to nonsurgical periodontal therapy; strain/product rankings are indirect and outcome-specific.
11. **collagen-supplements — PMID 37432180** — 26-RCT/1,721-participant skin-aging meta-analysis; hydration/elasticity/wrinkle signals coexist with design, publication and funding concerns.
12. **hydrolyzed-collagen — PMID 33742704** — 19-trial/1,125-participant skin meta-analysis; hydration/elasticity signals are historical positive evidence with product and trial-quality limitations.
13. **green-tea — PMID 33729563** — Systematic review/meta-analysis of green tea as an adjunct in periodontal therapy; adjunctive context and local/systemic preparations remain distinct.
14. **collagen-supplements — PMID 40826844** — Ten-RCT/646-participant collagen-based skin meta-analysis; hydration and elasticity signals with predominantly female samples and moderate heterogeneity.
15. **vitamin-c — PMID 39768921** — 2024 systematic review of ascorbic acid and periodontal/oral outcomes; dietary exposure, supplementation, periodontal status and biomarker outcomes remain separated.
16. **probiotics — PMID 40186219** — 2025 meta-analysis of 24 studies in gingivitis/periodontitis; effects differed by condition and outcome, and several gingivitis measures were not significantly improved.
17. **probiotics — PMID 34391565** — Historical 24-RCT adjunctive periodontal meta-analysis; short-term CAL/PPD/BOP signals were not consistently maintained at six months.
18. **hydrolyzed-collagen — PMID 42342959** — 2026 systematic review of 25 skin RCTs; efficacy and safety were inconsistent across products, doses, durations and measurement instruments.
19. **probiotics — PMID 41969406** — 2026 umbrella review of 11 oral-health probiotic meta-analyses; inherited methodological quality and strain/outcome heterogeneity prevent a generic oral-health claim.
20. **ginkgo-biloba — PMID 20236541** — Historical dementia meta-analysis of nine trials/2,372 participants; cognitive signal with activities-of-daily-living null, retained against newer evidence.
21. **chamomile — PMID 38784853** — 2024 systematic review of 10 oral-chamomile anxiety trials; most trials reported improvement but heterogeneity and limited pooled certainty require caution.
22. **hyaluronic-acid-oral — PMID 40911749** — 2025 seven-RCT oral hyaluronic-acid meta-analysis; hydration, elasticity and wrinkle-depth signals with firmness, wrinkle-volume and transepidermal-water-loss nulls.
23. **riboflavin — PMID 41769676** — 2026 migraine systematic review/dose-response meta-analysis of 12 trials/749 participants; frequency and duration improved with very high heterogeneity; study exposure is not a dosing recommendation.
24. **migraine-supplements — PMID 39404918** — Twenty-two-trial GRADE meta-analysis across magnesium, CoQ10, riboflavin, ALA, probiotics, vitamin D and omega-3; supplement-specific effects and omega-3 nulls retained.
25. **n-acetylcysteine — PMID 42510611** — 2026 endometriosis systematic review; non-postoperative pain/NSAID/endometrioma signals contrasted with a postoperative randomized comparison showing no added benefit, while fertility evidence remained preliminary.
