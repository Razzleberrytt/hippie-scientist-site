# Research enrichment Waves 801–825 — 2026-10-03

Thirteenth pass of the Waves 501–1000 program.

## Waves 801–825
1. **taurine — PMID 40852891** — 2025 meta-analysis of 23 randomized trials/308 participants; acute single-dose taurine showed a small-to-moderate overall exercise-performance signal, but prediction intervals often crossed null and several subgroup effects weakened after sensitivity analyses.
2. **taurine — PMID 35769396** — Five-RCT/209-participant diabetes meta-analysis; HbA1c, fasting glucose and HOMA-IR signals with insulin/triglyceride effects not statistically significant; diabetes-specific surrogate evidence.
3. **omega-3 — PMID 37222574** — RCT meta-analysis in metabolic syndrome/related cardiovascular disease; TG, BP and several inflammatory-marker signals with LDL and multiple endothelial markers null.
4. **bcaa — PMID 41655197** — 2026 systematic review of BCAA/leucine in endurance sport; biochemical changes must be separated from functional performance, recovery and adverse-effect outcomes.
5. **bcaa — PMID 41346907** — 2025 systematic review of 22 RCTs/511 participants; possible post-exercise soreness benefit but inconsistent strength, endurance and body-composition evidence.
6. **beta-alanine — PMID 41971372** — 2026 multilevel RCT meta-analysis found no ergogenic benefit for repeated-sprint ability, an important counterweight to broader high-intensity performance claims.
7. **hmb — PMID 39797501** — 2025 umbrella review of 11 meta-analyses; small muscle-mass/strength/fat-free-mass signals with fat-mass/body-weight nulls and mixed review quality.
8. **dietary-nitrate — PMID 35580578** — 123-study/1,705-participant exercise meta-analysis; very small overall performance benefit with important formulation, exercise-task and participant modifiers.
9. **leucine-rich-protein — PMID 35777246** — Six-RCT/699-participant meta-analysis in sarcopenic older adults; strength/mass/performance signals remain older-adult and combined-protein-formulation specific.
10. **protein-supplements — PMID 38830547** — 2024 review of 38 articles in older adults; small muscle-mass benefit in community-dwelling populations, while dose/frequency/timing did not significantly modify the effect.
11. **whey-protein — PMID 39303495** — 2024 RCT meta-analysis in older adults; muscle strength, physical performance and body-composition outcomes are training- and outcome-specific rather than uniformly positive.
12. **protein-supplements — PMID 40200135** — 2025 systematic review/meta-analysis in physically inactive older adults; muscle mass, strength and physical-performance evidence remains population- and co-intervention specific.
13. **beetroot-nitrate — PMID 42523624** — 2026 38-study/703-participant meta-analysis; beetroot juice produced a small VO2max/VO2peak signal with moderate heterogeneity, not a universal endurance-performance effect.
14. **carbohydrate — PMID 37449467** — 136-study endurance meta-analysis; acute carbohydrate feeding improved performance overall, with larger effects in time-to-exhaustion and longer-duration tasks; sport-context evidence only.
15. **hmb — PMID 39999663** — 2025 sarcopenia-specific RCT meta-analysis; HMB effects on muscle mass, strength and performance remain diagnosis-specific and should not be generalized to healthy adults.
16. **collagen-peptides — PMID 39060741** — 2024 systematic review/meta-analysis of collagen peptides with longer-term training; strength, musculotendinous remodeling, recovery and body-composition findings remain exercise- and formulation-specific.
17. **protein-supplements — PMID 39955964** — 2025 systematic review of protein alone or with exercise in adults >=65 with sarcopenia/frailty; co-intervention and frailty-status boundaries retained.
18. **sodium-bicarbonate — PMID 42781544** — 2026 team-sport meta-analysis of acute/short-term sodium bicarbonate; high-intensity intermittent performance and physiologic responses are protocol-specific and tolerability matters.
19. **caffeine — PMID 42255518** — 2026 20-study/320-participant HIIE meta-analysis; small performance improvement with lactate/glucose changes and protocol/rest-work modifiers.
20. **creatine — PMID 42027564** — 2026 39-trial meta-analysis in healthy young men; body-composition, strength and performance effects differed by resistance-training versus non-resistance context.
21. **beta-alanine — PMID 39724872** — Systematic review in older adults; exercise-capacity/strength/function evidence is sparse and mixed, limiting generalization from younger athletic populations.
22. **caffeine — PMID 42591203** — 2026 women-specific three-level meta-analysis of acute caffeine before resistance exercise; sex-specific performance domains and prediction intervals retained.
23. **caffeine — PMID 41601907** — 2025 systematic review/meta-analysis of acute caffeine and cycling performance; training status, age and dose were evaluated as moderators rather than assuming a uniform effect.
24. **caffeine — PMID 42445506** — 2026 cycling time-trial meta-analysis; acute caffeine effects were evaluated separately for short- and long-duration tasks, preserving task-duration dependence.
25. **beta-alanine — PMID 42370349** — 2026 women-specific systematic review/meta-analysis; time-to-exhaustion, VO2, power, anaerobic performance and body-fat outcomes remain sex- and endpoint-specific.
