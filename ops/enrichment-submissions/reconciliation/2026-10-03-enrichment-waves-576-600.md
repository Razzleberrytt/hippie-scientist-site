# Research enrichment Waves 576–600 — 2026-10-03

Fourth pass of the Waves 501–1000 program.

## Waves 576–600
1. **omega-3 — PMID 37028202** — Systematic review/meta-analysis of long-chain omega-3 for anxiety/depression in adults; depression signals were stronger in EPA-enriched interventions while anxiety evidence was sparse and heterogeneity/publication-bias concerns remain.
2. **omega-3 — PMID 37726108** — Large dose-response depression meta-analysis; symptom reduction was concentrated in people with existing depression, while prevention of incident depression in general populations was not established.
3. **l-citrulline-watermelon — PMID 40789388** — 15-RCT/415-participant review in middle-aged/older adults; blood-pressure effects combine isolated L-citrulline and watermelon interventions, so compound-versus-food-matrix identity is preserved.
4. **l-citrulline — PMID 41797970** — Six-RCT/162-participant meta-analysis in cold exposure; blood-pressure findings are environmental-context specific and not a general resting-BP claim.
5. **magnesium — PMID 38213402** — Seven-RCT/325-participant meta-analysis in depressive disorders; mood evidence is diagnosis-specific and limited by small trials/formulation differences.
6. **vitamin-e — PMID 41063466** — RCT meta-analysis of adiponectin/leptin; overall effects were limited/null with selected NAFLD or longer-duration subgroup signals, making subgroup dependence explicit.
7. **lycopene — PMID 40507061** — Systematic review of randomized human trials examining lycopene intake and mental-health outcomes; evidence remains heterogeneous and outcome-specific.
8. **iron — PMID 29626044** — Systematic review/meta-analysis in non-anemic iron-deficient adults; subjective fatigue improved while objective physical-capacity outcomes did not, preserving symptom-versus-performance distinction.
9. **omega-3 — PMID 40049535** — Recent depression meta-analysis; depressive-symptom signal did not translate consistently into response/remission or adverse-event superiority, and any proposed EPA/dose pattern remains evidence metadata rather than a dosing recommendation.
10. **magnesium — PMID 38817505** — Systematic review of magnesium for self-reported anxiety and sleep; most included studies reported some benefit, but samples, formulations and outcome methods were small/heterogeneous.
11. **l-carnitine — PMID 38101999** — Blood-pressure meta-analysis; overall systolic/diastolic effects were null, with an obesity-related DBP subgroup signal that should not be generalized.
12. **l-arginine — PMID 42520885** — 2026 meta-analysis of ambulatory blood pressure from five RCTs/202 participants; ambulatory-BP evidence remains small and protocol-specific.
13. **vitamin-a — PMID 38816049** — Systematic review of 120 randomized prevention trials involving roughly 1.67 million participants; clinically important outcomes and safety vary strongly by population, baseline status and prevention context.
14. **l-carnitine — PMID 37656233** — 48-RCT/3,255-participant meta-analysis of inflammatory and oxidative-stress biomarkers; biomarker effects are surrogate evidence and do not establish disease modification.
15. **l-carnitine — PMID 38594107** — Dose-response meta-analysis in type 2 diabetes; glycemic/cardiometabolic findings remain T2D- and endpoint-specific.
16. **l-carnitine — PMID 40470390** — Cardiac-surgery meta-analysis of postoperative outcomes; perioperative evidence cannot be inherited by routine outpatient supplementation.
17. **l-carnitine — PMID 36704801** — 41-RCT/~2,900-participant glycemic meta-analysis with high heterogeneity; pooled biomarker changes require population/formulation boundaries.
18. **l-arginine — PMID 34967840** — Randomized-trial blood-pressure meta-analysis; modest pooled effects are heterogeneous and should remain distinct from ambulatory-BP evidence.
19. **l-carnitine — PMID 32932644** — Metabolic-syndrome related meta-analysis; cardiometabolic biomarker evidence remains surrogate and phenotype-specific.
20. **omega-3 — PMID 33806078** — Systematic review/meta-analysis of omega-3 for prevention/treatment of depression; prevention and treatment strata remain separate.
21. **omega-3 — PMID 31647041** — Large randomized-trial synthesis found little or no preventive effect on depression/anxiety in people without baseline disorders—important counterweight to broad mental-health claims.
22. **vitamin-d — PMID 41674721** — Updated randomized-trial synthesis in steatotic liver disease; hepatic/metabolic outcomes stay disease-specific and separate from generic vitamin-D efficacy.
23. **zinc — PMID 39377022** — 2024 systematic review of eight human trials on sleep; most adult studies suggested better sleep quality, while sleep-disorder and nonadult findings were inconsistent and more trials are needed.
24. **selenium — PMID 38243784** — 2024 GRADE meta-analysis of 35 Hashimoto-thyroiditis studies; TSH/TPOAb/MDA signals with several thyroid hormones, TGAb, thyroid volume and immune markers null; autoimmune-thyroid population boundary retained.
25. **vitamin-c — PMID 41784995** — 2026 umbrella review of six meta-analyses/17 RCTs; modest systolic-BP signal, overall DBP null with diabetes subgroup signal, and low certainty of evidence.
