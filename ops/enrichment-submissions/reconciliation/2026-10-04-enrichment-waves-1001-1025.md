# Research enrichment Waves 1001–1025 — 2026-10-04

First pass of the Waves 1001–1500 deep-enrichment program.

## Operating rule
Every PMID was screened against prior enrichment waves. Human randomized evidence and systematic reviews are separated from formulation-, combination-, category-, route-, identity-, and evidence-quality-only receipts. Null findings remain first-class. No publication, recommendation, dosing, or governance promotion occurs from research receipts alone.

## Waves 1001–1025
1. **glycine — PMID 42687500** — 2026 scoping review mapped 22 trials of supplemental glycine/collagen; all three glycine sleep trials reported at least one favorable sleep outcome, but collagen-to-glycine mediation has not been directly tested and efficacy evidence remains small/mechanistic.
2. **phosphatidylserine-dha — PMID 42345137** — Eight-week randomized placebo-controlled trial in 45 medication-naive children with ADHD/subthreshold symptoms; the higher-dose phosphatidylserine plus DHA arm improved selected attention, impulsivity, hyperactivity, reaction-time and Stroop outcomes, but DHA/formulation and pediatric-context boundaries prevent phosphatidylserine-only attribution.
3. **phosphatidylserine-taurine-caffeine — PMID 42654170** — Four-arm randomized triple-masked trial in 97 professional male football players; taurine+caffeine+phosphatidylserine combinations improved selected cognitive, psychomotor, sprint and locomotor outcomes, but the multi-ingredient design prevents phosphatidylserine-specific attribution and fatigue-resistance change was not established from baseline.
4. **l-theanine — PMID 42761839** — 2026 human-evidence review summarizes small cognition, stress/anxiety and sleep-quality signals from randomized evidence, often stronger with caffeine; dose-range summaries are study exposures rather than consumer dosing guidance and heterogeneity/interaction uncertainty remain.
5. **hmb — PMID 41934514** — Thirteen-RCT meta-analysis in 561 adults age 50+ found no additional benefit of HMB plus resistance training for fat mass, muscle mass or muscle strength; direct null counterweight to routine HMB adjunct claims in exercisable older adults.
6. **whey-hmb-strength-training — PMID 42700006** — Seven-RCT sarcopenia meta-analysis found strength-training plus supplementation improved several functional/body-composition outcomes overall, but subgroup benefit was clearer for whey protein than HMB; combined-intervention evidence cannot be assigned to HMB alone.
7. **amino-acid-sarcopenia — PMID 41540398** — Nine-RCT/496-participant sarcopenia meta-analysis found low-to-very-low-certainty improvements in handgrip, gait speed and physical-performance measures when amino-acid-based supplements were added to resistance training, with no clear muscle-mass benefit and substantial intervention heterogeneity.
8. **non-antibiotic-ruti-prevention — PMID 42133003** — Systematic review of recurrent-UTI prevention in women found therapy-specific differences: methenamine and vaginal estrogen had stronger support, while D-mannose and cranberry showed mixed efficacy; category evidence must not be collapsed into a generic supplement claim.
9. **fenugreek — PMID 42558777** — Sixteen-study/1,171-participant meta-analysis found no significant overall effect of fenugreek on ALT, AST or ALP; an ALP subgroup signal at higher studied exposures does not overturn the overall liver-enzyme null and is not dosing guidance.
10. **fenugreek — PMID 42688439** — Thirteen randomized placebo-controlled trials in adult men found a small total-testosterone signal but no stable free-testosterone benefit; all primary evidence was very low certainty and the authors rejected clinical/commercial testosterone-boosting claims.
11. **hydrolyzed-collagen — PMID 42666580** — Systematic review of six human studies found cautiously positive adjunctive signals for pain/function/imaging after tendon or ligament injury/repair, but evidence was sparse, heterogeneous, low certainty and many oral interventions were multicomponent.
12. **astaxanthin — PMID 41710469** — Seven-RCT/188-participant meta-analysis found lower advanced oxidation protein products after exercise but no significant changes in several other oxidative, inflammatory or muscle-injury biomarkers; broader physiological/performance benefit remains unconfirmed.
13. **astaxanthin — PMID 42197030** — Twenty-four-RCT meta-analysis found lower creatine kinase and LDH with astaxanthin but no significant improvement in VO2max, time-trial performance or maximal workload/power; recovery-biomarker signals remain distinct from performance efficacy.
14. **algae-exercise-supplements — PMID 42075102** — Twenty-two-RCT/822-participant review pooled Spirulina, Chlorella, brown-algal polysaccharides and astaxanthin; selected aerobic/recovery signals were species-, dose- and training-context dependent, so the evidence remains category-level rather than transferable across algae products.
15. **citicoline-phosphatidylserine — PMID 42538853** — Systematic review of 11 controlled trials across ADHD and adult mental disorders found heterogeneous, inconsistent cognitive effects; current evidence did not support citicoline or phosphatidylserine-containing compounds as established cognitive enhancers.
16. **spirulina — PMID 41873104** — Eight-RCT meta-analysis found a small reduction in CRP with Spirulina but no significant TNF-alpha or IL-6 effect, with significant heterogeneity; this supports a narrow biomarker statement rather than a broad anti-inflammatory claim.
17. **zinc — PMID 42594341** — Eighteen-RCT/1,023-participant diabetes meta-analysis found improved insulin-resistance and selected inflammatory/oxidative biomarkers, while LDL cholesterol increased modestly; disease-specific surrogate outcomes and mixed lipid direction remain explicit.
18. **probiotics — PMID 41501907** — Twelve-RCT/3,350-participant meta-analysis in healthy working adults found a small pooled improvement in subclinical depression/anxiety/stress symptoms and lower cortisol, with no CRP effect and insufficient sleep evidence; strain/dose/duration heterogeneity remains substantial.
19. **probiotics — PMID 41573044** — Six-RCT/341-participant meta-analysis of probiotic monotherapy in unmedicated depression found a small symptom benefit, but sensitivity analysis excluding industry-funded/adjunctive-agent studies became nonsignificant; formulation and funding-bias boundaries are first-class.
20. **probiotics — PMID 42032711** — Forty-one-RCT meta-analysis found a modest cortisol reduction with probiotics, but heterogeneity was extreme and GRADE certainty very low; subgroup strain patterns and exposure ranges are not optimization guidance.
21. **gut-microbiota-interventions-mdd — PMID 42611838** — Network meta-analysis of 38 RCTs in major depression found signals across microbiota-based therapies, prebiotics and probiotic categories, but certainty was low-to-moderate and network rankings do not establish superiority of a specific strain or product.
22. **probiotics — PMID 42778037** — Ten-RCT/486-participant meta-analysis in depression found lower CRP and TNF-alpha with probiotic supplementation, borderline IL-1beta change, and null IL-6/IL-10; biomarker findings remain distinct from antidepressant efficacy and strain-specific attribution.
23. **resveratrol — PMID 41455817** — Twenty-three-RCT/1,005-participant meta-analysis in overweight/obesity found no significant effect on body weight, BMI, fat mass, body-fat percentage, fat-free mass, waist-hip ratio, adiponectin or leptin, with only waist circumference significantly reduced overall.
24. **resveratrol — PMID 41987155** — Umbrella review of 45 systematic reviews covering 129 associations found only four high-certainty positive associations, including waist circumference and selected lipid/BP outcomes in specific populations; most health claims remain lower-certainty and population-specific.
25. **vitamin-c — PMID 41687812** — Twelve double-blind placebo-controlled exercise trials found no significant IL-6 or malondialdehyde effect and a CRP reduction based on only two small trials; overall recovery biomarker evidence was low-to-very-low certainty and hypothesis-generating.
