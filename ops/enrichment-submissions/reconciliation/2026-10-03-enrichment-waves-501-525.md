# Research enrichment Waves 501–525 — 2026-10-03

First pass of the 500-finding Waves 501–1000 program.

## Operating rule
Fresh literature is resolved to exact PubMed identities and screened against all prior waves before staging. Null findings, safety, funding, study quality, population, formulation, route and category boundaries remain first-class. No runtime/public admission until Wave 1000.

## Waves 501–525
1. **chamomile — PMID 41394268** — 2025 systematic review/meta-analysis of eight human musculoskeletal-pain studies; possible analgesic effect with substantial heterogeneity and no proof of equivalence to standard analgesics.
2. **chamomile — PMID 41767834** — 2026 PCOS review combining four human and four animal studies; human pooled lipids and testosterone were null, so clinical-feature signals must not be conflated with hormonal/lipid efficacy.
3. **medicinal-plants-allergic-rhinitis — PMID 38603736** — 2024 review of 29 RCTs/1,879 participants; low-certainty nasal-symptom signals and no clear difference from antihistamines in limited comparisons; category-level only.
4. **ashwagandha — PMID 39348746** — 2024 nine-RCT/558-participant stress-anxiety meta-analysis; PSS/Hamilton-anxiety/cortisol signals with mild-moderate adverse events and uncertain long-term safety.
5. **collagen-supplements — PMID 40324552** — 2025 23-RCT skin-aging meta-analysis; overall hydration/elasticity/wrinkle signals disappeared in non-industry-funded/high-quality subgroups, making funding and study quality first-class modifiers.
6. **hibiscus-sabdariffa — PMID 38878905** — 2024 six-RCT/339-participant obesity meta-analysis; body weight, BMI and waist outcomes were null overall, providing a direct counterweight to weight-loss claims.
7. **cinnamon — PMID 40611215** — 2025 49-study GRADE meta-analysis of cardiovascular/metabolic risk factors; multiple pooled surrogate signals with broad heterogeneity and no clinical-event inference.
8. **pomegranate — PMID 39499092** — 2025 RCT meta-analysis of insulin resistance/sensitivity; overall evidence did not establish meaningful HOMA-IR/fasting-insulin benefit across pomegranate products.
9. **green-tea — PMID 40160899** — 2025 38-RCT synthesis; oxidative-stress markers improved while CRP, IL-6 and TNF-alpha were null, separating antioxidant biomarker effects from broad anti-inflammatory claims.
10. **pomegranate — PMID 40617711** — 2025 dose-response meta-analysis of 53 RCTs/2,306 participants; cardiometabolic risk-factor evidence remains endpoint- and preparation-specific.
11. **green-tea — PMID 40497293** — 2025 GRADE dose-response meta-analysis of adult blood-pressure RCTs; blood-pressure effects require dose/duration/baseline-status context and should not imply cardiovascular-event prevention.
12. **collagen-supplements — PMID 41809116** — 2026 umbrella review of 16 systematic reviews/113 RCTs/7,983 participants; skin and musculoskeletal signals were more consistent than oral/cardiometabolic outcomes; category-level heterogeneity retained.
13. **creatine — PMID 39070254** — 2024 16-RCT/492-participant cognition meta-analysis; memory/attention/processing-speed signals with global cognition and executive-function nulls and low-to-moderate certainty.
14. **coenzyme-q10 — PMID 41457257** — 2025 systematic review/meta-analysis of 24 exercise studies; circulating CoQ10 increased but exercise-performance effects were limited/inconsistent—useful counterweight to ergogenic marketing.
15. **coenzyme-q10 — PMID 40833470** — 2025 seven-small-trial mood meta-analysis; some depression-scale signals, BDI and anxiety nulls, with small samples and clinical-population heterogeneity.
16. **pro-pre-synbiotics — PMID 41310510** — 2025 RCT meta-analysis of probiotics/prebiotics/synbiotics for anxiety, depression and sleep; intervention class and strain/product identity remain distinct.
17. **plant-based-ibs-therapies — PMID 42572206** — 2026 60-study/6,329-participant IBS review; peppermint-oil and Chinese-herbal signals coexist with limited-quality evidence and insufficient data for several plants.
18. **coenzyme-q10 — PMID 39904656** — 2025 T2D-specific systematic review/meta-analysis of CoQ10 effects on lipids and blood pressure; disease-population and endpoint boundaries retained.
19. **omega-3 — PMID 38468309** — 2024 24-RCT/9,660-participant dose-response cognition meta-analysis in adults >=40 without dementia; domain/time/dose trends should not become a universal cognitive-enhancement claim.
20. **l-theanine — PMID 41227106** — 2025 systematic review/meta-analysis of placebo-controlled RCTs in healthy adults; cognitive effects remain domain-specific and the authors characterize evidence as promising but not conclusive.
21. **olive-leaf-extract — PMID 38287654** — 2024 systematic review of 12 RCTs/703 adults; cardiometabolic effects remain short-duration and pure-OLE specific.
22. **artichoke — PMID 35949557** — Seven-RCT/472-participant BP meta-analysis; modest SBP/DBP reductions with nonlinear dose/duration signals and subgroup dependence.
23. **saffron — PMID 40414301** — 2025 12-week DB-RCT in 202 adults with subclinical depressive symptoms; modest depression benefit, most secondary outcomes null, sleep benefit only exploratory in a more-impaired subgroup; proprietary Affron identity retained.
24. **saffron — PMID 38913392** — 2024 meta-analysis comparing saffron with SSRIs; depression/anxiety differences were nonsignificant while adverse events were fewer with saffron; direct-comparator evidence does not establish broad interchangeability.
25. **saffron — PMID 35325766** — Sleep meta-analysis across heterogeneous populations; PSQI/ISI/RSQ signals with limited long-term safety evidence and no generic dose recommendation.
