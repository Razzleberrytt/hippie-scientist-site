# Research enrichment Waves 501–525 — 2026-10-03

Pass 1 of the third 200-finding deep-enrichment program (Waves 501–700).

## Why this pass exists

The canonical claim corpus still contained high-quality human randomized source identities that had never entered the enrichment-wave ledger. This pass reconciles 25 of those historical nodes so the site can distinguish **what the early human evidence actually showed** from what newer syntheses later concluded.

These papers are **not treated as fresh evidence** merely because they are new to the enrichment ledger. Current-consensus upgrades, safety updates, contradictions, retractions and newer systematic reviews are handled in later passes.

## Operating rule

No entity creation, governance override, publication/indexing promotion, recommendation promotion, monetization mutation or consumer-dose inference is authorized. Preserve exact intervention/formulation, population, comparator, duration, endpoints, null/mixed findings and adverse-event context. Full admission/runtime/build/site-health/Atomic/CI validation is deferred until Wave 700.
