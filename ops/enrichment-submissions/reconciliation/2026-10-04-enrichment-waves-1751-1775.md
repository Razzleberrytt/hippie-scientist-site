# Research enrichment Waves 1751–1775 — 2026-10-04

Tenth pass of the Waves 1501–2000 deep-enrichment program.

## Operating rule
Every PMID was screened against the cumulative branch index and source-verified. Retractions, preprints, observational evidence, product/food formulations, combinations and null results remain explicitly fenced; no publication, recommendation, dosing, or governance promotion occurs during authoring.

## Waves 1751–1775
1. **omega3-allergic-rhinitis-adjunct — PMID 41420581** — Small double-blind placebo-controlled trial in persistent allergic rhinitis found high-dose EPA+DHA was safe but did not improve nasal symptoms or quality of life beyond intranasal steroid plus immunotherapy; adjunctive null evidence.
2. **omega3-migraine-pain-path-analysis — PMID 41515121** — Secondary path analysis of randomized diet-trial data linked higher achieved blood EPA/DHA—especially EPA—to lower pain interference in migraine; mediation/association analysis does not replace the parent randomized outcome interpretation.
3. **omega3-fish-oil-atrial-fibrillation-ukbiobank — PMID 41368832** — Large UK Biobank observational analysis found higher circulating omega-3 associated with lower incident AF while self-reported fish-oil use was neutral after continuous age adjustment; observational biomarker and supplement-use associations should not be treated as randomized safety evidence.
4. **omega3-psychological-distress-retracted — PMID 41461240** — PubMed marks this omega-3 psychological-distress randomized trial as a retracted publication; its efficacy claims must not be used for evidence grading or consumer guidance and are retained only for retraction/citation-integrity tracking.
5. **beetroot-cad-fatty-acid-oxidative-markers — PMID 41473738** — Secondary analysis of a randomized CAD trial found beetroot-containing interventions changed selected fatty-acid ratios and oxidative markers, with larger shifts in a beetroot-plus-vitamin-C arm; secondary biomarker and combination effects limit clinical interpretation.
6. **beetroot-vs-creatine-resistance-exercise — PMID 41048226** — Small double-blind crossover study in 11 active men found both beetroot and creatine improved selected velocity measures versus placebo, while beetroot produced distinct heart-rate, oxygenation and HRV effects; acute mechanistic performance study only.
7. **l-carnitine-pediatric-cardiac-surgery-liver — PMID 41377238** — Double-blind randomized pediatric cardiac-surgery trial found preoperative oral L-carnitine was associated with faster normalization of selected liver markers after surgery; perioperative setting and biochemical endpoints limit generalization.
8. **collagen-vitamin-zinc-telogen-effluvium — PMID 41346549** — Large pre-post study of a branded hydrolyzed-collagen/B-vitamin/zinc supplement reported improved hair parameters after one month with persistence after washout, but absence of a concurrent control and multi-ingredient product design prevent causal ingredient claims.
9. **zinc-status-autism-meta — PMID 41368180** — Meta-analysis of 25 case-control studies found lower blood/plasma zinc in children and adolescents with autism while hair and urinary zinc were not different; very high heterogeneity and observational design preclude supplementation-efficacy conclusions.
10. **selenium-stroke-meta — PMID 41426996** — Systematic review/meta-analysis linked higher circulating and dietary selenium with lower ischemic-stroke and stroke-mortality risk, but the evidence is observational and heterogeneous; no supplementation effect can be inferred.
11. **excess-zinc-metabolic-risk-review — PMID 41459237** — Systematic review describes potential metabolic and cardiovascular harms of excessive zinc exposure and emphasizes dose-dependent risk, interaction and toxicity uncertainty; useful safety context rather than a quantified safe-threshold source.
12. **zinc-biofortified-staples-status-review — PMID 41475016** — Systematic review of seven trials found zinc-biofortified staple crops produced inconsistent improvements in zinc biomarkers, with methodological issues including inflammation adjustment and cooking losses; food-biofortification evidence should not be generalized to supplements.
13. **multi-nutrient-fortified-salt-meta — PMID 41352484** — Systematic review/meta-analysis of double- and multi-fortified salt interventions found iron-containing formulations improved hemoglobin overall, but nutrient combinations and food-fortification context prevent translation into single-supplement efficacy.
14. **fortified-preschool-milk-micronutrients — PMID 41356825** — Open-label randomized study in Filipino preschool children found fortified milk increased nutrient intake and some growth measures but did not significantly improve the primary micronutrient-status endpoints versus habitual diet after 16 weeks.
15. **micronutrient-deficiency-t2d-glycemic-meta — PMID 41402393** — Systematic review/meta-analysis found weak associations between several micronutrient deficiencies and glycemic-control indices in type 2 diabetes, with stronger signals for magnesium and vitamin D in selected measures; association does not prove supplementation benefit.
16. **maternal-selenium-birth-outcomes-women-first — PMID 41461273** — Secondary analysis of the multicountry Women First trial found no significant association between maternal selenium status and birth size, prematurity or small-for-gestational-age outcomes and no clear selenium increase from the study supplement.
17. **pediatric-chronic-disease-supplements-review — PMID 41613379** — Systematic review of 13 pediatric chronic-disease studies found probiotics, fatty acids, vitamins, minerals and other supplements often changed biomarkers but produced inconsistent clinical benefits; DMSA raised specific safety concerns.
18. **cardiovascular-supplements-review — PMID 41669146** — Narrative review synthesizes omega-3, CoQ10, magnesium and selenium across cardiovascular mechanisms and trials; useful as an evidence map but not independent proof of efficacy because conclusions depend on underlying heterogeneous trials.
19. **nonviable-l-salivarius-preschool-oral-microbiome — PMID 41906504** — Randomized pilot in preschool children found two weeks of nonviable L. salivarius HM6 altered plaque S. mutans/Lactobacillus patterns, but microbiologic outcomes and short follow-up do not establish caries prevention.
20. **b-longum-lactulose-synbiotic-presenteeism — PMID 41909908** — Single-arm open-label study found a B. longum BB536 plus lactulose fermented-milk product improved bowel-movement and presenteeism/well-being scores alongside microbiome/metabolite changes; no control group prevents causal inference.
21. **postbiotics-chronic-disease-review — PMID 41852428** — Narrative review describes immune, barrier and antioxidant mechanisms proposed for postbiotics across chronic diseases but emphasizes the need for component-specific human trials; largely mechanistic/heterogeneous evidence.
22. **personalized-supplementation-inflammation-observational — PMID 41465826** — Twelve-week observational program in 48 adults associated personalized multi-agent protocols with reductions in CRP, homocysteine and other biomarkers, but interventions included multiple nutrients, hormones and peptides without a control group.
23. **antioxidants-periodontal-disease-review — PMID 41440327** — Review found inconsistent observational and adjunctive-trial evidence for vitamins/minerals in periodontal disease because of small samples, mixed nutrient combinations, variable doses and heterogeneous outcome measurement.
24. **omega3-atrial-fibrillation-preprint-meta — PMID 41445624** — Preprint meta-analysis of 34 omega-3 RCTs reported increased AF risk mainly in high-CVD-risk populations receiving higher EPA/DHA doses; because the source is a preprint, it remains research-only pending peer-reviewed confirmation.
25. **flaxseed-migraine-rct — PMID 41478596** — RCT in 68 migraine patients found eight weeks of flaxseed powder improved headache severity, sleep and headache-related quality of life but not headache frequency or duration; small single trial requires replication.
