# Research enrichment Waves 1176–1200 — 2026-10-04

Eighth pass of the Waves 1001–1500 program, broadening into inflammation, joint health, metabolic disease, gut microbiome, migraine-adjacent evidence, and mood while preserving null findings and evidence boundaries.

## Waves 1176–1200
1. **ginger-cardiometabolic — PMID 42492258** — Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled crossover trial in 35 healthy adults found six weeks of dried ginger increased ex-vivo cholesterol efflux capacity and improved oxidative-stress response, while conventional lipids, body weight and blood pressure were unchanged; mechanistic cardiovascular signals should not be equated with clinical event reduction.
2. **glucosamine-tmd — PMID 42539490** — Systematic review and meta-analysis of seven TMD RCTs found oral glucosamine did not provide a clinically important pain reduction in temporomandibular joint osteoarthritis or other TMD, although selected quality-of-life or functional outcomes may improve; adverse-event certainty was very low.
3. **glucosamine-knee-osteoarthritis — PMID 42643227** — Umbrella review of 19 reviews found glucosamine produced small VAS pain and joint-space-narrowing signals but no significant WOMAC pain or functional improvement; most source reviews were low or critically low quality and clinical importance remained uncertain.
4. **nutritional-supplements-obesity-inflammation — PMID 42739108** — Network meta-analysis of 60 RCTs in adults with overweight or obesity found outcome-specific inflammatory biomarker signals across green tea extract, curcumin, synbiotics, zinc and probiotic-plus-omega-3 combinations; indirect rankings and mixed interventions do not support a single universal supplement recommendation.
5. **boswellia-knee-osteoarthritis — PMID 42760675** — Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in 60 adults with mild-to-moderate knee osteoarthritis found a standardized water-dispersible Boswellia extract improved WOMAC, pain, stiffness and hs-CRP over eight weeks; results are proprietary-formulation specific and need independent replication.
6. **curcumin-t2d-inflammation — PMID 42797011** — Meta-analysis of randomized trials in type 2 diabetes found curcumin/curcuminoids lowered hs-CRP and TNF-alpha on average, but the evidence base was small, TNF-alpha heterogeneity was substantial and several reporting/unit issues increased uncertainty.
7. **curcumin-knee-osteoarthritis-mechanism — PMID 42825904** — Secondary molecular analysis from a prior 30-person placebo-controlled knee-osteoarthritis trial found nano-micelle curcumin reduced T-bet expression and correlated with CD8-positive T-cell changes, while GATA3 was unchanged; mechanistic secondary data do not establish independent clinical efficacy.
8. **vitamin-d-depression-youth — PMID 42489742** — Partially randomized patient-preference trial in vitamin-D-insufficient young people with major depression found no overall randomized-group improvement in depression versus placebo, although anxiety improved and moderate-to-severe subgroups showed exploratory signals; preference and subgroup analyses require caution.
9. **multistrain-probiotic-early-cognitive-impairment — PMID 42663491** — Double-blind placebo-controlled 24-week trial in 87 older adults with early cognitive impairment found multistrain probiotics lowered plasma pTau181 and selectively preserved visuospatial/certain clinical cognition measures, while MMSE, verbal memory and executive outcomes showed no significant between-group differences.
10. **bacillus-coagulans-healthy-microbiome — PMID 42675742** — Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in 48 healthy adults found Bacillus coagulans BCP92 caused no major microbiome-composition shift, with only subtle taxa and short-chain-fatty-acid changes; useful evidence against overstating microbiome remodeling in healthy users.
11. **probiotics-post-colonoscopy-uc — PMID 42678923** — Single-center open-label randomized study after colonoscopy found probiotic supplementation was associated with faster microbiota-diversity recovery, particularly in ulcerative colitis, but outcomes were primarily microbiome/predicted-pathway measures and open-label design limits clinical inference.
12. **lactobacillus-plantarum-ckd — PMID 42697907** — Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled six-month trial in 72 adults with stage 3-4 chronic kidney disease reported improved eGFR trajectory with Lactobacillus plantarum versus placebo, while urinary indoxyl sulfate did not differ; single modest-size trial requires replication before disease-modifying claims.
13. **probiotic-combat-sports-weight-cut — PMID 42702994** — Four-week placebo-controlled study in 32 combat-sport athletes during weight reduction found probiotic supplementation was associated with larger body-fat and selected lipid/fatty-acid changes; concurrent weight cutting and small sample size make probiotic-specific performance or weight-loss claims inappropriate.
14. **probiotics-copd — PMID 42705044** — Systematic review and meta-analysis of 13 RCTs/920 participants with COPD found modest pooled pulmonary-function and inflammatory-marker improvements with probiotics, but strain/regimen heterogeneity and disease-specific populations limit class-wide generalization.
15. **probiotic-juvenile-idiopathic-arthritis — PMID 42720258** — Multicenter randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in 44 children with juvenile idiopathic arthritis found VSL#3 did not improve the primary disease-activity response and increased fecal TLR4 agonist activity, while microbiota diversity, permeability and systemic cytokines were unchanged; important null/safety-mechanism counterweight.
16. **multistrain-probiotic-overweight-women — PMID 42731127** — Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled 12-week trial in 102 women with obesity found a three-strain probiotic altered selected endocrine/appetite markers, body-composition measures, mood scores and gut taxa; multi-strain formulation and multiple endpoints require formulation-specific interpretation.
17. **lactiplantibacillus-plantarum-exercise-recovery — PMID 42738954** — Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in 48 healthy men found four weeks of Lactiplantibacillus plantarum GKK1 attenuated selected post-exercise performance decrements and muscle-damage/inflammatory markers after a muscle-damage protocol; strain-specific and short-term exercise context remain explicit.
18. **psychobiotic-stress-gut-brain-axis — PMID 42739081** — Systematic review of 29 adult RCTs found lifestyle and psychobiotic interventions sometimes improved stress-related outcomes alongside microbiome changes, but no consistent taxonomic or diversity signature explained favorable responses; mechanistic microbiome narratives remain heterogeneous.
19. **weissella-confusa-overweight — PMID 42750456** — Twelve-week randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial in overweight adults found Weissella confusa WIKIM51 improved triglycerides and selected abdominal-fat measures, supported by preclinical GLP-1 findings; strain-specific clinical outcomes should not be generalized to probiotics as a class.
20. **gut-microbiota-interventions-osteoarthritis — PMID 42761148** — Meta-analysis of 18 osteoarthritis RCTs found gut-microbiota-targeted interventions reduced several pain/function and inflammatory outcomes, while stiffness, body weight, knee flexion, COMP and IL-6 were null; all trials had at least some risk-of-bias concerns and interventions were heterogeneous.
21. **probiotics-type-2-diabetes — PMID 42765182** — Meta-analysis of nine placebo-controlled studies/649 adults with type 2 diabetes reported pooled reductions in fasting glucose, HbA1c and insulin with probiotics; strain differences, selection criteria and adjunct-to-standard-care context prevent a generic treatment claim.
22. **probiotics-pcos-metabolic — PMID 42797074** — Secondary analysis of a randomized double-blind placebo-controlled PCOS trial found 12 weeks of a nine-strain probiotic produced no significant between-group improvement in glucose homeostasis, HOMA-IR or lipid profile; useful metabolic null evidence despite limited precision and attrition.
23. **coq10-paclitaxel-toxicity — PMID 42401951** — Open-label randomized trial in 60 breast-cancer patients receiving weekly paclitaxel found adjunctive CoQ10 was associated with less grade-2-or-higher neuropathy, delayed neuropathy onset and selected fatigue, insomnia, hematologic and cardiac-function signals; open-label supportive-care context requires confirmation.
24. **knee-osteoarthritis-comparative-adjuvants — PMID 42558409** — Network meta-analysis of 23 knee-osteoarthritis RCTs compared celecoxib, probiotics, metformin, krill oil and glucosamine/chondroitin; auxiliary-intervention rankings were mostly low or very-low certainty and indirect, so SUCRA ordering should not be treated as head-to-head superiority.
25. **lactobacillus-h-pylori-eradication — PMID 42791348** — Meta-analysis of 32 RCTs/4,010 patients found Lactobacillus added to H. pylori eradication therapy modestly increased eradication success and reduced adverse events overall, with benefits varying by antibiotic regimen and age; evidence concerns adjunctive treatment, not probiotic monotherapy.
