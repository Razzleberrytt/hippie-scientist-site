## Disposition

All 50 entries are **research/reconciliation candidates** only. No canonical profile, consumer dose, recommendation, public claim, or indexability state was changed.

## Integrity controls

- Preserve intervention identity, botanical species, plant part, extract/formulation, route, population, comparator, duration, and endpoint.
- Do not infer consumer dosing from trial regimens.
- Separate biomarkers and surrogate endpoints from patient-important outcomes.
- Retain null, mixed, and low-certainty evidence.
- Keep combination and adjunctive interventions as such.
- Keep safety and interaction evidence independently visible.
