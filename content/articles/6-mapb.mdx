# 6-MAPB

## Quick answer

**6-MAPB is an N-methyl benzofuran entactogen related to 6-APB and 5-MAPB.**

The chemistry, metabolism, and analytical detection are reasonably described.

Direct human toxicology is not.

## Metabolism

A dedicated metabolism study found that 6-MAPB undergoes N-demethylation and that **6-APB is one of its metabolites**.

The study also developed methods to distinguish 6-MAPB from the positional isomer 5-MAPB.

## Pharmacologic context

Benzofuran entactogens alter serotonin, norepinephrine, and dopamine signaling.

Class-level acute concerns include tachycardia, hypertension, hyperthermia, agitation, anxiety, serotonergic toxicity, and seizures in severe cases.

Those are class-level concerns, not measured 6-MAPB incidence rates.

## Human evidence gap

The direct 6-MAPB clinical literature is far thinner than the evidence for 5-APB, 6-APB, or 5-MAPB.

That means online potency, duration, and “MDMA-like” comparisons should not be presented as established clinical facts.

## Bottom line

6-MAPB belongs in the evidence map because it is chemically real and analytically characterized.

Its page should also make clear that human safety remains poorly characterized.
