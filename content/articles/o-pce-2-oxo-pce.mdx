# O-PCE (2-Oxo-PCE)

## Quick answer

**O-PCE, also called 2-oxo-PCE, is an arylcyclohexylamine dissociative with unusually strong human poisoning evidence for an RC.**

A 2017 cluster produced 56 analytically confirmed hospital cases.

## What the 56-case cluster showed

Among patients whose exposure involved only 2-oxo-PCE, common findings included:

- impaired consciousness;
- confusion;
- abnormal behavior;
- hypertension;
- tachycardia.

Convulsions occurred in 16% of the overall cases, and three patients required intensive care.

## Why this dataset matters

Many RC articles depend on a handful of case reports.

Here, almost half the patients had no other drug detected, giving much better compound-specific clinical context than is available for many dissociatives.

## Routine-screen limitation

The bedside ketamine immunoassay used in the investigation did not cross-react with 2-oxo-PCE.

A negative ketamine test therefore did not exclude a closely related dissociative exposure.

## Bottom line

O-PCE has a well-documented capacity to produce severe dissociative poisoning, including loss of consciousness and seizures. It should not be treated as merely a longer-lasting version of ketamine.
