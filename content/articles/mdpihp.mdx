# MDPiHP

## Quick answer

**MDPiHP is an emerging pyrovalerone-family synthetic cathinone with very limited direct human research.**

It is now documented in forensic investigations, but controlled human pharmacokinetics and dose-response data are not established.

## What is actually known?

A 2026 forensic case series included MDPiHP alongside alpha-PHP, alpha-PiHP and MDPHP.

That confirms real-world exposure but does not justify importing every effect or potency estimate from those related compounds.

## Class-level concerns

Pyrovalerone stimulants as a group can be associated with strong dopaminergic reinforcement, agitation, paranoia, sleep deprivation, cardiovascular stress and compulsive redosing.

For MDPiHP specifically, incidence and thresholds remain poorly defined.

## Bottom line

MDPiHP belongs on the site because it is appearing in real forensic work and online RC markets—but the page should remain visibly sparse where the science is sparse.
