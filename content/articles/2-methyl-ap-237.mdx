# 2-Methyl-AP-237

> **Overdose warning:** Treat profound sedation, inability to wake or slowed/abnormal breathing after an unknown opioid-like product as an emergency. Call 911 and use naloxone if available.

## Quick answer

**2-Methyl-AP-237 is a novel synthetic opioid in the cinnamylpiperazine family.** Related compounds include AP-238.

A 2022 forensic/pharmacology study confirmed mu-opioid receptor activity and described fatal postmortem cases.

## What the forensic study adds

Researchers characterized 2-methyl-AP-237 and AP-238 in vitro and analyzed authentic forensic specimens.

Multiple postmortem cases involved 2-methyl-AP-237, while AP-238 also appeared in fatal casework.

Polysubstance exposure was common, so blood concentrations should not be converted into a simple lethal threshold.

## Why the chemical family matters

These drugs are structurally different from fentanyl, nitazenes and U-47700.

They can still converge on the same clinically important endpoint: mu-opioid receptor activation and respiratory depression.

## Product identity and testing

Novel opioids may not appear on routine immunoassays. Confirmation often requires targeted mass spectrometry.

## Bottom line

2-Methyl-AP-237 is not just an obscure RC name. Published receptor and postmortem evidence establishes it as a genuine novel opioid with potentially fatal real-world exposures.
