# N-Desethyl Protonitazene

> **Overdose warning:** N-desethyl protonitazene is an opioid agonist. Suspected respiratory depression should be treated as an opioid emergency.

## Quick answer

**N-desethyl protonitazene is an active metabolite of protonitazene that has also appeared in drug material as a compound in its own right.**

That dual role makes toxicology interpretation unusually tricky.

## Metabolite vs separately consumed drug

Finding N-desethyl protonitazene in a biological sample can reflect metabolism of protonitazene.

But CFSRE also confirmed it directly in a drug material.

That means future toxicology cannot always assume the compound arose only through metabolism.

## Pharmacology

Available in-vitro data confirm mu-opioid receptor agonism.

As with other nitazenes, receptor potency should not be translated directly into a human exposure recommendation.

## Evidence limits

At the time of the CFSRE monograph, N-desethyl protonitazene had not been found in toxicology cases without protonitazene.

That sharply limits attempts to describe an independent human toxicity profile.

## Bottom line

N-desethyl protonitazene is both a metabolite and an emerging market drug.

Its main safety significance is potent opioid pharmacology combined with difficult forensic interpretation.
