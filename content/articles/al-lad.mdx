# AL-LAD

## Quick answer

**AL-LAD is a lysergamide psychedelic and structural analogue of LSD.**

It has been analytically characterized in research-chemical products and produces LSD-like 5-HT2A-linked behavior in animal models.

Direct controlled human safety studies remain lacking.

## What the laboratory evidence shows

Researchers characterized AL-LAD in powder and blotter material and confirmed psychedelic-like behavioral activity in mice.

The same research program later showed that 1P-AL-LAD converts to AL-LAD in human liver preparations, supporting a prodrug relationship.

## What we do not know

The scientific literature does not provide a robust controlled human dataset for pharmacokinetics, cardiovascular changes, rare psychiatric complications, seizure risk, interaction risk, or toxic concentrations.

## Why online descriptions are not enough

AL-LAD is commonly described online as more visual, shorter, or psychologically easier than LSD.

Those themes may be useful for identifying community questions. They are not controlled comparative evidence.

## Bottom line

AL-LAD is a well-characterized molecule with clear psychedelic pharmacology and weak human safety characterization.

Its page should remain evidence-limited rather than turning community reputation into clinical fact.
