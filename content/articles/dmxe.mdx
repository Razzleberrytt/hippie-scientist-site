# DMXE (Deoxymethoxetamine)

## Quick answer

**DMXE, or deoxymethoxetamine, is an arylcyclohexylamine dissociative related to methoxetamine (MXE).**

It has been analytically identified in illicit products, and laboratory studies confirm NMDA-receptor antagonism.

The major limitation is that **direct controlled human toxicology is extremely sparse**.

## What has actually been confirmed?

Japanese analytical researchers identified DMXE, MXPr, and MXiPr in illegal products.

That confirms real market presence.

A separate receptor study found that DMXE and related methoxetamine derivatives potently block NMDA receptors.

## What has not been established?

The published evidence does not provide a mature human dataset for:

- safe exposure;
- toxic concentrations;
- dependence risk;
- interaction risk;
- reliable duration;
- comparison with ketamine or MXE.

Those gaps should remain visible.

## Why online familiarity can be misleading

DMXE is widely discussed in dissociative communities, often with detailed descriptions of duration and subjective character.

Those reports can help identify what readers want to know.

They do not replace controlled pharmacology or analytically confirmed clinical cases.

## Bottom line

DMXE is a chemically and analytically real RC dissociative with confirmed NMDA-antagonist pharmacology.

Its human safety profile remains poorly characterized, so precision claims about potency or safety are not justified.
