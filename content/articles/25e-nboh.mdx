# 25E-NBOH

## Quick answer

**25E-NBOH is an emerging psychedelic N-benzyl phenethylamine with very thin human evidence.**

A severe published poisoning involved serotonin-syndrome features and loss of consciousness. Crucially, the powder had been labeled as a different compound.

## The mislabeling case

A 23-year-old presented with severe intoxication after using powder labeled “25I-NBOH.”

High-resolution toxicology found **25E-NBOH and MDPHP**; 25I-NBOH was specifically tested for and not detected.

This is an unusually clear demonstration that an RC label can be chemically wrong.

## New 2026 metabolism research

A 2026 study mapped extensive 25E-NBOH metabolism in experimental systems and explicitly noted that pharmacology and toxicology remain insufficiently characterized.

That work improves analytical detection more than it answers human-safety questions.

## Bottom line

25E-NBOH is a high-uncertainty psychedelic where the strongest human lesson currently comes from a poisoning case involving a mislabeled product.
