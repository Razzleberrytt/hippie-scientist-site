# 3-HO-PCP

## Quick answer

**3-HO-PCP is a phencyclidine analogue in the arylcyclohexylamine dissociative family.**

It has been identified in law-enforcement seizures and authentic human forensic specimens, and researchers have mapped metabolites that can help confirm exposure.

The direct human clinical-toxicity literature is still sparse.

## What the human forensic evidence shows

A 2020 study quantified 3-HO-PCP in human samples and characterized its metabolism using authentic specimens plus pooled human hepatocytes.

That work improves identification and interpretation of exposure but does not establish a safe dose or a reliable toxic concentration.

## Pharmacology and class-level concerns

PCP analogues primarily act through NMDA-receptor antagonism and can produce profound dissociation, impaired judgment and psychotomimetic effects.

Class-level severe concerns include:

- dangerous confusion;
- agitation;
- loss of coordination;
- impaired pain perception;
- injuries;
- loss of consciousness;
- cardiovascular stress.

Those risks should be clearly separated from 3-HO-PCP-specific clinical incidence, which remains poorly quantified.

## Reddit signal

3-HO-PCP is regularly discussed online alongside 3-MeO-PCP, O-PCE and 3-MeO-PCE.

Descriptions of it as unusually potent, warm, opioid-like or manageable are subjective reports and cannot establish receptor balance, potency equivalence or safety.

## Bottom line

3-HO-PCP is analytically real and present in human forensic casework, but its clinical evidence base remains thin. The correct safety stance is high uncertainty—not invented precision.
