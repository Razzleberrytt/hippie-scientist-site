# alpha-PHP

## Quick answer

**alpha-PHP is a pyrovalerone-family synthetic cathinone closely related to alpha-PiHP.**

Published reviews document fatal intoxications and recurring cardiac, psychiatric, and neurologic adverse effects.

## The isomer problem

alpha-PHP and alpha-PiHP are structural isomers.

A 2026 forensic study emphasized that laboratories need methods capable of separating them to avoid misclassification.

That means even toxicology names can be less certain than they appear when the analytical method cannot resolve the isomers.

## Main toxicity pattern

Reported concerns across the human and forensic literature include:

- severe agitation;
- paranoia or psychosis;
- tachycardia;
- hypertension;
- neurologic toxicity;
- fatal intoxication.

## Bottom line

alpha-PHP is a high-risk pyrovalerone stimulant with documented fatalities and a real forensic identity problem.
