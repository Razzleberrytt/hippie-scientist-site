# N-Pyrrolidino Protonitazene

> **Overdose warning:** This is an opioid-class NPS. Unresponsiveness or slow, irregular, or absent breathing is an emergency; call 911 and give naloxone if available.

## Quick answer

**N-pyrrolidino protonitazene is an emerging nitazene synthetic opioid.**

CFSRE reported it in U.S. toxicology specimens and seized material beginning in 2023.

## What is actually known?

CFSRE documented multiple toxicology detections from several U.S. states and identified the compound in drug material.

Laboratory data indicate strong mu-opioid receptor agonism.

Those findings establish a high-overdose-risk pharmacologic class.

They do not define a human safe dose or a reliable potency conversion.

## Why potency headlines need caution

In-vitro receptor assays can show that a drug is extremely active.

Human overdose risk also depends on absorption, metabolism, formulation, tolerance, co-drugs, and actual product concentration.

For that reason this page does not convert receptor potency into a human dosing chart.

## Naloxone

Naloxone is appropriate for suspected opioid overdose regardless of whether the exact nitazene is known.

Emergency monitoring is still needed because duration and co-exposures may be unpredictable.

## Bottom line

N-pyrrolidino protonitazene is a real U.S. forensic opioid signal with strong laboratory opioid activity and sparse direct human pharmacology.
