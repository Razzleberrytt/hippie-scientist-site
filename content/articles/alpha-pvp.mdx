# alpha-PVP (Flakka)

## Quick answer

**alpha-PVP is a potent pyrovalerone synthetic cathinone with a well-documented severe stimulant toxidrome.**

It became widely known under the street name “Flakka,” but its clinical importance is better described through confirmed toxicology than through sensational media stories.

## The 42-case confirmed series

A Swedish study examined 42 analytically confirmed cases in which alpha-PVP was the only stimulant detected.

Common findings included:

- tachycardia;
- agitation;
- hypertension;
- hallucinations;
- delirium.

Severe poisonings and fatalities occurred.

## Concentration does not equal severity

Serum and urine concentrations varied widely, and concentration alone did not reliably map to clinical severity.

That is important because tolerance, timing, co-drugs, and individual physiology can alter presentation.

## Severe stimulant toxicity

Major risks include:

- extreme agitation;
- psychosis or delirium;
- dangerous overheating;
- seizures;
- cardiovascular collapse;
- injury during severe behavioral disturbance.

## Bottom line

alpha-PVP is one of the better-characterized high-risk synthetic cathinones.

The evidence supports a severe sympathomimetic and psychiatric toxicity profile, not internet potency charts.
