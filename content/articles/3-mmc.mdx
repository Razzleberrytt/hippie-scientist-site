# 3-MMC (Metaphedrone)

## Quick answer

**3-MMC is a synthetic cathinone stimulant with substantially more human data than many current RC stimulants.**

Its evidence base includes poison-center cohorts, analytically confirmed hospital cases and controlled human pharmacology.

## Dutch poison-center data

The Dutch Poisons Information Center was consulted on 184 3-MMC poisonings from 2013 through mid-2021.

Among reports involving 3-MMC alone, sympathomimetic findings were common, especially tachycardia, hypertension and agitation. Most cases were minor to moderate, but severe effects occurred, including marked hypertension and a nonfatal cardiac arrest.

## Swedish confirmed cases

The STRIDA project analytically confirmed 3-MMC in 50 patients. Co-exposures were common, but tachycardia and agitation were frequent, and seizures, hyperthermia, hallucinations and reduced consciousness also occurred.

## Controlled human evidence

A controlled human study later confirmed that 3-MMC can increase heart rate and blood pressure and produce stimulant subjective effects.

That improves pharmacology knowledge. It does not establish that unregulated powders have predictable concentration, purity or safety.

## Dependence and repeated-use concerns

Repeated strong stimulant exposure can reinforce redosing, sleep loss and psychiatric destabilization.

The human poisoning literature supports treating escalating cardiovascular or psychiatric symptoms seriously rather than assuming they are an expected part of the experience.

## Bottom line

3-MMC is no longer a thin-evidence RC. The human data clearly support sympathomimetic toxicity and occasional severe outcomes.
