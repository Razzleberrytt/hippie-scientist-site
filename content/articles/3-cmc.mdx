# 3-CMC (3-Chloromethcathinone)

## Quick answer

**3-CMC is a chlorinated synthetic cathinone stimulant with emerging human and forensic toxicity evidence.**

A recent human case series describes psychiatric, cardiovascular and respiratory findings, and a separate fatal case has been reported.

## Human intoxications

A 2026 study summarized Italian poison-center cases from 2014–2025. Reported findings included psychomotor agitation, psychosis, aggression, CNS depression, cardiac arrhythmias, chest pain and rapid breathing.

## Fatal case

A separate 2026 forensic report described a fatal intoxication involving 3-CMC.

As with most NPS fatalities, the full toxicological context matters; a case report should not be turned into a universal lethal concentration.

## New preclinical organ-toxicity evidence

Recent mouse studies have found biochemical and histologic changes involving multiple organs.

These findings support toxicologic concern but cannot be converted into a human dose threshold.

## Bottom line

3-CMC now has enough clinical and forensic evidence to justify a standalone safety page. The major practical risks are stimulant psychiatric/cardiovascular toxicity plus a still-poorly-defined human exposure profile.
