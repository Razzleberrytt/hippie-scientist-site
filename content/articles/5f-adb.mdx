# 5F-ADB (5F-MDMB-PINACA)

## Quick answer

**5F-ADB, also called 5F-MDMB-PINACA, is a high-potency synthetic cannabinoid receptor agonist with a substantial fatality record.**

It should not be treated as “strong synthetic weed.”

## Fatal forensic evidence

One study analyzed specimens from **70 fatal cases** in which 5F-ADB was the only detected drug in the study set.

Another forensic paper described **43 fatalities** involving 5-Fluoro-ADB.

Postmortem concentration interpretation is difficult, but these datasets establish that severe and fatal poisoning is a real feature of the drug.

## Non-fatal and behavioral toxicity

A five-case series included:

- collapse;
- loss of consciousness;
- confusion or possible psychosis;
- unsafe driving;
- abrupt mood and behavior changes;
- fatalities.

## Why THC comparisons are misleading

THC is a partial CB1 agonist.

5F-ADB can act as a much higher-efficacy cannabinoid receptor agonist.

That difference helps explain why synthetic cannabinoid toxicity can include profound neurologic and cardiovascular instability not expected from ordinary cannabis.

## Active metabolites

Research shows that some 5F-ADB metabolites retain meaningful CB1 activity.

That means toxicity may reflect not only the parent drug but also biologically active metabolites.

## Bottom line

5F-ADB has one of the clearest fatality records among modern synthetic cannabinoids.

If a product produces unexpected collapse, seizure-like activity, severe confusion, or loss of consciousness, ordinary cannabis should not be assumed.
