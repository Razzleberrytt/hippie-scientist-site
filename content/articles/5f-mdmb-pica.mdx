# 5F-MDMB-PICA

## Quick answer

**5F-MDMB-PICA is a potent synthetic cannabinoid receptor agonist with substantial human toxicology evidence for an NPS.**

Studies include a 71-patient analytically confirmed clinical cohort and a separate forensic case series with fatalities.

## 71-patient cohort

Researchers confirmed 5F-MDMB-PICA exposure in serum and measured cannabinoid receptor activity.

Higher ex-vivo CB1 activity was associated with lower Glasgow Coma Scale scores, providing an unusually direct bridge between analytical pharmacology and human consciousness impairment.

## Forensic cases

A 12-case series included three fatalities plus impaired-driving and criminal-investigation cases. Reported effects included impaired balance, confusion, aggression, disorientation, slowed reactions and slurred speech.

Co-use was common, so those cases do not define a single lethal concentration.

## Why it differs from THC

Modern SCRAs can act as much higher-efficacy CB1 agonists than THC, producing far more abrupt and severe toxicity.

## Bottom line

5F-MDMB-PICA has enough confirmed human evidence to make the risk clear: severe consciousness impairment and fatal outcomes occur in real-world exposure, and ordinary cannabis experience is not a reliable guide.
