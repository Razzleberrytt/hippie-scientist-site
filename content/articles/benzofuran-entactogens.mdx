# 6-APB, 5-MAPB and Benzofuran Entactogens: MDMA-Like Does Not Mean MDMA-Equivalent

## Quick answer

6-APB and 5-MAPB are benzofuran new psychoactive substances often described online as **MDMA-like entactogens**.

That comparison is useful for understanding why people encounter them, but it is not a safety equivalence.

Published human evidence includes acute toxicity with agitation, psychosis, hypertension, tachycardia, hyperthermia, clonus, seizures and altered consciousness.

## What are benzofuran entactogens?

Benzofurans such as 5-APB, 6-APB, 5-MAPB and 6-MAPB share structural and pharmacological features with serotonergic stimulants.

They can affect serotonin, dopamine and norepinephrine systems through transporter interactions and release.

The balance across those systems differs by compound.

## 6-APB human toxicity

A published 6-APB case described acute psychosis and agitation after confirmed exposure.

The literature remains small, but that case is enough to establish that 6-APB is not merely a benign “legal MDMA” substitute.

## 5-MAPB human toxicity

A confirmed 5-MAPB intoxication case described a syndrome including:

- tachycardia;
- hypertension;
- sweating;
- hyperthermia;
- tremor;
- hyperreflexia and clonus;
- agitation and hallucinations;
- convulsions;
- reduced consciousness.

Those findings overlap with severe sympathomimetic and serotonergic toxicity.

A later forensic report also documented 5-MAPB in a fatal multi-drug intoxication, while appropriately recognizing the difficulty of assigning causality in a co-exposure.

## Serotonin toxicity is a major concern

Serotonergic stimulants can become especially dangerous when combined with other drugs that increase serotonin.

Warning signs can include:

- agitation and confusion;
- heavy sweating;
- rapidly rising temperature;
- tremor;
- hyperreflexia;
- clonus;
- diarrhea;
- muscle rigidity.

Severe serotonin toxicity is a medical emergency.

## Why duration matters

Some benzofuran compounds are described as longer-lasting than MDMA in anecdotal reports.

Longer exposure can increase the period during which overheating, dehydration, sleep deprivation and cardiovascular stress accumulate.

This site does not publish a dosing or redosing guide because controlled human dose-response data are limited and gray-market identity is uncertain.

## “MDMA-like” is not a product guarantee

A pellet sold as 6-APB or 5-MAPB can contain another stimulant, a mixture, or a different concentration than expected.

Even an analytically confirmed benzofuran should be interpreted using its own evidence rather than assumptions transferred from MDMA.

## Bottom line

6-APB and 5-MAPB belong in the **serious stimulant/serotonergic toxicity conversation**.

The available human literature is too limited to define a reliable safety margin, and an “MDMA-like” reputation should not be mistaken for a clinically established MDMA-equivalent risk profile.
