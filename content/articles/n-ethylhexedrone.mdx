# N-Ethylhexedrone (NEH)

## Quick answer

**N-ethylhexedrone is a synthetic cathinone stimulant with documented prolonged intoxications and fatal forensic cases.**

Its human evidence base is not enormous, but it is far stronger than a purely anecdotal RC profile.

## 2024 clinical case series

Three analytically confirmed patients developed prolonged symptoms including:

- anxiety;
- feelings of persecution;
- psychomotor slowing;
- asthenia;
- anhedonia;
- loss of consciousness.

Drug concentrations declined over days, highlighting that some RC stimulant effects and detectability can persist much longer than users expect.

## Fatal intoxication

A published forensic case attributed death to N-ethylhexedrone intoxication.

That establishes the potential for severe toxicity even though a universal lethal concentration cannot be defined.

## Bottom line

N-ethylhexedrone has enough human case evidence to reject the idea that it is simply another interchangeable cathinone.

Prolonged psychiatric and neurologic effects are an important part of its real-world risk.
