# Clonazolam: Designer Benzodiazepine Toxicology, Blackouts, Deaths & Withdrawal

> **Safety note:** Frequent benzodiazepine-type use can produce physical dependence. Abrupt cessation after dependence may cause severe withdrawal and seizures. Mixing clonazolam with opioids, alcohol, or other sedatives increases overdose risk.

## Quick answer

**Clonazolam is a designer triazolobenzodiazepine** that became well known in research-chemical markets for strong sedative, anxiolytic, amnestic, and disinhibiting effects.

Unlike a prescription medication with standardized tablets and a therapeutic labeling program, clonazolam's human evidence comes mainly from:

- poison-center reports;
- emergency intoxications;
- forensic toxicology;
- impaired-driving cases;
- fatal investigations;
- online use reports.

DEA permanently placed clonazolam in **Schedule I effective April 1, 2026**.

## Why clonazolam has a high-risk reputation

Benzodiazepine effects can impair the very abilities needed for safe self-monitoring:

- memory;
- judgment;
- coordination;
- awareness of intoxication.

This creates a specific hazard with potent, unregulated products: a person may become impaired, forget what was taken, and take more.

That mechanism helps explain the recurring online association between clonazolam and blackouts or compulsive redosing, even though forum reports cannot define a controlled dose-response curve.

## Fatal intoxication evidence

A 2022 case report described a 14-year-old who died after ingesting tablets believed to be Xanax.

Toxicology detected the clonazolam metabolite **8-aminoclonazolam**, while alprazolam was not identified. The authors attributed the death to acute clonazolam intoxication with cerebral and respiratory depression.

Other fatal reports involve clonazolam with additional depressants, including opioids.

The evidence therefore ranges from rare isolated fatal intoxication to much more common polysubstance risk.

## Effects expected from GABA-A modulation

Clonazolam belongs to the benzodiazepine pharmacologic family and produces effects associated with enhanced GABA-A receptor signaling:

- sedation;
- muscle relaxation;
- anxiety suppression;
- psychomotor slowing;
- impaired memory;
- loss of coordination;
- disinhibition;
- loss of consciousness at sufficiently high exposure.

Those effects can persist longer than a person subjectively expects, especially when product concentration is uncertain.

## Why blackouts matter scientifically

A blackout is not simply “falling asleep.”

Benzodiazepines can interfere with formation of new memories while the person remains awake and behaviorally active.

That means a person can:

- send messages;
- travel;
- take additional drugs;
- make purchases;
- drive;
- appear partially functional;

and later have little or no memory of the period.

This is one reason retrospective self-reports about exact exposure can be unreliable.

## Dependence and withdrawal

Repeated clonazolam exposure can produce tolerance and physical dependence.

Abrupt discontinuation after dependence can cause benzodiazepine withdrawal, including:

- severe rebound anxiety;
- insomnia;
- tremor;
- sweating;
- agitation;
- perceptual disturbance;
- confusion;
- seizures.

A person who has been using an unknown-concentration RC solution or counterfeit tablets may not know the true daily exposure, which makes self-directed conversion to another benzodiazepine especially uncertain.

## Opioid combinations

Clonazolam has been identified in fatalities involving opioid exposures.

The practical risk is additive or synergistic CNS depression.

An opioid can suppress respiratory drive while a benzodiazepine reduces arousal and airway-protective responses.

A person may therefore become difficult to wake before others realize breathing is compromised.

## Legal status in the United States

DEA's 2026 final rule permanently placed:

- clonazolam;
- diclazepam;
- etizolam;
- flualprazolam;
- flubromazolam

in Schedule I of the Controlled Substances Act.

The rule became effective **April 1, 2026**.

## What Reddit can and cannot tell us

Clonazolam is one of the most frequently discussed historical RC benzos on Reddit.

Themes include:

- unusually strong amnesia;
- long periods of impairment;
- rapid tolerance;
- difficult withdrawal;
- comparisons with alprazolam and clonazepam.

Those repeated themes are useful signal detection.

They do not establish a safe amount, a valid prescription-equivalence ratio, or product purity.

## Bottom line

Clonazolam has enough forensic and clinical evidence to move beyond the label “mystery research chemical.”

It is a potent benzodiazepine-type depressant with documented severe intoxication, amnesia, dependence risk, and fatal cases.

The combination of high impairment potential and unregulated concentration makes it particularly poorly suited to dose comparisons based on internet anecdotes.
