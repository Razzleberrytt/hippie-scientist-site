# 3-FPM (3-Fluorophenmetrazine)

## Quick answer

**3-FPM is a fluorinated analogue of phenmetrazine** sold as a research-chemical stimulant.

Published human evidence is limited but includes an analytically confirmed Swedish poison-center series and a fatal polysubstance case.

## Pharmacology

WHO's review describes 3-FPM as a monoamine-transporter substrate/releaser with stronger effects at dopamine and norepinephrine systems than serotonin.

That pattern is broadly consistent with amphetamine-like stimulant toxicity.

## Swedish hospital cases

The STRIDA project confirmed 3-FPM exposure in 19 patients.

Reported presentations were variable because many people had additional drugs on board, but severe events occurred in a meaningful minority.

The study's main value is that it provides real clinical evidence rather than relying on forum reports.

## Fatal polysubstance case

A forensic case report documented 3-FPM together with U-47700 and multiple other psychoactive drugs in a fatal intoxication.

That does not establish a single-drug lethal concentration for 3-FPM.

It does demonstrate how RC stimulant and opioid markets can overlap in high-risk polysubstance use.

## Reddit signal

3-FPM has historically been discussed as a relatively functional or less euphoric stimulant.

Clinical evidence does not validate “functional” as a low-risk category.

## Emergency signs

Seek urgent medical care for chest pain, seizure, severe agitation, psychosis, dangerous overheating, collapse, or abnormal heart rhythm.

## Bottom line

3-FPM has more human evidence than many RC stimulants, but not enough to define a broad safety margin.

Its published hospital and forensic record supports treating it as a genuine stimulant intoxicant rather than a benign research chemical.
