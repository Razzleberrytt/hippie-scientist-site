# 5-APB

## Quick answer

**5-APB is a benzofuran stimulant/entactogen with a documented record of severe and fatal human intoxication.**

It shares some MDMA-like monoaminergic properties but should not be treated as clinically interchangeable with MDMA.

## Fatal cases

Multiple published case reports describe deaths involving analytically confirmed 5-APB.

One report attributed death to 5-APB after the person became unconscious and stopped breathing.

Another fatal case involved 3-MMC, 5-APB, and alcohol with agitation, seizures, hypertension, tachycardia, hyperthermia, and cardiovascular collapse.

## Why isomer identification matters

5-APB and 6-APB are positional isomers.

Forensic methods need to separate them correctly because a generic benzofuran signal does not identify which isomer was consumed.

## Main toxicity pattern

Reported severe effects include:

- tachycardia;
- hypertension;
- agitation;
- hyperthermia;
- seizures;
- reduced consciousness;
- cardiovascular collapse.

## Bottom line

5-APB has enough human fatality evidence to warrant a clear high-risk safety profile rather than a generic “MDMA analogue” description.
