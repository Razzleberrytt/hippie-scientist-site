# 2-MMC (Ortho-Mephedrone)

## Quick answer

**2-MMC is a methylmethcathinone synthetic stimulant and positional isomer of 3-MMC and 4-MMC.**

Human evidence is still limited, but a 2026 French paper described 13 clinical and forensic cases, confirming that it has moved from an internet-market name into real toxicology practice.

## Why isomer comparisons are risky

Moving a substituent on the aromatic ring can change transporter pharmacology, metabolism and time course.

That means “it is basically 3-MMC” is not an evidence-based safety conclusion.

## Current evidence level

The 2026 French case series provides valuable analytical and clinical context, but it is far smaller than the poison-center literature available for 3-MMC.

The appropriate evidence grade is therefore lower.

## Expected stimulant risks

Based on its cathinone pharmacology and emerging casework, major concerns include cardiovascular stimulation, agitation, sleep deprivation and psychiatric destabilization.

Compound-specific incidence remains uncertain.

## Bottom line

2-MMC deserves a separate page because it is appearing in real clinical/forensic work, but its human evidence remains too thin for precise comparative claims.
