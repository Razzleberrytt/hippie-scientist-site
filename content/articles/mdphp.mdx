# MDPHP

## Quick answer

**MDPHP is a pyrovalerone-family synthetic cathinone with documented hospital and fatal forensic exposure.**

It is structurally related to MDPV and sits in a stimulant subgroup associated with strong dopamine/norepinephrine effects and compulsive-use potential.

## Human metabolism

A study of urine from hospitalized intoxication patients mapped extensive MDPHP metabolism and identified metabolites useful for confirming exposure.

## Fatal casework

A later forensic paper quantified MDPHP across postmortem samples in a fatal intoxication.

A 2026 case series further documents MDPHP and closely related pyrovalerones in forensic investigations.

## Product-identity warning

A particularly important modern lesson comes from a separate case where a powder labeled MDPHP was actually high-purity ADB-BUTINACA, a potent synthetic cannabinoid.

So even a correct understanding of MDPHP pharmacology cannot make an unverified product predictable.

## Bottom line

MDPHP has enough human and forensic evidence to establish meaningful stimulant toxicity and fatal-case relevance, while actual RC-product identity remains a separate hazard.
