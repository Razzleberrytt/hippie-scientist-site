# 1cP-LSD

## Quick answer

**1cP-LSD is an N1-acylated lysergamide research chemical that appears to act as an LSD prodrug.**

Analytical research has identified it in blotter products, and incubation with human serum produces LSD.

## Prodrug evidence

A 2020 analytical/behavioral study found that 1cP-LSD formed LSD when incubated with human serum.

That supports a prodrug mechanism.

It does not replace controlled human pharmacokinetic or safety data.

## Blotter identification

Japanese researchers analytically identified 1cP-LSD in paper-sheet products sold as designer drugs.

They also noted that some N1-acylated lysergamides can deacylate during GC-MS analysis, which can complicate chemical identification.

## Evidence limits

Direct controlled human data remain thin for:

- pharmacokinetics;
- rare adverse events;
- interaction risk;
- long-term effects.

## Bottom line

1cP-LSD has strong analytical and prodrug evidence but weak direct human safety evidence.

That distinction should remain visible.
