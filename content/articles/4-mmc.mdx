# 4-MMC (Mephedrone)

## Quick answer

**4-MMC, better known as mephedrone, is a synthetic cathinone stimulant with a much larger human toxicology record than many newer RCs.**

It can produce severe cardiovascular, neurologic, thermoregulatory, and psychiatric toxicity.

## What the systematic review found

A 2025 systematic review identified **77 cases** with individual blood mephedrone concentrations:

- 34 deaths;
- 43 non-fatal intoxications.

Other drugs or alcohol were detected in most evaluable cases, which is why blood concentration cannot be treated as a simple toxicity cutoff.

## Common acute toxicity

Published clinical literature describes:

- tachycardia;
- hypertension;
- agitation;
- anxiety or panic;
- chest pain;
- hyperthermia;
- seizures;
- hallucinations;
- severe stimulant psychiatric symptoms.

## Fatalities

Mephedrone has been directly implicated in fatal intoxications, but many deaths involve additional drugs.

The safest interpretation is therefore not a single lethal concentration—it is that severe and fatal poisoning is well documented.

## Dependence and compulsive use

Mephedrone can be strongly reinforcing, and repeated use has been associated with craving, compulsive redosing, sleep deprivation, and psychiatric destabilization.

## Bottom line

4-MMC is one of the better-characterized synthetic cathinones.

That stronger evidence makes its risks clearer, not safer: serious cardiovascular, neurologic, and fatal outcomes are documented.
