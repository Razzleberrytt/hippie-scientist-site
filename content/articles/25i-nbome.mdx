# 25I-NBOMe

## Quick answer

**25I-NBOMe is a potent 5-HT2A agonist psychedelic with a documented severe-toxicity record.**

Analytically confirmed cases include seizures, dangerous overheating, severe agitation, rhabdomyolysis, kidney injury and deaths.

## Seven-patient confirmed series

A UK case series confirmed 25I-NBOMe in seven young adults.

Clinical findings included tachycardia in all seven, agitation in six, hallucinations in six, seizures in three and hyperpyrexia in three. Elevated creatine kinase was found in all seven.

## Broader record

Review literature documents dozens of non-fatal intoxications and multiple deaths internationally.

These published cases cannot tell us the percentage of all users who develop severe toxicity, but they clearly establish that catastrophic outcomes are possible.

## Mislabeling as LSD

25I-NBOMe has historically appeared on blotter and been represented as LSD.

Appearance alone cannot distinguish them.

## Bottom line

25I-NBOMe should not be treated as merely “another psychedelic.” It has one of the clearest severe acute-toxicity records among modern psychedelic NPS.
