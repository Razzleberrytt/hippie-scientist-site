# THS Growth and Revenue Decision Skill
This is an analysis skill, not permission to change monetization, analytics consent, publishing or production software. Consult canonical `docs/SCOREBOARD.md`, consent policy and experiment history.

## Period-matched dashboard
- Use GA4, GSC, Bing, platform analytics, affiliate reports and subscriber confirmation when connected/authorized. Prefer existing Windsor.ai/vidIQ/Metricool/GA4/GSC integrations rather than collecting screenshots manually. Reporting tool availability ≠ a verified event pipeline.
- Match 28 complete days vs preceding 28 by exact timezone, definition and source. Distinguish: site impressions, clicks, landing sessions, engaged sessions, meaningful decisions, affiliate clicks, confirmed orders/commission, subscribed contacts, video views/retention and actual labor/operational costs.
- No fabricated conversion/revenue estimates from views/clicks. Confirm event schema, privacy/opt-out rules and production transport receipts. Distinguish YouTube/TikTok populations; disclose small denominators and platform-specific limitations.
- Evaluate gross vs marginal outcomes, attribution quality and experiment exposure before scaling. Compare projects/offers on **contribution profit per development hour invested** (net of variable costs, refunds, platform and time costs); also watch maintenance burden and verification debt.
- Candidate opportunities are not validated channels. For FirstCert consult its regulatory-validation skill and regulatory hold before attempting marketing experiments involving certificates.

## Action rules
1. Report facts, source, window, observed vs inferred, confidence and data gaps.
2. Select ONE next experiment, measurable hypothesis, guardrail, stop condition and smallest viable iteration.
3. Use existing experiment ledger; don't rerun previously falsified ideas without new evidence.
4. Recommend pivot only on validated economics/traction/cost-of-maintenance signals, not impatience or week-to-week noise; do not indefinitely protect unprofitable projects from evidence.

## Output
`Observed change | why likely | attribution limits | effort+cost | contribution/hour | go/hold/pivot | next validated experiment`. If denominator missing, `Unknown`, never fictional percentage growth.
