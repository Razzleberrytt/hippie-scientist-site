# FirstCert — Feasibility and Regulatory Gate

## Do not advance prematurely
- No platform, assessment engine, 230-minute curriculum or operational promises until written licensing/reporting answers and Phase 0/1 demand gates permit development. New regulatory evidence can override stale state only when attributed to authoritative source and dated.
- Keep commercial pricing, reporting cutoffs, exam standards and provider commitments as **proposed only** until checked against private, current business specifications and the regulator’s written approval. Do not publish private deal terms or correspondence.
- Differentiate approved vendor/program, eligible individual certificate, state reporting frequency and actual same-day submission. A competitor reportedly submits once weekly, but one customer's anecdote isn't a verified channel advantage or permitted TABC commitment.
