# EvidenceMotion reusable, topic-independent director grammar rules

Owner scope #6743; development/version freeze rescinded. Still fail-closed on source/voice/science and requires protected CI and separate publishing authority.

**Canonical reusable module:** `previz-director.mjs` already defines six grammars. `reusable-director-program.mjs` uses that single existing implementation instead of creating a competing artistic engine or hardcoding camera scenes to 7-hydroxymitragynine. It converts a source-/narration-locked, spoken-anchor-aligned, claim-ID-linked episode into an inspectable shot-plan with:

- **G1 Object → mechanism:** recognizable scientific subject to explanatory detail
- **G2 Follow the process:** sequential steps only when process evidence has been reviewed
- **G3 Evidence under the lens:** reveal an actual source measurement/document
- **G4 Two-world contrast:** fair comparison with both sides upstream reviewed
- **G5 Uncertainty in depth:** show exactly what is computed, unknown or indirect
- **G6 Scale and consequence:** source-referenced quantity/context with explicit scale review

All proposals carry exact source/narration SHA identities, original anchors, shot duration, grammar, camera/transition, claim IDs, source asset IDs and explicit `SOURCE_LOCKED_DIRECTOR_PREVIZ_ONLY`. Tests compile the same six grammars for two different **synthetic topic identities**, and deliberately fail on unknown claims, missing research assets, unreviewed G2/G4/G6, fabricated human effects, time-stretched narration, gaps, and a failed research-only policy. **Tests do not claim the synthetic topics have actual PMIDs, full-film renders or measurable artistic quality.**

This extracts deterministic reusable decision rules independent of 7-OH shot coordinates. It is an engineering research rule extraction, not proof of premium lighting or excellent voice. Final media must still satisfy separately-source-grounded science, phone safe area, ASR/phoneme work, craft reference and publishing gates. No paid inference, laptop, new source dataset or automatic posts.

After exact-head dedicated and protected CI succeed and this PR merges, the existing **E** checklist item "Extract reusable director rules rather than hardcode 7-OH shots" is eligible for **one** evidence-based checkbox, moving 24/46 to 25/46. No excellent film is awarded by this subsystem alone.
