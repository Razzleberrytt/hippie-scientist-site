# EvidenceMotion: coherent editorial 2D motion research comparator

**Status:** Research-only, no production integration or release. Owner direction (Oct 13, 2026): the renderer and art style are interchangeable; the criteria are premium finished videos, accessible explanations, repeatability, consistency, safety and lowest total cost/effort. Do not equate high-end technology with quality.

This candidate reuses the exact `validated_7oh_chemistry.verified_graph` pinned PubChem source, renders a 2D molecule diagram with the OH oxygen highlighted using RDKit, composes a single coherent editorial portrait design in Pillow and writes an **actual silent 3-second MP4** with FFmpeg. No AI visual source, Blender requirement, owner's laptop, GPU, credits, hidden network access, fresh clinical claim, narrator replacement, publishing rights, production import, new R version, or scheduling.

The purpose is to test whether a simpler, intentional visual language beats expensive/complex CGI on **legibility, source clarity and consistency**. It is **not a completed article, finished film, four-act narrated story, medically validated illustration of an effect, or approved premium visual design**.

### What to compare before adoption

1. Use an identical locked scientific source and professionally narrated, beginner-friendly story for both editorial 2D and 3D test treatments. Compare actual full videos at the **same** resolution, fps, and required runtime on a phone, not different isolated stills.
2. Correctness and craft receive **separate** decisions. Craft must be evaluated against real best-in-niche references and one unmistakably strong hero image and scene progression. A formulaic flat card or generically orbiting molecule can fail even with clean pixels.
3. Measure **total** labor/revision/render/QA and Actions minutes for the same topic/voice. Prototype wall times on different runners are not comparable; real billing is Unknown until verified.
4. Whatever looks better **and** is repeatable should become the eventual candidate. Blend styles only when the actual story needs it. Premium all-2D is acceptable; premium 3D is acceptable; compulsory hybrid is not.
5. Keep the existing full quality gates: qualified source/caption review, original human-auditioned WAV, word alignment, mobile UI safe-zone and muted comprehension, actual full-resolution encoded video, iPhone owner review, source-identical receipts, external craft references, one excellent first film and five consecutive excellent first renders.

### Research scope

- 48 actual 540×960 frames at 16fps. Difference-based mechanical proof that molecule pixels really reveal. One source-locked candidate, not an approved finished script.
- Art direction: dark, typography-led field-note layout, real source-bound 2D chemical drawing, highlighted hydroxyl atom, explicit 'not evidence of effects in humans' text, full source name, no fake mechanism claims. Phone readability and performance still require owner review.
- CI checks exact source identity, no unreviewed claims/voice, reproducibility/compute caps, actual MP4 frame count, no audio, and a decoded visual subject-change test; saves exact MP4, 3 frames, hashes/receipt for independent human eyes.
