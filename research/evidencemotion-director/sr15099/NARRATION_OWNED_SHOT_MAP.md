**Status:** research-only shot direction, October 13, 2026. The long-form V4 narrative is authoritative; this map is NOT a fixed-time storyboard, a new EvidenceMotion release, or owner/V craft approval.

## Audience objective

A viewer with zero pharmacology knowledge should be able to answer, in their own words: (1) what tolerance means, (2) why withdrawal is a separate issue, (3) what both mouse groups did, (4) why the other mouse assay matters, and (5) why none of this establishes SR-15099 effects in humans.

## The six shot grammars (constrained variety; one motivated camera move per shot)

- **Source-bound molecular portrait** — exact compound identity, verified heavy-atom connectivity and stereochemistry. No generative chemistry or fictitious receptor docking.
- **Physical analogy** — one clearly labeled metaphor for tolerance or withdrawal; never graph empirical magnitude from imagination.
- **Two-track experimental stage** — verified control and intervention groups with identical time baseline and matching outcome markers.
- **Method reveal** — demonstrate that a hot-plate behavior test and warm-water tail-withdrawal response assess different behaviors; no animals visibly harmed, no invented apparatus details.
- **Contradiction pivot** — an assertion stays tied to the particular test and fails to cross into another.
- **Evidence boundary** — explicitly stop the 2020 and 2021 SR-17018 results from being re-labeled as SR-15099 human results.
