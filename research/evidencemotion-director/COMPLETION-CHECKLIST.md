## Definition of "complete"

Platform publishing still requires separately established owner authorization. **The director system** is canonically complete only after that first excellent first-render master **and five consecutive excellent first renders on varied topics**, with reproducible locked sources, model/execution budgets, robust failed-shot regression tests, and no safety bypass.
