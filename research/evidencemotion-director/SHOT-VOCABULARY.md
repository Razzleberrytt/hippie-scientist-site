# EvidenceMotion — Six-shot composition language (research only)

**Owner authority:** Phone-first, remote CPU preview workflow; RTX 3050 laptop parked as an optional alternate. No new shipped R version, production import, generative paid model or publication permission. Version freeze holds on the production path. Baseline scientific content, numbers, sources, voice, labels and timings come only from the approved deterministic pipelines.

## The six grammars (selection is purposeful, not hash-driven)

| ID | Name | Narrative use | Visual structure | Camera/motion | Allowed transition | Never use when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G1 | **Object-to-Mechanism** | An everyday object/phenomenon conceals a mechanism | A recognizable near-field subject with physically spatially separate underlying scientific region; source overlay on untouched 2D plane | 24–50mm equivalent, motivated macro push followed by masked reveal; depth/parallax from subject geometry | Occlusion reveal tied to a spoken mechanism anchor | The scientific mechanism is speculative, visually ungrounded, or depicted as validated docking |
| G2 | **Follow-the-Process** | Explain order, route, uptake or binding | One persistent hero object traverses anchored environment; object IDs persist; initial, intermediate, terminal states | Constrained tracking orbit/dolly with constant motion direction and purposeful speed | Match-on-action, continuous camera cut only where action remains traceable | No actual temporal/process relationship is evidence-backed; impossible to visualize recognizable mechanism |
| G3 | **Evidence Under the Lens** | Contrast what a study measured and what it did not | Verifiable study artifact in depth beneath a deterministic 2D extraction mask; clear measured/unmeasured separation | Slow scan, rack focus, study card moved under reading aperture | Lens wipe justified by a narrated evidence shift | Generative imagery would replace authentic study findings or create fake study pages |
| G4 | **Two-World Contrast** | Compare claims, exposures or populations | Two distinct environments share a spatial visual anchor; true comparisons only; equal data-scales when quantifying | Perpendicular camera move through an anchored center divider; restrained cross-axis translation | Motivated split-to-continuous spatial match | Comparison lacks equivalent denominators, subjects, endpoints or stated uncertainty |
| G5 | **Uncertainty in Depth** | Communicate a confidence boundary, missing measurement or open question | What we know stays crisp and source-locked; what we do not know remains physically occluded or deliberately abstract and labeled | Controlled focus pull, changing silhouette visibility, no fake scientific geometry | Occlusion curtain/focus boundary timed to explicit uncertainty words | It suggests a precise probability, dose, effect size or biological process not established by the cited sources |
| G6 | **Scale and Consequence** | Show magnitude, human relevance or a closing synthesis | Familiar reference object and grounded scale relation; visible spatial hierarchy for all evidence-bound quantities | Physical pullback, carefully controlled dolly zoom without distorting numeric truth | Match-scale or consequence reveal led by locked narration | Scale lacks a cited denominator/reference or the subject could sensationalize uncertain harms |

Each grammar has **independent** shape/layout, hero material, lens, lighting and motion rules; changing the palette alone is NOT a new grammar. Do not use a rotating rectangle template as six different compositions. No stock-feel transition library, clip-art replacements, made-up molecular structures or arbitrary decorative UI.

## Finite camera vocabulary
- `MACRO_PUSH`: forward tracking with anchored hero and bounded near/far parallax.
- `LATERAL_TRACK`: follow motion with unchanged orientation of the action axis.
- `ORBIT_REVEAL`: physically interpretable partial arc, no looping spin.
- `FOCUS_TRANSFER`: move visual attention between real, anchored subjects.
- `PULLBACK_SCALE`: reveal verified context only when its comparative dimensions are source-bound.
- `LOCKED_HOLD`: permitted only for an intentional emotional or scientific pause, with no static interval >6 s without an approved meaningful compositional change; caption-only motion does not count.

## Transition vocabulary
- `MATCH_ON_ACTION`: preserve object ID and action position across cut.
- `OCCLUSION_REVEAL`: real/illustrated foreground masks hide and reveal context.
- `SPATIAL_CONTINUITY`: camera motion and visual direction persist across states.
- `FOCUS_HANDOFF`: true depth/focus change transfers the subject.
- `EVIDENCE_APERTURE`: reviewed artifact becomes legible without inventing study graphics.
- `HARD_CUT`: strongest default when content changes, rather than decorative transitions.
No generic whip pans, arbitrary flashes, stock crossfade chains, spinning charts or numerical distortion.

## Director selection: rule-based candidate generation
Director receives only **approved**: `beatId`, `role`, `visualIntent`, `spokenAnchorTimes`, `scientificAssetIds`, `uncertaintyClass`, `claimIds`, `previousGrammar`, `budgetClass`. It NEVER invents voice text, data, mechanism geometry or studies.

1. Identify semantic purpose first, mapping `hook: everyday→mechanism` to G1; `sequence` to G2; `study-measurement` to G3; `comparator` to G4; `uncertainty` to G5; `scale/consequence` to G6.
2. Filter unsafe candidates: G2 requires a supported sequence and anchored action geometry; G4 a reviewed fair comparison; G6 reviewed quantities, otherwise it renders a qualitative consequence WITHOUT a numeric size claim.
3. Build **two distinct candidate shot treatments** per beat, using the same verified claim and voice: primary grammar + safe alternative; one alternate can be a reframed shot under a different grammar only if semantic intent is preserved.
4. Rank candidates by explanatory fit, clear hero subject, narrative continuity, semantic motion opportunity, contrast against neighboring shots and raster/runtime cost. Penalize adjacent repeated layout/camera combinations, not legitimate deliberate recurrence.
5. The director chooses **a proposal**, not a final approval. It emits grammar ID, camera ID, transition ID, scene actors, exact source-bound data IDs, temporal cues and reference inspirations. No arbitrary pseudo-random style seed as a substitute for taste.

## Evaluator pairing — BEFORE final render
**Pipeline:** reviewed source/claim lock → original approved audio and acoustic anchors → two storyboard candidates → cheap 360p/low-fps previz or still animatic on existing CPU runner → decoded preview checks and human/reference-shelf perceptual review → accept/reject concept → ONE final 1080x1920/30fps render → end-to-end decoded exact-master scientific/voice/device QA.

Evaluator may **KILL** during previz: generic template/undifferentiated hero, stock transitions, unexplained clip-art, spoken-audio stretching, >6s of compositionally static beat (caption-only changes excluded), or factual visual appearing fabricated. A killed proposal gets a fundamentally new concept, not visual paint-over. Block unsafe comparisons, unverified structure/docking and non-source-bound quantities.

Machine measurements (pixel motion and scene actor telemetry) are **negative filters**, not proof of excellent craft. An automated evaluator cannot self-certify a score of 90, a genuinely interesting shot or phone-playback acceptance. If the reference-shelf reviewer is unavailable, mark `CRAFT_HOLD` rather than `PASS`. V independent craft review requires V actually reviewing the material; ChatGPT review may be recorded separately, never represented as V_VERIFIED. The owner's creative choices take precedence over advisory tastes, while scientific and playback safety remain non-negotiable.

**Previz receipts:** approved audio hash, narration anchor timestamps, candidate shot graph/version, proposed grammar + camera + transitions, decoded preview path/hash, actual frame dimensions, preview frame samples with caption region excluded, source/asset IDs, research-only model/art provenance, cost/time and explicit human approval state. A storyboard's own booleans do not prove its frames were decoded.

## Synthetic B-roll honesty decision
Default on PHONE-FIRST track: **no synthetic photorealistic B-roll**. Illustrative generated backgrounds must be visibly stylized, source-licensed/cleared, checksum-pinned and labeled `AI-generated illustrative artwork` whenever needed to avoid viewers mistaking them for recorded real-world events, persons, products, or biological imaging. Documentary-looking generations require a distinct conspicuous synthetic-footage label and owner-specific authorization prior to any preview intended for publication. The truth layer (source, captions, numbers, data, molecules, clinical/regulatory claims) is never generative. Fictional microscopy and purported molecule/medical visuals cannot masquerade as actual observations.

## Why this is research, not an R-version
Outputs sit only under `research/evidencemotion-director/`, are not imported into `scripts/distribution/`, trigger no CI render jobs automatically and create no new release capabilities. The milestone remains one owner-accepted excellent MP4 on first final render, then five consecutive first-render excellent MP4s under the existing quality standards. The first quality shot can't be guaranteed from a design document; real preview/review samples must establish it.
