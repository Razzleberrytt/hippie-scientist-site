**Owner approved creative direction, research-only.** PR #6734 stays DRAFT and UNMERGED. Owner operates from iPhone; remote GitHub/cloud CPU renders. RTX 3050 laptop is PARKED. No upgrades, purchases, paid model downloads or GPU integration.

## Creative operation sequence
1. **Visual concept and visual reference still first.** Select a compelling, recognizable hero subject for each beat. Author/obtain the art at a controlled native pixel resolution. Approve LOOK and story intent before movement. Lock literal input image/layer bytes by SHA256 (not simply a prompt, seed or verbal style description).
2. **Split into actual layered still plates:** independently approved far background / middle subject / foreground occlusion. Use a complete clean background behind the subject. Never leave transparent extraction holes, hallucinate a scientific object, or ask the AI to fill factual mechanisms. Depth Anything V2 Small and lightweight segmentation are optional *research candidates* that must earn their keep against manual layers and CPU limits. A model is not a magic cinematic-quality switch.
3. **Audition original voice in small sentence-sized chunks:** saved house Kokoro voice, phonetic dictionary dry-run with exact audible words, owner human ear-check; source text and visible captions retain correct scientific spelling. Never turn mitragynine into unnaturally separated phonemes. Lock original WAV bytes/hash, then obtain genuine original-audio word anchors. Timing, cuts and transitions follow that locked voice. NEVER stretch the WAV to fit footage.
4. **Compose scenes from the exact approved reference bytes and voice anchors.** One motivated camera move PER SHOT; no unnecessary pan+zoom+orbit stacks. Parallax drifts are bounded to small movements (~5% width is a preflight ceiling, not an artistic default); leave space around cutout edges. Changing palette or random camera seed does not constitute a new composition.
5. **Phone-first opening:** by two seconds, all three are present: readable hook text, perceptible subject movement, strong line of spoken narration. No greeting. One *meaningful* compositional change approximately every 2–3 seconds is a creative target; pointless stock transitions are worse than an intentional hold. Existing >6-second static KILL remains in force.
6. **Design against phone UI overlays:** conservative right-side interactive overlay reserve >=20% of width, bottom reserve >=18% of height, top >=10%; visually test actual TikTok/Shorts placement and caption legibility at native phone scale. Readable captions and source overlays take precedence over decor.
7. **Evaluator sits beside the director:** create TWO treatments under one locked asset/voice source; produce low-cost 360p/low-FPS previews; decode real output pixels with scientific/caption safe zones excluded. Reject placeholder hero, clip-art shape language, unresolved subject silhouette, stock motion, dishonest synthetic footage, overlap, stretched voice, source drift and static holds. Artistic originality still needs actual owner/reference-shelf critique.
8. **Only after acceptance:** single approved final-resolution render, encoded-media decoding, science/source/voice checks, owner phone playback and independent art review. Preview passing is not an excellent-video certification.

## Decision on generator usage
- **Use pretrained models as specialist tools, not the taste authority.** Deterministic VTK, Pillow, Blender if remotely provisioned, FFmpeg and scene layout form the factual and motion layer.
- **Image-understanding/selection:** consider light CLIP-family embedding similarity only if a measurable ranking need arises. It cannot certify visual taste or science.
- **Depth and segmentation:** Depth Anything V2 Small and MobileSAM-class variants are low-priority CPU benchmarks; strong depth may help a real scene but is NOT a substitute for genuine geometry, recognizable hero art or compelling direction.
- **Generative atmospherics:** only clearly illustrative/nonfactual assets, with provenance, licensing, immutable source bytes and required labeling. No generated humans/faces for the research path. Documentary-looking AI footage must have conspicuous disclosure and separately approved use.
- **Scientific trust plane:** always deterministic and source-locked; no AI-created molecules, doses, text, PMIDs, percentages, diagrams implying measured biology, or numerical comparisons.
- **RTX 3050 experiment:** the legacy **LTX-Video 0.9.x 2B distilled quantized** family has lightweight versions and community low-VRAM reports, but 4GB execution on the owner's Windows 8GB-RAM machine is UNVERIFIED; LTX-2 and newer LTX-2.3 have materially higher official requirements. Retain only as parked, specifically versioned research. No hardware request or install in this track. Official upstream: https://github.com/Lightricks/LTX-Video and https://ltx.io/blog/run-video-generation-model-locally
