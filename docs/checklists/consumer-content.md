## PC-02 — Evidence-Based Editorial & Content Lifecycle

**End-state:** Publish compelling understandable articles that are accurate, current, not auto-promoted from research and useful enough to earn returning readers.

**Existing authority:** [Editorial standard](../PLAIN_LANGUAGE_SCIENCE_EDITORIAL_STANDARD.md), [Publishing workflow](../PUBLISHING_WORKFLOW.md). **Crosswalk:** P0 sections 5,12; A lane.

- [ ] **PC-02-01** — Enforce complete source-to-draft mapping including claim text, PMID/DOI, studied outcome, caveats, disclosures and human-readable terms.
- [ ] **PC-02-02** — Define a clear why-this-article job, unique intent owner and specific sections improved by each research update.
- [ ] **PC-02-03** — Produce full beginner-friendly explanations of technical terms while preserving causal uncertainty and safety context.
- [ ] **PC-02-04** — Validate article structure, accessibility, citations and trusted sources with adversarial hallucination and stale-reference tests.
- [ ] **PC-02-05** — Keep proposed drafts/hold decisions separate from explicit scientific, editorial and publication authorization.
- [ ] **PC-02-06** — Demonstrate a representative fully reviewed article through PR, exact-head CI, approved publish and deployed URL/source verification.
- [ ] **PC-02-07** — Maintain dated review, correction, withdraw/noindex and roll-back actions with stable page identity.
- [ ] **PC-02-08** — Measure reader outcomes, citations, return use, qualified visit/conversion and editorial effort, not article volume alone.
