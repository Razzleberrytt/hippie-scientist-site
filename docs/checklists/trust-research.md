**Existing authority:** [Enrichment pipeline](../enrichment-pipeline.md), [Enrichment definition of done](../enrichment-definition-of-done.md). **Crosswalk:** P0 sections 1–2; integration source register.

- [ ] **TR-01-02** — Deterministically identify intake jobs, deduplicate PMID/DOI/title and distinguish papers from underlying independent studies.
- [ ] **TR-01-03** — Enforce source verification, freshness, correction/retraction/quarantine and explicit candidate-only boundaries with adversarial fixtures.
- [ ] **TR-01-04** — Make batch reservations, retries, crash recovery and terminal disposition inspectable without duplicate imports or abandoned work.

## TR-02 — Scientific Review, Safety & Harm Reduction

**End-state:** Prevent unsupported efficacy, unsafe product advice, misleading chemical depictions and commercialization pressure from changing research truth.

**Existing authority:** [Project charter](../PROJECT_CHARTER.md), [Evidence-quality gates](../THS_MASTER_P0_ACCEPTANCE_REGISTER.md), [Safety editorial standard](../content/WITHDRAWAL_RECOVERY_MASTERCLASS_STANDARD.md). **Crosswalk:** P0 sections 3,5,8,12.

- [ ] **TR-02-01** — Publish a canonical claim/evidence vocabulary separating source identity, peer review, human clinical outcomes, preclinical mechanisms and unknowns.
- [ ] **TR-02-02** — Gate every high-stakes medical/safety claim against exact relevant source, population, dose context, comparator, endpoint and limitation.
- [ ] **TR-02-03** — Detect and resolve paper duplication, study lineage, contraindications, interacting drug classes and stale/withdrawn evidence.
- [ ] **TR-02-04** — Maintain quarantined research-only findings and protect high-risk substances from inappropriate affiliate promotion or dosage advice.
- [ ] **TR-02-05** — Validate plain-language explanations of risks, evidence uncertainty and when professional care matters across representative reader scenarios.
- [ ] **TR-02-06** — Run a representative calibrated adversarial safety suite including fabricated effects, paper/outcome mismatch and corrections.
- [ ] **TR-02-07** — Prove independent review/approval or genuinely calibrated autonomous positive quality judgments with reasons, confidence and override/HOLD.
- [ ] **TR-02-08** — Show production-held bad claims and source-linked successful safe corrections with exact site/deployment evidence.
