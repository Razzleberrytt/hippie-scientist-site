## Editorial rules

- One canonical reader job per route; avoid duplicate intent pages.
- No dosing, redosing, administration optimization, sourcing, synthesis, concealment, or potency-conversion instructions.
- Separate receptor/animal potency from human potency.
- Separate "detected in a case" from "caused the case."
- Preserve product/batch uncertainty.
- When a brand is named, distinguish confirmed laboratory findings from poison-center suspicion or anecdote.
- For overdose-capable drug classes, include emergency warning signs and relevant reversal context.
- Reddit/community posts are discovery signals, not evidence of identity, purity, potency, or safety.

## Product-evidence rule

For named retail products, the site must distinguish:
1. government/independent laboratory-confirmed contents;
2. poison-center or public-health suspicion;
3. self-reported effects;
4. manufacturer label claims.

Those four evidence types must never be collapsed into one statement.
