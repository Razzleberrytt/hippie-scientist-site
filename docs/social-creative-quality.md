# THS R8.05 — Attention-First Story Architecture & Semantic AV Lock

## Status

R8.05 is the canonical creative-production rule for new THS social video. It inherits R8.04 sovereign zero-credit production and all R8.03 evidence, uncertainty, continuity, trust, and artifact-bound QA rules.

R8.05 exists because a video can be scientifically accurate, technically valid, visually clean, and still be bad social content.

> Accuracy is necessary. Attention and audiovisual coherence are release requirements too.

## Pre-render premise gate

A concept does not earn production merely because the evidence is interesting to us.

Before scripting, the work order must identify:

- why a cold viewer should care immediately;
- one viewer question — one mental job;
- the first concrete payoff;
- why the angle is materially different from recent THS posts;
- which details can be deferred to the caption or site.

The premise must score at least 4/5 internally for cold-viewer interest. A concept that only becomes interesting after methodology, several setup lines, or expert context is reframed or killed before rendering.

Paper-summary openings are forbidden. An immediate evidence token is welcome; a methodology-first hook is not, unless the method itself is genuinely the story.

## One mental job

Every spoken line and every visual must advance the same viewer question, qualify the answer, or be removed.

By default, introduce no more than one new primary entity or relationship per semantic beat. A true comparison may use two. Do not make viewers memorize study arms, ingredient inventories, labels, mechanisms, and outcomes merely to reach the payoff.

## Payoff before methodology

Give the viewer a useful partial answer, consequence, contradiction, or reveal before study logistics.

Sample size, study design, source, and evidence class still matter, but they support the story after the viewer understands why the result matters.

## Natural duration

There is no creative target of 30 seconds.

The video lasts as long as the shortest natural narration-plus-visual sequence needed to deliver the promise and its required qualifier. Never pad with dead air, slow motion, repeated copy, or generic outro just to hit a template runtime.

## Voice-first semantic beat map

The approved narration is the timing master.

Before visuals are built, mark the semantic beats, pauses, emphasis words, contrasts, reveal points, and payoff. The visual timeline is authored from that voice-first semantic beat map.

Do not construct a visual edit independently and then stretch, chop, or overlay narration until it approximately fits.

## Semantic clip ownership

Every clip or scene owns an exact spoken idea.

A clip may not arrive before its concept, persist underneath a new concept merely for convenience, or depict a neighboring idea after narration has moved on. Scene count does not prove synchronization.

Cuts happen on meaning: completed phrases, pauses, contrast pivots, reveals, emphasis, or the end of a thought.

## Internal motion synchrony

Correct clip selection is not enough.

Within the clip, the meaningful reveal, movement, highlight, diagram change, comparison, or emphasis must land on the spoken word or phrase that gives it meaning. If a scene is technically aligned but its internal motion fires early or late, it fails.

## Whole-piece cohesion gate

PerceptualQA reviews the exact master as one authored audiovisual object.

The voice can sound good and the visuals can look good separately while the combination still fails. Technical A/V sync is necessary but insufficient. If the master feels like narration pasted over clips, it is rejected.

## Rescue limit

One macro rebuild is allowed when a promising concept has a fixable story or timing defect.

If the rebuilt concept still needs explanation to become interesting, still overloads the viewer, or still cannot make voice and visuals feel co-authored, classify it as a creative loss. Record the failure in FieldLab and move to a materially different angle.

There is no endless polish loop. Sunk cost never waives the quality floor.

## Canonical R8.05 production order

R8.05 reverses the old visuals-first repair pattern. The order is mandatory:

1. EvidenceBridge-authorized research object and lossless governed copy.
2. Approved R8.05 creative brief with one mental job, early payoff, semantic beats, supported motion primitives, and exact factual-copy reconstruction.
3. Local Kokoro narration at natural speed. The exact WAV creates the semantic beat timeline; no fixed-duration template owns timing.
4. Exact narration QA for Natural Presence and pronunciation.
5. Visual package generated from the exact voice-derived beat timings. Narrated beats must use a renderable `reveal` or `highlight` primitive; silent/source beats use `hold`.
6. MP4 render using the same natural runtime and beat cue precision.
7. PerceptualQA watches the exact encoded MP4 and explicitly approves whole-piece cohesion, narration↔visual sync, internal-motion sync, cognitive continuity, and hook→payoff delivery.
8. PublishOps stages that same approved MP4 hash. R8.05 staging may not rerender or replace the reviewed master.
9. Manual native upload remains the baseline publication transport.

A failure at any step moves backward to the owning step. It never silently routes around the problem through a premium provider or by publishing a different artifact.

## Factual-copy boundary

R8.05 creative pacing does not create a new scientific-authority layer. Finding and limitation beats must reconstruct the canonical governed copy exactly. Evidence, disclosure/context, CTA, and source beats must match their governed canonical forms. The hook may use only the evidence-safe canonical hook unless EvidenceBridge separately authorizes a new framing. An arbitrary brief cannot inherit a safety receipt produced for different words.

## Renderable internal motion

A text description of motion is not proof that motion exists. R8.05 therefore accepts only motion primitives the local renderer can actually execute:

- `reveal` — the governed text is absent before the voice-derived anchor cue and appears at the cue.
- `highlight` — the governed text remains visible and a visible emphasis appears at the cue.
- `hold` — static hold for a silent/source beat only.

The voice-derived anchor cue is an authored estimate based on the measured exact speech duration and the position of the spoken anchor in the line. It is not treated as final proof of perceptual synchronization; the exact-master QA must still watch the finished MP4 and pass internal-motion sync.

## Release floor

R8.05 passes only when all inherited R8.04 sovereignty and R8.03 evidence/trust gates pass **and**:

- cold-viewer premise interest passes;
- one mental job is explicit;
- payoff precedes methodology;
- duration is natural rather than template-driven;
- narration owns the timing skeleton;
- every clip owns a semantic beat;
- cuts follow meaning;
- internal motion is synchronized to spoken anchors;
- cognitive load stays within budget;
- the exact master passes whole-piece cohesion.

If any of those fail, reframe, rebuild once, or kill the angle. Do not publish a technically correct video that still sucks.
