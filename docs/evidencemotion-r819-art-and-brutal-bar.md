## The visual brand is cross-channel
Use one navy/teal **dark cellular / organic** direction across vertical video, article hero imagery, carousel covers, social previews and site illustration. Reuse `public/brand/ths-cellular-organic.svg` as the pinned nonfactual art seed; palettes and lighting may evolve only through approved versioned asset changes. Art is not a second representation of scientific truth.

- AI generation allowed for **static nonfactual illustrations, textures and backgrounds only**. No AI text, numbers, molecules, diagrams, study charts, PMIDs, labels or fabricated scientific imagery.
- Pin art bytes by SHA-256; storyboard **must contain both asset ID and SHA-256**, and fail on mismatch. No remote runtime fetching, mutable asset handles, or silent stock replacement.
- Compositing order bottom-to-top: **art → teaching mechanism/illustration → deterministic trust layer**. Preserve on-screen citations, word-level audio-aligned captions, source-bound quantities and denominators, evidence-class qualifiers and honesty labels.
- Example branded honesty labels: **symbolic 3D model — not docking data**, **qualitative reports ≠ clinical efficacy**, and claim-adjacent PMID footers. They are an aesthetic feature, not tiny legal text.

## Two independent verdicts (new baseline after art pipeline exists)
**CORRECTNESS:** primary-source validation, source/denominator binding, scientific caveats, symbolic honesty, captions, factually deterministic graphics. A correct script is not enough to certify the final encoded master.

**CRAFT:** emotional opening, readable hero object, art direction, narration-led cutting, semantic composition changes, transition quality, memorable visual payoff, audience relevance. A technically correct PowerPoint fails. **90/100** means visually and experientially competitive with the best current niche science explainers, not a comparison with our historical 81/83. Do not assign numeric craft scores from tests or old data.

**Four hard craft kills:** (1) static template/no hero, (2) audible narration stretching/slowing to fit runtime, (3) more than six seconds without genuine compositional motion (exclude subtitle and particle pixel noise), (4) clip-art/stock-template feel. Kill means do not publish or perform a cosmetic repair loop; return to direction/shot design. Scenes cut to natural speech length, never stretch narration.

**Reference shelf:** exactly **3–5 independently selected science Shorts/TikToks, chosen by Willie** with URLs and explanation. It is not ready until he curates it. V is the independent craft reviewer and can veto publication using an exact-master phone-size pixel/audio comparison. An assistant cannot self-assign `V_VERIFIED` or forge owner reference selections.

**Required objective gates:** one named hero visual; genuinely moving hook element within three seconds; narrated multi-item sequencing; object/state displacement verified via R8.18 telemetry; longest meaningful-motion drought ≤6 seconds measured on real artwork, not caption changes. Pixel truth must be reviewed independently of telemetry.

**Full release path:** one source research brief → source-bound article → full video → derivative Short(s) → carousel → site → email list → affiliate journey. **No autoplay social publishing**, affiliate claims, or human-review bypass. All rendered outputs remain REVIEW ONLY pending science/craft exact-master review.
