## Mission and measured baseline

Make The Hippie Scientist a consistently retrievable, accurately cited evidence reference for supplement efficacy, safety, ingredient/product identity, cognition, sleep, stress, and emerging compounds. Optimize for **reliable factual usefulness and breadth**, not mere article output or citation volume. No site can guarantee answer-engine citations.

Owner-supplied **Bing 2026-10-12** snapshot: **49,177 page-level citations; 180 cited URLs; 535 grounding-query rows; two strongest pages 20,029 citations / 40.73%**. Page and Query exports overlap and cannot be added, nor can the difference from an overlapping prior snapshot prove daily growth. The 7-/30-day **daily Overview** baseline, exact query→page attribution, non-Bing citation reach, traffic, conversion and causal growth are currently **Unknown / require source**.

Sources: [Oct 12 snapshot](AI-CITATION-2026-10-12-SNAPSHOT.md), [existing growth loop](AI-CITATION-GROWTH-LOOP.md), [AI search standards](ai-search-optimization.md). No private dashboard CSVs may be committed.

**P0 meaning:** high strategic priority as a portfolio; **not** 18 urgent tickets or an extra workstream. Current [sprint](CURRENT_SPRINT.md) exclusively admits scoped execution. Never displace a true safety/security incident or an already admitted owner. Total normal WIP 3 maximum: one each D (Discovery/SEO), A (Authority/Content), R (Revenue/Conversion). P0 program IDs below are not the original unrecovered 150-item P0 checklist and must not be counted against its progress.

## Promotion and stopping rules

1. At each selection, first reconcile **GitHub issue/PR and deployed site truth** and the authoritative Active/Now queues. Existing #6690 / #6685 / #6670 / #6431 retain their owners; do not fork those systems.
2. Check one task's unmet dependency, direct editorial/safety risk, independent source evidence, affected canonical source owners, existing publications, duplicate intent and current D/A/R WIP. Split or batch only within one coherent risk owner.
3. Apply only the Master Backlog's established `(Business Impact × User Value × Traffic Potential × Strategic Leverage × Confidence) / Effort` formula, dated verification scope and hard science/SEO/safety gates. **No second P0 bonus score.**
4. Promote *one scoped implementation* into both `CURRENT_SPRINT.md` and `MASTER_BACKLOG.md` only through the required exact-base admission transaction. Candidate list alone cannot authorize an implementation PR or publication.
5. All scientific conclusions and content corrections need source and editorial review. Mechanistic/preclinical evidence cannot become human clinical efficacy; provide negative/null results, population/formulation/time boundaries and interaction caveats. Do not present AI-assisted review as independent human or V verification.
6. After exact-head validation and deployment receipts, retire the WIP owner and update scoreboard with source/date or **Unknown**. Prefer repair/reuse of canonical routes over new articles; require a distinct reader job and evidence/SEO checks before any new URL.
7. Block actions on missing authenticated exports/permissions; never scrape restricted dashboards, invent mapping/share, claim an unattested competitor count or confuse AI citations with visitors and revenue.
